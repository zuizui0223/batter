# Coarse terrain and circuit-topology pilot (V8)
Prospective relative to terrain outcomes; follows exploratory V5/V6 FD results. No entrance-scale or 3D flight claim.
Source: DarkCideS doi:10.1038/s41597-022-01234-4 explicitly reduces public coordinate resolution without quantifying uncertainty. Use only released locations, internally quantized to 0.25-degree grid centres; no output of coordinates, cave keys, maps or fine-scale values. 50 km radius primary and 100 km sensitivity, 5 km graph grid. Results are regional proxies, not proven robust to the unknown original displacement. NOAA ETOPO 2022 v1 60 arc-second surface GeoTIFF (doi:10.25921/fd45-gt74); WorldClim 2.1 10 arc-minute BIO1, BIO4, BIO12, BIO15. Download whole public files rather than transmit cave points to services.
Outcome: paired flight and resource MPD SES, same verified composite cave records and complete taxa; source-wing direct-or-formula study-median V7 method, corroborated encoding repair; primary frequency-weighted leave-focal-out regional pool; sensitivity unweighted and strict names. Keep no site-level output. Fixed trait definition across folds; response reference is descriptive, not independent presence observation. At least 3 complete taxa, 80% coverage, 999 draws, region pool >=max(15,2n), >=10 other caves. 
Predictors: climate plus log richness, coverage, realm, public landscape proxies (canopy height, tree density, freshwater distance, nighttime light), latitude; elevation mean, relief P90-P10, median/90th percentile edge slope, fraction nonnegative elevations. Nonnegative elevation is a coarse land proxy and can misclassify below-sea-level land; no interpretation of coastal barriers. ETOPO local mean on grid sample points, metric distances; source centre neighbourhood and sink boundary of connected land graph (not cave entrance or foraging endpoints).
Four-neighbour undirected electrical network, c=exp(-|delta_elevation|/(5000*0.05)); arbitrary symmetric cost, not calibrated flight resistance. Compare observed log R to uniform-cost same mask, and to mean log R of 16 edge-conductance permutations on the same graph (distribution of local costs and graph topology fixed; spatial arrangement changed). Latter distinguishes arrangement from cost histogram; edge permutation need not be realistic terrain. No inference of wind or 3D. No trait-specific graph until independent movement calibration.
Models fixed ridge alpha=10, numeric standardization and categorical encoding fitted on training folds only, common rows for M0 base, M1 + terrain summaries, M2 + graph features. Leave-one-country-out primary, leave-one-realm-out sensitivity. Country-equal RMSE, within-block MSE differences and count of improved blocks; no treating cave sites as independent. Require >=40 eligible common observations, >=8 countries (>=3 realms for realm analysis), report attrition; no claims of causal effects or confirmatory generality. Negative results retained. Climate and landscape predictors are proxies, no standardized sampling effort available. Country source overlap and site identity concerns remain.
