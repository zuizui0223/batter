## Fixed source candidates and known literature constraints
- O'Malley et al. 2025, doi:10.1038/s41598-025-14315-2, Figshare 10.6084/m9.figshare.26124277.v3. 400 reconstructed flight paths, nine road sites, Barbastella barbastellus, road-surface reference from camera and slope measurements. Free-flying paths are not individually identified by the stated method; site-heldout trajectory prediction may be a distinct endpoint, not individual-level replication.

Data repository not established; inspect Crossref relations/links and DataCite metadata search for exact title/authors only, not contact.
