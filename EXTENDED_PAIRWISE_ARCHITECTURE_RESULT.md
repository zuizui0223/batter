# Extended pairwise architecture robustness

Date: 2026-09-26

## Purpose

Primary species endpoints compare a held-out session with:

- a predictor from the same individual's other session(s);
- a population predictor averaged across other individuals in the same cohort.

This post-primary robustness analysis removes the population average. The same self predictor is
compared directly with **one alternative bat at a time**, always on common horizontal support.

The question is whether specialization architecture survives direct individual-to-individual
competition.

## Results at 5 km

| Panel | Evaluable individuals | Conditional pairwise gain | Conditional self-win | Marginal pairwise gain | Marginal self-win | Place × height |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| *Hypsignathus monstrosus* 2020 | 24 | **+0.115** | **0.765** | +0.008 | 0.603 | **+0.107** |
| *Phyllostomus hastatus* 2022 | 33 | +0.285 | 0.851 | **+0.323** | 0.744 | **−0.038** |
| *P. hastatus* 2023 | 16 | **+0.148** | **0.761** | +0.028 | 0.597 | **+0.120** |

Earlier pairwise checks gave the same qualitative pattern in the focal/first-replication systems:

- *Tadarida* AGL: conditional +0.611, marginal −0.196, interaction +0.807;
- *Tadarida* MSL: conditional +0.643, marginal +0.096, interaction +0.546;
- *Eidolon*: conditional +0.398, marginal +0.013, interaction +0.385.

## Interpretation

The architecture differences are not created by averaging heterogeneous alternative animals.

### *Hypsignathus*

A target night is substantially more identifiable from the same bat when place and height are
combined than from marginal height alone. The weak population-mean effect therefore reflects a
small but widely distributed place-coupled fingerprint, not a pooled-baseline artefact.

### *Phyllostomus* 2022

The altitude-wide architecture becomes even clearer pairwise. Same-bat marginal height
outperforms individual alternatives by +0.323 nats/fix, exceeding the conditional pairwise gain
of +0.285 and producing a negative place × height increment.

Thus the 2022 result is not “no individuality.” It is a different organization of individuality:
**who the bat is is more recoverable from its overall vertical distribution than from additional
place-specific modulation.**

### *Phyllostomus* 2023

The architecture reverses relative to 2022. Conditional pairwise gain is +0.148, marginal only
+0.028, and the place × height increment is +0.120.

This directly supports context variation in the architecture of vertical specialization within
the same species.

## Claim ceiling

These are post-primary robustness analyses. They strengthen interpretation of already frozen
species/context endpoints but do not convert architecture differences into a causal year,
season, colony or behavioural-state effect.
