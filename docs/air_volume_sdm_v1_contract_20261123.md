# Prospective physical-air-volume SDM v0.1 — geometry, observation kernels and synthetic identifiability

**Date: 2026-11-23 JST. Status:** separate source-free mathematical/implementation research branch based on `batter/main`; no modification to JAE manuscript, individual vertical-identity results, ODSP-N2, esdm-E5 or existing data-driven results. This is **not** a new empirical bat result or confirmation that open lidar and bat locations coincide.

## Physical target: the air itself, not stacked 2D surface maps

Let `V` be a set of **audited, known accessible/free-space voxels**, `v`, each with centre `u_v=(x_v,y_v,z_v)` in a consistent local projected metric CRS and **terrain-relative** height in meters (AGL), positive air volume `ΔV_v` m³, or `unknown` when source LiDAR coverage cannot classify it. Let `x_v` be a geometric feature vector measured over the same spatial support. The latent biological intensity `λ_s(u_v,t)` has units expected **call availability / movement event / individual passage opportunity per m³ per unit time**, depending on explicitly defined biological event process. Without individual/event definition, an otherwise fitted λ is only a source-defined *activity intensity*. It is **not** occupancy probability and it must not be called an ordinary 2D SDM suitability score.

The default model (later to be fitted) is
`λ_s(u_v) = exp(β0_s + βH_s z_v + βC_s c_v)`.
For a binary clutter feature `c_v`, this is an illustrative generative family, not a species mechanism. For continuous neighbourhood clutter or signed vegetation distance, units, scale, and source uncertainty need separate controls.

**LiDAR caveat:** a TLS/ALS return cloud records reflected vegetation/ground points and may have *occluded unknown regions*, i.e. **no return is not evidence of empty/free air**. A reliable `known_air` mask requires a documented scanner ray/visibility model or a declared source-specific conservative survey domain. A voxel that intersects known vegetation or is outside reliable illumination is not included in the air integral; neither should be fabricated as air from the absence of returns. Voxel density from point counts is a **return-density proxy**, not vegetation biomass unless angular scanning effort/occlusion are calibrated. Coordinate reference/datum, source day/leaf-on status, airborne vs terrestrial LiDAR geometry and pointwise scanner registration uncertainty must be audited. These are prereqs of empirical interpretation, not discoverable from a 2D coordinate.

## One latent intensity, distinct observation operators

For each original observation unit j (e.g., 3D localised call bin, acoustic detector array footprint, mist-net at a measured height, 2D site label), let `K_{jv}∈[0,1]` be the **known detector/observation probability** that a latent event originating at voxel v is counted in source unit j, with known exposure `E_j` time. The expected count, assuming a valid independent Poisson thinning design, is

`μ_j = E_j Σ_v K_{jv} λ_s(u_v) ΔV_v`.

The **observed count log-likelihood** (up to source count factorials) is `Σ_j (y_j log μ_j − μ_j)`. For exact 3D binned events, an observed bin kernel may be approximately diagonal; for a planar/GBIF observation, K integrates through the vertical column; for acoustic/mist-net observations K is a field-derived spatial footprint with species-specific directivity, clutter attenuation and recording effort. If the same source event could register simultaneously in multiple detector units, the resulting counts may be **dependent** and cannot be multiplied as independent Poissons without a joint observation/capture-history process; this v0.1 family does not silently treat such replicates as independent.

**Critical identification boundary:** declaring a common λ is **not enough** to recover true λ. If K has a nontrivial null space (2D columns only), a species-specific height reallocating λ can leave every y unchanged. If detection `q_{s,z,j}` is unknown and unconstrained, even height-resolved acoustic counts can be generated by mutually incompatible latent 3D worlds. The mathematical information matrix for **free voxel intensities** has rank no greater than K's rank, so a full-rank measurement support (or justified restrictive low-dimensional λ family plus calibrations) is essential for recoverable 3D shapes. A parametric fit can make a **regularised/extrapolated estimate** when the underlying arbitrary 3D intensity is not identified; never present the posterior confidence as direct data identification.

## V0.1 concrete implementation

Write `src/batter/air_volume_sdm_v1.py` with:
1. **Air voxel representation** with exactly one common local (x,y,z) metre CRS and positive cubic metre volume. Do not mix WGS84 degrees and metre distance, MSL and AGL or supported/occluded/vegetation voxels. `known_air` mask is caller-supplied, not inferred by point-return absence. A simple `build_regular_volume(bounds,step_m,known_air_mask)` constructs positions and volumes with a consistent 3D grid.
2. **Return-cloud geometry** `nearest_vegetation_distance_m` and occupancy/proximity proxy computed from a set of points already registered in the same local CRS, with chunked NumPy Euclidean distances for a *small synthetic research panel*. This is NOT an industrial LAS/LAZ streaming solution and does not independently certify empty air/leaf-density. Point cloud semantic classification (vegetation vs ground), sample angular ray effort and occlusion are upstream responsibilities; tests must show empty return cloud and unknown mask cannot be equated to clear air.
3. **2D column kernel** grouping voxels into same horizontal cells, with known per-voxel `q` (default all 1 only for **explicit illustrative experiments**), plus `identity_3D_kernel` and a spatially varying `acoustic_kernel` based on hypothesised field detection probability `q_s(u)`; no assumed omnidirectional range without being declared. For 2D records, q=1 is not justified without an effort model.
4. Generic `expected_observed_counts(lambda, volumes, K, E)` and independent Poisson logscore; validation of all signs/shapes.
5. **Fixed-grid two-feature estimation demonstration only**: take calibrated K known and per-source observed counts y, learn a common log-intensity slope over standardized height + clutter features using fixed [-2,-1.5,...,+2] grid and analytically profiled intercept, supporting train vs heldout independent **plots**. Compare same fixed y and same K under B0 (height only) and B1 (height + point-cloud clutter). The dataset and grid in CI tests are wholly **synthetic known-truth**; these tests show what could be learned with known q, **not** that LiDAR proves bat niche, nor that any real plot generalizes.
6. Identifiability diagnostics: rank/nullity of observed K for arbitrary voxel intensities and an explicit **same planar counts / different species×height latent worlds** witness, with documented difference in true species-height information vs 2D projection.

## Locked synthetic acceptance checks

- Two latent multispecies height worlds with identical XY sums yield the same planar expected y but true species×height information differs. In one world two species stratify as [90,10] and [10,90]; the other is [50,50] both. Correct "unidentified" verdict even when a parametric fitted estimate exists.
- Correct physical `ΔV` and exposure accounting: doubling volume doubles μ, doubling exposure doubles μ; one known 3D observation per voxel has full rank, 2D aggregation rank-deficient.
- A source calibrated K can predict nontrivial synthetic intensity if the known-clutter source mechanism is stable in a heldout synthetic plot; 3D clutter B1 can beat height-only B0. In a known-truth world with β_clutter=0, B1 should not get an unjustified systematic benefit. Results are purely engineering unit tests, not original bats.
- Missing/unknown air cells do not enter the intensity integral. Nonempty vegetation returns cannot be considered verified complete forest, and NaN/unknown exposure or detector K outside [0,1] are hard errors.

## Real source priority and provenance

**Best directly colocated precedent but source RESTRICTED**: Hermans, Koblitz, Bartholomeus et al. 2023, *Movement Ecology* DOI [10.1186/s40462-023-00387-0](https://doi.org/10.1186/s40462-023-00387-0), seven Dutch forest-edge experimental sites, 3 plots/site, eight-microphone arrays at 27 nights, 138 array nights and TLS LiDAR spatially co-registered via the array. Author DataverseNL dataset [DOI 10.34894/CHW0HB](https://dataverse.nl/dataset.xhtml?persistentId=doi:10.34894/CHW0HB), V2, **nine files all restricted** and explicit "Data available upon request". File metadata show `case_study1_bats.csv` (4.0 MB) + `case_study1_lidar_raw.las` (522 MB), `case_study2_bats_pip.csv` + `case_study2_lidar_raw.las` (2.4 GB), `case_study2_lidar_voxel.laz` (583.9 KB), `case_study2_distance_corridor.csv`, `case_study2_distance_species.csv`, `multiple_tracks.csv`, `single_track.csv`. **Do not attempt to bypass access restrictions or assert any full-source result**. These data, even with access, are 3 acoustic **guilds**, dominated by pipistrelles, not reliable species IDs. Published article demonstrates 3D alignment and distance/clutter effects already, hence ecological novelty must be a predictive, holdout-tested use-intensity model rather than co-registration itself.

**Potential open survey resources with non-identical event support:** Smithsonian 2023 Panama BCI airborne LiDAR [10.60635/C34W2W](https://doi.org/10.60635/C34W2W) covers BCI and other plots (~20 pulses/m²); separate EIDC 2023 Jan bat audible/ultrasound data [10.5285/eeb7c6dc-2ad8-4375-ad86-523a6e570170](https://catalogue.ceh.ac.uk/documents/eeb7c6dc-2ad8-4375-ad86-523a6e570170) cover four BCI/Gamboa sites but do not by themselves publish bat 3D positions; access and exact station CRSs must be checked. USGS 3DEP open Entwine point cloud and NABat acoustic grids are not automatically same resolution/3D position and must not be falsely joined.

**Real-data admission next:** original bat xyz or calibrated array-footprint rows and original corresponding LiDAR vegetation-point CRS, explicit rigid transform, plot IDs and repeated exposure with independent heldout **whole sites**, q/visibility proxy bounded, and geographic time alignment. Until then publish only the source-free mathematical engine and source restriction ledger, not a 3D-bat biological effect.

`batter` JAE manuscript, `odsp` N2 and `esdm` E5 remain protected. No reuse of already opened GPS/height datasets for confirmatory new clutter predictors, and no renaming 2D column-integrated acoustic counts as bat flight points. This work is a **prospective independent methodological direction**.
