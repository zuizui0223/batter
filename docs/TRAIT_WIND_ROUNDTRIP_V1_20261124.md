# Trait-specific directional commuting costs — 2026-11-24
Status: exploratory synthetic witness only; no empirical outcome opened or fitted in this branch. No change to main or the roost/air-volume draft branches.

## Biological question
Does morphology × directional atmospheric transport predict the choice of reachable foraging patches beyond Euclidean distance, ordinary wind response, and foraging guild? Central-place foragers pay both outbound and return costs. A location-only scalar power layer cannot express heading dependence, or a time-dependent return trip.

Level-flight illustrative power P=A/u+B*u^3+C with A=2k(mg)^2/(rho*pi*b^2), B=rho*S*CD0/2, span b, area S, mass m. For each direction, optimize P/positive ground speed over an explicitly bounded airspeed grid. This is a reduced mechanical proxy with uncalibrated drag/profile assumptions, not metabolic power. Future directed voxel-graph transition energies must represent ascent/descent, wind/terrain scales and independent reachability and observation masks; no arbitrary negative edge costs or unphysical free flight. Compute minimum round-trip energy separately for outbound and return time slices (or time-expanded graph); add independent time budgets. Do not infer energetic accessibility using straight-line distance if physical route is blocked.

Synthetic witness (same mass 20 g, not real species): span 0.27/0.35 m, area 0.014/0.010 m², AR 5.21/12.25, WL 14.01/19.62 N/m². Two equal-quality resources: A 1000 m along wind; B 1123 m across wind. Default 4 m/s stationary wind gives broad A/B 156.53/155.50 J and narrow A/B 121.74/122.46 J; calm favors A for both. This is a deliberately constructed existence example, not a robust ecological prediction: sensitivity scenarios include loss of reversal. Script: scripts/trait_wind_roundtrip_v1.py.

## Candidate sources and non-overlapping roles
- O'Mara et al. 2021 Current Biology, 10.1016/j.cub.2020.12.042; reported repository 10.5441/001/1.52nn82r9 (access and raw schema not verified in this branch). Tadarida teniotis uplift is prior art and prior batter data use must be audited: not a new independent test by default.
- O'Mara et al. 2019 Frontiers, 10.3389/fevo.2019.00200; annotated supplementary dataset and raw GPS+ACC repository 10.5441/001/1.k8n02jn8. Published 3391 locations, 36 Eidolon helvum, 138 nights. First candidate for comparing proxy against independent within-individual ODBA, not a direct metabolic measurement or an insectivorous species replication. Known headwind–ODBA relationship is not novelty; test predictive increment over wind and speed alone.
- O'Mara et al. 2019 RSOS 10.1098/rsos.181942; Dryad 10.5061/dryad.63q3283; weather-annotated Nyctalus noctula pressure altitude and wingbeat data, original Movebank 10.5441/001/1.7t4b97qf. Known negative weather-height result; verify whether XY/heading is available before admitting directional model. Prior batter use must be audited.
- Hedenstrom & Johansson 2015 10.1242/jeb.031203; von Busse et al. 2014 10.1098/rspb.2014.0452 (verify DOI before citing). Measured aerodynamic power and optimal-speed discrepancies mean absolute energetic predictions require calibration.
- Movebank Env-DATA provides wind/topographic uplift annotations: https://www.movebank.org/cms/movebank-content/env-data-derived-variables . ERA5 0.25-degree wind cannot resolve meter-scale slope turbulence; topographic uplift proxy w~u_h·grad(h) is not a measured altitude-resolved flow, and must not double count resolved vertical motion. Pressure vertical velocity Pa/s requires conversion, not direct use as m/s.
- Resource follow-on: Shi et al. 2025 Dryad 10.5061/dryad.7pvmcvf4s tests bird/insect mixture separation. European BALTRAD/UVA VPTS mostly bird-optimized; raw biological reflectivity is not insect prey density. Require same place/time, appropriate altitude bins and calibrated target separation.
- Sensory follow-on: Stidsholt et al. 2021 10.1126/sciadv.abf1367 already quantifies changing sensory volumes across foraging phases. Bat perception (outgoing and return signal, prey target strength, hearing threshold, beam, clutter, T/RH) is not observer detection kernel K. Frequency alone insufficient.

## Empirical outcome gates (prospective, to lock source-specific thresholds after schema-only audit)
1. Source keys, time, units, individual, roost IDs, heading, wind independent of chosen step, GPS/height uncertainty, commuting vs foraging state, accessible alternatives. Raw source DOI/hash before fitting. Wind at alternative steps, not only observed locations, is essential for choice comparison. Traits mass/span/area measured with consistent definition and independent unit audits (roost V6/V7); never impute all three from guild.
2. Engineering calibration: leave animals/sites out; ODBA and wingbeat are distinct proxy endpoints, not Joules. Compare direct wind/ground and airspeed model vs mechanical prediction; no additional fitted trait-specific slopes using heldout animals. This stage alone does not establish a multi-species trait result.
3. For choice: shared matched available endpoints/steps, equal time and measurement process. B0 distance+state+habitat+guild; B1 B0+wind/support/crosswind with common flexible effects; B2 B1+trait-specific directional predicted cost, evaluated with heldout individuals, independent sites/species and no heldout recalibration. Use fixed alternative sampling with proposal correction if required, per-trip logscore and site/species-level uncertainty. Trait shuffle within comparable taxa/guild where exchangeability defensible; direct unconstrained trait×wind baseline as sensitivity. Need multiple independently tracked species with divergent observed traits; species = unit of cross-species transfer, not GPS row. Explicit calibration uncertainty and sensitivity of aerodynamic coefficients, airspeed bounds, wind and location error.
4. Independent roost composition connection only if real roost coordinates, colony species lists, availability pool, phylogeny and repeated site/time data exist; test round-trip reachable airspace beyond simple distance/climate and source-conditioned wing AR. Do not claim community filtering from one-species trajectory fit.
5. Null physical model + independent prey and sensory terms later. Residual niche overlap/departure is not evidence of competition or avoidance unless independent interaction contrasts, resource/observation uncertainty and alternative causal hypotheses are tested.
