## Decision and next gate
Conditional mathematical ecology: wind in this uniform model does not necessarily promote physical niche separation; area-size heterogeneity compresses and relative overlap increases even as available area shrinks. Can generate small directional crossings, but dominant geometry is nested. This is a candidate physical null for individual-specialization work, not evidence of field overlap or competition. For true 3D add altitude, heterogeneity and independently supported speed/metabolic constraints only after validation. Actual individual wingbeat frequency, power, movement and temperatures not yet paired. AFPT individual analysis would need assumed frequency or measured frequencies; preserve uncertainty.
