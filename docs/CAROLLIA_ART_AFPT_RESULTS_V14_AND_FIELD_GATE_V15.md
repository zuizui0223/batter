## Exact active constraint diagnostic, uniform steady wind, equal fixed max speed
For reciprocal straight legs and one-way distance L, define g±(u,theta)=sqrt(u²-w²sin² theta) ± w cos theta. Require U>w. The unconstrained time-maximal radius is R_T=T/(1/g+(U)+1/g-(U)); both legs at U maximize ground progress. Its energy cost is T P_i(U), independent of theta and w. Thus if T P_i(U) <= E, the time-maximal radius is feasible and joint optimal; with common T and U, individuals have identical geometry. If T P_i(U)>E, the time-maximal radius cannot be attained with both legs at U; mixed or purely energy-active outcomes possible. Conversely, the energy-optimal radius with independently minimized costs is time-feasible iff its fastest energy-optimal pair satisfies travel time <=T. Use tolerance/ties. This is an analytic conditional result, not an empirical ecological law; geometry assumes no resource or obstacles and stable wind, and individual maxima/thermal budgets can differ. Classify time-saturated, energy-saturated and mixed using both bounds rather than comparing arbitrary T/T* ratios.
