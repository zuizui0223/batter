## Published curve check
Currie et al. 2023 DOI 10.1098/rspb.2023.0045 (PMCID PMC10154928) directly measured metabolic (8 bats) and mechanical (4 of these bats) power of P. nathusii. Eq 2.2 Pmech=9.21e-5*u^3+0.176/u+0.0297; Eq 2.4 Pmet=3.73e-4*u^3+2.52/u+0.490 W. Use identical restricted 5–9 m/s airspeed band (Fig 3 common speed bins), constant wind and level direct out-and-back routes. Local numerical calculation with 0.0001 m/s grid. At 4 m/s wind:
- 1 km along wind: mechanical 31.094548 J, metabolic 288.530862 J.
- 1 km across wind: mechanical 28.480221 J, metabolic 258.467797 J.
- Critical across/along distance ratio: 1.09179448 mechanical, 1.11631261 metabolic. At 1100 m across vs 1000 m along, the cheaper path changes with objective. Mechanical across 31.328243 J vs 31.094548 along; metabolic across 284.314577 J vs 288.530862 along.
- Mechanical optimal return and every metabolic optimum hit the 9 m/s upper speed bound in this 4 m/s scenario. Therefore this is a conditional bounded-speed example, not an established reversal for free-flying bats, not robust to speed bound or source parameter uncertainty. Both objectives favor crosswind at equal distance.
- Rounded displayed equations independently minimized over a broad computational grid give minimum P/u at 7.4711 and 10.8554 m/s, unlike the article's reported 6.8 and 9.0. The latter numerical result is outside the admitted 5–9 m/s band. Do NOT claim the article's reported optimum was replicated; require raw-data/code audit of fit, optimization domain, definition and coefficients. No extrapolated optimum used for a biological conclusion.
- Previous synthetic 20 g morphology witness independently replayed locally: default reversal exists but disappears under low and high coefficient scenarios (both prefer A or both prefer B respectively).
