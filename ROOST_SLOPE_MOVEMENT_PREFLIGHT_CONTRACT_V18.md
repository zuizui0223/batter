## Distinct question and gates
A slope is not automatically resistance. Separate directional terrain rate q = (ground elevation at t+dt - ground elevation at t)/dt [m/s], absolute vertical velocity w_bat = (absolute altitude at t+dt - absolute altitude at t)/dt, and modeled windward uplift w_oro = u*dz/dx + v*dz/dy. Ground-relative rate is exactly w_bat-q; never regress that against q and interpret the algebraic coupling as an effect. Ground-following hypothesis: positive q predicts w_bat along paths, independent of wind and step context. Uplift hypothesis: windward gradient predicts additional climb or ascent choice beyond along-track q, with wind matched in time and height; distinguish use vs availability. Predicted slope response can differ between open-space and clutter-adapted guilds but no cross-species inference from one individual/species. Need known vertical datum, independently sampled DEM at compatible horizontal resolution, actual timestamps and native simultaneous x/y/z, individual/session IDs, reasonable step interval vs vertical error, signed wind vector at compatible support, and sufficient opposing slope/wind states. No inferred cave entrance locations published. No claiming local uplift from coarse ERA5 without resolution audit.
