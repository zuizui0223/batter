# Integrated ecological-contingency paper spine v1

## Status

Integrated narrative architecture after the frozen Pteropus terrain-confound audit.

The existing JAE v0.3.8 manuscript and its original frozen analyses remain intact on `main`. This branch develops a single integrated paper without rewriting the historical evidence hierarchy.

## Central question

**When does individual identity organize vertical space use beyond horizontal occupancy, and what ecological boundary conditions determine whether that organization is expressed?**

The integrated paper does not claim that centered vertical individuality is universal. It treats heterogeneity among systems as the biological result that must be explained.

## Evidence hierarchy

### Tier 1 — original confirmatory comparative result

Six original paper panels.

- coarse-horizontal standardized vertical identity: supported across the original panel family;
- session-centered distribution-shape identity: supported in five comparative panels;
- *Tadarida teniotis*: required boundary case for centered shape.

Primary statement:

> Repeatable individual organization of vertical space use can persist after coarse horizontal occupancy and additive altitude level are removed.

### Tier 2 — post-freeze mechanism localization

These are stress tests / localization analyses, not independent confirmations.

Where centered individuality is present it persists after:
- speed conditioning;
- speed x turning conditioning;
- 2-km, 500-m and, where supported, 250-m place x state matching;
- multi-day separation;
- broad same-night-context controls.

These results narrow the mechanism space but do not identify a single cause.

### Tier 3 — genuinely external source tests

Report every source under its own frozen design and retain negative results.

- *Nyctalus noctula*: frozen prospective primary FAIL;
- *Hipposideros armiger / H. pratti*: frozen source-level prospective FAIL;
- *Myotis vivesi*: separately frozen four-individual small-panel FAIL;
- *Pteropus poliocephalus*: separately frozen four-individual small-panel MSL-centered PASS; post-outcome frozen terrain audit shows terrain-only identity (+0.37887, p=0.0034) and a smaller but retained terrain-adjusted signal (+0.03231, p=0.0023).

Do not summarize these as an estimated prevalence and do not treat the four tests as one exchangeable binomial experiment.

## Biological synthesis

The safe synthesis after the terrain diagnostic is:

> Centered vertical individuality is recurrent but heterogeneous among bat systems.

The leading ecological explanation is explicitly post-hoc:

> Stable centered vertical individuality may be favored when animals repeatedly solve vertically structured foraging problems anchored to persistent spatial resources; systems dominated by mobile prey, surface-constrained feeding, or strong transient environmental forcing may show weaker stable vertical organization.

Preferred mechanistic language:
- **resource anchoring**
- **vertical opportunity**
- competing roles of flight morphology and environmental forcing

Avoid using a simple frugivore-versus-insectivore binary as the causal claim.

## Unit of comparative evidence

Repeated years/panels of *Phyllostomus hastatus* are not independent species-level confirmations of a trophic predictor.

For the post-hoc ecological contrast, species/system identity—not panel count—is the appropriate qualitative unit.

Current positive-side taxa/systems:
- *Eidolon helvum*
- *Hypsignathus monstrosus*
- *Phyllostomus hastatus*
- *Pteropus poliocephalus* (terrain-adjusted residual signal retained; diagnostic remains post-outcome)

Current weak/unsupported side:
- *Tadarida teniotis* centered-shape endpoint
- *Nyctalus noctula*
- *Hipposideros* source
- *Myotis vivesi*

No formal guild-effect p-value is authorized from this opened set.

## Terrain-audit consequence for the integrated story

The frozen post-outcome terrain audit resolves the main confound raised against the external *Pteropus* PASS.

- centered MSL primary: calibrated excess +0.16873, p=0.0001;
- centered terrain-only identity: +0.37887, p=0.0034;
- centered MSL-minus-DEM proxy: +0.03231, p=0.0023;
- descriptive terrain-adjusted/original excess ratio: 0.192.

The ratio is not a causal mediation fraction. The defensible conclusion is that the source is strongly terrain-sensitive while retaining a smaller terrain-relative individual signal. This preserves *Pteropus* as one external positive system, but not as evidence that coarse 5-km horizontal standardization removes fine-scale landscape structure.

## Claims prohibited in the integrated paper

- "centered vertical individuality is universal in bats";
- "external validation succeeded in 1/4 bats" as a prevalence estimate;
- "plant feeding causes vertical individuality";
- treating three *Phyllostomus* panels as three independent species-level trophic confirmations;
- presenting configurable spatial individuality as a predictive general law;
- relabelling post-outcome Nyctalus analyses as prospective confirmation;
- treating the resource-anchoring hypothesis as preregistered or confirmed.

## Target conceptual contribution

The broad ecological contribution is not merely that individuals differ.

It is:

> **The expression of individuality may itself depend on ecological opportunity: stable individual organization becomes visible when the environment repeatedly presents individuals with structured, repeatable choices.**

This is a general hypothesis generated by the bat evidence, not a confirmed cross-taxon law.
