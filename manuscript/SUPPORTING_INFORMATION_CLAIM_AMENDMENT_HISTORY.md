# Supporting Information: claim amendment history

This file records changes in interpretation that followed analyses whose rules were frozen before their outcomes were opened. It is included for transparency; the main manuscript presents the current biological questions and results rather than narrating the full development history.

## 1. Original conditional-versus-marginal architecture

The first comparative synthesis used the sign of the session-level contrast

`G_adv = G_cond - G_marg`

to describe panels as more conditional- or marginal-dominant. At that stage, the contrast was interpreted relative to zero.

## 2. Estimator calibration superseded the architecture classification

A frozen whole-session label-permutation audit showed that the finite-sample exchangeability expectation of `G_adv` was negative and panel-specific rather than zero. The same audit showed that the ordinary marginal vertical distribution inherited each individual's own horizontal occupancy weighting.

The most important biological consequence was the 2022 *Phyllostomus hastatus* panel: its original `G_adv = -0.120` changed to a common-cell conditional increment of approximately +0.0066 after self and other profiles were integrated over identical horizontal weights. The apparent qualitative architecture contrast therefore disappeared.

The architecture labels were withdrawn rather than retuned. The corrected paper-level estimand became common-cell vertical identity relative to a panel-specific session-label exchangeability distribution.

## 3. Cross-panel confound audit

After the common-cell result was frozen, two reviewer-facing gaps were identified.

First, a fixed x-y/time-only 1-km endpoint-neighbourhood exclusion was extended prospectively to the comparative panels. Four of five comparative panels passed the frozen rule. *Eidolon helvum* retained a strong calibrated signal but fell below its predeclared minimum evaluable-individual threshold. The motivating *Tadarida teniotis* panel had already failed its corresponding endpoint-neighbourhood test.

Second, intuitive biological translations were calibrated against their own finite-sample nulls. Pairwise self-identification null means ranged from approximately 0.50 to 0.58 rather than being universally 0.5. The raw focal absolute AGL separation also had a positive null mean rather than zero.

These results strengthened the methodological conclusion that the complete prediction pipeline, not an intuitive reference value, defines the relevant exchangeability baseline.

## 4. Final additive tag/device altitude-bias audit

A final audit tested whether repeatable altitude identity could be generated by constant device-specific altitude offsets.

Before numeric stationary heights were opened, an x-y/time-only preflight froze which panels had enough shared stationary support for empirical offset correction.

The primary test was available to every panel: each retained session was translated to zero median before vertical binning. This transformation exactly removes any additive constant altitude offset and also removes genuine session-specific mean-height differences.

Five of six panels retained calibrated vertical-distribution shape identity after this transformation:

- *Eidolon helvum*: PASS;
- *Hypsignathus monstrosus*: PASS;
- *P. hastatus* 2022: PASS;
- *P. hastatus* 2023: PASS;
- *P. hastatus* 2016: PASS;
- *T. teniotis*: FAIL.

The resulting paper-level interpretation changed accordingly. Additive tag/device offsets are not a general explanation for the comparative pattern, but the absolute vertical-location component in *Tadarida* cannot be separated from possible device offset with the archived data.

The stationary preflight admitted only *H. monstrosus* and *P. hastatus* 2016. Both retained calibrated identity after empirical stationary-height offset correction.

## 5. Declared stopping point

The tag/device altitude-bias audit was declared in advance as the final new scientific audit before submission.

Residual limitations—including fine-scale within-cell horizontal fidelity, central-place structure, tag-specific differences in error variance, behavioural-state heterogeneity and incomplete temporal overlap—are reported as limitations rather than used to open further outcome-driven analysis families.

No failed result was rescued by changing radii, grids, bins, sample-size gates, permutation counts, seeds, source admission rules or panel membership after its outcome was known.
