# Strategy-maintenance literature positioning v1

## Conceptual gap

The individual-specialization literature has focused mainly on **causes of between-individual niche variation**—especially intraspecific and interspecific competition, ecological opportunity and predation (Araújo et al. 2011). The present study addresses a different stage of the process: once persistent individual strategies exist, **what keeps an individual returning to its own solution?**

That origin-maintenance distinction matters because a factor can generate specialization without being required for its continued expression. Historical competition, for example, could help establish differentiated strategies even when current co-presence no longer produces active spatial avoidance.

## Existing precedents for experience-dependent persistence

Votier et al. (2017) proposed an exploration-refinement mechanism in northern gannets: immature birds were more exploratory, whereas experienced breeders showed stronger route and foraging-site fidelity. The interpretation was that initially explored solutions become refined and canalized with experience.

Barchi et al. (2013) showed experimentally that big brown bats developed individually stereotyped flight paths in familiar clutter, retained them across altered starting positions, and resumed accustomed paths after approximately one month.

Toledo et al. (2020) showed that wild Egyptian fruit bats repeatedly make goal-directed flights and shortcuts consistent with learned spatial representations rather than random search.

These studies establish biological plausibility for persistent solution reuse in movement, but they do not demonstrate that learning causes the vertical individuality in the present datasets.

## What is distinctive here

The present evidence adds a geometry that the above work does not directly test:

- personal vertical organization persists across multi-day self-history;
- it remains after 500-m place x broad kinematic-state matching;
- it persists relative to local terrain;
- it usually does **not** produce added vertical segregation or co-presence-dependent separation.

This combination suggests that maintained individuality need not mean exclusive ownership of a spatial niche. Multiple individuals can retain different, overlapping solutions to the same broad movement problem.

## Safe interpretation

Preferred:
> Persistent personal solution reuse is a mechanistic framework consistent with the observed persistence-and-overlap pattern.

Do not write:
> Memory or learning causes the observed individual strategies.

Remaining proximate carriers include learned information, stable morphology/performance, sub-500-m route or resource fidelity, and fine environmental reaction norms.
