## Formatting

Movement Ecology currently requests:
- double-line spacing;
- line and page numbering;
- editable DOC/DOCX/RTF or TeX/LaTeX manuscript;
- figure title ≤15 words;
- figure legend ≤300 words;
- figures typically ~300 dpi at final dimensions;
- structured Research abstract ≤350 words.

## LLM disclosure

Current Movement Ecology Research guidance states that LLMs do not satisfy authorship criteria and their use should be documented in the Methods or another suitable section.

- [x] Draft AI-use statement included in Methods.
- [ ] Human author(s) review wording and confirm it accurately describes actual use.

## Journal strategy

**First shot: Movement Ecology.**

Reason:
- explicit scope covers patterns, mechanisms, causes and consequences of organismal movement;
- this manuscript's central question is movement-state exchangeability / individual heterogeneity;
- closely related individual-space specialization work has appeared in Movement Ecology.

**Stretch alternative: Journal of Animal Ecology.**

JAE explicitly seeks animal-ecology work that advances broad ecological principles and conclusions beyond the focal species. A JAE submission would require pushing the paper harder toward the general theory of population niche breadth versus individual exchangeability and accepting higher desk-rejection risk.

## Remaining submission blockers

1. authorship/affiliations;
2. source ethics/permit metadata;
3. figures;
4. permanent repository DOI;
5. final editable manuscript formatting.
