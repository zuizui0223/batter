## Wording ceiling

Preferred:
> Vertical-state organization was temporally repeatable within individual bats but poorly exchangeable among individuals.

Allowed:
> The result is consistent with individual specialization in vertical space use.

Do not state:
- individuals have fixed behavioral personalities;
- the vertical differences are learned;
- differences are adaptive;
- the result measures height above vegetation or terrain;
- individual specialization is universal in the species;
- the same result would hold in another colony or season;
- the original ODSP species-level transfer endpoint becomes positive.
