## Figure 1 — Why pooled vertical airspace can hide individual non-exchangeability

Conceptual schematic only.

Show three layers:

1. a vertically thick pooled population distribution;
2. two individuals that overlap horizontally but differ in predictive vertical identity;
3. the two estimands:
   - marginal identity: self-versus-other prediction using P(z);
   - conditional identity: self-versus-other prediction using P(z|x,y).

The diagram must explicitly state that a positive conditional advantage is **not** proof of a
stable individual-specific cell × height interaction.

ODSP motivation can appear as a small factual anchor:
- 4.022 effective vertical states;
- pooled P(z|x,y) failed in both sealed bats.
