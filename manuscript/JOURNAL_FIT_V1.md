# Journal fit audit v1 — Movement Ecology versus Journal of Animal Ecology

Date checked: 2026-09-26.

## Movement Ecology — primary

Current official scope:

Movement Ecology publishes empirical and theoretical work in which ecology of whole-organism movement is the central theme, including research on movement patterns, mechanisms, causes and consequences across taxa.

Fit to batter:
- vertical flight state is the focal response;
- identity exchangeability is evaluated through movement prediction;
- the paper explicitly connects individual heterogeneity to the construction of population movement niches;
- prior closely related work on seasonal individual specialization in bat space use has appeared in this journal.

Main weaknesses:
- eight individuals;
- one population;
- secondary reanalysis;
- vertical axis is MSL altitude rather than AGL.

These weaknesses constrain generality but do not move the paper outside journal scope.

## Journal of Animal Ecology — stretch route

Current JAE scope emphasizes research that develops, tests or advances broad ecological principles and expects conclusions extending beyond the focal species or region.

The manuscript can be positioned for JAE around:

> population niche breadth and individual exchangeability are separate ecological properties.

That is a broad principle.

Desk risk is nevertheless materially higher because:
- empirical n is eight individuals;
- the dataset is a single-species public reanalysis;
- mechanism decomposition ends with a composite/unresolved identity signal rather than a clean causal driver.

## Decision

Submission order:
1. Movement Ecology
2. Journal of Animal Ecology only if the manuscript is strengthened by an independent replication or a stronger general comparative component
3. specialized bat / behavioural ecology journal if needed

Do not delay the current paper solely to chase JAE fit.
