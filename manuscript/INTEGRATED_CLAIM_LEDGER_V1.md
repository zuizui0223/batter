## Purpose

This ledger defines the maximum defensible claim for the single integrated manuscript. It preserves the chronological distinction among the frozen original archive, post-freeze mechanism localization, genuinely external source tests, and post-outcome ecological synthesis.

## Required manuscript hierarchy

1. **Original confirmation:** establish the centered-shape phenomenon and the *Tadarida* boundary.
2. **Mechanism localization:** show which broad explanations fail to absorb the signal where it exists.
3. **External boundary tests:** retain all prospective PASS/FAIL outcomes under their own designs.
4. **Terrain diagnostic:** show that *Pteropus* is strongly terrain-sensitive but not eliminated by DEM subtraction.
5. **Ecological hypothesis generation:** derive resource anchoring × vertical opportunity as the next falsifiable prediction.

## Prohibited compression

Do not compress the evidence into:
- "6/6 plant users PASS versus 0/4 predators FAIL";
- "1/4 external replications succeeded" as a prevalence statistic;
- "terrain explains 81% of Pteropus";
- "resource anchoring caused the observed heterogeneity";
- "three *Phyllostomus* years are three independent species-level confirmations".

The integrated paper is strongest when failures define the boundary of the phenomenon rather than being treated as failed attempts to accumulate positive replications.
