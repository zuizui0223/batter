# Source-study ethics provenance v0.3.3

Verified: 2026-09-27

This file records the animal-use approvals reported by the original studies whose public tracking
data are reanalysed in `batter`. No new animal capture, handling or instrumentation was
conducted by the present study.

Reported approval:

- ICNF — Instituto de Conservação da Natureza e Florestas, Portugal

## Manuscript policy

The main manuscript may summarize these approvals, but this provenance file should remain the
auditable source-by-source ledger. Any future addition of animal tracking data would require a new
ethics-provenance entry; however, the current public-data source universe is frozen and no new
datasets belong in v0.3.3.
