# Vertical specialization architecture synthesis v1

Date: 2026-09-26

## Concept

Cross-night vertical identity can be separated predictively into two components:

```text
conditional identity gain
= marginal-height identity gain
+ identity × location increment
```

where:

- **marginal-height identity** asks whether the same animal repeats an overall vertical
  distribution across nights;
- **identity × location** asks how much additional identity appears only after horizontal place
  is known.

This distinguishes two ecological architectures of vertical individual specialization:

1. **altitude-wide specialization** — individuals differ mainly in their overall vertical
   distribution;
2. **place-coupled specialization** — individuals differ mainly in which vertical layers they use
   at particular horizontal places.

These are endpoints of a continuum, not fitted latent classes.

## 5-km architecture table

| Taxon / context | Evaluable individuals | Conditional identity | Marginal height | Place × height | Positive conditional |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| *Tadarida teniotis* | 6 | +0.428 | +0.052 | **+0.376** | 5/6 |
| *Eidolon helvum* | 20 | +0.219 | +0.002 | **+0.217** | 17/20 |
| *Hypsignathus monstrosus* | 24 | +0.029 | −0.021 | **+0.050** | 13/24 |
| *Phyllostomus hastatus*, 2022 | 33 | +0.056 | **+0.176** | **−0.120** | 14/33 |
| *P. hastatus*, 2023 | 16 | +0.033 | +0.013 | +0.020 | 11/16 |
| *P. hastatus*, 2016 La Gruta | 10 | +0.058 | +0.016 | +0.041 | 6/10 |

The first three taxa show interaction-dominated vertical identity: their individual signature
becomes substantially stronger when vertical state is conditioned on place.

The 2022 *P. hastatus* panel is qualitatively different. Its self-information is stronger in the
overall vertical distribution than in place-conditioned vertical organization. The resulting
place × height increment is negative.

## A prospective stability test within *Phyllostomus*

After the 2022 dry-season endpoint had produced an altitude-wide architecture, an untouched 2016
dry-season panel was frozen as a prospective replication of that architecture before its numeric
height outcomes were opened.

The preregistered prediction required positive marginal identity, a majority of marginal-positive
individuals, negative mean and majority identity × location effects, and marginal identity larger
than conditional identity.

It **failed**.

At 5 km in La Gruta 2016:

- evaluable individuals: 10;
- conditional identity: +0.058;
- marginal identity: +0.016;
- identity × location: +0.041;
- 60% marginal-positive;
- only 50% negative identity × location.

Thus the strong altitude-wide 2022 pattern is not a stable dry-season property that simply recurs
in an earlier dry-season panel.

The same species also returned a weakly positive place-coupled architecture in 2023
(conditional +0.033, marginal +0.013, interaction +0.020), with substantial colony
heterogeneity.

## Direct pairwise robustness

The architecture contrast survives removal of the pooled other-individual baseline.

At 5 km:

- *Hypsignathus*: conditional pairwise +0.115, marginal +0.008, interaction +0.107;
- *Phyllostomus* 2022: conditional +0.285, marginal +0.323, interaction −0.038;
- *P. hastatus* 2023: conditional +0.148, marginal +0.028, interaction +0.120.

Together with the earlier *Tadarida* and *Eidolon* pairwise checks, this shows that both
place-coupled and altitude-wide architectures persist when the target bat is compared against
individual alternatives one at a time.

## Ecological interpretation

The general result is no longer that vertical specialization must be place-specific.

Instead:

> **Individual vertical specialization has an ecological architecture. Identity can be encoded
> in an animal-wide altitude distribution, in a place-specific vertical routing rule, or in a
> mixture of both, and that architecture is not necessarily fixed even within a species.**

Three phylogenetically and ecologically distinct focal taxa are place-coupled under their primary
endpoints. *P. hastatus* demonstrates that this is not a mathematical inevitability: one
independent panel is strongly altitude-wide, while other temporal panels do not reproduce the
same architecture.

This makes the negative *Phyllostomus* replication biologically informative rather than a failed
generality test.

## What is supported versus open

Supported:

- cross-night individual vertical information exists in multiple bat systems;
- in several systems that information is mostly revealed by place × height coupling;
- an altitude-wide architecture can also occur;
- the strong 2022 altitude-wide *Phyllostomus* architecture did not prospectively repeat in the
  2016 dry-season panel.

Open:

- which environmental or behavioural contexts switch the architecture;
- whether architecture changes within the same individual;
- whether route memory, resource distributions, wind fields, social context or reproductive state
  drives the switch;
- whether the spatial grain of specialization is a stable species trait.

## Claim ceiling

Architecture differences are observational and measured with source-specific native vertical
coordinates. They do not establish personality, learning, optimality, foraging state, or a causal
season effect. The failed 2016 dry-season prediction specifically argues against a simple
dry-versus-wet explanation.
