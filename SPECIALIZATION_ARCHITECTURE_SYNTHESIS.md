# Vertical identity architecture synthesis v2

Date: 2026-09-27

## Concept

Cross-night vertical identity is summarized by two predictive quantities:

```text
G_cond = self-versus-other gain for P(z | x,y)
G_marg = self-versus-other gain for P(z)
G_adv  = G_cond - G_marg
```

We use:

- **conditional-dominant** when knowing horizontal place increases predictive self-information;
- **marginal-dominant** when the animal-wide height distribution carries more self-information.

These are predictive architectures. `G_adv` is not automatically a latent statistical
individual × location interaction and does not prove stable place-specific routing.

## Focal reconciliation

*Tadarida* has strong conditional dominance in the session-level analysis, and conditional
identity is temporally repeatable in a separate early/late identity-assignment test.

But a stronger frozen residual-map test after marginal altitude adjustment gives p=0.160 and does
not pass.

Therefore:

- supported: horizontal location adds predictive individual information;
- not established: a stable individual-specific cell × height residual map.

## Pairwise robustness

The pooled population baseline is not driving the comparative architecture:

- *Tadarida* MSL: conditional +0.643, marginal +0.096;
- *Eidolon*: +0.398, +0.013;
- *Hypsignathus*: +0.115, +0.008;
- *Phyllostomus* 2022: +0.285, +0.323;
- *P. hastatus* 2023: +0.148, +0.028.

Thus conditional-dominant and marginal-dominant directions survive direct self-versus-alternative
comparisons.

## Prospective within-species test

The strong 2022 marginal-dominant *P. hastatus* architecture was prospectively tested in an
untouched 2016 dry-season panel and did not repeat.

This blocks a simple “dry season causes marginal dominance” account.

## Ecological interpretation

The general result is not one universal 3-D strategy.

> **Repeatable vertical identity has multiple predictive architectures: in some systems,
> individual information becomes much stronger after horizontal place is known; in another,
> individual-wide height is more informative. The balance itself can be context dependent.**

The biological mechanism producing these predictive architectures remains unresolved.
