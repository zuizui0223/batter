# Post-freeze bat mechanism programme: decision checkpoint 2026-10-08

**Scope:** This is a decision / stop ledger, not a claim of new biological evidence. It reconciles mature JAE, the laboratory five-bat source, externally published sensorimotor systems and the synthetic null corrections.

## The biological question that remains alive
Do different bats maintain *distinct mappings from the same ecological opportunity to actions*, and do those mappings **change prey success/energy budget** while bats spatially overlap, without requiring exclusive territory or fine-scale vertical partitioning?

This goes beyond (a) means differ between five bats, (b) within-archive label correspondence, (c) broad personality/context consistency, or (d) another model that happens to reconstruct the observed 3D routes.

## Grounded prior programme
1. Wild JAE comparative work: repeatable centered vertical-use shape under constrained spatial co-use; no supported *positive* excess contemporaneous segregation in the previously examined panels. No demonstrated mechanism, exclusive resource access or fitness response.
2. Laboratory *Rhinolophus nippon*: 45 trajectories/5 identified bats/7 obstacle configurations, strong existing cross-configuration motor-signature correspondence; post-outcome target-blind scalar gain +0.443714 vs zero, p=.0003 conditional label permutation but five-bat cluster CI crosses 0 and 2 bats worsen. It does NOT identify a unique physical flight equation.
3. Subsequent synthetic-only calibration: identity relabelling does not provide a robust test of **mean** policy without nuisance variance assumptions; whole-cell sign-flip is an exact known-context-mean oracle under independent symmetric residuals only. The current public five-bat design does not supply those independently known means. A simple same-sample mean replacement violates algebraic residual-sum support.
4. These qualifications **do not refute** distributional individuality, the existing raw effect sizes, or the separately frozen JAE outcomes. Mean specificity, distributional-scale identity, reaction-norm identity, sensor-level policy and wild adaptive consequence are *different estimands*.

## Source opportunities ranked by evidence fit (not by file size)
- **Direct 3D flight repeated challenge:** none of the publicly screened sources verifiably cross *physical bat × same matched obstacle contrast × independent occasion* with simultaneous functional outcomes. STOP primary field-causality inference.
- **Perched sensory behavior, 2025 Wilkinson:** public MAT data keyed by five bats and differing clutter locations, but not free 3D flight, dates/crossover unknown. HOLD as separate sensory-context pilot only; do not substitute sensor angles for wild niche shapes.
- **Nishiumi 2024 bat-moth pursuits:** public 3D/head/pulse files and physical bat IDs, but maximum three distinct dates for any bat and no verified repeated matched obstacle challenge. STOP for frozen four-occasion gate.
- **Forli & Yartsev 2023 group flight:** ample bat/session support in published paper but full trajectories request-only; context crossing unknown. HOLD_SOURCE_ACCESS, not 'population proven'.
- **Wohlgemuth et al. 2018 obstacle flights:** n=3 with unmatched individual flight-space geometries. STOP population-level individual reaction norm.

## Single falsifiable next ecology criterion
After independently verified bat×challenge×occasion source gate, freeze a jointly observed:
(A) individual-specific contrast in a **matched physical 3D obstacle/sensory task**, and
(B) independent target acquisition / search time / prey capture / measured energy cost
with blinded conditioning and no metric selection after looking at results.

**Falsifiers**:
- Personal contrast not reproducible on independently held-out same-challenge occasions after bat noise/device correction → repeatable policy-response claim unsupported.
- Contrast reproducible but success/cost unchanged (adequate sensitivity) → context-specific individuality may be biologically real but **adaptive benefit unestablished**.
- Group-average benefit only, no bat×challenge response → supports shared flexibility, not individual specialization mechanism.
- Device geometry itself changes soundfield across treatment → functional acoustic-buffering mechanism remains confounded (see #93 physics gate).

## Stop rule
No more reanalysis of 45 Rhinolophus trajectories for ad hoc endpoints or relabelled "independent confirmation". No more numerical simulations solely to chase an apparently significant result. Only proceed when (1) an eligible independent source becomes available, (2) a human reviewer identifies a concrete methodological defect needing verification, or (3) a separately specified prospective study obtains the needed design information. Document this as an evidentiary ceiling rather than negative proof that learning, stable personal control or adaptation cannot exist.


## V4→V5 decision update: verified design-based path, but not individual mechanism

- The prior unknown-environment-mean V4 no-go is now [officially executed](https://github.com/zuizui0223/batter/actions/runs/37786267344): Gaussian *synthetic* 120-archive p<=.05 rejection frequencies **equal sigma** bat-label 5.83%, externally known-mu oracle 8.33%, same-sample-mu plug-in 6.67%; **unequal sigma** label 14.17%, known-mu 6.67%, plug-in 12.50%. Monte Carlo variability is high. The stronger proof is algebraic: individually signed fitted residuals violate their fixed zero-sum constraint, so plug-in sign testing is not an exact group-invariant procedure even if any particular simulation rate looks acceptable.
- The **prospective treatment-assignment alternative V5** has been frozen and **executed**: [Fisher exact paired synthetic CI 37792159876](https://github.com/zuizui0223/batter/actions/runs/37792159876), [authoritative receipt](PAIRED_RANDOMIZATION_EXACT_SHARP_NULL_EXECUTED_RESULT_V5.md). Under 15 genuinely coin-randomized matched pairs (5 *fictional* bats × 3 sessions), every one of 32768 legal allocations is enumerated. Conditional false rejection = 1608/32768 = **4.9072%** at nominal 5%, regardless of unequal bat baseline/slot magnitudes or unknown additive context means.
- **Critical identifiability ceiling:** a truly **homogeneous** effect across bats still yields strong rejection of the *no-treatment* sharp null (illustrative p=1/32768), hence **does not establish personal reaction-norm heterogeneity**. That ecological question still requires independent bat-specific response reproducibility and variance/device controls, plus task success and function separately. This is standard design-based causal inference, **not a newly discovered bat result or methodological theorem**.
- The published previous recordings did not randomize matched obstacle challenges; V5 cannot retrofit treatment-assignment semantics to observational records. In particular, it cannot revise PR #79 conditional p or infer a learned bat-specific policy, acoustic-spatial substitution or fitness benefit.
- **Operational priority unchanged:** do not create further same-archive nominal p-values. Scientific manuscript and synthetic design records are ready for human assessment; a biological mechanism requires an externally eligible source/prospectively randomized data, not a new analytic variation of existing five-bat measurements.
