## Interpretation

The direction is positive:

> enriched bats showed greater residual divergence in the frozen three-trait behavioral-change vector.

But the randomized evidence is not sufficient to establish that enrichment caused increased among-individual differentiation.

Therefore the allowed conclusion is:

> **Randomized enriched early experience did not provide confirmatory evidence that the amount of laboratory personality differentiation increased beyond the common treatment-group shift.**

This result does **not** negate the source paper's environmental effect on later wild foraging.

Instead it separates two ecological effects:

[
	ext{environment changes behavior}
]

from:

[
	ext{environment increases among-individual differentiation}.
]

The first is supported in the source study.

The second is not supported by the frozen primary.

## Biological consequence

The data do not support a simple story in which enrichment generates individuality by merely increasing dispersion in Boldness / Exploration / Activity.

A more defensible current interpretation is:

> **early experience can calibrate later behavior without necessarily increasing the amount of laboratory personality variation.**

This is important for the batter programme because it narrows the formation mechanism.

The juvenile-history result still shows that own history becomes increasingly informative.

But randomized environmental enrichment, in this source, does not establish that external experience creates extra behavioral divergence among individuals.

Thus:

[
	ext{history dependence}

eq
	ext{automatic variance amplification}.
]

## Same-source ceiling

**STOP_CONFIRMATORY_INDIVIDUALIZATION_RESCUE.**

The source may still be cited as:
- a randomized early-experience manipulation;
- evidence that early environment can affect later foraging behavior;
- a negative boundary for the stronger claim that enrichment increases multivariate laboratory individuality.

Any additional analyses are exploratory unless independently motivated and clearly separated from this primary.

## Programme-level implication

The public-data evidence now separates:

1. **Formation / refinement**
   - own movement history becomes more identity-informative during development;
   - randomized enrichment does not confirm increased laboratory personality divergence.

2. **Maintenance under perturbation**
   - *Pipistrellus kuhlii* sensory-masker identity retention supported;
   - *Eptesicus fuscus* central auditory perturbation identity retention supported.

3. **Cross-task portability**
   - *Rhinolophus* low-dimensional movement policy predicts unseen obstacle contexts.

Therefore the strongest current public-data synthesis is not:

> experience creates individual specialization by increasing behavioral variance.

It is:

> **persistent individual organization can be history-refined and can survive substantial context perturbation, while the causal process that first creates the between-individual differences remains only partly identified.**
