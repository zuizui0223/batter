For each unordered distinct label pair s,t in same paired tower-night-hour, counts A=y_above,s*y_below,t, B=y_above,t*y_below,s; retain A+B>0. This composite response A/(A+B) compares orientation of two recorded passes, not independent individual observations. Equal pair weights within each informative event, equal event weights within tower-night, equal tower-night weights.
