# GEDI trait macro-SDM — prospective design v1 (2026-11-25)

Status: design frozen before new model outcomes; no new ecological estimates. Separate from PRs #102 (resource/activity) and #103 (physical voxels/observation kernel), and frozen JAE. Do not reuse individual GPS tracks as macro-level validation. Scope: echolocating bats, forest/woodland landscapes within GEDI coverage; first Iberia, then continental Europe and independent North American region, then other realms if source overlap supports them.

## Prior art and falsifiable novelty
- Froidevaux et al. (2016), 10.1016/j.rse.2015.12.038: local airborne LiDAR and trait-dependent forest use.
- Andalusia (2022), 10.1038/s41598-022-07229-w: nine forest specialists, detailed inventory including clutter proxies; forest structure/composition more important than climate, roost ecology mattered, flight traits did not convincingly explain training gain. Training-gain dependency is not the same estimand as a directional trait × structure slope; not proof of climate dominance or omission of all vertical structure.
- Portugal (2024), 10.1080/15481603.2024.2374150: 59 locations, three spatial replicates, GEDI+SAR occupancy and imperfect detection. GEDI bat SDM itself is NOT novel.
- Conenna et al. (2021), 10.1111/geb.13278: global aridity–flight/acoustic traits (915 species, multiple imputation); structural mediation was proposed, not established by their study.
- Novelty to test: does the vertically resolved vegetation profile, beyond equivalent 2D cover/total plant area/height, restore transferable trait-specific structure associations across spatial grains and regions? Is the aridity–trait association attenuated when vertical structure is measured? This is conditional association, not causal mediation without additional assumptions.

## Model and estimands
At each horizontal cell x, let d(x,z) denote mean PAVD at stratum z, p(x,z)=d(x,z)/sum_z d(x,z) where denominator positive. Summary D=5 sum d, H=RH98; use PAVD 0–5 and 5–10 m lower-stratum density, above/below relative canopy-half fractions, foliage height diversity. Primary contrast: lower-canopy allocation fraction conditional on total PAI, RH98 and cover, within forested cells (forest fraction >=0.3; 0.1/0.5 sensitivity). No assertion of true flyable gap from PAVD. Zero vegetation is a separate land-cover category, not missing PAVD.
- Nested matched models with same observations/effort, training rows, weights, spatial folds, hyperparameter budget: M0 climate + terrain + forest fraction/composition + accessibility/roost covariates with species intercepts and species-specific climatic responses; M1 adds cover, total PAI, RH98 and their trait interactions; M2 adds vertical allocation features and species random slopes without trait interactions; M3 adds measured trait × allocation interactions, retaining M2 random slopes. M1 also controls species traits × climate/2D covariates, roost × forest/height and body size. M2 vs M1 isolates profile incremental prediction; M3 vs M2 isolates trait sharing beyond profile; M3 vs M1 joint benefit. Match model regularization (nested CV); no selection from test data. No model can learn absolute intensity from presence-background alone.
- P1 primary directional prediction: higher AR/WL have more negative responses to lower-stratum vegetation density/allocation (at fixed total and height); treat AR primary, WL secondary, correlated predictors with joint uncertainty; peak frequency exploratory conditional on call type/body size because high frequency alone does not uniquely imply clutter tolerance. General guild/roost controls are not outcomes.
- P2: profile predicts held-out relative occurrence better than scalar vegetation at 1 km; repeat 6/12 km, using SAME eligible support and fold assignments from coarse blocks, aggregation not new independent samples. Compare differences in normalized out-of-sample conditional log score (species-balanced) and uncertainty. Profile permutation within region/forest-cover/PAI/height bins is a structure-null; preserve missingness and group spatial blocks. Also randomized trait labels within sufficiently large phylogenetic strata as diagnostic, not proof.
- P3: fit Iberia and within-Europe held-out regions; North America with observed traits and common standardized variables; holdout region not used to tune. Disjoint taxa require zero-shot trait-based slopes with no fitted species intercept (conditional score on each species's target presences/background). Separate cross-region same-species vs cross-species transfer; report environmental overlap and extrapolation. Independent within-region fit for sign replication.
- P4 secondary: 100-km assemblage trait summaries, aridity interaction with profile, compare aridity coefficient with/without vertical descriptors on matched support; distinguish range-map-derived assemblage composition vs occurrence-biased sample; no causal mediation claim, no claim that vegetation is the only mechanism (flight thermoregulation, prey, roosting alternatives).

## Identifiability and 3D output gate
A horizontal model learns Lambda_s(x)=integral_z lambda_s(x,z) dz (relative, observation dependent), not the decomposition: for ANY normalized q_s(z|x), lambda_s=Lambda_s*q_s integrates to same marginal. Even multiple GBIF publishers do not identify q. Therefore:
1. Primary output: horizontal SDM with vertically resolved environmental predictors, a macro habitat-filtering test.
2. Optional explicit hypothesis surface q_s(z|x) proportional to exp[-a_s d(x,z)] times a prespecified height/stratum prior. If not calibrated with independent height-resolved effort/detection, label as scenario/relative structural opportunity, NOT actual flight probability or diversity. PAVD is not volume; no unsupported acoustic clutter frequency conversion.
3. Calibrated stratum use only if independent paired-stratum observations and detection/effort support identify stratum response at their measurement resolution; use log ratio of canopy vs ground detection-adjusted activity, block held-out by site/study, compare against guild/height-only models. Unknown detection -> predict observed activity only. Richness/stacked occupancy by stratum requires calibrated occupancy and defined support, not sum of relative presence-background scores. Never claim a validated global 3D occupancy map from 2D records.

## Acceptance gates before ecological fit
S0 provenance: raw file/manifest SHA, licensing, units, candidate metadata vs actual bytes separated; report failures. S1 coverage: at least 20 observed-trait species for primary region (AR available), >=30 unique occupied cells per species, >=4 spatial blocks with >=5 positive cells, eligible backgrounds and GEDI covariate support. Report number excluded and why; if unmet report feasibility only rather than changing outcome thresholds. 200-km fixed blocks for main, 100/300-km sensitivity; assign folds before responses, no shared cells across splits. S2 compare paired held-out score gains with block bootstrap (resample blocks and species; report interval, region-specific scores, phylogenetic sensitivity); evidence of transfer requires positive gain with 95% lower bound >0 in both preregistered regions independently, directional coefficient and interval reported; no equivalence claim from nonsignificance. S3 publish negative/inconclusive outcomes and ceiling; no post hoc redefinition of canopy or trait sign to rescue failed tests. No manuscript submission, no merge to main.
