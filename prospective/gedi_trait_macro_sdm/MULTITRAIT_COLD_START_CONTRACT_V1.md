# Multitrait cold-start diagnostic V1 (post-pilot, 2026-11-25)

This is a single diagnostic extension of the inspected 14-species forest label-sorting pilot, not new independent data, not the original confirmatory SDM or a replacement of S1. Fixed source: common forest run 45585033345, source SHA256 5bd65cd8c56bac84c033a4972064622c172554a676d198868ef0776c49a2786b. All train/test comparisons come from exactly the frozen pair construction and hash-ordered block folds of the prior transfer diagnostic. No response-driven trait choice. Do not reopen this source to tune variants if this diagnostic fails.

Trait source: previously verified Haarsma & Siepel DOI 10.5281/zenodo.7226367 raw CSV, SHA256 d3ebe7656a60b44933f4da1a8302ca800bed07e0864d557698bf322eb7b85f49; physical crosswalk SHA256 43bb5284308a9fe732beda51886172cb87268e5d00620eb27956d5808dae809f. AR and WL reported physical values (compiled, not necessarily direct or independent measurements); positive body mass. Acoustic proxy is geometric midpoint of positive compiled minimum and maximum echolocation frequencies, not peak energy frequency or a clutter measurement. Positive compiled mean call duration. Keep log(WL), log(mass), log(freq midpoint), log(duration) dimensionless after training-only scaling. Explicit categorical FirstMainMaternityRoost from verified EuroBaTrait v2 07_roost_type.csv (SHA256 c0431e2353867bad4d92a0ec6bd35e5951e407bec2b165d275c04572e1d0bc17) if unique nonmissing category per taxon. Inspect foraging trait dictionary and report availability of explicit hunting/foraging mode/guild; exclude observed habitat affinity indices, modelled specialization, and climate/distribution-derived descriptors to avoid circularity. Do not substitute those for a missing functional foraging mode.

Predeclared tiers:
- W: AR + log WL + log body mass.
- WA: W + log frequency midpoint + log mean call duration.
- WAR: WA + categorical maternity roost (one unique category, train-known category required for held-out taxa).
Tier-specific complete cases only, require >=10 taxa. No imputation, no data-dependent feature selection. Compare AR-only baseline/profile and tier baseline/profile on EXACT same train/test rows per tier, including same training taxa. Training excludes either held-out taxon anywhere plus all held-out 200-km block fold data; test consists only of the two held-out taxa. Preserve prior support minima (>=300 train comparisons, >=10 test comparisons, >=10 training blocks, >=3 test blocks). At least 8 training taxa. One fixed ridge penalty 0.1 on standardized columns; no tuning. All models use trait-difference × environmental-difference features; profile extension adds trait-difference × R50 difference. Fit baseline and profile independently. Unknown roost categories are nonadmitted for that tier, not reference category by default. Report all tiers including insufficient support. Do not compare gains across differing coverage without matched AR model.

Primary diagnostic summaries: equal-block conditional log-score gains of profile vs baseline within each tier, AR-only profile vs baseline on matched rows, full profile vs AR profile, difference-of-gains; # positive blocks, coverage fraction, scored taxa, pair/fold support, error counts. Conditional swap probability on recorded pairs, not occupancy or causal mechanism. No naive comparison-level p-values. Interpret multivariate improvement only as a post-pilot prediction signal for recorded labels, with substantial small-species and source-confounding caveats. 20-species full-environment gate and independent future data still required. Do not interpret negative AR-only gain as proof that morphology is irrelevant.
