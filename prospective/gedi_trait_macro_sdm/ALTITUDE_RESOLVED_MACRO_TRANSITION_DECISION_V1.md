## Concrete next research target

For multi-region, height-stratified surveys, collect original survey-event tables with documented detector height/coordinates, effort/exposure, count or detection of named species or explicitly harmonized guild, simultaneous multi-height design, event/site identifiers, and separately documented detection geometry. The first data-driven target can be **recorded height-stratum composition conditional on a recorded pass**, evaluated leaving whole independent sites and then entire studies out. It is not actual flight altitude. To promote it to height-resolved biological intensity, require source-calibrated height-dependent observation kernels or an independently justified detection bound and matching physical volume/AGL geometry; reuse the already implemented air-volume observation-kernel engine rather than inventing a second one. With only the horizontal GBIF marginal, the height distribution is unidentifiable. Do not treat GVSM height quantiles as a distribution of bats.
