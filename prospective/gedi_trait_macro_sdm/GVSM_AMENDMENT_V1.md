Negative low quantiles retained (ground waveform), no arbitrary clipping. Secondary descriptors: (RH90-RH10)/RH98 and (RH75-RH25)/RH98; do not convert to vegetation density, open-air fraction or echo clutter. RH98 itself is scalar height comparator. M0 climate/land cover/terrain/effort/roost covariates; M1 adds RH98 and trait×RH98 and trait×baseline covariates; M2 adds R50 and species-specific random slopes; M3 adds physical AR×R50 with M2 random slopes. WL sensitivity, acoustic frequency conditional on call type/body size, additional profile descriptors secondary.
