# Unified manuscript figure, SI and editing plan v1

## Editorial choice

The integrated article must not read as a BE paper followed by an unrelated JAE appendix, or as a JAE paper with anecdotal controlled experiments attached.

**One question:** What persists when individuals remain predictable, and when does that persistent information require ecological spatial partitioning?

**Positive core:** Retained individual information under acute perturbation; retained wild terrain-relative vertical configuration.

**Boundary core:** Detailed realized geometry need not transfer; total developmental dispersion is not simply treatment-determined; positive additional vertical segregation is not supported in four eligible wild panels; the proposed laboratory-to-field scalar carrier gate failed.

**Synthesis level:** A decomposition of independently tested empirical propositions. Not a verified laboratory-to-wild causal sequence.

### Spatial figure implementation constraints

- Label each spatial group with biological species plus study/year, not six independent species names.
- Plot V_rel and S_rel in adjacent but separate scales (their values are different estimands); use statuses and calibrated null information, not unitless overlay.
- Show H, P2022, P2023 and P2016 in exactly the same order across all field panels.
- Label all four actual co-use designs: H. monstrosus and P. hastatus 2022 = 60 s/endpoint-excluded; P. hastatus 2023 = 600 s/all-space; P. hastatus 2016 = 600 s/endpoint-excluded. Do not imply that 2023 is the only 600-s panel.
- Three positive 95% upper endpoints are **compatibility bounds** from dyad resampling, not equivalence or post hoc power tests.
- Mark evidence tier in every figure caption: original frozen, predeclared secondary, post-outcome diagnostic, descriptive, or structural STOP.
- No new inferential endpoint is computed in this integration; all plotted values are derived directly from source-pinned frozen/original result receipts. Figures 1–6 have been rendered as SVG and must pass automated reproducibility checks and human visual review before journal production.

### Compression logic

1. Retain **JAE original vertical identity and ecologically decisive partitioning tests** in main Results.
2. Summarize each BE intervention in a common inferential unit table while keeping n and original p/null interpretation in Results.
3. Include developmental nulls as a two-system contrast, without promoting feature decompositions to significant results.
4. Keep external wild failures in either main Results or a main figure note; full numerical table stays in SI.
5. Shift pipeline-level details and extended source-specific experimental protocols to SI, never omit source citations, support gates or license restrictions.
6. Do not create a misleading "single mechanism" Discussion from pooled endpoints; develop separate maintained-information, representation and ecological-overlap paragraphs.

## Manuscript transitions to protect coherence

**Introduction → Methods:** "We treated persistence, expression and ecological partitioning as distinct estimands rather than assuming that repeated identity implied a particular spatial consequence."

**Acute experiments → Portability:** "Retaining a correct individual mapping under changed conditions does not establish that fine-scale trajectories themselves are portable."

**Portability → Wild distributions:** "We therefore evaluated, independently, which parts of individual movement organization remain predictive in ecological space."

**Wild fidelity → Partitioning:** "A repeatable individual distribution is not evidence that conspecifics keep away from one another; these propositions require separate null-calibrated comparisons."

**Partitioning → Bridge:** "The joint pattern does not itself specify the biological carrier or validate a scalar experimental-to-field representation."

**Discussion landing:** "Individualization, persistence and partitioning differ in what they predict; future experiments must measure or manipulate the connecting task/history variables in the same bats."
