## Central biological question and editorial claim

**When does maintained individual behavioral organization yield predictive use of flight height within shared horizontal supports, and does that individuality entail added terrain-relative vertical segregation?**

The integrated paper is viable only if it is read as a **source-provenanced demonstration that maintenance, realization and partitioning are different testable properties**, NOT as direct causal proof that one experimentally manipulated behavioral state produces the wild observed 3D distribution shape.

Strongest within-domain ecological contrast:
- JAE post-outcome 500-m local-terrain analyses: individual V_rel supported **4/4** eligible panels across **two species**.
- JAE post-outcome positive S_rel added-segregation test: **0/4** supported. The *H. monstrosus* negative-tail two-sided p=0.0362 must not be relabeled a no-effect or attraction result.
- Synchronous extra co-use separation: **1/4 nominal per original source gate**, *P. hastatus* 2023 (+3.57 m, phase-shift upper-tail raw p=0.0231, 600 s all-space with eight dyads). A retrospective **within-co-use-family** four-panel Holm check instead gives p=0.0924 and 0/4. The original nominal source verdict is preserved but not promoted to multiplicity-robust evidence.
- Other co-use conditions: H (60 s endpoint-excluded, 22 dyads), P2022 (60 s endpoint-excluded, 10 dyads), P2016 (600 s endpoint-excluded, eight dyads). Positive dyad-bootstrap 95% upper compatibility endpoints +0.985/+1.918/+1.128 m in the three nonsupporting panels are not formal equivalence bounds.

Strongest perturbation interpretation: correct-individual correspondence persists after four independently controlled acute source-level perturbations, but because behavior, nulls and species differ and biological n=3–6, this does not identify a common bat-wide mechanism or a common effect-size scale.

Developmental interpretation: an individually positive mean B first-flight slope p=0.0102 did **not** meet the predeclared 10/14 sign gate (observed 8/14), so the primary FAIL remains. Two randomized treatment effects on total individualization were not supported (Rachum p=0.167785, Elie p=0.716667); these do not establish equivalence or absence of treatment effects on individual dimensions.

Field-bridge interpretation: original scalar FlightIntensity carrier results PASS/PASS/FAIL/structural STOP = overall FAIL, with no direct matched laboratory and field individual cohort. The bridge cannot be rescued by post-outcome H/V components.

The within-endpoint four-test sensitivity yields V_rel 4/4, but a more inclusive **retrospective 12-test** Holm comparison over the three displayed field endpoint families yields V_rel **3/4** (H, P2022/P2023 p_adj = 0.0012; P2016 p_adj = 0.276221), S_rel 0/4 and co-use I 0/4. Do **not** present the earlier 4/4 adjusted result as generally resistant to other multiplicity definitions. This 12-test set remains only a selected fraction of all analyses and is defined post-outcome. A difference in detection rates among V_rel, S_rel and I with different designs and power is **not proof of a greater true effect or ecological equivalence**. The defensible story is individually predictive conditional flight-height use in tested systems, with no consistently detected added terrain-relative segregation — not its proven absence. ## Terrain reference is an ecological measurement condition, not a cause — 2026-10-15

The original exact source geometries show substantial changes in added 3D segregation after transforming recorded height to **source GPS height minus interpolated DEM**: *H. monstrosus* S_native +0.1029 → S_rel −0.0158, *P. hastatus* 2016 +0.1304 → +0.0289; the other two panels changed less and in the other direction. V_native to V_rel changed in **both directions** across panels while V_rel remained originally source-supported 4/4; the H XY source contrast was invariant. Table S13 documents matched original processing changes only, with **no p-values/intervals for the deltas**. Since original H/P2023 altitude is ellipsoid referenced but P2022/P2016 MSL, the resulting geometry is not a validated measurement of bat clearance above ground/canopy. The code interpolates local DEM height **per GPS fix**, and 500 m refers to subsequent horizontal overlap cells. **Do not** advertise that topography causes a universal learned personal flying rule or explains away original individuality. Pending independent raw GPS/DEM replay, vertically harmonized instrumentation and actual resource-context causal controls remain hard gates.


## Scientific-editor stop: don't claim actual physical height gaps — 2026-10-16

**High-priority source finding.** The original 3D overlap is the overlap of session-centered, α=0.5-per-bin-smoothed modeled height distributions (with 50 pooled shared-cell fixes), **not physically observed XY×absolute-height habitat co-occupancy**. A supported pair can have positive smoothed overlap despite no observed height-bin intersection. More importantly, the corrected source co-use I runner subtracts each original session's own median before comparing height deviations at fixed 500-m cell/time encounters. Its 2023 +3.57 m nominal source excess is *relative-height-deviation dissimilarity* versus the centered phase-shift reference, **not an observed physical separation between bats**. Thus even absence/non-support of I would not demonstrate lack of physical vertical avoidance. Source nominal I 1/4, within-four Holm 0/4 and twelve-test Holm 0/4 remain unaltered and include different encounter windows.

The original integrated title can stay only if "added segregation" is carefully defined as a comparison of centered estimated height-use distributions under original source nulls. A stronger physical airspace partitioning thesis would require a new, independently calibrated true common-datum altitude/co-presence dataset and a prospectively frozen analysis; **do not rescue via data dredging**. The lab-to-wild causal carrier remains unvalidated. Human/coauthor editorial review must explicitly accept the narrowed biological contribution.
