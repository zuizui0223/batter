## A. What is actually tested

The unifying question is: **Which individually predictive vertical-use properties persist after coarse place/context matching, and do those properties yield added terrain-relative segregation beyond the observed horizontal baseline?**

Four outcomes must NEVER be made interchangeable.

| Empirical object | Observable question | What a positive result means | What it does NOT mean |
|---|---|---|---|
| Identity correspondence | Can the correct animal still be recognized from a measured within-source behavioral signature across conditions? | Source-specific individuality information survives the manipulated condition | Same neural mechanism across species; unchanged behavior; population-wide invariance |
| Developmental individualization magnitude | Does a randomized condition change residual among-individual multivariate dispersion? | A treatment shifts that prespecified amount | How every behavioral dimension changed; same quantity as identity correspondence |
| Wild vertically centered shape fidelity | Does own history predict held-out vertical-use probability shape after coarse common-cell weighting and session centering? | Non-exchangeable individual distribution shape under the calibrated pipeline | A fixed learned route; independently identified behavior; absolute tag-bias-free altitude |
| Added segregation / co-use separation | Do individuals spatially separate *more than the appropriate label/time null predicts*? | Evidence of positive additional vertical separation under the chosen scale/definition | Absence or presence of competition as an identified cause |

The main field finding is positive **distribution fidelity** with unsupported positive **segregation**, observed concurrently in four eligible panels from TWO species. This is the ecological result; laboratory perturbations cannot substitute for it.

## D. Combined claims authorized and prohibited

**Authorized headline:** In independent bat experiments, identity-bearing behavior can remain predictive under acute context change, while within a narrower two-species field family individual vertical probability distributions persist without positive evidence for extra vertical segregation. Maintenance, realization and partitioning are therefore different empirical questions.

**Authorized interpretive hypothesis:** Reuse of a personal solution is consistent with multi-day held-out self-history being more informative than matched conspecific behavior in overlapping space.

**Not authorized:**
1. A single common measured individual policy across laboratory and field systems.
2. Neural, learned-route or memory causation of the observed wild altitude profiles.
3. No competition or no resource partitioning at any resolution.
4. Confirmatory causal inference from the post-outcome field geometries.
5. A developmental treatment has zero effect on individualization.
6. Four genuinely independent ecological species-level partitioning replications (there are two species and three temporal Phyllostomus panels).
7. Presenting repeated trials, fixes or permutations as equivalent to additional biological individuals.
8. A universal trend for all bats based on the closed six-panel source subset and external failures.
9. Treating the 2023 co-use positive result as identical in design to the other three co-use tests.
10. Publishing the same JAE wild primary claims once in this integrated manuscript and again as an independently claimed JAE research article.

## D.1. Same-source horizontal 2D prior-art and cross-year ID scope

Calderón-Capote et al. (2025; DOI 10.1371/journal.pone.0313782) analysed *P. hastatus* foraging and social structure using the original 2016 and 2022 La Gruta source archives. They reported horizontal foraging-area overlap near 0.20 (Bhattacharyya coefficient), without a clear social-group distinction. The new Figure S1 instead summarizes **source-person-specific ten-bin relative-height probability shapes** using the 1D total-variation overlap identity; its median 0.80–0.92 must not be called 80–92% of the same physical 3D foraging volume. Both measures differ from the post-outcome terrain S_rel and synchronous phase-shift co-use I tests.

The original Figure 5B has **103 panel-indexed profiles but only 101 distinct literal ID strings** across panels; two recur in P. hastatus 2016 and 2022 at La Gruta. Given PIT-tag/low-recapture source reporting, biological continuity is possible but is **not independently verified** by the current archive. No untested six-year retained within-individual altitude strategy is claimed, and no global 103-independent-animal count or pooled inferential effective n is used.

**Primary editorial red line:** The paper's possible contribution is a **source-specific, null-calibrated ecological test** linking (a) individually predictive 3D vertical-distribution shape under common horizontal support, (b) maintenance after 500-m place × speed–turning matching, (c) terrain-relative extra segregation and actual synchronous co-use, while distinguishing experimental identity retention from an unvalidated field carrier. It is **NOT** the first conceptual demonstration of overlapping individual niches: Carlson et al. (2021, DOI 10.1038/s41467-021-24826-x) documented consistent nested/overlapping white-stork environmental niches and Wang et al. (2023, DOI 10.1186/s40462-023-00394-1) documented bat spatial specialization with seasonal overlap. It is **NOT** proof of a causally shared carrier among independent laboratory and wild data sources.

### Further post-outcome mechanism-identifiability qualification

Four original tropical bat field panels retained centered height identity after matching 500-m horizontal place × coarse speed × turning (n=19/23/11/9; calibrated excess +0.3840/+0.0868/+0.1015/+0.4514, upper-tail p=0.0002/0.0002/0.0006/0.0286). The 2016 same-night comparator separately failed its frozen p gate at 0.0544, while other same-night panels passed 3/4. Lag supports are 4/4, 3/3 and 2/2 across *different eligible subsets*, not one time-decay curve. These analyses **constrain** broad place and kinematic composition explanations but cannot identify unobserved foraging-task allocation versus stable within-task execution. Those two mechanisms can generate exactly the same observed p_i(z|h,c) under a latent state mixture, demonstrated in [ECOLOGICAL_IDENTIFIABILITY_AND_DECISIVE_TESTS_V1.md](ECOLOGICAL_IDENTIFIABILITY_AND_DECISIVE_TESTS_V1.md). This logical toy is not a new biological analysis and is not a source S_rel effect.

Do not claim: within-task flight policy directly estimated, memory identified, competition absent, stable overlapping *dietary* niches demonstrated, or formal equivalence to zero co-use separation.
