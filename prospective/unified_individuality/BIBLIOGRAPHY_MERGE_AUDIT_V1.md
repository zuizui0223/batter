## Editorial gaps and boundaries

- **No primary source discarded:** experimental studies Harten, Rachum, Elie, Taub, Foskolos, Diebold, Aharon, Teshima and Eveland remain part of the consolidated scholarly references. Wild original and external boundary programmes likewise retain their cited source papers.
- The Movebank archival DOIs used by JAE were recorded in the unified SI and source-screen ledger rather than counted as new papers in the above 45-item publication-reference union.
- Some important source-data records are **not permanent DOIs** in the original BE manifest: Taub's public Dropbox mirror has unverified raw-data redistribution permissions; Eveland's source public GitHub corpus has CC0 for the verified pinned analysis subset; Teshima's Figshare dataset should be cited at its actual dataset record.
- **Source-study duplicate policy:** 2022/2023/2016 *Phyllostomus hastatus* tracking panels are different panel datasets of one biological species, not three independent taxa.
- **Journal submission:** remove uncited secondary background records or cite them with an appropriate factual claim; do not introduce unverified papers to pad the bibliography.
- Reference style (year suffix, author separators, capitalization, publisher requirements) remains provisional until target-journal verification and human coauthor review.
