# Ecological priority/novelty risk audit: persistent niches can overlap

**Prepared:** 2026-10-14. **Scope:** Targeted review of publisher-hosted ecological articles relevant to the revised ecological claim. This is **not** a systematic literature survey and does not alter any source-level numerical results.

## The three claims that must be kept distinct

**Not original:** Individual niche specialization and individual environmental differences can coexist with overlapping space, and may be persistent over time.

**Potentially distinct empirical increment within the present closed source set:** On a 3D flight-height axis, an individual's held-out vertical probability shape remains predictive after (a) standardizing coarse horizontal exposure and (b) subtracting session-specific constant vertical offsets. A later **post-outcome** analysis in four panels of two tropical species retains individual terrain-relative fidelity (4/4) but does not support **positive** added segregation (0/4), while a separate synchronous co-use phase-shift test supports one coarse-definition exception (1/4). A 500-m place × binary speed × binary turning state stress test also retains individual information in the four eligible panels. These are source-specific results with different measurement and null families; they are **not** proof that competition is absent or that all meaningful niches overlap at every scale.

**Still unidentified:** Whether stability comes from task mixture, within-task flight technique, microhabitat, past experience, morphology, weather reaction norms, dominance or competition. No integrated laboratory–field causal carrier was validated.

## Why this changes the manuscript

The prior draft made overlap-versus-individuality sound like the main general conceptual novelty. The revised Introduction and Discussion now cite Carlson and Wang explicitly and avoid a historical priority claim. The correct reviewer-facing formulation is **a new within-source 3D measurement/conditioning distinction**, not the first example of non-exclusive individually differentiated niches. Because several experimental sources differ from the tracking animals and the scalar lab–wild bridge failed, the cross-system synthesis is also **comparative rather than causally continuous**.

This does not automatically make the one-paper submission the best publication strategy. Journal fit and single-paper cohesion remain subject to independent coauthor review.
