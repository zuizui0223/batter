# Independent turkey replication gate

Recorded 5 November 2026 before inspecting candidate GPS spatial outcomes.
The original source results and published candidate-paper findings are already
known. This is a new-source, prospectively fixed eligibility check, not an
independently registered preregistration.

Question: does April-minus-February mean monthly utilization-centroid separation
remain positive after matching seven common calendar dates per month and
deleting each individual in turn? Retain the same daily-equal spherical-center
metric, 24 fixed draw seeds and individual-deletion diagnostic as the original
2020 six-animal core. Draws, dyads and deletion results are dependent sensitivities,
not biological replicates, confidence intervals or p-values.

Candidate order from primary-source metadata, before GPS outcomes:
1. Dryad 10.5061/dryad.s4mw6m9gg: Maine females, original GPS Used file; strongest
   geographic separation from the southeastern-US source and sex comparability.
2. Dryad 10.5061/dryad.rv15dv4fb: male GPS, Savannah River Site, 2020/2021;
   a sex/context extension only, with possible southeastern-source overlap
   requiring source-project and track/deployment checks.
3. Additional primary-source candidates are recorded regardless of eligibility.
   A second DOI from the same Maine tracking programme is not an independent
   population. Movement summaries, SSF available points, nest-only summaries and
   stage-filtered GPS excerpts are not unfiltered monthly GPS trajectories.

Before any spatial outcome calculation require: original identifiable animals,
timestamps and measured coordinates; source integrity and reuse information;
known source/site/year and sex where available; at least four animals retained
in both February and April, each with at least seven dates and seven dates shared
across the compared group in each month. At least three animals must remain
after a deletion. Missing February cannot be replaced with March, an author's
mating season, a model-generated location or an extrapolated centroid.

Independence: distinct DOI is insufficient. Compare source authors/projects,
study sites, deployments/years and, where possible, original individual and
timestamp-position records privately. Different ID conventions alone do not
prove independence. Classify VERIFIED_DISTINCT_SOURCE, POSSIBLE_OVERLAP or
UNRESOLVED explicitly. Same population in multiple years is repeated evidence.

Use independently specified study-site membership before geography. The original
annual-5-km graph component rule may be reproduced as a declared geometric
sensitivity only when the observation window and support are comparable; it is
not population identity. Do not construct a new 'single mover' after viewing
distances or relabel a cohort-level extension as an exact six-core replication.

STOP the exact primary test if the original usable data lack required months,
common-date support, track provenance or independent-source evidence. Report
counts and exclusions, not an ecological null. Secondary March and June/July
contrasts cannot rescue a failed primary eligibility gate. If a usable candidate
passes, record the exact site/cohort and source hashes before fitting outcomes.

Only aggregate source/cohort counts and final summaries may be committed. No
original animal IDs, precise timestamps, positions or trajectories are published.
