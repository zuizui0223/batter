# PR #100: question and locked follow-up, 2026-11-05

Source commit: 45017e0f7f35fd79468db07f16ee162816efc0d6.
This is post-inspection exploratory falsification, not preregistration or an independent confirmation. The February/April outcome, annual-centroid groups and single-animal influence are already known.

Central question: Does seasonal geographic reconfiguration arise solely from the already identified animal, or does expansion remain among the other animals after matching observation effort and removing each animal in turn? Geographic configuration and day-specific near-position surplus are separate endpoints.

Primary endpoint: April minus February mean interanimal monthly-centroid distance (km), within the original six-animal 2020 core. Monthly centroids use equal-weight daily spherical centers. The mean, rather than median, permits an exact additive decomposition.

H1 (distributed core expansion): primary contrast is positive after each of six core-animal deletions, using full available days and in all 24 fixed seven-common-day/month subsamples. Failure of this stringent diagnostic means sensitivity, not biological absence. Subsamples and deletions are dependent diagnostics, not independent replicates or confidence intervals.

H2 (concentrated whole-cohort contribution): decompose the 21-pair mean contrast exactly into (6/21)*isolate-core contrast plus (15/21)*core-core contrast. A positive isolate-core contribution exceeding half the positive total is defined here as a majority contribution; report both signed terms regardless. This is pair-distance accounting, not a causal contribution of an animal's own displacement.

H3 (within-source consistency): apply the same primary comparison to both known 2019 components, showing original edge sets and all pairs among their members. Report sign and leave-one-animal range. These are not independently certified populations and not an out-of-sample validation.

H4 (persistence): report February-to-March and February-to-June/July contrasts, averaging the latter two monthly pair-distance means. Do not substitute a pooled June/July centroid. These are secondary diagnostics, not alternate primaries.

Observation control: select exactly seven calendar dates shared by all animals in each group/month, same dates for all animals; 24 seeds (0..23), no outcome-based seed selection. Within each selected day retain original fixes and equal daily weights. Retain complete days as a separate baseline. Leave-one-animal deletions keep the sampled date set fixed. Stop a group comparison if fewer than seven common dates or fewer than three animals remain. Do not impute missing months.

Original data must pass the repository's publisher MD5, pinned SHA256, animal/tag/outlier checks and timestamp-position multiset checks. Reproduce the baseline monthly result before evaluating follow-up outputs. Original IDs, precise dates, coordinates and individual tracks must not be exported; only aggregate diagnostics are saved.

No significance tests based on dyads, resampling draws or months; no claim about direct contacts, social flocks, migration date, resources, competition or a universal cross-taxon rule. Original annual graph selection uses the full observed year and is outcome-informed. No independent untouched holdout exists here.
