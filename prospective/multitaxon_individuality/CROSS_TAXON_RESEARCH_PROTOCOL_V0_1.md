# From individual predictability to ecological partitioning: a cross-taxon Movebank programme

**Version v0.1 — proposed eligibility and analysis design, 18 October 2026.**  
**Status:** database **metadata census complete**; full underlying animal track screening, biological inference, and macro-analysis are **NOT YET RUN**. A draft *prospective*, independent research direction — NOT the frozen bat JAE/BE PR and NOT a finding that a universal law has already been discovered.

## Why change the central question?

The bat studies found that individual relative-height probability profiles can be predicted from an individual's own history in eligible repeated sessions; however, the originally compared V and S 3D overlap functionals are **algebraically coupled**, co-use I measures session-centered residual altitude (not physical inter-bat gap), vertical datums are mixed, and the ecological endpoints use partially distinct bat cohorts. These results are a **methodological pilot/boundary case**, not a cross-taxon generality test.

A universal claim cannot be generated by pooling post-outcome p-values from four panels representing two bat species. Instead treat bats as **a validation and failure case for an analytical design that is applicable to birds, terrestrial mammals, reptiles and (where the sensor is suitable) marine animals**.

## Fundamental biological question

> When do *repeatable individual-specific movement decisions* become *ecologically distinct realized space/resource use or encounter segregation* — and when can the former persist without the latter?

This reverses the previous central response: **the outcome of interest is the ecological transformation**, with individual-predictive movement information as one explanatory driver among others. *Neither individual niche repeatability nor partial overlap is itself a novelty claim.*

**Candidate working title:** *When does individual movement individuality translate into ecological niche partitioning? A cross-taxon analysis of archived animal tracking.*

The cross-taxon analysis will yield a negative result if conditional individuality does **not** generalize beyond the accessible bats, or if its relationship with ecological segregation disappears after opportunity and environmental heterogeneity are measured. That falsifiability is necessary.

### Concrete cross-taxon hypotheses

**H1: conditional identity is general but not universal.** Some, not necessarily all, independently tracked populations retain an individual-specific **held-out predictive increment** for movement choices given independently measured position, environmental conditions and broad movement context. Test against a **whole-animal/per-session permuted null with identical data support**. No prevalence claim unless multiple independent populations and taxonomic groups have passed the predefined inclusion gate.

**H2: identity–partitioning translation is context dependent.** Conditional predictability alone need not imply reduced between-animal *physical co-use* or independently measured habitat/resource niche overlap. Predictive individual information and encounter/resourcedifference are different **predeclared endpoints and sampled biological units**; the latter is not another algebraic transform of the same V/S probability overlaps. Habitat patch heterogeneity, temporal resource variability and independently coded social context are candidate **moderators**, not assumed validated drivers.

**H3: mechanisms differ by available task/axis.** Distinguish differences in **which resources or behavioral tasks** animals choose from differences in the **movement choices made within independently verified tasks**. Broad GPS speed/turn classes are not proven feeding, commuting, migration or resting tasks. A strict altitude/depth **3D tier** is allowed only when sensor field, units, vertical datum, measurement error and actual physical location (not per-session centered residual) are defensible. Terrestrial 2D studies must never be treated as fake-3D zeros.

## Eligibility checklist — freeze before extracting any comparative outcome

**D0. Metadata/right/citation:** Valid DOI, source reference data obtainable under its exact dataset terms (CC0/BY/BY-NC allowed according to use and funding); taxon, sampling population/site and sensor type resolvable; dates and source/version identified.

**D1. Event and individual integrity:** Track events with valid ID, UTC time, WGS84 position, source availability, deployment start/end and outlier/visible flags; repeated individual IDs within study not treated as globally unique; tag changes and sensor-reuse handled. The dataset can be 2D only.

**D2. Repeatable source phenotype (provisional pre-analysis pilot gate):** The new privacy-safe **stage-1 source event preflight** requires ≥**8 independent identified animals**, ≥**4 tracking days per animal each with ≥50 effective GPS fixes**, ≥**7 elapsed calendar days** and ≥**3 overlapping tracked dates with at least two passing animals**. Actual population/species/site/season membership and sufficient shared horizontal/environmental support must then be verified separately; ≥2 nonadjacent, independently held-out behavioral bouts or days are needed for any identity predictor. One dense day, repeated GPS fix, or nonindependent siblings in a single behavioral state cannot substitute. Block bootstrap and observation-level effective sample size must be reported. Eligibility gates will be frozen after a metadata-only feasibility check, before any identity or niche effect outcomes are opened.

**D3. Real ecological contrast:** For **contemporaneous opportunity-corrected physical co-use**, at least two animals with genuine overlapping deployment times, validated source GPS accuracy, a preregistered ecologically meaningful encounter distance and ≥5 distinct eligible dyads if testing this endpoint. For **environmental/resource segregation**, independently available resource or land-cover/phenology covariates, source-specific availability background, replicated individuals and comparable seasons are required. If neither ecological contrast qualifies, a source is eligible for **identity-only** inference and remains missing, not zero, for transformation/segregation.

**D4. 3D extension only:** Measured absolute altitude (consistent geoid/ellipsoid reference) or depth with pressure uncertainty, calibrated sampling and sign conventions. Do NOT regard DEM-subtracted mixed native altitude or session-centered scores as direct observed physical 3D gaps. Bird/flight and marine depth are analyzed separately.

**Freeze and report:** source DOI → eligibility tier → reason(s) of failure, biological n, sessions/blocks, spatial opportunity, observation interval, vertical device metadata, independent source labelling. The effective dataset must be described as something like “N eligible independent source populations from M verified taxa”, **never** 437 “studies” as if all met the analysis gate.

## Three *distinct* original quantitative endpoints

### A. Individual predictive information (nonspatial movement choice)

For animal i and **held-out whole blocks** t with movement response \(Y_{it}\) (time-normalized next-step displacement/turning state) and independently defined context \(C_{it}\), fit:
- one **shared conspecific population** prediction \(\widehat p_{\mathrm{pop}}(Y\mid C)\);
- one source-comparable **individual-conditional** \(\widehat p_i(Y\mid C)\) trained on *other sessions* of i, with identical feature sets and shrinkage choices.

Define individual held-out gain \(G_i=(1/n_i)\sum_t[\log\widehat p_i(Y_{it}\mid C_{it})-\log\widehat p_{\mathrm{pop}}(Y_{it}\mid C_{it})]\), averaged **across biological animals**, not events. This is predictive individual correspondence/behavioral state, not proof of adaptive specialisation, learnt flight routes, personality, or competition. Freeze movement time lag/turning representations using sensor interval and taxon movement mode *before* test outcomes, ensuring fair support and model complexity. Whole-session or animal-block permutation tests calibrate identity, and a device/batch/time negative-control gate is required.

### Intermediate 2D utilization-overlap descriptor — **not** a direct ecological resource outcome

Before interpreting resource-use or real co-presence, calculate an **estimated 2D geographic utilization overlap** (O_{XY}=sum_c min[hat p_i(c),hat p_j(c)]) on a common, source-appropriate spatial grid, for **the same ID-verified animals and season/source sampling frame** used by conditional movement identity G. Let (P=1-overline{O}_{XY}). This descriptor quantifies **marginal observed relative-space use**, not actual prey, habitat resources or realized competition; it is a secondary intermediate/measurement check. The ecological outcomes **B (independently sourced environment/resource exposure)** and **C (physical synchronized encounter/avoidance)** below must remain separate and may be structurally missing even when 2D P exists. No "no partitioning" conclusion can follow from an unsupported spatial test without externally justified equivalence bounds and sufficient cohort n.

### B. Ecological environmental/resource differentiation (separate measured outcome)

Among source-tracked **same-population** individuals and independently defined accessible resource patches/environmental niches, model differences in remotely sensed temperature/habitat/vegetation phenology, prey-resource proxy or dive depth *after defining a common available environment*. Report pair/individual resource-condition intersections and source uncertainty **without using an algebraic V-derived difference as the outcome**. Environmental exposure is **not automatically prey/diet or competitive exclusion**; each environmental variable has a declared physiological/ecological interpretation and source-based spatial accuracy.

### C. Real spatiotemporal co-use (separate optional outcome)

For ID-qualified, truly contemporaneous GPS deployments with acceptable error and overlap of location opportunity, define \(\Pr(\mathrm{same\ predeclared\ patch\ at\ same\ fine\ time})\) or an opportunity-calibrated log observed/shifted encounter ratio using independently defined **physical positions**, not session-median-centered altitude residuals. Its null preserves per-individual route/session/time/diel structure, season, patch availability and dyad dependence. Do not infer avoidance from nominal low co-presence without such opportunity controls. Animals without overlapping deploy times are **missing for this endpoint**.

**Caution:** A and B/C may share a broad source dataset, but use **preassigned disjoint temporal evaluation windows** to limit deterministic coupling. Repeated dyads do not become independent species, and the primary cross-taxon inference unit is a biologically verified population, not a GPS fix.

## Cross-taxon inference

**Primary meta-regression (after actual track screening and effect calibration):** Does the *between-population association* of held-out individual information G with B or C change with independently measured spatial resource heterogeneity, resource predictability, source social ecology and movement mode? Use a multilevel model with independent population/study, species/taxon, source publication and clade random effects, plus habitat/season and data-quality moderators, and a phylogenetic covariance only when validated taxon identity plus a trustworthy phylogeny are available.

Report heterogeneity, leave-one-source-study/leave-one-species/clade-out predictive performance, sample-selection sensitivity, confidence/compatibility intervals and taxonomic representation. Do **not** pool heterogeneous originally reported p-values or translate “significant G, nonsignificant C” into proof of nonpartitioning.

**Predeclared generality gate for publication language:** No claim of ecological **cross-taxon generality** unless at least **20 biologically independent source populations** spanning **at least three independently populated higher taxonomic groups** meet D2, and at least **10** of these independent populations meet D3 for a particular ecological endpoint. These are programme planning targets, not a guarantee the current 437 packages achieve them. If the ecological D3 gate fails, report a narrower database analysis of **individual predictability** only; do not rescue the ecological claim by relaxing gates after reading response results.

## Competing literature and no false priority claims

- Carlson et al. (2021), *Individual environmental niches in mobile organisms* (Nature Communications, DOI **10.1038/s41467-021-24826-x**): overlapping/nested stable individual environmental niches in white storks. The coexistence of individual identity and environmental overlap **is established prior art**.
- Takola (2026), *The individualized niche in motion: Quantifying individual specialisation with movement data* (DOI **10.3897/ibe.2.203247**): mixed-effects resource selection functions and niche hypervolumes for realized/potential individual environmental use. Estimating individualized hypervolumes or overlaps alone **is not a new contribution**.
- Caldwell et al. (2026), *Individual movements drive community-level space and resource use across Yellowstone ungulates* (Current Biology, DOI **10.1016/j.cub.2026.05.041**): individual movement variation and community resource overlap in five ungulate species. Links between movement individuality and community niches **already have direct empirical precedent**.
- This design's *potential* niche is the **predefined cross-species conditional identity → independent ecological endpoint transition and its moderators** across all qualifying database sources, with explicit negative/selection cases. Novelty is **conditional and not yet confirmed by a literature-level exhaustive originality review**.
