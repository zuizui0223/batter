# Public bat source screening for compositional 3D maneuver transfer (2026-10-08)

**SOURCE / DESIGN PRE-FLIGHT ONLY. No bat outcome values opened, no new reanalysis, no changed thresholds.**

## Question requiring evidence
Randomly assigned **which 3-D route was additionally practiced** must be independent of prior physical ability and treatment order; the same previously baseline-exposed but not additionally trained **sentinel route** must have measurable pre/post performance, with route component sharing and bat IDs known. To separately identify motor versus sensory, independent acoustic/physical similarity manipulation is further required.

A study that merely varies obstacle density or shows memory after a delay is not enough.

## Public candidate 1 — varied acoustic clutter (2018)
Accomando, Vargas-Irwin, Simmons, *Spike Train Similarity Space (SSIMS) Method Detects Effects of Obstacle Proximity and Experience on Temporal Patterning of Bat Biosonar*, Frontiers in Behavioral Neuroscience 12, DOI 10.3389/fnbeh.2018.00013.

Article: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC5809465/

- Nine corridor shapes; condition identities pseudo-randomized; 379 successful flight attempts and 11 failures.
- Published focus is **temporal biosonar call patterning** across obstacle configurations, not extra practice on a randomly assigned motor component followed by a fixed R4 sentinel.
- The article's linked open supplementary materials are AVI and DOCX, not an explicitly enumerated complete trialwise 3-D bat×route×randomized-training×performance table.
- No verified independent randomized route-seed or shared motor vs acoustic-cue assignment. **NO ELIGIBLE CAUSAL TRANSFER TEST**, even if a complete audio dataset might later become available.

## Public candidate 2 — echo flow and recorded 3-D path (2016)
Warnecke et al., *Dynamic Echo Information Guides Flight in the Big Brown Bat*, DOI 10.3389/fnbeh.2016.00081.

Article: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4843091/

- 13 Vicon IR cameras recorded 3-D movements of bats in poles with altered wall density; 4 ultrasonic microphones simultaneously recorded echoes.
- Published behavior shows shifts toward the less densely cluttered side, illustrating that **sensory geometry is a plausible competing mechanism for route transfer**.
- Article-associated supplementary files include DOCX and TIF. A separate Zenodo Bat Literature Project mirror (record 13424379) lists **restricted files** and is not a verified public raw trajectory archive.
- Experiment did NOT establish randomized training of one of the three alternate paths against a constant never-additionally-trained sentinel in a factorial 2×2 geometry. **NO ELIGIBLE CAUSAL TRANSFER TEST** under the frozen new proposal.

## Public candidate 3 — individual route memory and mirror obstacle scene (2013)
Barchi, Knowles & Simmons, *Spatial memory and stereotypy of flight paths by big brown bats in cluttered surroundings*, J Exp Biol, DOI 10.1242/jeb.073197, https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/23447667/ .

- Six big brown bats developed stereotyped personal routes; after roughly one month, three returned to the prior scene and three confronted a mirrored obstacle arrangement.
- Strong prior art for personal path memory and contextual reorganization; **not** prospectively randomized H/V component extra practice, not a common untrained sentinel, and published metadata here do not establish source raw recordings for that factorial test.
- **Literature/context only**, not an eligible numeric partial-transfer primary.

## Source 4 — same already-opened public Rhinolophus archive
The previous 45 flight trajectories across seven obstacles in `batter` contain 3-D movement features and individual identity but *not* the needed randomized route-component practice assignment and standardized B-R4 sentinel. Chronology across obstacle configurations is not recovered. The same five individuals have already reached the post-outcome mechanism ceiling. **NO SAME-DATA MOTOR-MODULE RESCUE.**

## Decision
`STOP_PUBLIC_CAUSAL_COMPOSITIONAL_TRANSFER_WITH_CURRENT_VERIFIED_SOURCES`.

This means only that **the four checked studies do not supply the specified causal input**, not that such a dataset does not exist in the world. The prior literature supports bat route learning, context dependence, and the possible sensory confound. It does **not** support the new causal motor-module claim.

## Next eligible independent source and action
Only reopen when a new dataset or full protocol explicitly verifies: randomized practice-route identity (physical H/V/none arms), matched fixed untrained sentinel pre/post outcomes, per-bat trial identities with repeated physical task measure, coherent missingness, and safe route/cue engineering. Prefer author-released 3-D flight data with assignment schedules rather than another species-independent identity screen. Do not adjust these causal criteria to fit a convenient public recording.
