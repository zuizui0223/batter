## Fixed-sequence causal chain

### P1 — route-choice individual specialization

Direct ecological primary.

Question:

> Does OPEN acquisition history create stronger held-out individual route organization than CONSTRAINED acquisition history when both matched families are later four-route OPEN?

Statistic:
`Delta_R`.

P1 must pass before a confirmatory downstream mechanistic claim.

### P2 — transparent I/M organization

Only after P1 support.

Question:

> Does the induced specialization also appear in the fixed FlightIntensity / ManeuveringExtent policy representation?

Statistic:
`Delta_A`.

No PCA rescue, H/V substitution or weight refitting.

### Downstream

Only after the upstream hierarchy is respected:

- early-versus-late history refinement;
- suppression -> exact reopening;
- transformed transfer;
- fine trajectory realization.

A downstream positive cannot rescue an upstream failure.

## Randomization

After capability screening, eligible animals enter complete blocks of four:

- A-open / A-start;
- A-open / B-start;
- B-open / A-start;
- B-open / B-start.

Treatment assignment is therefore balanced against matched-family identity and starting-family order.

Exact conditional treatment assignment space:
- 16 animals / 4 blocks: 256;
- 20 animals / 5 blocks: 1,024;
- 24 animals / 6 blocks: 4,096.

## Sample gate
