## Pilot firewall

Engineering pilot work is allowed before the confirmatory freeze only to determine:
- obstacle spacing;
- route feasibility;
- safe gap widths;
- camera/tracking quality;
- feasible trial counts and rest schedule;
- whether A/B families are physically matched.

Any animal whose free-choice behavior is inspected while changing these design quantities is an **engineering pilot animal**.

Engineering pilot animals must not enter the confirmatory randomized cohort.

Preferred pilot hierarchy:
1. CAD / geometric simulation;
2. non-biological tracking tests;
3. separate pilot animals;
4. only then freeze the confirmatory apparatus.

Do not use confirmatory-cohort route choices to tune:
- obstacle geometry;
- route labels;
- reward placement;
- number of trials;
- capability thresholds.

## What can be piloted without contaminating P1

Allowed on separate pilot animals:
- isolated-route traversability;
- collision risk;
- apparatus safety;
- tracking dropout;
- session duration / fatigue;
- whether 4-route choice is behaviorally usable at all.

Not allowed for confirmatory design tuning:
- selecting the geometry that produces the strongest individual differences;
- selecting the route count that maximizes identity;
- choosing I/M weights after pilot identity analysis;
- choosing early/late window because a particular split looks significant.
