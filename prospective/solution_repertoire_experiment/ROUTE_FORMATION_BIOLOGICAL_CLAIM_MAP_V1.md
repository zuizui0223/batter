# Biological claim ladder for personal route formation — programme decision memo

**Status: prospectively proposed research direction, not empirical discovery.**

This memo is a synthesis across PR #72 (solution opportunity), PR #81 (short-inertia counterexample), the independent both-family matched reset critique, and the randomized-seed proposal in this branch. Neither frozen JAE primary nor any animal experiment is modified.

## Biological question — not another individuality detection result
> When animals face several comparably adequate ways of reaching the same target, is their later choice determined mainly by pre-existing performance constraints, by arbitrary early experience, or by feedback from differential performance?

These possibilities imply different causes of individuality **even if all animals share the same 3-D airspace** and exhibit repeated personal corridors.

### Competing causal mechanisms

**M1 Pre-existing feasibility / morphology.**
Individuals already differ in which turning angle, vertical acceleration, obstacle approach and wing-loading combination they can execute cheaply. They repeatedly use the personally cheaper path. Predictions: route use is associated with measured independent biomechanical performance; randomly assigned seed route does not have a delayed causal effect once equal familiarity and a common immediate route state are imposed.

**M2 Contingent history / stochastic symmetry breaking.**
All routes are approximately equivalent in present task value. Which one is first encountered modifies later route propensity. Predictions: after a common route-state reset, independently randomized early seed route still predicts delayed choice. This does not imply that chosen personal routes have higher payoffs or that memory originates in a particular brain region.

**M3 Performance-feedback-mediated learning.**
Random seed changes early experience, but preference is updated selectively based on experienced energetic/time/error cost. Predictions: seed effect interacts with *independently validated route-specific cost*, and behavior tracks the route providing better measured performance under unchanged current opportunity. Only a prespecified task payoff/energetic performance contrast, not route identity alone, distinguishes this from neutral path dependence.

**M4 Acute motor inertia.**
Only the most recent route is predictive. Predictions: same-route repetition during consecutive flights, but no seed-specific correspondence after a complete shared first-order state reset. Higher-order or latent Markov states remain distinct plausible alternatives.

**M5 Treatment-gated predisposition.**
Multiple persistent movement propensities existed before experimental assignment. Opportunity selects which becomes expressed. Predictions: both P1 and delayed matched route identity can pass without any newly acquired latent preference. An independent randomized *which-route* seed is needed to test route-specific experience, but still not every form of learning.

## Distinguish the actual estimands
| Intervention | Outcome | Causal interpretation if supported | Explicit ceiling |
|---|---|---|---|
| Existing randomized 4-versus-1 acquisition opportunity (P1) | Held-out 8-to-8 individual route prediction | Opportunity history changes immediate route organization | Does not establish durable memory |
| Proposed both-family matched forced R1 reset | Randomized post-reset OPEN-minus-CONSTRAINED route correspondence | Opportunity history changes re-expression after common state interruption | Gated predisposition remains possible |
| Independently randomized route seed within balanced four-animal blocks | Delayed proportion choosing **assigned** seed route after interruption | Arbitrary route-specific assigned experience has a causal effect on later choice | Priming / route familiarity also possible; no fitness conclusion |
| Independent morphology and 3-D kinematic performance | Interaction of assigned seed, route cost and delayed use | Tests whether physical constraints or feedback shape the use of learned routes | In-lab performance is not demographic fitness |

## Conceptually new hypothesis (to test, not assert)
Ecological opportunity may maintain individuality without ongoing spatial partitioning because a population can contain **multiple individually learned but overlapping solutions**, with *history selecting one acceptable solution* while *individual biomechanics and environmental conditions determine how efficiently it can be expressed*.

That hypothesis is qualitatively different from:
- individuality merely reflecting persistent topographic differences;
- a unique universal 2-D flight equation;
- animals needing to avoid one another vertically;
- ecological opportunity always increasing the magnitude of individualization.

Its strongest falsifier would be that randomized route-seed identity has no delayed effect while morphology or experimentally manipulated task costs consistently predict later routes, even in designs where the seed test has adequate prospective precision.

## Ecological performance and adaptation
Measure collision / miss probability, target-approach success, time to target, distance or energy-related proxies for *each* path. To claim that route specialization is adaptive, prospectively demonstrate a performance advantage under comparable task difficulty, and ideally connect to foraging intake, energy balance or reproduction/survival in ecological conditions. Shorter paths or faster flight alone do not establish evolutionary fitness.

The cleanest hypothesis set is therefore:
1. **Origin:** does independently randomized personal history create route-specific preference?
2. **Retention:** does the effect survive state equalization and independent time gaps?
3. **Selection:** does feedback from route-specific performance reinforce or overwrite the arbitrary historical seed?
4. **Consequence:** does the maintained strategy improve biological performance compared with viable alternatives?

No known public *Rhinolophus* 45-trajectory archive has a randomized route seed or independent route-value manipulation. Reanalysis of those same five bats cannot adjudicate these causal questions. Any bat study using this ladder requires a separate prospectively registered, welfare-reviewed protocol and infrastructure feasibility work.
