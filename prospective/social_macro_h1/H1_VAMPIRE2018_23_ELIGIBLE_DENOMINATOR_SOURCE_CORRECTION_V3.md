# Vrtilek 2018 24 source naive rows versus 23 eligible participants: published reason

Source-correction update, 2026-11-19, after reading actual original article full-text (DOI 10.1098/rsos.172483) but no new parameter tuning. Earlier source-phase gate V2 noted 24 'during' naive-role rows versus manuscript 5/23 and left cause unresolved.

**Resolved from the paper's Results §3.2:** the authors excluded **one of the 24 initially naive female bats** from the demonstrator-trial analytical denominator because she *exited the maze when the experimenter collected bats after the PRE-demonstrator trial*. Hence she had learned/experienced the exit **before** demonstrator exposure, despite not exiting within the 15-min pretrial window. The original final demonstrator-naive eligible rate is therefore 5/23, not 5/24; the original recorded file may nevertheless include 24 naive-role trial records. Distinguish observed pretrial performance 0/24 from *pre-exposure naive* analytic denominator 23.

Do not treat the 24th source trial row as a statistical missing value or assign it a zero. Use original source 'naive' flag at the demonstrator trial to identify experienced participant only **if** the flag and role coding confirm the paper statement; otherwise STOP exact replication / report unresolved mapping. All 24 naive-role physical bats remain in descriptive row-count audit. In posttrial, a bat may be 'experienced' precisely BECAUSE of exposure; do not exclude such bats using posttrial 'naive' status from the retention transition.

**Published post phase nuance:** four of five bats that exited *during demonstrator trials* exited again when the demonstrator was removed. A sixth individual, previously not exited, first succeeded in posttrials; authors noted this bat might have followed an 'experienced bat' playing a demonstrator-like role. Thus a posttrial success cannot automatically be counted as asocial individual memory, nor does the conditional 4/5 identify a causal learned-private-state parameter.

The source's 188 original records include 104 training records of 12 experienced demonstrators and 84 test role records: 24 pre-naive, 24 during-naive-role +12 trained demonstrators, 24 post-naive-role. A legitimate outcome audit must distinguish **role, experience state, phase, known physical bat IDs, and shared three-bat triad exposures**.
