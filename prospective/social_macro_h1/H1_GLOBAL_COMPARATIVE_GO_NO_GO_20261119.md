# H1 species-level macro comparative feasibility decision v1 — 2026-11-19

**Outcome of current execution: STOP new global social-information coefficient. Source and measurement compatibility are audited; no new biological effect size or independent holdout claim has been produced.**

## Actual completed checks

1. GitHub `batter` PR #101 was open, draft and unmerged when these tasks began. Its 2019 held-out buzz×baseline test had no positive mean new-lake predictive gain in four receiver taxon/group categories, a single German study; this is not evidence that individual bats cannot use social information.
2. Original Safi & Kerth 2007 (DOI `10.1086/520116`) publishes `42248data.txt` (~9.70 KB) and `42248data.xls` (~34 KB). A fresh source-only GitHub runner attempted both exact official publisher URLs. Each returned **HTTP 403**; [Actions run 44511407420](https://github.com/zuizui0223/batter/actions/runs/44511407420) intentionally FAILED, with metadata-only stop receipt saved. **No 2007 original data lines or coefficient estimates were opened.** The fact the files are listed does not prove this runner has access.
3. 2026 Wilson et al. (*Animal Behaviour* 231:123406, DOI `10.1016/j.anbehav.2025.123406`) identifies 4 recipient species but its original treatment×site trial table could **not be independently retrieved or SHA verified** from source searches. The abstract specifically reports *Lasiurus cinereus* response to its conspecific hawking calls, *Lasionycteris noctivagans* repulsion by L. cinereus hawking, and no detected responses for *Myotis lucifugus* to tested sequences. **The abstract does not provide the direction for Eptesicus fuscus**, and any missing/non-significant effect MUST NOT be coded as an absolute biological no-response species.
4. A previous purported direct external 2018 hoary-bat source, Corcoran & Weller (*Proc R Soc B*, DOI `10.1098/rspb.2018.0441`), exists at Dryad `10.5061/dryad.pc1q122`; **the usage notes explicitly describe `AudioVideoData.xlsx` as an inventory of filenames**, not a signed acoustic-playback response table. The source article reports 17 conspecific and 12 heterospecific speaker approaches over two nights and 0 during silence, but **cannot identify unique visiting individuals**. Counting 17 approaches as 17 independent bats would be erroneous.
5. Fenton et al. (1976, DOI `10.1139/z76-167`) observed *M. lucifugus* responses to conspecific distress calls in a New Hampshire **barn**. Wilson (2026) studied bat activity in a Saskatchewan **creek foraging** setting. The two studies differ in stimulus, landscape, observation protocol and year; this is an ecological *context contrast* and hypothesis generator, **not matched evidence that site itself caused the difference**.
6. Hałat et al. (2024) (DOI `10.1016/j.anbehav.2024.08.020`) reported direct temporally synchronized male *Vespertilio murinus* foraging in Białowieża, Poland, and released Mendeley `10.17632/sfhzmtkg92.1`; direct local enhancement/group facilitation is **already established prior art**, not independent replication of controlled buzz×baseline response.
7. Calderón-Capote et al. (2025) (*PLOS ONE* DOI `10.1371/journal.pone.0313782`) compared Panama *Phyllostomus hastatus* GPS and groups with historical Trinidad social recruiting. The 25 Panama female bats did not coordinate commutes; that study explicitly highlights within-species population variation and source Movebank IDs. It cannot be recoded as all conspecific information transfer being biologically absent, or as a new species independent of preexisting `batter` panels.
8. The 2024 global 1,279-bat-species anthropogenic roosting database (DOI `10.1016/j.isci.2024.110369`) is large, but measures **use of human-made roosts**. It is **not** a substitute for male social grouping, recruiting, co-foraging, private memory, reciprocal sharing or information transfer. A 1,279-species model of the wrong response is not a large-scale H1 test.

All explicit comparison labels are machine-audited in `H1_INDEPENDENT_REPLICATION_ELIGIBILITY_V4.csv` and unit tests. The 2022 selected review Table 2 (35 cases, 20 strategy-coded cases) remains a prior-art seed only.

## Main scientific go/no-go

| Question | Decision | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Does resource guild predict male grouping across temperate bat species? | **Prior-art supported, re-analysis STOP** | Already Safi & Kerth 2007; historical source blocked; no newly coded independent male-group observations |
| Does feeding-buzz content×pre-activity improve transfer to new lakes? | **No support in one archived 2019 source** | Four 2019 receiver groups held-out means ≤0, all effect intervals include zero; not a zero-effect proof |
| Does the same sensory cue consistently attract a species across populations? | **INDETERMINATE** | Same species but stimulus and site conditions differ; 2026 original site×cue rows unavailable |
| Do bats use individual acoustic identity, social cues about food timing, or suppress calling under predator risk? | **Published within-source observations supported** | Disparate species, channels and responses; published prior art; no comparable meta-estimand |
| Do personal 3D strategies replace shared social information or reciprocity? | **NOT IDENTIFIED** | Existing 3D vertical identity is not measured cognitive memory; other sources do not jointly measure both axes |
| Does a 1,279-species roost dataset rescue H1? | **NO (invalid response proxy)** | Anthropogenic-roost adaptation is not male group formation or social foraging |

## What would change the decision

**Route A: true macro sociality comparison.** Recover original Safi & Kerth 2007 source and independently code NEW (post-2007) male grouping/roost status per species, including suitable tested negative states, with year, sex, geographic region, research effort and taxonomy crosswalk. Do not use the 2007 paper as novel validation. Only fit a phylogenetic model once the original and *independent new* response frame pass source and overlap gates. An arbitrary numerical threshold of 30 independent taxa is a prewritten conservative gate, not an achieved sample size.

**Route B: true signed social-cue response meta-analysis.** Obtain the 2026 Wilson bat playback original trial table and at least one more independent study/population with *same or harmonizable sound class*, measured bat activity before/during/after or matched silence/noise, and documented sampling denominator. Freeze common effect/effort estimand and count independent study×population units before open outcome tests. With current source catalogue, **zero independently verified post-2019 study sources replicate the exact graded-buzz×baseline interaction**.

Until one route passes, no additional p-value searches, cross-species H3 correlation, or global-population-evolution claims should be attempted.
