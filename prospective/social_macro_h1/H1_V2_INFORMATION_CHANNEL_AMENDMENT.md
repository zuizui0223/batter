# H1 v2 — personal and social information can be complementary, not merely alternative

Status: PROSPECTIVE REVISED HYPOTHESES + PRIOR-ART AUDIT, 2026-11-19. Not new estimates, not the frozen JAE manuscript.

## Why the simple binary is no longer tenable

The 2022 Kohles et al. conceptual framework already predicted where four social-foraging strategies arise. Its Table 2 is a theory-development and evidence-synthesis set; 35 rows and 20 resolved strategy rows are not an external test. New 2024 and 2026 original studies show ecological degrees of freedom that a fixed-fruit vs ephemeral-insect axis hides.

Critically, Lourie et al. (2024, DOI 10.1098/rstb.2024.0060) experimentally introduced Ficus sycomorus odour on bats entering an Egyptian fruit bat roost. Naive, previously tagged bats visited the fruitless known tree at 10/56 within six nights, significantly more often than routine non-fruit tracking. The evidence supports *socially cued timing* of a change in fruiting state plus spatial-memory-based selection of a preknown destination, rather than obligatory physical following of the odour-carrying bat. It is one experiment in one species, not a general meta-analytic effect. An uncontrolled environmental-odor vector and individual variation remain alternatives.

Wilson et al. (2026, DOI 10.1016/j.anbehav.2025.123406) observed attraction, repulsion and no detectable response among four North American bats during field playbacks. They share one study cluster; experimental cue contrasts and recorder detection differ, and Eptesicus outcome was not fully retrievable from the abstract. Glossophaga mutica handleyi (2026, DOI 10.1111/nyas.70427) showed sex-specific attraction/avoidance to the same calls at artificial flowers (16 biological bats). Co-located signals can indicate competition rather than prey availability. There is also an active taxonomy question: G. mutica is part of a historically broader G. soricina complex; do not silently merge accepted species.

Already known: a bat-wide density × buzz-rate response (Lewanzik et al. 2019, DOI 10.1111/1365-2656.12989); hump-shaped capture payoff in Molossus (Krivoruchko et al. 2024, DOI 10.1073/pnas.2321724121); restricted co-use and lower prey-attack attempts in Myotis macrodactylus (Fujioka et al. 2026, DOI 10.1371/journal.pone.0343485). These are distinct behavioral targets and not independently invented results.

## Updated ecological construct

Define independent features for each species × population × food context:
- S (spatial location persistence across bouts), T (temporal food availability unpredictability), Q (food patch divisibility / shareability), K (whether a personal spatial-location map is independently documented), C (detectability and semantics of social cue), D (social density and competition), R (receiver sex/state/context).
- Social cue meaning M belongs to separate classes: *where food is*, *when a familiar food source is profitable*, *how profitable/competitive the site is*, *recruitment/directional signal*, *unknown*.
- Observed decision Y = signed response with experimental control and uncertainty, or explicit following/recruitment/social discovery. Do not map no significant response to absolute absence.

### Testable patterns

H1a (information-channel separation): With high S and low temporally predictable T, cues about **when** resources become available will more often co-occur with remembered **where** locations than with verified physical following; this MUST be tested outside Lourie 2024, where it is established prior art.

H1b (patch-relative signed response): When food is shareable but conspecific crowding is low, prey-related acoustic cues may attract, while competition or interference can reverse the response. The new contribution would be transferable *quantitative moderator thresholds* across independently sampled populations, not simply observing attraction/repulsion.

H1c (receiver-dependent interpretation): Identical signal content can have opposing responses by sex/condition; test cue × receiver × resource context when the same treatments and replicated independent individuals are available. Glossophaga 2026 is a motivating precedent, not a new test.

H2 (independent vertical strategy): The frozen batter dataset validates repeatable individual vertical-use probability distribution shape. It does not measure spatial memory, learning, cue-use, or resource predictability, so H2 cannot be coded as a cognitive memory metric.

H3 (individual vs social knowledge): No universal negative correlation is forced. Possible hypotheses are additive independent contributions, synergy (interaction), or context-dependent substitution. Require same-species matched individually identified memory/source-of-information response estimates measured separately with original outcomes held out. Do not infer from resource guild labels.

## Do-able analysis with currently available evidence

The review index has 35 case rows, of which 29 explicit species and one pooled taxa row, and 20 fully classified strategies. Among 25 review-selected cases with both persistence and cue-location known, no discordance with the published 2022 classification was found; this is a circular evidence crosswalk, not replication. See REVIEW_TABLE2_BASELINE_V1.csv.

A targeted post-2022 primary-source ledger has 12 distinct *study × taxon × cue × receiver-context* rows, eight named taxa and six published study clusters. ZERO rows independently resolve one of the four classical strategies in a newly sampled population and provide measured predictor covariates under the new protocol. No phylogenetic species-wide strategy response model is eligible. See POST2022_PRIMARY_EVIDENCE_V1.csv and deterministic audit.
