# H1 macro-comparative branch: independent-outcome feasibility gate V1

2026-11-19. **Source-schema eligibility and novelty firewall, not a new comparative result.** Existing JAE `batter` and `main` frozen.

## Why refocus
2015–2026 original acoustic playback studies were re-analyzed but measure discordant outcomes: calls emitted, detected flight activity, head-waggles, and patch visits. A 4-species qualitative 2026 playback result does not by itself create an independent phylogenetic test. The prospective signed-cue dataset remains missing study-level original conditions and defensible zeros.

Safi & Kerth (2007), DOI 10.1086/520116, provides **a genuinely species-level outcome**—male group formation (NOT social foraging or sharing)—and supplies historical TXT/XLS comparative data:
- `https://www.journals.uchicago.edu/doi/suppl/10.1086/520116/suppl_file/42248data.txt` (~9.70 KB)
- `https://www.journals.uchicago.edu/doi/suppl/10.1086/520116/suppl_file/42248data.xls` (~34 KB)
- `https://www.journals.uchicago.edu/doi/suppl/10.1086/520116/suppl_file/42248apa.pdf` (appendix)
Official publisher lists all three. These are **prior art**, and simply re-fitting their published foraging-ecology versus male-roost sociality link is not new evidence.

## Source access and blind gate
- Download official TXT first then XLS fallback; record HTTP status, response bytes and SHA256, not any fitted associations or taxon effect directions.
- For TXT, expose only first original header + delimiter + source row count; do not report inferential outcomes until field provenance, sex, non-roost behavioral status and 2007 taxonomy are verified.
- If not retrievable, STOP and record paths/status. Never fill records using inferred resource guild, other papers, 1279-species anthropogenic-roost database, or 2022 selected positive-only cue cases.
- Identify whether each species' male sociality is one binary state or multiple study/season-specific observations; original trait coding must be preserved with NA.

## Future valid macro target (only after original and *new independent species response* obtained)
- Predictor fields: original resource temporal persistence (independent source), measured/open-space flight morphology, foraging mode and body size.
- Outcome one: **male roost group presence**, distinguished from breeding colonies, foraging social information sharing, and echolocation interactions.
- Outcome two: **direct information-use responses**, only in a separately sampled, effort-controlled independent original set (never derive from male grouping).
- Duplicated taxa, historical splits and synonym mapping: use current MDD canonical taxonomy with explicit original-name crosswalk. Never silently collapse Glossophaga mutica into G. soricina, etc.
- Expected novelty hinges on updating **the same behavioral male-sociality response** with independent new taxonomic/region groups, out-of-source validation, not just recomputing a published PGLS.
- If new suitable response material only covers the old 2007 species and no genuinely independently sampled new species/regions, **STOP new global association test** and retain the original published result as prior art.

## External fact-check: 2018 versus 2026 Lasiurus case
- Corcoran & Weller (2018), DOI 10.1098/rspb.2018.0441, west North America observed hoary bats approach *conspecific and heterospecific* echolocation playbacks (17 approaches during 13 5-min conspecific playbacks over two nights, 12 heterospecific approaches, 0 silence); these are approaches, **not 17 independent bats**. The original paper states individual IDs cannot be resolved.
- Corcoran Dryad 10.5061/dryad.pc1q122 includes `AudioVideoData.xlsx`, but the source itself defines this XLSX as **file name inventory**, not randomized playback response table. Do not download multigigabyte audio files and relabel them playback observations.
- Wilson et al. (2026), DOI 10.1016/j.anbehav.2025.123406, Canada report *Lasiurus cinereus* attraction to **conspecific hawking** sequences but not other conspecific/heterospecific distress or attack calls; *Lasionycteris noctivagans* avoidance of *L. cinereus* hawking; *Myotis lucifugus* no response in this study. **The abstract does not resolve Eptesicus fuscus response**, so missing is NA, not zero.
- Fenton et al. (1976), DOI 10.1139/z76-167, historical *M. lucifugus* **New Hampshire barn** reaction to conspecific distress sound (positive), unlike 2026 Canadian stream-field no-detect result. Acoustic stimulus, roost/foraging task, observation effort and region all change: **not a controlled environmental moderation test or contradiction of a common estimand**.
- Wilson (2026) has no original trial-by-stimulus table/SHA-verified archive located in available public searches as of this gate; quantify sign only as explicitly described, no invented p or response absence across the species.
- Comparing 2018 and 2026 without original matched treatment, control, detector/speaker effort does NOT yield a new ecological law, even if qualitative apparent contrast looks compelling.

## Priority
First acquire 2007 TXT source, check original species/field structure; second independently search genuinely additional species-level male-sociality measurements after 2007 with effort metadata. If neither, STOP macro model and avoid repeated incompatible acoustic-source mining.
