# 2026 PNAS ear-gaze source: uniqueness and eligibility boundary

**PRE-DATA SOURCE-ONLY. No original ear orientation or kinematic numeric values opened.**
Source: Häfele et al. PNAS 2026 DOI 10.1073/pnas.2605701123; published data DOI 10.5281/zenodo.20927789 and author GitHub repo fhaefele/target-focused-hearing-of-echolocating-bats-pnas.

## Key genuine published sensorimotor fact
Under high-speed prey targeting bats direct both pinnae forward toward the target, reducing the interaural intensity difference and improving directional gain in the target direction. That is the authors' principal published result. **Not new** in batter: target-focused hearing, acoustic field-of-view narrowing, or conserved species-level ear-head coupling.

The public Zenodo record has compiled model and posture exports, and authors explain the complete raw material needs direct request. Some exports are *species-averaged* or conditional fitted curves, not necessarily bat-identified trialwise sensorimotor observations. The author Figure 2 MATLAB script uses `BatID` attributes in an intermediate MATLAB structure; that alone cannot establish those identifiers are retained in the public CSV and cannot license unsupported individual classification.

## Conditional model questions — only if genuine repeated physical IDs and trials survive the gate

1. Is each bat's target-relative pinna-gaze *offset* stable on held-out bouts once target distance, speed and species are controlled, rather than merely reproducing the universal forward-ear finding?
2. Are left/right asymmetry differences more portable within the same individual across independent bouts than the pooled species mean? This is **not** a guarantee of left-right handedness; test only if bilateral measurements and calibrations truly exist.
3. Does individually predicted ear/flight-heading coupling add a new held-out predictor beyond ear position and global prey distance, with camera calibration and session effects prevented from functioning as permanent bat identifiers?

Never test these sequentially to pick the most positive. A new numerical outcome contract, once Gate 2 passes, must select **one** question and one endpoint before values are opened. Retain the numerical non-significant possibilities and exact animal count.

## Scientific context
The flight condition is isolated wind-tunnel/prey targeting, **not simultaneous bat conspecific interference**. Any identified personal ear policy is therefore an instance of individual sensorimotor organization, not a demonstration of acoustic substitution for spatial niche partitioning. In particular:
- It cannot explain the original single-animal Rhinolophus 3D obstacle identity as social jamming adaptation;
- It cannot prove learning, nor rule out morphology/age/device calibration, without intervention;
- 3 animal species are not 3 independent bat cohorts if each compiled export is one or a few individuals;
- repeated frames are not independent biological animals.

## Decision
No more new same-data Rhino PCA / residual rotations; no reinterpretation of inaccessible published CSV; no voluntary changes to existing JAE RC2. Source-only inspected dataset schema is the next bounded work unit. If no persistent physical bat and independently repeated bout fields, report STOP_NO_INDEPENDENT_INDIVIDUAL_BOUTS and prioritize existing JAE paper editing over further dataset hunting.
