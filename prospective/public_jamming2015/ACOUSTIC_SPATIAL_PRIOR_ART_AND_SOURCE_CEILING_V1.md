## Published direct counterexample to a simple 'acoustics instead of space' story

Goldshtein et al. 2025, PNAS 122:e2407810122, DOI 10.1073/pnas.2407810122; data DOI 10.17632/k7pt2j84f7.1:

- Species: Rhinopoma microphyllum emerging in a crowd of ~2000 bats.
- **96 individual 3D/geolocation tracks** across 2018 (59 bats) and 2019 (37 bats), ATLAS at ~1–2 Hz.
- Onboard microphones yielded analyzable acoustic data for **four actual individual bats** (two from main 2018 tracking cohort plus two additional from another 2018 tagging round); acoustic N must be counted separately from tracking N.
- As the collective emerged, spatial expansion / falling local density rapidly reduced conspecific acoustic masking even without a conventional spectral jamming-avoidance response.
- The paper estimates masked-call fraction changing from **0.45±0.13 to 0.36±0.20** as distance increased from 25 m to 200 m (~25 s). Its acoustic and collision claims and the computational sensorimotor redundancy mechanism were **already reported by the original authors**.
- This is **collective density dilution**, not permanent mutually exclusive height or individual 3D airspace division. Both 'no individual niche partition' and group-scale dispersion can be true simultaneously. Never use this as empirical evidence that stationary CF2 colony convergence itself prevents spatial partitioning.

Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12002023/
Data: https://data.mendeley.com/datasets/k7pt2j84f7/1

## Receiver-side functional adaptation is also prior art

Luo, Goerlitz, Brumm & Wiegrebe (Scientific Reports 2015, DOI 10.1038/srep18556) quantified behaviorally relevant bat sonar signal detectability improvements arising from intensity (the Lombard effect), duration and calling redundancy in Phyllostomus discolor. Published response magnitude included signal amplitude increases **1.8–7.9 dB**, call-duration increases **13–85%**, and duration-specific detectability gains **1.0–5.3 dB**, plus modeled extra redundant-call benefit. Therefore a generic claim that call amplitude, duration and timing can help in noise is **NOT** novel in this programme.

Source: https://www.nature.com/articles/srep18556

## Direct incompatibility with the 45-flight controlled obstacle result

The pre-existing five-bat *Rhinolophus* movement-policy analyses use **separate individual flights through obstacle configurations**, not a randomized multi-bat acoustic crowding intervention. Therefore the successful per-individual 3D movement-policy identity in that source cannot be mechanistically *explained* by contemporaneous conspecific sonar signal divergence/convergence on the basis of that source alone. Even a perfect individual route-identity result would be compatible with intrinsic biomechanics, single-animal sensorimotor preferences, prior learning outside the experimental sequence, or repeated task constraints.

Likewise, the 2025 collective-emergence 96-bat movement study does not independently replicate the five-bat 3D personal movement-policy tests: its ecological task, tracking frequency, social environment, and contrast are different. The properly formulated general claim is **possible coexistence across scales**, not an already established shared physiological mechanism.

## What would prove acoustic-spatial functional substitution rather than correlated response?

In SAME stable physical bats, prospectively and ethically manipulate **acoustic interference** independently of **safe spatial avoidance opportunity**, holding bat reward/geometry/sound intensity controls, and measure:
- emitter call statistics/time AND true auditory masking/received echo detection;
- actual 3D conspecific separation, not only group width and not only stationary CF;
- independent prey success/error or biologically calibrated task performance;
- repeated cross-condition bat-level responses, with social-group and trial effects in uncertainty.

The strong causal target is a prespecified intervention-by-intervention contrast on receiver-side payoff and **within-bat policy**. Mere correlation between social density, emitted frequency and space, or a significant interaction on a bounded raw success probability alone, does not prove mechanism switching or substitution. Apparatus geometry can also change echo clutter, and genuine received-masker exposure must be calibrated.
