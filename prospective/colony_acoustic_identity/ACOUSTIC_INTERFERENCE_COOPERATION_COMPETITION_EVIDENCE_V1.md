# Acoustic interference is an ecological interaction, not one universal segregation strategy

**Source-backed literature synthesis and prospective causal question; NO new empirical finding.**
No raw CF2 data or new bat source outcomes were opened. JAE and preregistered Rhinolophus 3-D movement primaries remain frozen.

## Reframe the central ecological question
The wrong universal question is: 'Do crowding bats acoustically separate instead of spatially segregating?' It presupposes that interference is always mitigated by increasing distinctness.

**Better question:** What determines whether proximity to a conspecific is resolved by
(A) spectral identity separation,
(B) time-sharing of an acoustic channel,
(C) CF convergence for a shared sensory window,
(D) exploitative acoustic jamming of rivals,
or (E) leaving the 3-D neighborhood?

These are alternative rules with different ecological effects: reducing cost to both individuals, reducing self cost while retaining overlaps, sharing possible public sensory information, or reducing competitor foraging success. Recording only nearest-neighbor distance or only frequency gaps cannot distinguish them.

## A concrete directional payoff manipulation is required
In a prospective species-specific controlled design, manipulate whether foraging reward is **exclusively contested** versus simultaneously available to all individuals while controlling prey density and acoustic crowding as far as mechanically/ethically feasible. Simultaneously randomize acoustic masking in a balanced, safely pilot-validated design. Measure individual echolocation signals/time, 3-D interactions, prey acquisition, and recipient versus emitter identity. This distinguishes:
- strategic exploitative signal jamming preferentially around rival feeding buzzes (competition-dependent);
- nonspecific masker avoidance (mostly sound-dependent);
- resource-blind spatial avoidance (distance dependent);
- receiver-centered silent-window optimization (spectral geometry dependent).

Purely observational comparison among 2014 Tadarida, 2018 Miniopterus and 2026 Rhinolophus cannot identify a transition rule because taxa, contexts, density, resource type and sound physics differ.

Minimum negative controls: randomized no-op/sham masking, rival-neutral acoustic control, comparable real echo strength and physical clearance, stable per-bat IDs, balanced role/reward, blinding and a preregistered bat-level analysis. No animal work is authorized by this programme. Jamming recordings and note concepts are not instructions to disrupt real bats in the wild.

## The first proof needed for the original 3-D niche paper
Under a **single** new multi-modal dataset with fixed individual IDs and repeated group contexts, demonstrate a treatment-linked change in a BAT'S ACOUSTIC OR SPATIAL strategy with a measured recipient-side ecological cost/benefit. Distinguish direct antagonism from self-signal discrimination and receiver noise protection. Without that, do not reinterpret a null 3-D partitioning statistic as evidence that bats compensated acoustically.

This is a causal falsification programme, not a claim that any of these mechanisms generated the previously observed Rhinolophus 2-D identity, wild vertical niches or individual histories.
