## A seemingly paradoxical pattern is ALREADY explained in previous literature

**FM example, published:** Hase et al. (2018), Communications Biology, DOI 10.1038/s42003-018-0045-3. Miniopterus fuliginosus, 19 physical bats allocated with overlaps to six four-bat groups, 3 recording conditions (single/group/single). On-board mic and 3-D recorded, but the publicly deposited Dryad DOI 10.5061/dryad.4f99c46 is a ~614MB SOUND ARCHIVE; no synchronized raw 3-D coordinates are listed. Mean adjacent terminal FM frequency gap increased 0.6±0.6 to 1.1±0.6 kHz during group flight. Group×neighbor comparisons are correlated, not 24 independent animals across groups.

**CF colony example, published:** Matsumoto et al. (2026), Journal of Comparative Physiology A, DOI 10.1007/s00359-026-01821-5. Rhinolophus nippon, 101 unique bats, 177 bat×mixing-event appearances, 15 events, resting CF2 over up to 30 days. Captured groups significantly LOWER than residents at baseline in 7 events; the other 8 had no significant group mean gap. There were ZERO events with significantly HIGHER captured than residents. CF2 usually converged upward via the initially low individuals. CF silent-spectral-window explanation is DIRECTLY ALREADY PROPOSED by these authors; not a new idea from this project.

**Functional CF consequence, published:** Yoshida et al. (2026), Communications Biology, DOI 10.1038/s42003-026-10217-9. In a six-bat within-animal noise experiment, 4 repeats per bat×condition, moth-attack (takeoff) rate was 100% in control and below-f_ref noise, versus 37.5% (9 of 24 trials) when noise masked the upper spectral window (above f_ref). Author-reported Firth likelihood-ratio p=1.5e-8. This supports ecological functionality of a silent window, NOT that actual social frequency convergence CAUSES better prey detection. Data and original code were published separately at Mendeley DOI 10.17632/wnx6zftj88.1 (not opened in this project).

## The unclosed causal triangle
  (1) social interference/crowding → (2) individual acoustic adjustment →
  (3) real individual foraging/detection payoff and 3-D space use.

2018 establishes (1→2) for a particular FM system. The two CF 2026 papers separately establish a group-related time-course of acoustic convergence and strong functional consequences of a masked spectral window. Neither alone directly shows a SAME-ANIMAL group-driven frequency change CAUSING a prey-detection advantage while holding social stress/time constant.

Therefore do NOT calculate a between-paper pooled CF/FM acoustic effect size, rebrand previously published separation vs convergence as a novel discovery, or infer a proved spatial–acoustic substitution.

## The key distinguishing counterfactual missing from the 2026 colony study
In the published 15 mixing events, **newcomers were never significantly HIGHER in CF2 than residents**. Three mechanisms can all explain newcomer CF2 increasing when it starts below resident:

| Mechanism | If newcomer HIGHER than established resident | If newcomer LOWER than resident | If newcomer-alone (no colony) |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Silent spectral-window protection:** the LOW-frequency animal pays interference cost from HIGH-freq calls | Resident LOW should shift UP; newcomer high roughly stays | Newcomer LOW should shift UP; resident high stable | No social gap-driven shift |
| **Social conformity to established group:** new member adapts to resident convention | Newcomer high should shift DOWN | Newcomer low should shift UP | no group-standard direction |
| **Post-capture / captivity state recovery:** captured bats have upward temporal drift independently of residents | Newcomer high might shift UP again, not toward residents | Newcomer low shifts UP | newcomer shifts UP even without residents |

These are **qualitative rival predictions**; the numerical magnitude, timescale and physiological model need experimental calibration. A random high newcomer does not guarantee residents can immediately alter their acoustic foveae. Animal ethical restrictions may make mixing inappropriate.

### Preferred causal manipulation without mixing live populations
Test using carefully controlled, acoustically verified playback of **hypothetical neighbor CF2 frequencies** above/below the focal baseline, balanced against sham and signal intensity, with fixed own bat health/environment and no physical novel conspecifics; this does not automatically reproduce full group social learning. Use separately approved cohort designs for extended conditioning, not a rapid repeated-measures crossover if slow colony accommodation creates carryover.

**Predeclare a directional primary**:
- Under sensory-window asymmetry: focal response increases for higher-frequency neighbor playback (threatens upper window) and is small for lower-frequency playback;
- Under purely symmetric social conformity: focal response follows neighbor direction (higher→up, lower→down);
- Under captivity drift: both placebo and playback share an upward time trend unrelated to sign.

This sign reversal — *which bat shifts when the rank order is reversed?* — tests a mechanism, not merely whether bats gradually match group frequencies.

### Direct ecological performance mediator
Predefine glint/prey detection against controlled **above-reference versus below-reference** spectral masking, ideally in the SAME identified animals whose CF accommodation is tracked. A change in absolute CF2 does not establish a performance gain. Measure spectral signal-to-noise in the appropriate receiver-centered band and independently scored flight/attack success. Treatment should not vary reward, audible discomfort, ambient noise intensity, physical obstacle clearance, handling or photoperiod systematically.

If a separate 3-D flight assay is feasible, simultaneously measure nearest-neighbor distance/height occupancy, emitted call timing, emitted CF2/TF (do NOT conflate these), received echo frequency, and prey detection. Only a fully factorial independent acoustic-interference manipulation can test acoustic/spatial substitution rather than a common reaction to crowding.

### Novel but speculative downstream question
The 2026 Matsumoto paper itself SPECULATES that a shared CF silent window could enable bats to exploit echo/glint information from conspecific pulses (group or 'public' sensing). A decisive study would compare detection attributable to externally tagged conspecific-origin echoes versus self-emitted echoes, with their arrival times/spectral windows identified and receiver response measured. This is NOT demonstrated in current datasets and cannot be inferred from group mean convergence alone.

## Rejected pseudo-replications and evidence safeguards
1. Different species, sound component (FM TF vs resting CF2), observation units and time frames: no valid numeric pooling as one effect.
2. 2018: same 19 bats reused across six nominal groups. 2026: 177 event rows but only 101 unique bats. Use physical bat as cluster and mixing event/time as another correlated source.
3. The 2026 group-frequency mechanism is a **hypothesis** consistent with data, not a randomized social audio intervention, and the authors acknowledge untested high-newcomer cases, captivity effects and aging/season.
4. The CF prey payoff experiment reports N=6 actual bats, and 24 within-condition trials are not independent biological animals.
5. Public Mendeley dataset DOI 10.17632/4y98p5y8fc.1 is catalogued but official API 3/3 HTTP 403 here: STOP numerical data extraction; no fabricated bat-level rank persistence or confidence intervals.

## Decision
The next genuinely testable ecological phenomenon is NOT simply "more competitors → more separation" or "CF frequency convergence." Both are prior art.
It is whether **the acoustic receiver's interference geometry**, independently manipulated from social familiarity, determines WHO adapts their signal and whether that adjustment improves prey-related sensory performance WITHOUT requiring physical 3-D exclusion. The inverse baseline-frequency mixing case is decisive and absent from the existing 15-event archive.

No new animal collection, request to human authors or source transfer is performed by this file. This is a causal design ledger, not biological evidence.
