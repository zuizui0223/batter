# Mechanistic experiment: does alternative 3-D route opportunity buffer sonar masking?

**PROSPECTIVE, NOT PERFORMED — no new bat evidence or authorized animal work.**
This decision is independent of frozen JAE and PR #72–87; do not substitute this protocol for PR #72's randomized ACQUISITION-history primary. No numerical data or synthetic power values have been opened for this design.

## What the existing literature already established — priority for novelty
- Taub & Yovel 2020, Sci Rep DOI 10.1038/s41598-019-57346-2: Six individual Pipistrellus kuhlii in flight-room target landing; placing a background "acoustic masker" hedge/board 30 or 10 cm behind the target altered behavior. Each animal had previously developed personal approach paths. This is direct **movement under spatial echo clutter**, prior art.
- Goldshtein et al. 2025, PNAS DOI 10.1073/pnas.2407810122: Rapid spatial spreading after a collective cave emergence reduced conspecific acoustic masking. This is **unrandomized group-scale density dilution**, not mutually exclusive individual 3-D niches.
- Ma et al. 2025, BMC Biology DOI 10.1186/s12915-025-02435-0: In Pratt's roundleaf bats, both acoustic noise and prey task changed terminal buzz/calling, already documenting **task×masker interaction**. Do not claim that generic masker×task interaction or changing call rate in noise is novel.
- Häfele et al. 2026, PNAS DOI 10.1073/pnas.2605701123: Prey-focused pinna orientation changes available receiver localization cues. That receiver sensory focus is **published prior art**, not new.
- Amichai et al. 2015, Proc R Soc B DOI 10.1098/rspb.2015.2064: Original self versus time-reversed and conspecific masker playback changes call design. No fresh individual phenotype estimate was obtained from this project because native Dryad workbook retrieval was 403/401.

## Single missing ecological causal estimand

**Does experimentally adding physically adequate route alternatives preferentially REDUCE the loss of foraging/target-detection performance caused by conspecific-like acoustic interference?**

Assign independently:
- R in {0=one preapproved canonical route open, 1=all four preapproved approximately adequate routes open}, keeping target destination and food reward identical;
- M in {0=matched sham playback, 1=acoustic masking playback with independently verified receiver-side spectrum/dose}. Do NOT assume a physical reflective clutter hedge equals a loudspeaker echolocation masker or natural group interference.

Each of four groups receives ONE initial confirmatory exposure condition per bat after identical initial capability/familiarization checks. Randomize at physical-bat level within preplanned blocks of four (one bat per R×M assignment), with masking loudness, room schedule, site/route layout, rewards and assessment window fixed in advance. Use between-bat **parallel** primary design to avoid carryover from repeated route learning/acoustic adaptation. Any future separate crossover needs empirically justified reset/washout and new versioned protocol.

### Primary outcome and estimand
A fixed-number-of-attempts binary prey detection/target-landing success under the original randomized condition. Y_i(R,M) is that bat's *mean success across the prespecified first valid attempts*, with every randomized bat retained under a predeclared welfare/attrition rule. The biological unit for inference is **BAT**, not repeated attempts, microphone pulses or XYZ frames.

The interaction estimand on the absolute-success-probability scale is

  D = [E(Y|R=1,M=1)-E(Y|R=0,M=1)]
    - [E(Y|R=1,M=0)-E(Y|R=0,M=0)].

D>0 says extra route opportunity gives MORE task-success benefit when the masker is present. This is stronger than a masker main effect or a simple identity repeatability result. A null D does not prove no other adaptation or a frequency-specific reaction.

**Important inference guard:** whole-block exact treatment relabeling tests the much STRONGER sharp null of NO treatment effect of either factor, NOT the weak interaction-only null when main effects exist. Do not call a naive (4!)^B reallocation p-value an exact interaction test. Formal inference should be prospectively prespecified with bat-level adjusted contrast and appropriately calibrated whole-block confidence limits / randomization-robust interaction estimator, after pilot source variance has been determined. No invented sample-size or power claim.

### Secondary mechanisms only after primary plan is frozen
- Full 3-D realized choices/altitude and target/obstacle clearance, standardized physical geometry and safe escape/no-approach rate;
- Bat-emitted calls timing, center/terminal frequency and intensity; sensor-corrected **received** acoustic background / target echo spectral overlap at flight locations;
- separation of social competitors from physical echo clutter and changes in target-SNR;
- individual route fidelity, tested only with independent repeated sessions, not attempted within this parallel one-shot primary;
- recipient payoff/effort errors or real predation intake; target-landings alone are not biological fitness.

## Essential physical feasibility / falsification gates (no animal test authorized)
1. **All four routes individually feasible and welfare-safe** in the unmasked initial capability pilot. Bat species and 3D chamber size chosen based on proven safe species-specific flight ability; never force a bat into unsafe narrow apertures.
2. **Route intervention is separable from nominal acoustic masker intervention.** Closing routes with solid obstacles modifies echoes and clearance; changing gate materials can itself change sonarscape. Survey emitted/received echo geometry with calibrated transducers and microphones along each route and explicitly document residual physical-echo differences. If independence impossible, reframe R as a joint route+echo intervention, not clean spatial acoustic compensation.
3. **True masker dose verified at receiver positions** across all allowed routes, including the intentionally alternative routes. A speaker power setting is not a bat-received dB/spectral overlap measurement; spatial shadowing may be mechanistically relevant and should be measured, not hidden.
4. Pilot establishes reproducible masker impairment under R=0 vs sham with no undue collisions/stress, without tuning the confirmatory experiment based on endpoint sign in the same bats.
5. **Task reward/time/handling are matched** within four random cells. Route opportunity can itself change exploration/reward acquisition, so sham interaction is indispensable.
6. Fixed predeclared bat-level N from separately collected pilot variability and ethically acceptable welfare load; no bootstrapping 1000 XYZ fixes into 1000 independent animal replicates.
7. Authorize welfare approvals, independent intervention reliability, monitoring/abort thresholds and preregister entire experiment before viewing real endpoint values. If fail any of 1–4, STOP; do not label a pilot-only result as 3-D acoustic behavioral substitution.

## What a positive interaction would, and would NOT, establish

A positive D with truly randomized factors supports **availability of alternative movement solutions mitigating the specific acute noise challenge in this task**, with physical opportunity effect robust to the sham comparison. If acoustic measurements further show bats switching paths that materially reduce *experienced* masker overlap, that strengthens the proposed sensorimotor pathway.

But D alone does not establish:
- true mental auditory masking instead of arousal/physical acoustics;
- that bats compensate *acoustically instead of spatially*;
- personal learned motor policies or origin of persistent individuality;
- separate effects of perceived vs emitted calls;
- improvement in long-term reproductive fitness or wild 3-D niche partitioning.

A null D combined with a masker-induced call timing/intensity change could still support a different solution, but would remain secondary not a primary rescue.

### Most important alternative that makes this nontrivial
If a masker causes a bat to **focus ear reception toward the prey** (already published 2026), extra spatial routes may make no difference even when navigation succeeds. If maskers dominate and the available flight routes all have similar spectral overlap, extra routes cannot buffer detection. If one route provides cleaner target echoes, route choice can compensate without individual acoustic-frequency segregation. These rival sign predictions preclude assuming positive D by construction.

## Programme fit and freeze boundary
Original PR #72 manipulates how many adequate routes were available **during formation** and tests later self-history. Here R×M tests immediate **functional ecology / adaptive value of opportunity**; combining them into one confirmatory manuscript would change the estimand and trial burden. Treat as a distinct new experiment candidate only, not a retrospectively registered extension.
