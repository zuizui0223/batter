**This is a research-prioritization checkpoint, not a new effect estimate.** Frozen JAE, BE and the five-Rhinolophus archive, their preregistered inferential statuses, and all source-backed numerical results remain unchanged.

## Useful deterministic clock fact without further animal-result fishing

[Original Quarto-code-only CI 38015421158](https://github.com/zuizui0223/batter/actions/runs/38015421158) found source script start HH:MM among {22:45,23:00} and end among {02:30,03:00}. Every possible source-processed window contains the previously analyzed 23:00–02:00 core **if common source-clock and cohort semantics apply**, proven for all 180 minute keys and four combinations. Thus source-code-based author-window **bounds**, not point estimates/p-values, are:
- May 15 bat pairs with at least one same-station same-minute detection: 6–17 / 28;
- May 16: 8–14 / 28;
- repeated positive across both source nights: 5–13 / 28.
No exact author per-batch mapping, local sun phase, receiver uptime or clock drift was validated. Hence **do not** claim the original foraging-only method has been exactly rerun.

## Policy against low-impact novelty inflation

Reject manuscript pitches:
- "individual bats have different routes" alone, since trait and route idiosyncrasy are commonplace;
- "some bats share spatial UDs while not always foraging together" as first finding, since **same-species and same-site prior art exists**;
- "repeated same-receiver detections prove kin-mediated social attraction" without kin dyads (none in May 2024 UD cohort), clock/effort controls, or direct encounters;
- "Bat-specific behavioral policy optimizes survival/foraging" without an independent counterfactual reward measure;
- "Non-significant extra segregation proves zero competition" or "3D overlap means no food competition".

**Opportunity still worth pursuing when an eligible independent study is available:** premeasure a personal movement/sonar policy in held-out nights; randomize physically/acoustically calibrated 3D route availability and independently calibrated interference separately; measure verified prey captures per fixed bat-time and exit responses; test whether an independently stable policy moderates true causal reward effect. Unchanged 3D physical/acoustic #93 gate is mandatory. Shared treatment effects alone do not identify persistent individual response heterogeneity.

## Decision
1. **Submission effort first:** JAE and independent BE drafts need human manuscript/authorship/license/release review; stop restarting frozen inferential tests.
2. **PR #96 stays draft/source appendix:** source-backed V1a/V1b/V2 observed statements are retained with 2013 historical citation and clock/receiver bounds. No independent journal paper on generic space/time separation.
3. **No more unblinded two-night OSF hour-RSSI scan** or same-source functional mechanism p-values. Only authorize source method validation after independent station-uptime, cohort inclusion and time calibration evidence, or move to independent data.
4. **Do not** merge into `main` or JAE frozen release without an explicit review.

This checkpoint is more scientifically consequential than obtaining an additional nominally significant result from the same two nights.
