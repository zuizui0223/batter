## Major originality correction and why to STOP

**Melber, Fleischmann & Kerth (2013), Ethology DOI https://doi.org/10.1111/eth.12123**, already documented that female *M. bechsteinii* share foraging regions especially with maternal relatives yet most do **not** forage together (22 bats, long-term VHF tracks). **Kerth, Wagner & König (2001), DOI https://doi.org/10.1007/s002650100352**, already addressed roosting together/foraging apart. The current 2026 paper says its grid uses 2013 VHF findings **from the same study site**. Thus this OSF side-analysis cannot honestly claim `first observation of bat spatial overlap despite temporal partition`; it also cannot empirically contradict 2013 because 1-min BLE receiver footprints, roost proximity, batches/cohorts and social-foraging definitions differ.

**STOP** further post-outcome scans of additional arbitrary time windows on the same two-night source to "obtain" an excess-synchrony p-value or tighter interval. Source-only calibration can be pursued if independent uptime/clock and cohort-index information becomes available. The primary JAE work remains distinct in its 3D terrain-conditioned vertical shape question; no causal bridge or prey payoff is established by this 2D receiver dataset.
