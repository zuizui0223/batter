# Public-data mechanism ceiling v1

## Status

**PROGRAMME CLAIM BOUNDARY FOR A PUBLIC-DATA-ONLY ROUTE.**

This document defines the strongest claim that may be assembled without collecting new experimental data.

It does not modify JAE v0.4.0.

## Evidence already available

### 1. Persistent personal movement organization

Controlled repeated-flight archives support individual-specific movement organization that persists across task configurations.

This includes the *Rhinolophus* portable-policy programme, subject to its n=5 mechanistic-ceiling boundary.

### 2. Controlled current-context perturbation

The public *Pipistrellus kuhlii* sensory-perturbation source supports retention of an individual movement bias across an imposed sensory manipulation.

Thus:

> changing current sensory conditions does not necessarily erase personal movement organization.

### 3. Personal-history refinement

The juvenile history programme supports stronger prediction from recent own-history than from earliest equally sized history.

Thus later movement organization contains identity-specific history information beyond a common ontogenetic trajectory.

This is not a randomized history manipulation.

## New public sources under audit

### Aharon et al. 2017 — priority 1

Potential leverage:
- individual-coded variables;
- condition-coded matrices;
- repeated trials;
- controlled manipulations of navigation cues/current physical context;
- turning, slowing and trial-speed structure.

If the public schema supports >=4 repeated bats across a manipulation, this source can test whether individual navigation organization transfers across a controlled change in current cue structure.

### Ma et al. 2025 — priority 2

Published design:
- four foraging bats;
- four separate landing bats;
- prey-context and masking-noise manipulations;
- high-speed flight recording.

Best use:
within-task individual persistence under current ecological/sensory manipulation.

Because n=4 per task is the minimum exact-permutation scale and the two tasks use different individuals, this source cannot carry broad generality or cross-task portability by itself.

## Strongest public-data-only causal triangulation

If Aharon passes and supports individual transfer across manipulation, the public-data programme may combine:

1. personal history becomes identity-informative;
2. individual movement organization transfers across multiple task geometries;
3. controlled sensory/current-context perturbations shift expression without necessarily erasing individual bias;
4. an independent navigation experiment retains individual organization across altered cue structure.

The bounded synthesis would be:

> **Individual movement specialization is not stored as one literal realized trajectory. Personal organization can persist upstream of current behavioral expression, is refined through individual history, and can survive experimentally imposed changes in current sensory or navigational context.**

## What public data still cannot establish

Without a source in which personal history / solution opportunity itself was randomized across biological individuals, do not claim:

- multiple feasible solutions cause individual specialization;
- personal history causally creates the portable policy;
- learning accounts for a quantified fraction of the persistent state;
- biomechanics accounts for the complementary fraction;
- the same laboratory carrier explains the frozen wild JAE vertical-individuality signal;
- fitness advantage of the individual policy.

## Formation-language firewall

Allowed:

> individual history refines / predicts later personal organization.

Not allowed:

> history causes the personal organization.

Allowed after controlled context perturbations:

> personal organization persists despite a manipulated current context.

Not allowed:

> the manipulated context proves where the personal organization is stored neurally or developmentally.

## Decision

The public-data programme remains scientifically worthwhile.

Its realistic endpoint is not full causal origin.

Its strongest contribution is a **causal separation between persistent personal organization and its context-dependent expression**, triangulated across independent public experiments.
