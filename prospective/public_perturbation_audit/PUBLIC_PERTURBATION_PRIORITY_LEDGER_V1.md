# Public perturbation priority ledger v1

## Status

**PUBLIC-DATA-ONLY ROUTE.**

Purpose:
maximize causal information without new data while preventing renewed same-archive fishing.

## Priority 1 — Aharon 2017 path-integration archive

Why first:
- independent *Pipistrellus kuhlii* system;
- source explicitly encodes bat identity;
- repeated trial columns;
- multiple experimental conditions;
- navigation cue/current-context manipulations;
- turning/slowing/speed observables.

Structural requirement:
- >=4 common biological bats across at least two source-defined conditions;
- >=5 structurally valid trials per bat × condition for the chosen endpoint;
- exact identity permutation must be capable of p<=0.05.

Endpoint priority before values:
1. turning-location vector;
2. slowing-location vector;
3. trial mean speed.

Figure/experiment selection:
use the first source experiment, in published/source order, that satisfies the structural support rule for the highest-priority endpoint.

Do not skip an eligible earlier experiment because a later one appears more favorable.

Primary question if support passes:

> does a bat's condition-residual navigation organization remain closer to its own organization than to other bats' organization after a controlled current-context manipulation?

## Priority 2 — Ma et al. 2025 Hipposideros archive

Why second:
- independent species;
- explicit prey-context/noise manipulation;
- high-speed flight recording;
- public code/data package.

Boundary:
- foraging and landing groups are separate four-animal cohorts in the published design;
- no cross-task transfer claim.

Proceed only if trial-level individual structure can be recovered.

Best question:

> within one task, does a current context manipulation shift the population response while leaving a detectable individual component?

With four animals:
- exact identity-label permutation space is 4! = 24;
- minimum one-sided exact p is 1/24 = 0.0417;
- therefore n=4 is technically testable but maximally discrete and fragile.

Any positive result must carry a small-n boundary.

## Priority 3 — additional public manipulation sources

Search only for genuinely new information:
- within-individual reversible biomechanical manipulation;
- task learning/reset with raw movement trajectories;
- condition changes with repeated identified individuals;
- explicit scene geometry.

Do not add:
- uncontrolled repeatability datasets;
- another same-source basis rotation;
- observational species comparisons without a new causal variable.

## Stop rule

If Aharon cannot be structurally recovered and Ma is summary-only or underidentified, the public-data causal programme closes at:

> history-associated refinement + portable individual organization + controlled sensory perturbation retention + external published route/task memory triangulation.

At that point more archive searching is low-value unless a new public manipulation dataset is discovered.
