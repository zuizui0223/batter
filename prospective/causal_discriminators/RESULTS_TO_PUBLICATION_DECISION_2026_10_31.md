## Hard interpretation ceiling

- V_rel and S_rel are **algebraically coupled** smoothed overlap estimators, not two independent experimental confirmations.
- Model-estimated joint/conditional relative-height overlap is **not measured physically shared 3D airspace**; a non-significant S_rel is not equivalence or a proof of no resource competition.
- The approximate +0.98/+1.92/+1.13 m upper compatibility values refer to additional **session-centered deviation dissimilarity**, not absolute inter-bat flight distance.
- Neither learning, morphology, payoff, specific tree/route, fitness, competition release nor a shared lab-to-field causal mediator is proven.
- External supported/unsupported bat samples are neither a random prevalence sample nor a universal trait test.
