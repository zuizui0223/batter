# Empirical–theory correspondence v1

## Purpose

Map the empirical quantities in the bat programme onto the minimal decentralized-airway model without claiming that the model has been empirically identified.

## Model objects

- `theta_i`: persistent individual weights over viable latent movement solutions.
- `g_k`: realized 3-D geometry of latent solution k.
- `f_i = G theta_i`: individual realized 3-D use distribution.
- `gamma`: optional contemporaneous interaction / avoidance parameter.
- temporal memory architecture: short-memory Markov inertia versus persistent accumulated route propensity.

## Correspondence

| empirical pattern | minimal model counterpart | implication |
|---|---|---|
| same-individual history predicts held-out vertical organization | persistent `theta_i` | personal history contains stable information |
| persistence after session centering | route profiles can differ in shape while sharing mean altitude | individuality is not only additive height offset |
| terrain-relative fidelity V_rel > 0 | `g_k` defined relative to local terrain still differ | landscape elevation alone is insufficient |
| 500-m place × kinematic matching retains identity | `theta_i` is not merely coarse place/state allocation | persistent solution survives coarse context matching |
| temporal persistence across multi-day lags | persistent latent propensity / long-memory carrier | immediate previous-night inertia is insufficient as a complete explanation |
| positive terrain-relative S_rel in 0/4 | strongly overlapping columns of G | individual solution weights need not produce exclusive layers |
| synchronous co-use separation supported in 1/4 | gamma approximately unnecessary in most panels; possible context-specific interaction in one | current traffic is not required for the general persistence pattern |
| same-night other individuals usually do not replace self-history | shared nightly forcing does not erase `theta_i` | individual state travels across bouts |

## The critical distinction

### Identity / fidelity

Generated by persistent individual weights:

[
\theta_i \neq \theta_j.
]

### Spatial partitioning

Generated by how far apart the corresponding route geometries are:

[
g_k \text{ versus } g_l.
]

### Active avoidance

Requires an explicit contemporaneous interaction term:

[
\gamma > 0.
]

These are three mathematically separate quantities.

Therefore:

[
\text{individual specialization}
\not\equiv
\text{spatial segregation}
\not\equiv
\text{active avoidance}.
]

## What the empirical data currently constrain

The current data are compatible with a regime of:

- non-zero persistent individual route/state weights;
- substantial overlap among route geometries;
- weak or absent general contemporaneous avoidance;
- a persistence timescale longer than trivial adjacent-bout carryover.

They do not identify whether the persistent weights arise from:
- cumulative spatial learning;
- morphology/performance;
- exact resource/task fidelity;
- fine environmental reaction rules;
- a mixture of these.

## Strong next discriminator

Estimate self-history advantage as a function of lag under a known naivety/reset boundary.

### Short-memory inertia

[
\Delta(L) \propto \lambda^L
]

and approaches zero.

### Persistent personal propensity

[

]

approaches a positive plateau over the relevant ecological timescale.

### Environmental reset / forgetting

Intermediate pattern:
- old-history advantage collapses after reset;
- exploration rises;
- new-history advantage rebuilds.

This lag/reset experiment is the cleanest way to test what maintains the airway after it forms.
