## Purpose

Fix how the four-bat auditory perturbation result will update the broader mechanism programme.

No outcome state may trigger a new feature search, trajectory rescue, subset analysis, or alternative identity statistic.

## If SUPPORTED

Requirements:
- K > 0;
- exact p <= 0.05.

Programme-level addition:

> **Individual-specific vocal control organization can remain identifiable across reversible central auditory perturbation even after the shared treatment × trial-class shift is removed.**

This adds an independent controlled-perturbation example to the existing storage-versus-expression evidence.

Bounded synthesis:

[
\text{persistent personal organization}
\not\equiv
\text{one unchanging expressed behavior}.
]

Together with the *Pipistrellus kuhlii* masker result, the strongest safe statement becomes:

> **Across independent bat systems, more than one kind of sensory perturbation can alter common behavioral expression without necessarily erasing all individual-specific organization.**

Do not claim:
- the same carrier across species;
- inferior colliculus as the storage substrate;
- movement-policy persistence from this acoustic endpoint;
- broad population prevalence from n=4.

## If POSITIVE_BUT_UNRESOLVED

Condition:
- K > 0;
- exact p > 0.05.

Programme role:
**supporting triangulation only.**

Allowed statement:

> The observed same-bat direction is compatible with retained individual organization, but the four-animal exact permutation space cannot separate the true identity mapping from alternatives strongly enough for confirmatory support.

Do not run:
- leave-one-feature confirmatory tests;
- n=3 trajectory identity tests;
- sham-augmented identity permutations;
- one-bat deletion;
- trial-level pseudoreplication.

## If NO_POSITIVE_IDENTITY

Condition:
- K <= 0.

Programme-level boundary:

> **The frozen four-feature vocal organization is not portable across this central auditory perturbation.**

This would imply that persistence is not universal across perturbation strength / behavioral systems.

It does not imply:
- all individual information was erased;
- no other behavioral dimension persists;
- the earlier masker result was false.

The scientific consequence is a stronger context boundary, not a rescue search.
