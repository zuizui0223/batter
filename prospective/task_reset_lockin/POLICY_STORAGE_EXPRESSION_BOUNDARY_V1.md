Across the seven *Rhinolophus nippon* obstacle configurations, the full eight-dimensional movement-policy representation is classified:

- **BROADLY_TRANSFERRED in 6/7 environments**;
- context-dependent in only environment 7.

Environment 7 has only two evaluable bats, so its context-dependent label is also support-limited.

Thus the persistent individual signal is not concentrated in one favorable obstacle arrangement.

---

## 2. Scale-free route geometry is substantially more context dependent

The geometry-only representation removes:
- elapsed time;
- speed scale;
- absolute route size.

Across the same seven target environments:

- **BROADLY_TRANSFERRED in only 3/7**;
- context-dependent in 4/7.

Examples include:
- environment 1: +0.400, 4/4 positive;
- environment 2: +0.290, 4/4 positive;
- environment 4: +0.997, 5/5 positive;

but:
- environment 3: only 2/4 positive;
- environment 5: I_env = -0.266, only 2/3 positive;
- environment 6: 2/3 positive;
- environment 7: 1/2 positive.

Therefore a translation/scale-free route-geometry signature is not the most stable cross-context carrier.

---

## 3. Pulse/sensing policy is intermediate

The pulse-policy representation is:

- **BROADLY_TRANSFERRED in 5/7 environments**;
- context-dependent in 2/7.

This is more portable than geometry-only route structure, but less uniform than the full movement-policy representation.

Combined with the stricter cross-fitted movement/route-conditioned pulse analysis, the pulse result should not be promoted to an independent autonomous personal sensing parameter.

---

## 4. Storage and expression are empirically separable

The representation ranking is:

[
\text{full movement policy: }6/7
>
\text{pulse policy: }5/7
>
\text{scale-free route geometry: }3/7.
]

This pattern is consistent with a hierarchy in which:

1. a portable personal movement-policy bias carries the most context-stable individual information;
2. sensing expression is partly context dependent;
3. realized route geometry is even more strongly shaped by the current obstacle configuration.

The result does not prove a neural hierarchy.

It does show empirically that different observable layers of the same behavior have different cross-context portability.

---

## 5. Relation to the low-dimensional policy result

The strongest low-dimensional personal component is dominated by:
- speed;
- vertical-speed magnitude;

with a secondary maneuvering/route-organization dimension.

Therefore the highly portable full movement signal is consistent with:

[
\text{persistent control bias}
+
\text{context-dependent geometric realization}.
]

This is stronger than saying merely that each bat has a repeatable trajectory.

The exact trajectory is the less portable object.

---

## 6. Relation to long-term recall evidence

External perturbation experiments add temporal interpretation:

- Barchi: scene-specific routes can be stored and retrieved, but reset under mirror geometry;
- Taub & Yovel: a learned clutter sensorimotor strategy can reappear after months without continuous expression.

Together with the target-environment profile, the evidence supports distinguishing:

[
\boxed{
\text{stored control information}
\neq
\text{current route/sensing expression}
}
]

The persistent layer is more portable than its spatial realization.

---

## 7. Claim ceiling

Supported:
- full movement-policy identity transfers broadly across 6/7 tested configurations;
- geometry-only identity is more context dependent;
- sensing identity is intermediate;
- observable layers differ in cross-context portability.

Not established:
- that the full movement policy is literally stored in one neural state;
- that environment 7 suppresses a latent policy rather than being support-limited;
- a causal ordering among motor policy, sensing and geometry;
- universal portability across bat species.

---

## Bottom line

> **The most persistent individual information is carried at the level of movement policy, while route geometry and sensing expression are more contingent on the current task.**

This provides an internal empirical bridge for the storage–expression distinction used in the post-JAE mechanism synthesis.
