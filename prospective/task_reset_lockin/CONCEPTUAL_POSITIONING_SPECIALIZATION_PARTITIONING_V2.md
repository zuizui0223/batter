# Conceptual positioning — policy specialization versus spatial niche v2

## Status

**CURRENT LITERATURE-POSITIONING NOTE — supersedes V1.**

This note positions the post-JAE mechanism programme against individual-specialization, animal-personality, movement-ecology and low-dimensional-behavior literature.

It does not modify JAE v0.4.0.

---

## 1. Claims that should explicitly NOT be sold as novel

### A. Individual specialization can coexist with niche overlap

This is already established conceptually and empirically.

Sargeant (2007) distinguished individual niche width from niche overlap.

Carlson et al. (2021) showed that mobile animals can have temporally consistent individual environmental niches that are strongly overlapping/nested rather than occupying disjoint environmental regions.

Therefore do not headline:

> “individuals can be specialized despite overlap.”

That is too broad and too old.

### B. Behavioral type, plasticity and predictability are separable

Behavioral reaction-norm literature already distinguishes:
- individual mean/intercept;
- individual environmental slope/plasticity;
- residual individual predictability.

Hertel et al. (2020) explicitly adapts this framework to movement data.

Therefore the post-JAE intercept/slope/breadth decomposition is a falsification tool, not a conceptual invention.

### C. Complex behavior can be low dimensional

Computational ethology and motor-control literatures already seek low-dimensional behavioral manifolds.

A 2026 preprint on cross-domain human behavioral telemetry even reports that two latent dimensions can capture much of stable individual strategic variation across contexts.

Therefore do not claim:

> “we discovered that individuality is two-dimensional.”

The biologically important result is what the compressed individual information does — and does not — map onto.

### D. Personality can predict spatial niche differentiation

Schirmer et al. (2019, 2020) and related work directly link stable behavioral differences to:
- space use;
- habitat use;
- reduced overlap;
- competition structure;
- individual spatial niche specialization.

Thus a general behavior -> space linkage is well precedented.

---

## 2. The empirical contrast that remains unusual

The current programme directly measures three different layers in the same biological framework:

[
\boxed{
\text{persistent movement-policy individuality}
\neq
\text{realized spatial segregation}
\neq
\text{active co-presence avoidance}
}
]

This is not a semantic distinction.

Each layer has an independent estimator and null.

### Policy individuality

Does biological identity predict held-out movement-control behavior?

Evidence:
- cross-configuration movement-policy transfer;
- wild bivariate H/V carrier;
- peer-day residual identity;
- temporal self-history.

### Spatial segregation

Are individual realized spatial distributions separated?

Evidence:
- terrain-relative 3-D geometry;
- added segregation (S).

### Active co-presence avoidance

Does simultaneous local presence cause extra separation?

Evidence:
- fixed encounter sets;
- terrain-relative synchronous vertical separation;
- phase-shift null.

This architecture permits the ecological mapping itself to be tested rather than assumed.

---

## 3. The strongest same-system falsification

Wild *Phyllostomus hastatus* provides the cleanest bridge.

Persistent H/V policy individuality is supported:

### 2022
- K = +0.65314;
- 32/34 positive;
- p = 0.0001.

### 2023
- K = +0.27052;
- 9/11 positive;
- p ≈ 0.033.

Yet dyadic policy distance does not predict synchronous vertical separation:

### 2022
- Spearman rho = +0.188;
- p = 0.2743.

### 2023
- rho = -0.190;
- p = 0.6887.

Therefore the mapping:

[
D_{policy,ij}
\rightarrow
S_{space,ij}
]

is not supported in either year.

This is stronger than simply observing high overlap.

It asks whether **more behaviorally differentiated dyads are more spatially partitioned**, and the answer is no under the tested vertical co-use endpoint.

---

## 4. The result reverses a common spatial-niche narrative

A substantial individual-niche literature motivates consistent behavioral differences as mechanisms that:
- reduce similarity among competitors;
- reduce overlap;
- sort behavioral types into space;
- facilitate spatial niche differentiation.

That mechanism is valid in many systems.

The bat programme identifies a different regime:

[
\boxed{
\text{persistent behavioral differentiation}
+
\text{shared physical space}
}
]

The individual-specific object can persist in **how a movement problem is solved** without being represented monotonically in **where the individual is relative to conspecifics**.

This is the key ecological reversal.

---

## 5. Why nested/overlapping individualized niches are not the same result

Carlson et al. show that individual environmental niches can be nested and overlapping.

The present result differs because:

### A. The individualized object is upstream of realized niche occupancy

The policy carrier is measured in movement-control/history space.

It can transfer across obstacle configurations and is not identical to the realized vertical distribution.

Field policy similarity does not predict full centered vertical-distribution shape:
- 2022 p = 0.4548;
- 2023 p = 0.5627.

### B. The interaction layer is tested directly

The programme does not infer lack of partitioning from overlap alone.

It tests whether:
- synchronous local co-use creates extra vertical separation;
- persistent policy distance predicts that separation.

Thus the hypothesized maintenance-by-separation mechanism is directly falsified under the measured geometry.

---

## 6. A second novel distinction: storage versus expression

The latest mechanism synthesis adds:

[
\boxed{
\text{storage of individual control information}
\neq
\text{continuous behavioral expression}
}
]

Internal target-environment profiles show:
- full movement-policy identity broadly transfers in 6/7 obstacle configurations;
- pulse-policy identity broadly transfers in 5/7;
- scale-free geometry identity broadly transfers in only 3/7.

Thus the most persistent individual information sits at a more abstract movement-policy level than the exact route geometry.

External perturbation evidence adds temporal structure:
- learned scene routes can be retrieved after a delay and replaced after geometric reset;
- a learned clutter sensorimotor strategy can reappear after months without continuous use.

The claim is not that latent memory is new.

The ecological synthesis is that a specialized state can be **stored in the animal and conditionally expressed**, so continuous ecological partitioning is not required to maintain it.

## 7. What the second paper should NOT be

Do not frame it primarily as:

- an estimator paper;
- a PCA/dimensionality paper;
- an animal-personality paper;
- a generic reaction-norm paper;
- “bats have unique flight styles”;
- “individual niches overlap.”

All of those invite obvious-prior-art objections.

---

## 8. Strong second-paper question

> **Where is individual specialization maintained when it is not maintained in exclusive space?**

Current answer:

> **In persistent control information carried by the individual: a portable movement-policy bias expressed through context-dependent and learned solutions.**

The empirical novelty is the direct decoupling:

[
\text{policy specialization}
\not\Rightarrow
\text{spatial partitioning}.
]

The mechanistic novelty is the hierarchy:

[
\text{portable policy}
\rightarrow
\text{task/scene-specific expression}
\rightarrow
\text{realized movement},
]

with the downstream spatial phenotype more context dependent than the upstream individual signal.

---

## 9. Strongest publication-level claim

A defensible high-level statement is:

> **Persistent individual movement specialization can reside upstream of realized niche partitioning: animals can retain predictive differences in movement policy even when those differences do not map onto greater pairwise separation in shared physical space.**

A more mechanistic version:

> **Individual specialization can be stored in behavioral policy and conditionally expressed through the environment, rather than requiring continuous partition of physical space.**

These claims are narrower than “specialization without overlap” and more specific than “animal personality.”

## Bottom line

The novelty does not lie in overlap, personality, reaction norms or dimensionality separately.

It lies in **measuring persistent individuality upstream of space and then showing that the individuality-to-partition mapping itself fails**.
