# External bat source screen: personal 3-D flight reaction norm versus noise (2026-10-08)

## Purpose and provenance

**Source-metadata/literature screening only. No external numeric animal outcomes were downloaded, opened, reanalysed, or tested in this work.** Do not use a paper's reported significance as a new `batter` finding. This search implements the requirements of `LOSS_SIGN_IDENTIFIABILITY_AND_EXTERNAL_FALSIFICATION_V1.md`: stable bat identity, crossed environments, independent repeated days, 3-D behavioral outcome, and ideally independent foraging/success data or intervention.

This is a **narrow eligibility screen**, not an authorization to reopen an already published finding or amend the JAE or Behavioral Ecology paper.

## Scientific decision

1. **No newly located source is presently confirmed to satisfy the full 3-D bat × repeated controlled context × independent night × functional outcome gate.** Do not turn a rich paper or 3-D trajectory dataset into a fictitious longitudinal *individual* sample.
2. Accomando 2018 is the **closest protocol** to the required causal reaction-norm discrimination, but raw repeated bat×day acoustic/3-D measurements have not been located, the sample is four bats, and the broad fixed-versus-variable acoustic effect is already published.
3. The field GPS archives have attractive genuine longitudinal structure, but they answer a different level of ecological organization (site/colony/2-D commuting, repeated resource exploitation) until original measurement headers show otherwise.
4. **Do not extract numerical phenotype values or switch endpoints** on any of these merely to report a success. A future source may be reopened only after verifying public per-animal identifiers, independent repeats, exact environmental states, measured outcome columns and distinct novelty from the original paper. Record the design before opening numerical outcome values.
5. Same-archive `Rhinolophus` endpoint expansion remains closed. The completed configuration-blind scalar audit and exact B/C loss identity are the current limits.

## Publication boundary

**Already known, not new:** expectation affects bat pulse trains (Accomando 2018); physical echo geometry affects flight (Warnecke 2016); bat-specific foraging histories and long-lasting routes exist (Shohami 2020; Calderón-Capote 2024). A new claim would need the *independent test of repeatable individual environmental response beyond heteroscedastic noise*, preferably linked to actual task performance, not merely rediscover the above.
