## Question

When each obstacle configuration is treated as the unseen target, how broadly does previously observed individual identity transfer into that environment?

Three frozen representations were compared:

1. full movement policy;
2. scale-free route geometry;
3. pulse policy.

An environment is descriptively called broadly transferred when:
- mean target identity advantage is positive; and
- >=70% of evaluable bats are positive.
