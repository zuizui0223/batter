# Direct solution-abundance experiment v1

## Status

**PROSPECTIVE DECISIVE EXPERIMENT DESIGN.**

Purpose:

> directly test whether the number of functionally adequate movement solutions causally changes the formation and expression of individual movement specialization.

This design is motivated by:
- ontogenetic history-specific refinement;
- portable adult movement policy;
- controlled perturbation retention;
- scale-free geometry identity;
- the functional-abundance symmetry-breaking model.

The present public Teshima archive cannot measure environmental solution abundance independently of realized trajectories because obstacle coordinates/layouts are not publicly recoverable.

A direct experiment is therefore the clean route.

---

# 1. Core causal manipulation

Hold constant:
- start location;
- goal location;
- reward;
- total flight distance;
- minimum obstacle clearance;
- nominal corridor width;
- illumination/acoustic recording conditions.

Manipulate only the number of geometrically distinct but approximately equal-cost routes from start to goal.

## K=1 — unique-solution arena

One viable corridor.

All alternatives are blocked.

## K=2 — two-solution arena

Two mirrored, equal-length/equal-clearance corridors.

## K=4 — four-solution arena

Four approximately symmetric equal-cost corridors.

The exact geometry is fixed before animal testing.

K is therefore a design variable, not estimated from bat trajectories.

---

# 2. Critical design principle

The environments must differ in **solution multiplicity**, not generic difficulty.

Before animal exposure, quantify mechanically:

- shortest-path length of every route;
- minimum clearance;
- cumulative turning requirement;
- vertical displacement;
- corridor volume.

Accept an arena only if candidate solutions are matched inside frozen engineering tolerances.

If one route is objectively easier, the experiment no longer cleanly manipulates abundance.

---

# 3. Remove competition completely

Test one bat at a time.

No conspecific is present in the flight arena.

Thus any persistent between-individual route differentiation that forms under K>1 cannot be caused by:
- current competitor avoidance;
- territorial exclusion;
- social lane allocation.

This is the sharpest possible test of the central ecological alternative.

---

# 4. Replicated mirrored arenas

A simple left/right choice is vulnerable to pre-existing lateralization.

Therefore each K level should have multiple geometric replicates.

For every physical layout:
- include a mirrored version;
- rotate or relabel route identities where feasible;
- keep source and target positions centered.

A persistent "always turn left" bias must not masquerade as history-selected route specialization.

Route identity should be coded in obstacle-relative coordinates.

---

# 5. Experimental phases

## Phase A — naive formation

Expose each individual to a novel K-high arena.

Recommended:
- K=4;
- repeated flights over multiple sessions.

Measure:
- early exploration;
- route-choice entropy;
- self-history predictability;
- emergence of stable policy/route preference.

## Phase B — abundance narrowing

Without changing goal/reward, block alternatives so that:

[
K=4\rightarrow K=1
]

or

[
K=4\rightarrow K=2.
]

Question:
does expressed between-individual individuality compress when the feasible solution set narrows?

## Phase C — reopen

Restore the original K=4 solution set.

Question:
does each individual rapidly recover its pre-narrowing solution/policy?

Immediate recovery is evidence for latent storage/hysteresis.

Slow reformation suggests that narrowing overwrote the personal state.

## Phase D — independent novel K-high arena

Introduce a new physical geometry with the same K.

Question:
does the individual's higher-level policy transfer even though literal corridor identities are new?

This separates:
- route memory;
- portable movement policy.

---

# 6. Counterbalancing

Formation itself is path dependent, so ordinary fully randomized trial order is inappropriate.

Use independent matched arena families.

Example:

Group 1:
- Arena family A: K4 formation -> K1 -> K4 reopen;
- Arena family B: novel K2;
- Arena family C: novel K4.

Group 2:
- mirrored arena-family assignment.

The critical K manipulation is replicated across arena families rather than inferred from one sequence.

---

# 7. Primary P1 — abundance causes individualization

Define a predeclared policy representation independent of route label.

Use:
- FlightIntensity;
- scale-free route geometry;
- or a fixed transparent vector defined before this experiment.

For every arena and training stage compute between-individual policy divergence.

Primary causal contrast:

[
D_{\mathrm{individual}}(K=4)
-
D_{\mathrm{individual}}(K=1).
]

The functional-abundance model predicts:

[
>0
]

after sufficient repeated experience.

The biological unit is the individual.

---

# 8. Primary P2 — history selects among equivalent solutions

Within K=4:

use early strictly prior history to predict late route/policy use.

Compare:
- true personal history;
- identity-permuted histories.

Prediction:

[
K_{\mathrm{self-history}}>0.
]

More strongly:

early stochastic differences should predict later personal solution occupancy after accounting for any stable left/right or vertical bias measured in independent control arenas.

---

# 9. Primary P3 — narrowing compresses expression

Before and during K narrowing:

estimate between-individual policy/route dispersion.

Prediction:

[
D_{K4}
>
D_{K1}.
]

But stored identity need not disappear.

If a policy feature is still measurable under K1, same-individual prediction can remain positive even while route-choice diversity collapses.

This directly separates:

[
\text{storage}
]

from:

[
\text{expression}.
]

---

# 10. Primary P4 — reopen hysteresis

After restoring K=4:

compare the first post-reopen flights to:
- each individual's own pre-narrowing history;
- other individuals' pre-narrowing histories;
- de novo K4 formation trajectories.

Prediction under retained latent policy:

> own pre-narrowing history predicts immediate post-reopen behavior better than other histories.

This is the cleanest experimental test of latent personal-state storage.

---

# 11. P5 — portable policy versus literal route

Use a novel K=4 geometry.

The model predicts:

- literal route identity should not transfer perfectly;
- higher-level movement-policy identity should transfer.

This reproduces the current Teshima distinction prospectively under a designed abundance manipulation.

---

# 12. Measurement hierarchy

Record:

### 3-D trajectory
- position;
- speed;
- vertical speed;
- turning;
- path efficiency;
- scale-free geometry.

### Echolocation / sensing
- pulse timing;
- repetition;
- beam/head orientation if possible.

### Performance
- body mass;
- forearm length;
- wing area / wing loading;
- optional flight-muscle/performance proxies.

### Trial state
- session;
- trial;
- arena;
- solution availability;
- reward timing.

All variables and exclusion thresholds should be frozen before outcome opening.

---

# 13. Individual policy representation

Do not fit an identity-maximizing latent axis from the same experiment.

Preferred primary representation:

1. fixed transparent coordinates from the current programme;
2. scale-free geometry vector;
3. route-choice simplex over experimenter-defined feasible solutions.

A learned representation may be secondary only with strict cross-validation.

---

# 14. Direct theoretical prediction

The v2 abundance model predicts:

[
\Delta_K
=
\frac{K-1}{K(A+1)}.
]

Thus:

[
\Delta_1=0,
]

and:

[
\Delta_2<\Delta_4.
]

Do not attempt to estimate A unless the model is predeclared as a generative fit.

The primary experimental test is ordinal:

[
K1<K2<K4
]

for history-generated individual differentiation.

---

# 15. Strong falsifier

The hypothesis is weakened if:

- K=1 produces as much history-dependent individuality as K=4;
- K4 individuals fail to develop repeatable solution preferences;
- narrowing K does not compress route/policy dispersion;
- reopening K fails to retrieve previous personal organization;
- all apparent individuality is explained by stable morphology measured before learning.

---

# 16. Biomechanics extension

A second factorial manipulation can separate feasible-set structure from physical constraint.

Cross:

[
K
\times
\text{reversible load}.
]

For the same individuals:
- no-load;
- temporary standardized load;
- load removal.

Predictions:

### biomechanics-dominated coordinate
load shifts the personal policy systematically and recovery is immediate after unloading.

### history-dominated coordinate
personal route preference/history survives load manipulation and may show hysteresis.

### mixed architecture
a reversible load component sits on top of a retained history-dependent personal component.

This extension should follow, not precede, the clean K manipulation.

---

# 17. Recommended biological replication

The current programme repeatedly shows that n=4–7 gives fragile exact-permutation resolution.

A decisive new experiment should aim for substantially more biological individuals.

Recommended design target:
- at least **12–16 individuals** with complete repeated-measures support;
- multiple flights per phase;
- equal weighting at the individual level.

The final sample-size target should be set using simulation from a frozen generative model before data collection, not from post-hoc power on the current studies.

---

# 18. Why this experiment matters ecologically

The decisive comparison is not:

> do bats differ?

It is:

> **does giving animals more equally viable ways to solve the same ecological problem cause stronger history-dependent individual specialization?**

If yes, it establishes a route to individual ecological specialization that does not require resource depletion or competitor-driven niche partition.

The causal chain becomes:

[
\boxed{
\text{ecological opportunity in solution space}
\rightarrow
\text{history-dependent symmetry breaking}
\rightarrow
\text{persistent personal policy}
}
]

rather than:

[
\text{competition}
\rightarrow
\text{exclusive niche}.
]

---

# Bottom line

A direct K manipulation is now the highest-value experiment.

The present archive already supports:
- personal refinement;
- portable policy;
- geometry-level individuality;
- perturbation-resistant identity;
- optional spatial partition.

What remains unmeasured is the proposed cause:

> **the number of functionally adequate solutions available to the animal.**

This experiment measures that cause directly.
