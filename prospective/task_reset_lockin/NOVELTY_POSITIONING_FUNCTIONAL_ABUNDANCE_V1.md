# Novelty positioning — functional abundance to ecological individualization v1

## Status

**LITERATURE-POSITIONING NOTE FOR THE POST-JAE MECHANISM PAPER.**

This note does not claim absolute conceptual priority.
It identifies which parts of the current synthesis are established in adjacent fields and where the batter programme may contribute a new empirical bridge.

---

# 1. What is already established in ecology

## Individual specialization

The individual-specialization literature already establishes that:
- conspecific individuals can use different subsets of population resources/habitats;
- competition and ecological opportunity can alter among-individual niche variation;
- physiology, morphology, behavior and learning can contribute to individual differences.

Therefore do not claim:
> individuals can differ consistently.

## Individualized niches need not be disjoint

Recent individualized-niche frameworks explicitly allow:
- nested niches;
- overlapping niches;
- niche construction;
- niche choice;
- niche conformance;
- developmental and environmental shaping.

Therefore do not claim:
> individual specialization is possible even when niches overlap.

That conceptual point is older than the present bat programme.

## Development of individual differences

Animal-personality and eco-devo literature already recognizes:
- sensitive developmental periods;
- social experience;
- state-behavior feedbacks;
- plasticity in individual phenotype formation.

Therefore do not claim:
> individual behavioral differences can develop through experience.

---

# 2. What is already established in motor control

Motor-control theory already recognizes:
- motor abundance / redundancy;
- neurobiological degeneracy;
- multiple distinct coordinative solutions achieving the same function;
- individual differences in exploration of redundant task spaces;
- modulation of solution repertoires by environmental/task constraints.

Therefore do not claim:
> organisms can solve the same motor task in multiple ways.

---

# 3. Bat flight already provides a biomechanical example

Experimental mass loading in *Cynopterus brachyotis* shows that individuals can respond to the same added-load challenge using different combinations of wing-motion and wing-shape variables.

This supports:
- functional redundancy/abundance in a bat flight system;
- multiple biomechanically adequate compensatory strategies.

It does not show the ecological persistence of those strategies outside the experiment.

The current bat programme links four levels that are usually studied separately:

The empirical chain is:

### Formation
Recent personal history becomes substantially more identity-informative than the earliest matched personal history during juvenile movement development.

### Maintenance
Adult personal movement coordinates predict behavior in unseen movement tasks.

### Expression
Controlled sensory perturbation moves behavior while retaining individual bias; expression strength can be context dependent.

### Spatial consequence
Persistent policy differentiation does not require or monotonically map onto pairwise 3-D spatial separation.

The proposed novelty is therefore not a new index.

It is an empirical connection between:
- internal control-solution selection;
- developmental history;
- persistent movement individuality;
- individual niche/spatial overlap.

---

# 5. Distinction from individualized-niche frameworks

Current individualized-niche frameworks focus primarily on:
- phenotype-environment covariance;
- niche choice/construction/conformance;
- developmental plasticity;
- ecological interactions;
- evolutionary consequences.

They do not generally specify the internal motor-control architecture that makes several behavioral solutions available for the same ecological task.

The batter programme asks a lower-level mechanism question:

> **When several movement solutions are functionally available, can personal history stabilize a distinctive solution that later behaves as a portable ecological individual trait?**

That is the proposed cross-field contribution.

---

# 6. Distinction from personality

A personality framework typically starts from repeatable individual phenotype.

The current programme separately observes:
- formation/refinement;
- persistence;
- expression gain;
- spatial consequence.

Thus it can distinguish:

[
	ext{stable personal information}
]

from:

[
	ext{how strongly that information is expressed now}.
]

The result is more mechanistic than assigning a broad personality label.

---

# 7. Distinction from motor-abundance studies

Motor-abundance studies typically ask:
- how many solutions can achieve a task;
- how variability stabilizes performance;
- how constraints reorganize coordination.

The current programme asks an ecological downstream question:

> **Can history-dependent selection among adequate movement solutions become persistent individual ecological specialization without competitor-driven niche partition?**

The spatial-overlap and wild-policy results make this an ecology question rather than a laboratory motor-control problem alone.

---

# 8. Strongest defensible novelty claim

Avoid:

> We discover motor degeneracy.

Avoid:

> We show for the first time that individual niches overlap.

Avoid:

> We show that learning creates personality.

Prefer:

> **We show that persistent movement specialization can be decomposed into its formation, maintenance, expression and spatial consequence, and the resulting evidence is consistent with history-dependent selection among multiple feasible movement solutions as an internal route to ecological individuality.**

A shorter conceptual version:

> **Individual specialization can arise from solution selection inside the animal rather than partition imposed outside the animal.**

This is a hypothesis-driven synthesis whose direct abundance-manipulation experiment remains prospective.

---

# 9. Empirical claim versus mechanism hypothesis

## Directly supported

- individual history becomes more informative during juvenile development;
- adult policy coordinate transfers across tasks;
- controlled sensory perturbation retains personal bias;
- expression can be context sensitive;
- policy difference does not require stronger spatial separation.

## Externally supported premise

- bat flight mechanics admits multiple load-compensation strategies;
- motor systems generally exhibit abundance/degeneracy.

## Still a hypothesis

- personal history specifically selects one solution from a measured biomechanical solution manifold;
- abundance itself causally increases ecological individual specialization.

Do not collapse the premise and the hypothesis.

---

# 10. Decisive future experiment

Manipulate solution abundance directly.

For the same identified individuals:

1. baseline task with multiple feasible movement solutions;
2. experimentally narrow the feasible solution set while holding task goal constant;
3. reopen the solution set;
4. repeat before and after learning;
5. independently manipulate biomechanics.

Measure:
- personal policy dispersion;
- identity retention;
- exploration-to-consolidation dynamics;
- recovery/hysteresis;
- spatial overlap.

Prediction under the new mechanism:

> narrowing the feasible solution repertoire should alter the expression and formation of individuality, while reopening it should reveal whether personal history restores the prior solution or explores a new one.

This is the direct test connecting motor abundance to ecological specialization.
