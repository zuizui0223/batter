# One-question mechanism paper story v1

## Working question

> **If individual specialization is not stored as exclusive physical space, what persists with the individual?**

This is the single biological question.

Do not lead with:
- a new metric;
- policy-space terminology;
- motor abundance;
- Pólya urns;
- movement dimensionality.

Those are explanatory tools.

The biological problem is maintenance of individual specialization when realized niches overlap.

---

# Working answer

> **Persistent individual movement information can reside in a portable personal behavioral bias whose detailed spatial expression is reconstructed by the current environment.**

The evidence further suggests that:
- personal history helps refine that bias during development;
- the bias survives controlled environmental perturbation;
- its detailed geometric expression is task dependent;
- its existence does not require persistent pairwise spatial partitioning.

---

# Core hypothesis

Write the persistent empirical state as:

\[
\theta_i.
\]

Realized movement in environment e is:

\[
z_{i,e}
=
\mu_e
+
G_e\theta_i
+
\epsilon_{i,e}.
\]

The hypothesis has three separable claims:

1. **formation**
   - \(\theta_i\) is refined by individual history;

2. **maintenance**
   - \(\theta_i\) remains predictive across changed tasks/conditions;

3. **expression**
   - \(G_e\) changes with environment, so detailed trajectories/niches need not remain geometrically identical.

Spatial partition is then only a downstream possibility.

---

# Prediction 1 — individuality should be constructed through personal history

If adult individuality is a fully fixed trait from the beginning of independent movement, earliest personal movement should remain a strong predictor of later movement.

If personal experience substantially refines individuality, matched recent personal history should become more informative than the earliest personal history.

## Evidence

Juvenile fruit bats, late valid-day targets 11–20:

Earliest two valid days:
- E = **+15.19**
- p = **0.1655**
- 8/14 positive
- unsupported.

Recent two days minus earliest two:
- Q = **+144.08**
- p = **0.0001**
- 10/14 positive
- supported.

Interpretation:

> **later individual organization is not simply a frozen copy of the earliest observable independent movement; personal history substantially refines it.**

Do not claim smooth monotonic formation:
the original monotonic primary failed.

---

# Prediction 2 — the individualized state should transfer across new tasks

If individuality is only one memorized route, changing obstacle geometry should destroy individual prediction.

If a higher-level personal state exists, information estimated in other configurations should predict the individual's relative state in a held-out configuration.

## Evidence

Adult *Rhinolophus nippon*:

Transparent policy:
- FlightIntensity;
- ManeuveringExtent.

Held-out no-refit 2-D prediction:
- R² = **0.4666**
- p = **0.0002**.

Held-out pair displacement geometry:
- R² = **0.5797**
- p = **0.0001**
- 97.1% positive vector alignment.

Interpretation:

> **individuality transfers at a level above one literal route.**

This is the central maintenance result.

---

# Prediction 3 — environmental perturbation should change behavior without necessarily erasing individual information

If the observed behavior itself is the stored individual trait, a strong perturbation should destroy identity.

If identity is stored more abstractly, the common operating state can change while relative individual information remains.

## Evidence

Controlled *Pipistrellus kuhlii* sensory perturbation:

Baseline -> masker:
- K = **+4.941°**
- 5/6 positive
- exact p = **0.0403**.

Foam no-masker -> foam + masker:
- K = **+6.779°**
- 5/5 positive
- exact p = **0.025**
- no-refit R² = **0.641**.

Interpretation:

> **environmentally induced behavioral change and persistent individual information coexist.**

Post-primary attenuation shows expression strength can vary by context.

---

# Prediction 4 — detailed movement geometry should be less portable than the personal state itself

If one invariant geometric style is the personal state, the same detailed geometry representation should transfer across tasks/systems.

If environment maps a portable state into task-specific movement, detailed geometry should depend on the environment.

## Evidence within Rhino

Scale-free geometry itself carries identity:
- K = **+0.38857**
- p = **0.0153**
- 5/5 positive.

Within each environment:
- removing I alone leaves geometry identity;
- removing I+M eliminates it.

But a training-estimated I/M -> geometry map applied to an unseen environment leaves residual identity:

- residual K = **+0.20160**
- 5/5 positive
- p = **0.0156**.

Therefore the mapping is environment dependent:

\[
g_{i,e}
=
G_e\theta_i+\epsilon.
\]

## External boundary

Fixed Rhino geometry in *Carollia perspicillata*:
- K = **-0.0350**
- p = **0.2144**
- unsupported.

The coarser fixed policy representation transfers better.

Interpretation:

> **the personal state is more portable than its detailed coordinative geometry.**

This is the key expression result.

---

# Prediction 5 — persistent personal policy should not require physical niche partition

If specialization is maintained by exclusive spatial niches, more different personal policies should correspond to greater spatial separation.

If policy exists upstream of spatial realization, policy difference can persist without that relationship.

## Evidence

Wild *Phyllostomus hastatus*:

2022:
- rho(policy distance, synchronous vertical separation) = **+0.188**
- p = **0.274**.

2023:
- rho = **-0.190**
- p = **0.689**.

Thus:

\[
\boxed{
D_{policy}
\not\Rightarrow
D_{space}
}
\]

Interpretation:

> **persistent personal information is not equivalent to exclusive ownership of physical space.**

This closes the ecological loop.

# The one narrative

The result sequence is:

\[
\boxed{
\text{personal history refines individuality}
}
\]

then:

\[
\boxed{
\text{individuality becomes portable across tasks}
}
\]

then:

\[
\boxed{
\text{environment changes how individuality is expressed}
}
\]

then:

\[
\boxed{
\text{spatial partition is optional}
}
\]

In prose:

> **Individual movement specialization is not simply a fixed niche occupied by an animal. Personal movement history helps construct an individualized behavioral state; that state can remain predictive when the task changes; the environment remaps it into different detailed trajectories; and those persistent differences need not separate individuals into exclusive spatial niches.**

---

# What makes this more than "personality"

A personality paper says:
> individuals differ consistently.

This paper separates:
- how the difference forms;
- what component remains portable;
- how environmental perturbation changes expression;
- whether the resulting individuality becomes spatial niche separation.

Therefore the object is not merely repeatability.

It is the **location of persistent predictive information in the behavioral hierarchy**.

---

# What makes this more than "individual niche overlap"

Individualized-niche theory already allows overlapping niches.

The new empirical point is:

> **the persistent individual information can remain predictive upstream of the realized spatial niche, and the environment-specific mapping to that niche can change.**

This is a mechanistic explanation for overlap rather than a restatement that overlap exists.

---

# Role of motor abundance

Motor abundance is not the headline.

It is a candidate formation mechanism:

\[
\text{multiple feasible solutions}
\times
\text{history}
\rightarrow
\theta_i.
\]

Current evidence:
- compatible;
- mathematically sufficient;
- not directly tested because environmental solution abundance is unavailable.

Therefore keep motor abundance in:
- Discussion;
- conceptual model;
- future experiment.

Do not make it the empirical central claim.

---

# Role of payoff

Payoff is also downstream theory, not the central result.

A persistent personal state can be:
- beneficial;
- neutral;
- mismatched.

The current batter data do not directly estimate fitness.

Use payoff only to explain why persistence can remain flexible and why context-gated expression matters.
