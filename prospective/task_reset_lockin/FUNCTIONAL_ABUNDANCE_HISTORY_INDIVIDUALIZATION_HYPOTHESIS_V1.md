# Functional abundance × history-dependent individualization hypothesis v1

## Status

**MECHANISTIC SYNTHESIS / PROSPECTIVE HYPOTHESIS.**

This note separates:
- established results from the batter programme;
- established external motor-control/flight-mechanics literature;
- the new mechanistic hypothesis that links them.

It does not modify JAE v0.4.0.

---

# 1. The unresolved question

The current programme shows that:

1. personal movement organization is substantially refined through individual history during ontogeny;
2. a low-dimensional adult personal policy coordinate predicts unseen movement tasks;
3. that personal bias survives controlled sensory perturbation;
4. its expression strength can vary with context;
5. persistent policy difference does not require persistent physical-space partition.

The remaining question is:

> **Why does personal history have multiple viable movement solutions from which to construct a persistent individual bias?**

---

# 2. External mechanistic premise: biological movement systems are abundant / degenerate

Motor-control theory has long recognized that many different combinations of lower-level control variables can achieve the same task-level outcome.

This has been described as:
- motor abundance;
- functional redundancy;
- neurobiological degeneracy.

The important concept is not random behavioral noise.

It is:

> **multiple structurally different control solutions can be functionally adequate for the same task.**

Environmental constraints can change which solutions remain available or useful.

This idea is established and is not claimed as new here.

---

# 3. Bat-specific external evidence for multiple biomechanical solutions

Experimental loading of *Cynopterus brachyotis* provides a bat-specific example.

Under the same approximately 20% added body-mass challenge:

- individuals changed different combinations of wingbeat and wing-shape variables;
- some relied more strongly on wing-motion changes;
- another relied more strongly on wing configuration, camber and wing area;
- the source authors explicitly interpreted the flight system as permitting multiple functionally equivalent kinematic strategies.

Therefore a physical challenge does not necessarily determine one unique kinematic response.

Biomechanics constrains the feasible set.

It does not always uniquely specify the chosen solution.

---

# 4. Internal formation result: personal history changes which solution predicts the individual

The first-flight programme provides the missing developmental piece.

For late juvenile movement:
- the first two valid personal-history days are not a robust long-term predictor;
- the immediately preceding two personal-history days are dramatically more informative;
- recent-over-early gain remains far above a whole-history identity permutation that preserves general ontogenetic spatial expansion.

Thus the personal solution is not simply read out unchanged from the earliest independent movement state.

It is substantially updated/refined through individual history.

---

# 5. Internal maintenance result: the refined solution becomes portable

In adult *Rhinolophus nippon*:

- the personal movement coordinate transfers across obstacle configurations;
- a coordinate estimated from other configurations predicts a held-out configuration;
- even the relative displacement vectors among individuals transfer.

Therefore history-dependent individualization need not remain a purely local route habit.

A higher-level personal control bias can become portable across tasks.

---

# 6. Internal expression result: environment gates but need not erase the bias

In controlled *Pipistrellus kuhlii* sensory perturbation:

- baseline individual movement bias predicts relative state under masker conditions;
- foam-target no-masker bias strongly predicts foam+masker state;
- under one stronger masking context, identity expression is attenuated post-primary.

Thus the environment can change:
- the common operating point;
- how strongly the personal bias is expressed;

without necessarily erasing the personal bias.

---

# 7. Proposed mechanism

The strongest new hypothesis is:

[
\boxed{
\text{functional abundance}
+
\text{personal history}
\rightarrow
\text{history-selected personal policy}
}
]

followed by:

[
\boxed{
\text{personal policy}
\times
\text{current constraints}
\rightarrow
\text{realized movement}
}
]

In words:

> **The locomotor/sensorimotor system admits multiple adequate ways to solve recurrent movement problems. Individual experience progressively selects, refines or stabilizes a subset of those solutions. The resulting higher-level bias can remain portable across tasks, while current environmental constraints determine how strongly and in what spatial form it is expressed.**

This provides a mechanism for individual specialization that does not require competitor-driven spatial exclusion.

---

# 8. A minimal dynamic representation

Let:

[
\mathcal S(E,M)
]

be the set of feasible movement solutions under:
- environment/task E;
- morphology/biomechanics M.

An individual's current personal policy is:

[
\\theta_i(t).
]

Personal history updates that policy:

[
\\theta_i(t+1)
=
U(
\\theta_i(t),
x_i(t),
r_i(t),
E_t
),
]

where U is an unspecified history-dependent update process and r may represent experienced outcome/reward/information.

Realized movement is:

[
x_i(t)
=
G(
E_t,
M_i,
\\theta_i(t),
m_{i,E}
)
+
epsilon.
]

The present evidence does not identify U.

The point of the formulation is to separate:

- feasible solution repertoire;
- history-dependent personal selection/refinement;
- portable bias;
- current task-specific realization.

---

# 9. Why this is not merely "personality"

A fixed-personality model starts with:

[
\\theta_i = constant.
]

The ontogenetic result shows that later personal movement organization is much better predicted by recent than earliest personal history.

Therefore the adult-like coordinate need not be fully specified at the first observed independent movement.

The better model is:

[
\\theta_i(t)
=
\\theta_i^{intrinsic}
+
h_i(t),
]

where:
- intrinsic biomechanics/development influence the feasible solution set;
- personal history changes the realized policy within that set.

---

# 10. Why this is not merely motor degeneracy

Motor abundance/degeneracy literature asks how multiple control solutions can achieve the same function.

The present ecological programme adds a different question:

> **Can selection among those solutions become a persistent source of individual ecological specialization?**

The distinctive empirical chain is:

1. personal history becomes increasingly identity-informative during development;
2. adult personal policy becomes portable across movement tasks;
3. the same bias survives controlled environmental perturbation;
4. different personal policies need not produce physical niche partitioning.

Thus the proposed contribution lies at the bridge between:
- motor abundance;
- ontogenetic learning/history;
- individual specialization;
- spatial niche theory.

---

# 11. Testable predictions

## P1 — history-dependent consolidation

Early behavior should be less predictive of late personal state than appropriately matched recent personal history.

Current first-flight diagnostic:
**supported for recent-over-early identity-specific gain.**

## P2 — portable higher-level bias

After task geometry changes, literal route should change more than the personal policy coordinate.

Current obstacle-flight programme:
**supported.**

## P3 — constraint-dependent expression

A manipulation that narrows or rotates the set of feasible movement solutions may:
- compress between-individual expressed variation;
- change the mapping from latent policy to realized trajectory;
- while preserving partial identity.

Current sensory perturbation:
**compatible; post-primary context attenuation observed.**

Do not treat generic task difficulty as the independent variable.
The relevant property is the structure of feasible solutions.

## P4 — reversible biomechanical perturbation

Changing biomechanics within the same individual should shift the expressed policy.

After load removal:

### intrinsic-constraint dominated
the original coordinate should return rapidly.

### learned-policy dominated
substantial history-dependent hysteresis may remain.

### mixed architecture
load produces a reversible component plus persistent learned offset.

This is the highest-value unresolved experiment.

## P5 — solution-abundance manipulation

Holding the task goal constant while experimentally changing the number/geometry of feasible solutions should alter:
- between-individual policy dispersion;
- identity retention;
- exploration/consolidation dynamics.

This is a direct experimental test of the abundance-to-specialization mechanism.

---

# 12. Important falsifier

The hypothesis would be weakened if a sufficiently powered within-individual experiment showed that:

- task solutions are effectively unique under baseline conditions;
- personal history does not change solution selection;
- portable policy is completely explained by fixed biomechanics;
- reversible biomechanics fully determines the individual coordinate with no history-dependent remainder.

The present evidence does not exclude this.

---

# 13. Ecological consequence

Classical specialization stories often emphasize:

[
\text{competition}
\rightarrow
\text{resource/spatial partition}
\rightarrow
\text{persistent individual niches}.
]

The proposed alternative regime is:

[
\boxed{
\text{multiple feasible behavioral solutions}
\rightarrow
\text{history-dependent individualization}
\rightarrow
\text{persistent personal policy}
}
]

with spatial partitioning as only one optional downstream consequence.

That is the most mechanistically specific current explanation for why persistent individual movement specialization can exist in shared physical space.

---

# Bottom line

The current evidence is consistent with a new cross-level mechanism:

> **individual specialization may arise when an abundant movement-control system permits several adequate solutions and personal history progressively stabilizes one individual's way of solving the problem.**

Once formed, that policy can be portable and perturbation-resistant even though its expression remains context dependent and its spatial realization overlaps with conspecifics.

This is a hypothesis linking established motor abundance to ecological individual specialization; the direct abundance-manipulation experiment remains to be done.
