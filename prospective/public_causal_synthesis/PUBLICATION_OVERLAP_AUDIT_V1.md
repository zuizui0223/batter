# Publication overlap and manuscript separation audit v1

## Status

**AUTHORITATIVE INTERNAL PUBLICATION-ETHICS BOUNDARY.**

Date: 2026-10-07.

Purpose:
prevent redundant publication of the same frozen analyses across the JAE, Movement Ecology, and public-causal-synthesis manuscripts.

## Manuscript A — JAE v0.4.0

Working title:

**Persistent individual vertical strategies need not partition three-dimensional space in bats**

Primary scientific object:
- comparative vertical-distribution shape individuality across six public 3-D bat panels;
- removal of coarse horizontal occupancy and additive altitude level;
- terrain-relative and synchronous co-use diagnostics;
- ecological conclusion that persistent vertical strategies need not imply exclusive vertical partition.

Primary data universe:
- Tadarida teniotis;
- Eidolon helvum;
- Hypsignathus monstrosus;
- three Phyllostomus hastatus panels.

Tadarida role:
- motivating/boundary system.

The JAE manuscript explicitly includes the earlier Tadarida early/late identity assignment:
- diagonal gain ≈ +0.1682 nats/event;
- exact p = 0.000174;
and the stronger residual individual-by-cell test:
- p ≈ 0.160.

These are the same V1/V2 results around which the earlier Movement Ecology draft was built.

### Decision

**JAE is the publication home for the Tadarida V1/V2 result.**

## Manuscript B — earlier Movement Ecology draft

Working title:

**Repeatable individual signatures limit exchangeability of vertical movement-state maps in European free-tailed bats**

Primary result:
- Tadarida early-to-late identity transfer;
- diagonal gain ≈ +0.168 nats/event;
- exact p = 0.000174;
- residual individual-by-location V2 unsupported, p ≈ 0.160.

This is not merely conceptually related to the JAE manuscript.

It is the **same source, same frozen state representation, same identity-transfer analysis, and same numerical result** now included in JAE as the focal motivating/boundary analysis.

### Decision

**DO NOT SUBMIT AS A SEPARATE MANUSCRIPT.**

Status:
- analytical precursor / archived draft only;
- may be used as internal provenance for development of the JAE analysis;
- should not be submitted after or alongside JAE as an independent research article.

If the Tadarida-only paper were ever revived, the overlapping V1/V2 results would first have to be removed from JAE or the revived manuscript would need a genuinely non-overlapping primary analysis and explicit editorial disclosure. No such route is currently authorized.

## Manuscript C — public causal synthesis

Working title:

**Individual organization remains detectable across acute perturbations in bats**

Primary scientific object:
- comparative maintenance of same-individual correspondence under controlled acute perturbation;
- developmental boundaries on total individualization;
- coarse-versus-detailed portability;
- wild carrier-to-niche bridge as a downstream boundary.

Primary positive controlled systems:
- Pipistrellus masker;
- Myotis graded masking;
- Eptesicus auditory-midbrain perturbation;
- Aharon navigation-context manipulation.

Developmental systems:
- Harten first-flight history;
- Rachum randomized enrichment;
- Elie randomized auditory feedback.

Portability systems:
- Rhinolophus fixed I/M;
- Carollia external fixed I/M and geometry.

### Relationship to JAE

The synthesis does **not** reuse the JAE comparative vertical-shape result as a positive primary result.

It uses the later frozen wild FlightIntensity carrier gate only as a **downstream boundary**:
- 2/4 FAIL;
- post-outcome H/V exploratory only.

Because some wild panels derive from the same source datasets used in JAE, the final synthesis manuscript must disclose that:
- those public tracking sources also appear in the separate JAE analysis;
- the inferential endpoint here is different;
- no JAE primary vertical-shape effect is counted as independent evidence in the acute-perturbation synthesis.

### Decision

**SEPARATE MANUSCRIPT IS DEFENSIBLE**, provided:
1. the JAE paper is cited/disclosed if published, accepted, or available as a preprint by the time of submission;
2. Data/Code Availability and cover letter state that overlapping public source datasets are analysed for a distinct endpoint;
3. JAE primary effect sizes are not reused as independent positive evidence in the synthesis;
4. the wild bridge remains a negative/boundary result only.
