## Figure 1

A four-box conceptual diagram arranged from left to right. The boxes represent Formation/History, Acute Maintenance, Expression/Portability, and Wild Consequence. The Formation box lists a failed monotonic first-flight primary, a supported late-history secondary, and unsupported/no-difference randomized developmental individualization tests. The Acute Maintenance box lists four supported perturbation datasets. The Expression/Portability box shows supported coarse movement-policy transfer but unsupported detailed-geometry transfer. The Wild Consequence box shows a two-of-four failed frozen carrier gate and exploratory H/V diagnostics. Dashed arrows connect the boxes to indicate conceptual layers rather than one measured latent state.

## Figure 2A

A horizontal dot plot for fourteen juvenile bats. The x-axis is each bat's Spearman slope relating experience to own-history predictive advantage; the y-axis lists individual bats. Eight dots lie to the right of zero and six at or left of zero. The title states that the frozen rule required ten of fourteen positive slopes. An annotation reports programme B equals plus 0.16733, permutation p equals 0.0102, and primary fail.

## Figure 2B

Two bordered text panels summarize randomized developmental experiments. The left panel shows the Rachum enrichment experiment with 29 bats, larger observed dispersion in enriched than impoverished animals, D equals plus 0.734699, p equals 0.167785, and unsupported individualization. The right panel shows the Elie auditory-feedback experiment with ten bats, D equals minus 1.425497, exact p equals 0.716667, and no difference in total individualization. A note states that the two D statistics use different representations and should not be compared quantitatively.

## Figure 3

A point plot with four perturbation systems on the x-axis and fraction of biological individuals with positive identity advantage on the y-axis. Pipistrellus masker is five of six, Myotis masking is three of three, Eptesicus auditory-midbrain perturbation is four of four, and Aharon navigation manipulation is four of four. Marker size indicates biological sample size. Text above markers reports biological n and exact-p or mapping-rank information. A note warns that raw effect sizes are not pooled because endpoints differ.

## Figure 4

A five-row status table. Rows are Rhino coarse I/M, Carollia fixed Rhino I/M, Carollia detailed geometry, the wild scalar carrier bridge, and wild H/V localization. The first two are supported, detailed geometry is unsupported, the wild bridge failed its frozen two-of-four gate, and H/V is exploratory only. The figure emphasizes that coarse organization, detailed behavioral realization, and wild ecological consequence are separate empirical objects.
