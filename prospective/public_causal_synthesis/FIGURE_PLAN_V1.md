## Figure 1 — Four empirical layers of behavioral individuality

### Purpose

Introduce the conceptual architecture without implying a universal latent state.

### Layout

Four horizontally ordered boxes:

1. **Formation / history**
   - first-flight monotonic formation primary: FAIL
   - late-history secondary: supported
   - randomized enrichment individualization: unsupported
   - randomized auditory-feedback total individualization: no difference

2. **Maintenance under acute perturbation**
   - Pipistrellus masker: supported
   - Myotis graded masking: supported
   - Eptesicus auditory-midbrain perturbation: supported
   - Aharon navigation-context manipulation: supported

3. **Context-specific expression / portability**
   - Rhino coarse I/M: supported
   - Carollia fixed I/M: supported
   - Carollia detailed geometry: unsupported

4. **Wild ecological consequence**
   - frozen wild FlightIntensity carrier gate: 2/4 FAIL
   - H/V: exploratory only

### Main visual statement

Do not draw one uninterrupted causal arrow implying every dataset identifies one state.

Use dashed conceptual arrows between empirical layers.

Caption language:

> The programme separates questions about how individual organization emerges, whether identity-bearing organization survives current perturbation, how it is expressed across tasks, and whether it maps to wild spatial individuality.
