# Journal fit v1 — public causal synthesis

Date checked: 2026-10-07.

## Primary target — Behavioral Ecology

### Scope fit

Behavioral Ecology explicitly welcomes:
- empirical and theoretical behavioral ecology;
- studies linking behavior to ecological/environmental context;
- comparative analyses;
- individual-species experimental and field studies;
- comparative and meta-analytic approaches.

The current manuscript fits through:
- individual behavioral variation;
- developmental environment;
- context dependence;
- sensory/navigation perturbation;
- cross-system comparative synthesis;
- explicit ecological-consequence boundary.

Recent 2026 content includes work on:
- environmental variability and among-individual behavioral variation;
- personality and life-history change;
- multi-trait individual differences across species.

### Strengths for this journal

- central question is behavioral individuality rather than movement-method development;
- multiple experiments connect plasticity and individual differences;
- developmental and acute environmental manipulations are both represented;
- negative and boundary results are biologically interpretable;
- source-level provenance is unusually transparent.

### Main editorial risk

The study is a comparative secondary reanalysis rather than one new field experiment.

The manuscript must therefore lead with:
- one biological question;
- causal-layer separation;
- ecological/evolutionary significance.

Do not lead with:
- repository provenance;
- permutation architecture;
- the number of datasets.

### Data-policy fit

All source datasets are already public.

Analysis code, frozen contracts, result receipts and generated figures are version controlled in the public repository.

A clean Data Availability statement should identify each source archive and the analysis repository.

### Recommendation

**Best current fit.**

Submission order:
1. Behavioral Ecology.

## Stretch target — Proceedings of the Royal Society B

### Why it could work

The programme asks a broad biological question:
how environmental change acts differently on the maintenance, expression and developmental organization of behavioral individuality.

Strengths:
- multiple independent species/systems;
- controlled manipulations;
- randomized developmental experiments;
- cross-task portability;
- explicit failed ecological bridge.

### Why desk risk is high

Recent literature already establishes:
- cross-context persistence of individuality;
- environmental mean shifts without corresponding changes in individuality magnitude.

Thus this manuscript does not introduce a wholly new general principle.

Additional concerns:
- heterogeneous endpoints;
- several biological n = 3–5 experiments;
- cross-study synthesis developed iteratively;
- no pooled common effect size;
- formation mechanism remains unresolved.

### Recommendation

**Stretch only after a strong internal review.**

Do not delay Behavioral Ecology submission solely to force Proceedings B positioning.

## Secondary target — Animal Behaviour

### Scope fit

Animal Behaviour publishes fundamental and applied empirical work focused on animal behavior, including:
- behavioral ecology;
- development of behavior;
- ethology;
- neuroethology;
- sensory biology;
- physiology;
- population biology.

The current manuscript fits especially through:
- individual behavior;
- developmental manipulation;
- sensory perturbation;
- navigation;
- vocal behavior.

### Advantage

The journal may be more comfortable than a broad ecology journal with:
- heterogeneous behavioral endpoints;
- sensory/neurobehavioral manipulations;
- a comparative mechanistic synthesis centered on behavior.

### Risk

The manuscript's strongest contribution is cross-study architecture rather than one focal behavioral mechanism.

### Recommendation

**Strong fallback / alternative primary if Behavioral Ecology framing proves awkward.**

## Other plausible route — Royal Society Open Science

Good methodological/provenance fit and broad scope.

Use if:
- editors at more selective behavior journals view the heterogeneous public-data synthesis as insufficiently unified;
- transparency and reproducibility become the main selling point.

## Positioning sentence for Behavioral Ecology

> **Across independent bat experiments, individual-specific organization repeatedly remains detectable after acute sensory and navigational perturbation, whereas developmental manipulations that alter phenotype do not reveal a simple common rule for the formation or total amount of individuality.**

## Positioning sentence to avoid

> We reveal a universal principle that individuality is resistant to environmental change.

That is broader than the evidence and conflicts with current prior art.

## Manuscript-type recommendation

Submit as an original research article / empirical comparative reanalysis.

Do not label it:
- meta-analysis;
- systematic review;
- preregistered multi-study study.

The accurate description is:

> **provenance-controlled comparative reanalysis of independent public experiments.**
