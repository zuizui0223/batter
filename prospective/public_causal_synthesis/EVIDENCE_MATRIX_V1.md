## Programme-level conclusions

### Confirmatorily supported

1. **Persistent personal organization exists.**
2. **It can survive multiple kinds of controlled current-context perturbation.**
3. **Coarse personal organization can transfer across tasks where detailed geometry does not.**
4. **Late personal history can be strongly predictive, but a common monotonic ontogenetic build-up rule is not supported.**

### Confirmatorily not established

1. broad enrichment -> more total individuality;
2. developmental auditory feedback -> different total amount of individuality;
3. one universal bat policy coordinate;
4. laboratory carrier -> wild vertical-niche bridge;
5. direct feasible-solution opportunity -> formation of individual specialization.

### Descriptively suggested, not confirmatory

1. enrichment may produce coherent expansion-like individual-difference tendencies;
2. developmental auditory feedback may reallocate individuality across acoustic dimensions;
3. later behavioral state may often resemble personal baseline + shared environmental shift;
4. wild movement individuality may be multidimensional.

## Strongest bounded synthesis

> **Established individual organization is more robust to changes in current context than its behavioral expression is, while developmental manipulations can change phenotype without obeying a simple rule for the total amount of individuality.**

The unresolved formation question is therefore not:

> does environment matter?

It is:

> **what determines which dimensions and histories become individualized for particular animals, and why individuals diverge even when broad developmental treatment does not predict the total amount of divergence?**
