# Paper story v1 — behavioral individuality occupies separable causal layers

## Core biological question

> **Can behavioral individuality be treated as one biological quantity, or do formation, maintenance, expression and ecological consequence respond differently to environmental change?**

The public-data programme gives a clear answer:

> **They are empirically separable. Established individual correspondence repeatedly survives strong current-context perturbations; developmental manipulations alter phenotype without a simple rule for total individuality; coarse personal organization transfers more broadly than detailed realized geometry; and the laboratory-to-wild niche bridge remains unresolved.**

This is the paper.

It is not:
- another repeatability paper;
- a claim that individuality is context independent;
- a claim that enrichment causes personality;
- a claim that one latent policy exists across all bats.

---

# One-sentence result

> **Across independent bat experiments, different causal layers of individuality respond differently to perturbation: identity-bearing organization is repeatedly retained under acute context change, developmental treatment does not map simply onto total individualization, realized geometry is more labile than coarse organization, and ecological niche consequences remain a separate unresolved layer.**

---

# Conceptual distinction

The paper separates two objects.

## 1. Identity persistence

Question:

> after the current context changes, can the same biological individual still be recognized from its relative behavioral organization?

This is a mapping/correspondence property.

Operational evidence:
- same-individual state remains closer to its own history than to other individuals after shared context shifts are removed.

## 2. Individualization magnitude

Question:

> does a developmental manipulation change how far individuals diverge from one another?

This is a dispersion property.

Operational evidence:
- treatment contrast in residual among-individual multivariate dispersion after shared treatment effects are removed.

These are not equivalent.

A system can:
- strongly change mean phenotype;
- preserve identity correspondence;
- leave total among-individual dispersion unchanged;
- redistribute individuality across dimensions.

That separation is the biological core.

# Evidence chain

## Layer A — personal history is informative, but the formation trajectory is not universal

First-flight history programme:

Frozen monotonic formation primary:
- B = +0.16733;
- permutation p = 0.0102;
- positive experience slopes = 8/14;
- frozen requirement = 10/14.

Verdict:

**PRIMARY FORMATION RULE FAIL.**

Thus there is a positive programme-level tendency, but not the directionally consistent ontogenetic increase required by the frozen hypothesis.

Predeclared late-history secondary:
- L = +112.5554;
- p = 0.0001;
- 12/14 juveniles positive.

This supports:

> by later early ontogeny, a juvenile's own recent movement history is substantially more informative about its next spatial use than experience-matched conspecific histories.

Post-primary mechanism diagnostic:
- earliest two valid days: E = +15.19, p = 0.1655, unsupported;
- recent two versus earliest two: Q = +144.079, p = 0.0001, 10/14 positive.

This suggests identity-specific updating/refinement after the earliest seed, but it is **post-primary diagnostic evidence** and cannot rescue the failed formation primary.

Independent randomized early-environment history-carrier test:
- enriched mean H = 1.1476;
- impoverished mean H = 0.4395;
- T = +0.7081;
- randomized p = 0.3244;
- **FAIL TREATMENT EFFECT**.

Therefore the safe formation conclusion is:

> **personal history is strongly informative later, but neither a universal monotonic build-up nor a broad enrichment-driven strengthening of the history carrier is established.**

## Layer B — randomized developmental manipulation

### Environmental enrichment

Rachum et al. 2025:

- n = 29;
- enriched / impoverished = 14 / 15;
- D = +0.734699;
- p = 0.167785;
- no supported increase in total individualization.

Descriptive:
- all three trait contributions point enriched > impoverished;
- later state is best approximated overall by personal baseline + shared treatment shift.

### Developmental auditory feedback

Elie et al. 2024:

- n = 10;
- hearing / deaf = 5 / 5;
- D = -1.425497;
- exact p = 0.716667;
- no supported difference in total adult vocal individualization.

Descriptive:
- 15 acoustic dimensions positive;
- 13 negative;
- cancellation ratio = 0.840173.

Interpretation:

> developmental manipulations can alter phenotype and redistribute where individuality is expressed without predictably changing the total amount of individuality.

# Main biological model

Use the simplest possible hierarchy:

`individual history / predisposition -> persistent personal organization -> context-specific expression`

Formation determines:
- which dimensions become individualized;
- how personal states diverge.

Maintenance determines:
- whether identity-bearing organization survives current perturbation.

Expression determines:
- what the current behavior looks like.

Ecological consequence determines:
- whether those expressions create spatial/resource partition.

The data show these layers are empirically separable.

---

# What is actually new

Not new:
- animal personality exists;
- behavior is context dependent;
- individuality can persist across contexts;
- environmental challenge can shift mean behavior without changing individuality magnitude;
- developmental environment can alter behavior;
- multivariate personality covariance can change with environment.

Recent direct prior art includes:
- Mathejczyk et al. 2026: individuality across environmental contexts in Drosophila;
- Gallagher et al. 2026: ecological stress shifts behavior without changing individuality magnitude in clonal fish.

The contribution is therefore narrower:

> **Within one comparative bat programme, four layers that are often discussed together are empirically separated: formation, maintenance, behavioral expression and ecological consequence.**

The strongest bat-specific synthesis is:

1. acute/current-context perturbations repeatedly preserve same-individual correspondence;
2. randomized developmental manipulations alter phenotype without a simple effect on total individualization;
3. coarse personal organization transfers more broadly than detailed realized geometry;
4. the laboratory-to-wild niche bridge fails its frozen confirmatory gate.

This is a comparative causal decomposition, not a universal theory of personality.

# Discussion spine

## Point 1

Behavioral individuality is not one fixed expressed value.

It can be retained relationally while absolute behavior changes.

## Point 2

Maintenance is easier to demonstrate than formation.

Repeated perturbation evidence shows non-erasure.

But broad environmental richness and auditory feedback do not predict total individualization amount.

## Point 3

Formation may be about allocation, not amplitude.

Development may determine:
- which dimensions carry individuality;
- which histories become reinforced;
- how individual-specific information is distributed.

This is more plausible than a universal:
`more experience -> more individuality`.

## Point 4

Fine behavioral geometry is not the persistent object.

Coarser organization is more portable.

## Point 5

Do not automatically translate laboratory organization into niche partition.

The wild bridge failed its frozen gate.

# Claim ceiling

The paper may claim:

> **formation and maintenance are empirically separable in the analysed bat systems.**

It may claim:

> **individual correspondence survives multiple controlled acute perturbations.**

It may claim:

> **developmental manipulations can alter phenotype without predictably changing total individualization.**

It may not claim:

- one universal latent personality state;
- all bats behave this way;
- developmental environment does not matter;
- individuality is immutable;
- a specific neural storage substrate;
- the laboratory carrier explains wild niche structure;
- multiple feasible solutions causally create individuality.

# Current journal-level assessment

The story is biologically coherent enough for a standalone synthesis/reanalysis paper, but the 2026 prior art lowers the ceiling for a broad "new principle" claim.

Strengths:
- multiple independent controlled bat datasets;
- prospective/frozen re-analysis contracts;
- positive and negative results retained;
- randomized developmental manipulations included;
- explicit failed wild bridge;
- clear separation of causal layers.

Weaknesses:
- heterogeneous species and endpoints;
- several small biological-n experiments;
- much of the novelty is comparative synthesis rather than one decisive experiment;
- cross-context persistence is already directly demonstrated elsewhere;
- mean-shift-without-individuality-change is also now established elsewhere.

Therefore the paper should be sold on:

> **a bat-specific multi-layer causal decomposition with unusually strict evidence provenance**

rather than:
- a universal behavioral law;
- first demonstration of context-resistant individuality;
- first demonstration that environmental effects need not change individuality amount.
