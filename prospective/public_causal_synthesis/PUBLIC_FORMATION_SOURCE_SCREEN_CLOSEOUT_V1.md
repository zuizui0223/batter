# Public formation-source screen closeout v1

## Status

**SEARCH CLOSED UNLESS A NEW CAUSAL AXIS APPEARS.**

Purpose:
record why currently available public candidates do not justify another confirmatory branch.

## Candidate: Goldshtein, Harten & Yovel — maternal navigation learning

Study:
*Mother bats facilitate pup navigation learning*.

Public data:
Mendeley Data `10.17632/gpcg9m5758.1`.

What it adds:
- longitudinal mother–pup GPS;
- repeated maternal placement at specific trees;
- pups' first independent flights to the same sites;
- route similarity between carried history and later independent navigation.

Why it is not opened here:
- the source paper already directly analyzes the maternal-history / later-navigation relationship;
- maternal placement history is not randomized across pups;
- opening a new reanalysis would mainly restate an already published developmental-learning result;
- it does not independently identify the origin of between-individual divergence.

Disposition:

**LOW MARGINAL VALUE FOR CURRENT PROGRAMME.**

Useful citation for formation/history context, not a new confirmatory branch.

## Candidate: Fernandez et al. — maternal feedback and vocal ontogeny

Study:
*Maternal behavior influences vocal practice and learning processes in the greater sac-winged bat*.

Public data:
Dryad `10.5061/dryad.7wm37pw4s`.

What it adds:
- 19 wild pups;
- maternal behavior;
- longitudinal vocal practice;
- repertoire size;
- learned syllables;
- acoustic ontogeny.

Why it is not opened here:
- maternal activity is observational rather than randomized;
- the source study already tests maternal-behavior associations with vocal practice and learning;
- it would add another history–phenotype association, not a new causal identification of individualization.

Disposition:

**LOW MARGINAL VALUE FOR CURRENT PROGRAMME.**

Useful citation for socially mediated developmental history.

---

## Candidate: Iriarte-Diaz et al. — reversible body loading

Study:
*Kinematic Plasticity during Flight in Fruit Bats: Individual Variability in Response to Loading*.

What it adds:
- within-individual 20% reversible body-mass increase;
- three biological bats;
- controlled and loaded conditions;
- individual-specific combinations of kinematic compensation.

Why it is not opened as a new primary:
- biological n = 3;
- the published result already explicitly centers individual-specific compensation strategies;
- a new public raw-data package sufficient for an independent frozen identity analysis was not identified in the current screen;
- maintenance/context individuality is already strongly overdetermined by four independent perturbation datasets.

Disposition:

**BIOLOGICALLY RELEVANT, LOW CURRENT INFORMATION GAIN.**

Useful literature support for multiple biomechanical compensation routes.

## Candidate: Gallagher et al. 2026 — clonal fish developmental stress

Study:
*Born This Way: Individuality Is Seeded Before Birth and Robust to Ecological Stress*.

Public data:
Dryad `10.5061/dryad.5mkkwh7jx`.

What it establishes:
- genetically clonal offspring;
- developmental predator-cue manipulation;
- continuous tracking from birth;
- mean behavioral shifts;
- no change in magnitude of individuality;
- maternal identity effects in some traits.

Why it is not opened:
- it is not a bat dataset;
- it directly overlaps the general causal boundary already being used for literature positioning;
- its main value is to constrain novelty, not to add another bat-programme endpoint.

Disposition:

**STRONG PRIOR ART / NOVELTY BOUNDARY.**

# Search stop rule

Do not open another public dataset merely because it contains:

- repeated individuals;
- multiple contexts;
- a treatment;
- developmental behavior;
- public raw data.

A new source must add a causal axis not already saturated.

Open only if it provides at least one of:

1. randomized individual learning-history manipulation with repeated pre/post individuality measures;
2. independently specified solution opportunity followed by later specialization under equal current opportunity;
3. reversible biomechanics with enough biological individuals for a nontrivial confirmatory identity/strategy test;
4. a prospectively testable reallocation/orientation mechanism not already analyzed in the source paper.

Otherwise:

**STOP PUBLIC-DATA EXPANSION.**

## Current conclusion

The public programme has reached diminishing returns.

Maintenance and portability are well constrained.

Development clearly matters, but current public sources do not identify the rule that determines which individual-specific differences first emerge and where they are allocated.

The remaining high-value problem is formation, not another demonstration of persistence.
