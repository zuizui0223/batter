# Abstract and claim checklist v1

## Working title

**Individual organization remains detectable across acute perturbations in bats**

## Abstract

Behavioral individuality is commonly defined by consistent differences among individuals, but consistency alone does not distinguish persistent individual organization from the context-specific behavior through which it is expressed. We re-analysed independent public bat experiments using source-level endpoints and null models frozen before numerical outcome opening. Across four controlled current-context manipulations—external sensory masking, graded acoustic masking, reversible auditory-midbrain perturbation, and altered navigation conditions—the true same-individual correspondence remained supported after shared condition shifts were removed. Cross-task analyses further showed that coarse individual movement organization could transfer where detailed realized trajectory geometry did not. Developmental evidence showed a different pattern. In two randomized bat experiments, environmental enrichment and access to auditory feedback altered behavioral phenotype in the source studies but did not produce a supported change in the total amount of multivariate individual differentiation under frozen re-analyses. A preregistered laboratory-to-wild carrier bridge also failed, showing that persistent individual organization cannot be assumed to imply wild spatial niche differentiation. Together, these results support a layered view of behavioral individuality in which maintenance, developmental allocation, context-specific expression, and ecological consequence are empirically distinct. The strongest remaining uncertainty is formation: what determines which behavioral dimensions and historical trajectories become individualized for particular animals?

## Short significance statement

> **Across independent bat experiments, acute environmental and sensory perturbations repeatedly preserve information that identifies individuals, while developmental manipulations do not map simply onto how much individuals differ. Individual organization, behavioral expression, and ecological consequence should therefore be treated as distinct empirical layers.**

## Claim checklist

### Supported

- Individual-specific organization remains detectable across multiple independent controlled acute perturbations.
- Coarse movement organization can transfer across contexts where detailed geometry does not.
- Late personal history strongly predicts later spatial use, while a common monotonic increase in history dependence across juveniles is not supported.
- Developmental manipulations can change phenotype without a supported simple change in total multivariate individualization.
- Laboratory individual organization does not automatically imply a confirmed wild spatial-niche bridge.

### Descriptive only

- Enrichment shows a coherent expansion-like tendency across the three frozen personality traits.
- Auditory-feedback manipulation shows strong reallocation/cancellation across acoustic dimensions.
- Later personality state is best described overall by personal baseline plus a shared treatment-associated shift.
- Wild H/V component carriers are candidate multidimensional representations.

### Not supported

- One universal bat latent policy.
- Development has no effect on individuality.
- Environmental richness causes more individuality.
- Acute perturbation proves where individual information is stored.
- A laboratory policy carrier explains wild vertical niche differentiation.
- Multiple feasible solutions causally create individual specialization.

## Mandatory methods-provenance sentence

> Source-level re-analysis endpoints, support rules and null models were frozen before numerical outcome opening, whereas public-source discovery and the cross-study synthesis were developed iteratively and should not be interpreted as a prospectively preregistered multi-study meta-analysis.

## Mandatory small-n sentence

> Several controlled perturbation experiments contain only three to five biological individuals; exact permutation results therefore quantify within-experiment identity correspondence and should not be interpreted as precise population-prevalence estimates.

## Mandatory novelty sentence

> The contribution is not the general discovery that individuality can persist across contexts, but a provenance-controlled comparative decomposition showing different empirical behavior of acute maintenance, developmental individualization, context-specific realization and wild ecological consequence across bat systems.

## Abstract ceiling

Do not add:
- individual source p-values beyond what is necessary;
- wild H/V exploratory results;
- feature-specific post-primary decompositions;
- claims about neural storage;
- claims of universality;
- “first demonstration” language.

The abstract should remain a synthesis of causal layers, not a catalogue of datasets.
