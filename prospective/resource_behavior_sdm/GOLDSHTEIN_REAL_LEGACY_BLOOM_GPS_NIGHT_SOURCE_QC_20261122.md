## 2. Provisional original GPS × directly observed flower spatial–temporal support

The published methods specify monitoring drone cacti **early in the morning when flowers remained open** (Goldshtein et al. 2020 Current Biology DOI 10.1016/j.cub.2020.07.079). Original research locality El Pinacate, Sonora is Mountain Standard Time **UTC−7 without 2016 DST**. Source RMC GPS timestamps are UTC; a provisional source-aware nocturnal mapping uses **18:00–05:59 local** and associates an evening-to-morning bat excursion with the **following morning's** direct cactus survey. This uses ONLY independent GPS RMC horizontal latitude/longitude + UTC time and BIFF8 spreadsheet cactus/date/count; there are **NO GGA altitude values, height-defined visits or learned behavior-state labels in the selection**.

At the author's published horizontal near-cactus scale of **15 m**, the provisional source calendar/XY association yields:
- **227 individual GPS position reports**, among **five bats** on **six bat×local night/morning survey combinations**, near an independently measured cactus with a numeric flower count on that candidate morning;
- **42 distinct source-surveyed cacti**, **44 cactus×morning observation pairs** and **34 GPS positions** whose nearest measured cactus has a positive numeric count;
- 60-second contiguous same-bat, same-cactus XY proximity grouping yields **99 recorded proximity episodes**, of which **22** correspond to a positive recorded count.

**These are exposure candidate counts, NOT 227 feeding visits, 99 independent decisions, or 22 actual nectar consumptions.** The original source paper reports 8m horizontal GPS SD, which is large compared with a 15m radius; height-defined visit threshold (<20 m) MUST NOT be used when predicting that height. Ground vs drone resource survey effort is not uniform or source-verified. Six animal-nights among five bats are insufficient for a credible broad transfer test, and individual GPS fixes are serially autocorrelated. Even an exact physical source-nearby observation is not a random available-choice dataset; survey completeness and observation/detection must be audited before biological predictive inference.

## 3. Frozen independent ground/GPS height-datum diagnostic — incompatible raw reference

The [frozen sensor and source-height audit](GOLDSHTEIN_DRONE_GGA_DATUM_DIAGNOSTIC_FREEZE_20261122.md) was committed **before any raw numerical GGA altitudes were read**. The author-defined sensor method corrects GPS ellipsoid altitude by EGM2008 geoid height and Google Maps surface elevation; GPS horizontal SD ≈8m, vertical SD ≈11m (Goldshtein 2020 STAR Methods). The user ZIP contains NMEA GGA numeric metre altitude but the GGA *geoid separation* sentence field is **empty for all 57,378 source time+XY matched positive-quality GGA/RMC pairs** on eligible 2016 candidate survey dates; it is not an authored corrected AGL series.

Compare the raw GGA altitude field with nearest independent drone-sheet altitude using purely horizontal ≤15m/≤50m selection, no elevation-dependent selection. Within 15m on UTC drone survey dates: **499 sensor pairs from five bats/six bat-days**, raw (GGA altitude − drone altitude) distribution **5th/25th/median/75th/95th percentile = −211.28, −102.06, −86.51, −81.61, −74.21 m**. Within 50m (n=1,213), the median difference is **−92.43 m**. Under a hypothetical local calendar-day join, 15m has n=486 and median −86.24 m.

**This is a measurement-reference mismatch, not bats flying 87m below the ground.** The source drone-sheet alt could have an unknown GPS/Pix4D reference or cactus-top/ground semantics; a published sensor geoid/digital terrain correction may be absent. No constant offset correction can be inferred from true bat positions without a qualified common datum. It is invalid to subtract drone alt from raw GGA alt and label the result AGL, to tune an offset to avoid negative heights, or to call a height correlation with flower counts ecological evidence. The original source script also depends on absent dataInfo.xlsx and individual dataCacti.mat files for the author's processed visits/height pathway.

A first local matching code review found a validation error in the diagnostic prototype (RMC sentences have 12 comma-delimited fields, not 13). The initial zero-pair result was **rejected**, this length precondition was repaired, and the source was reprocessed to yield the above **57,378** pair count. The accepted diagnostics check exact same-NMEA animal, UTC acquisition time and raw XY, positive GGA quality, and no height-based inclusion. This is a provenance/software audit and not a new bat effect.
