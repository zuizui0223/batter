## 2. Ecological question and predicted separation of dimensions

**Primary question:** Under what conditions does independently observed resource opportunity increase the *transferable prediction* of animal **location, timing, behavior and vertical use**—and when does it change only one dimension while leaving others unchanged?

This resolves the conceptual problem that a species can have:
1. Stable distribution at country/horizontal scale;
2. Different resource-dependent *within-site* timing and behavioral states;
3. Repeatable individual vertical distributions;
4. Extensive 3-D overlap with other individuals.

These can co-occur; none entails active 3-D partitioning. In particular, a 2-D model could show `ΔAUC ≈ 0` even when an appropriately validated conditional `p(z,b,t | x,resource)` carries predictive information.

**Behavior-mixture mediation test:** compare `I(individual; z | XY,time)` with `I(individual; z | XY,time, independently observed behavior)`. The first can be positive and the second exactly zero in a known-truth world where individuals only differ in **how frequently** they forage/commute, not in how they fly vertically *within* each state. Positive residual after proper state conditioning would instead motivate a within-state policy, subject to independent estimation and detector calibration. Neither direction is yet measured in the current `batter` wild panel. Do not infer state from the same height response being tested. **That outcome is a falsifiable prediction, not an observed explanation for the current shadow failure.**

### Probability contract

Keep **three scientific targets** separate:

- **Regional species occurrence/availability:** `P(occupied_s(x,t) | abiotic, resource, access, effort design)`; this requires independent presence/absence or defensible presence-only assumptions and is **not** estimated by the prototype.
- **Conditional use of spacetime/behavior/height:** `p_use,s(x,t,i,b,z) = p_s(x,t | A,G,R) · w_s(i) · π_s,i(b | x,t,R,M,C) · φ_s,i(z | b,x,t,R,A)`; here `A` is abiotic environment, `G` generic plant opportunity, `R` documented-partner opportunity, `M` independent memory/protocol covariates, and `C` independently measured cues. Each component must normalize over its own conditional support.
- **Observed event distribution:** `p_obs(event) ∝ p_use(event) · q(cell,time,state,height,effort)`. `q` is an independently identified detection/recording model. Missing records are **not** inferred absences.

The prototype initially fixes `w_s(i)` independent of place and time; it does **not** fit individual habitat preferences, variable participation, source availability, local abundance, species occupancy, behavioral transition parameters, or feedback from depletion. Those are explicit future modules, not secretly estimated values.

The mandatory mathematical check is that integrating a joint use map over `i, b, z` recovers exactly the model's `p_s(x,t)`. Integrating additionally over time recovers the horizontal use projection. Therefore a vertical behavioral pattern cannot create a spurious horizontal range expansion merely from extra state labels.

## 3. Executable starter, not an empirical validation

`resource_behavior_sdm.py` provides:

- strict `habitat_log_scores(abiotic, generic_plant, linked_plant, beta_*)` — **externally assigned** predictor weights only, no parameter estimation;
- `resource_conditioned_state_policies` — a prespecified, resource-index-dependent softmax for behavioral state allocation, allowing resource changes to alter *conditional height use* while holding total horizontal use fixed; known-truth only;
- strict `joint_use_distribution` for horizontal × time × individual × behavioral state × height;
- marginal projection plus `I(individual;z | cell,time)` **and** `I(individual;z | cell,time,behavior)` mediation diagnostics;
- separate detector-conditioned recorded-event distribution `p_obs`;
- heldout event log-score that does **not** create probability mass for unseen support.

`test_resource_behavior_sdm.py` supplies synthetic unit tests showing that a positive individual vertical-information value can coexist with **identical horizontal use** by individuals, that resource-weight ablations change logits by construction, and that unequal detection changes recorded rates without changing true-use probabilities. These demonstrate **logical possibility and numerical bookkeeping only**, not fitted bat mechanisms or empirical gains.

### Worked known-truth counterexample (simulation mathematics only)

In the exact source-free behavioral-mixture fixture, two equally weighted individuals have **identical horizontal use** and share **both** height bins. They differ only in the proportion of two objectively assigned behaviors: one forages 80% and the other 20%; the *within-behavior* height probabilities are identical for both. The generated marginal (p(high)) is 0.68 versus 0.32. This yields `I(individual;z | XY,time) = 0.06627772` nats but `I(individual;z | XY,time,behavior) = 0`. Thus individual height fingerprints need **not** imply within-state flight differences, competition, or exclusive 3-D space use.

In a separate single-location, two-time resource-policy fixture, an **assigned** resource score change from 0 to 1 with `beta_forage=2` changes `p(forage)` from 0.5000 to 0.880797 and `p(high | time)` from **0.5000 to 0.804638** while keeping the entire horizontal projection at exactly one cell. These are probabilities computed from chosen toy coefficients, **not estimates or replications of wild bats**. This makes the paper's prospective question measurable: resource information might improve *conditional* movement-state/height prediction even if the 2-D distribution metric cannot change.

No raw source coordinates, personally identifying bat labels, copyrighted supplements, fine-resolution spatial arrays or field resource measurements are committed. This is a computational interface to which independently authenticated/fitted predictors can later be attached.

## 6. Source-matched actual food × guild prediction (2026-11-20; new exploratory fit)

An original user-supplied ZIP from [Capaverde et al. 2018 Dryad 9rb48c5](https://doi.org/10.5061/dryad.9rb48c5) has been hash-verified and analyzed without committing raw animal records or site coordinates. Its observed food is **field-recorded fruiting plants**, unlike NeoBat climate-only plant occurrence ranks. Original-source QC reveals 42 source rows dated 2015–2030, 2 noncapture placeholders, 1,189 bat-table rows versus the paper's 1,138 captures, and missing original vegetation-clutter covariate. Those data-quality conflicts remain unresolved.

Only 2013 Oct–2014 Feb source records with **exact normalized plot and date agreement** and recorded fruit survey were admitted: **46 plots, 103 plot-nights and 810 captures**. The response is frugivore share among observed mist-net captures, NOT species occupancy, resource consumption, or vertical position. The seven RAPELD spatial/design source blocks were held out. Adding plants with fruits in the paper's 12 potential bat-food genera to elevation + sampling-design + matched-night baseline produced **+0.016085 nats per captured guild fraction**, with **5/7 blocks positive**; leave-plot-out +0.007130, geography-controlled +0.014601, no-Carollia +0.008523. A block-constrained 399-permutation source-open *exploratory* diagnostic was 0.0225, **not a preregistered confirmatory p**. Generic 22-genera fruit amount had Δ=−0.002263; other ten genera themselves had Δ=+0.012974 and prevent a strict trophic-specificity claim. The within-plot last-night test gave +0.004047, 20/36 plots positive, and cannot establish short-term resource-induced behavior switching.

See [full original-source method and claim audit](DUCKE_ORIGINAL_RESOURCE_BLOCKED_CV_V1.md), [aggregate evidence receipt](ducke_source_result_v1.json), [hash-pinned original artifact_tool adapter](ducke_original_adapter_v1.py), and [pure-stdlib grouped CV prediction model](ducke_guild_cv_engine_v1.py). This is the first **source-local measured-food predictive increment** in the batter resource portfolio, but it is not a new discovery that fruit correlates with bat guild composition (the 2018 authors established that). It is independent of **neither** the publication's original source nor its sampling design. ## 8. Joint four-guild resource-composition prediction v3 (2026-11-20)

A **single multinomial response** is preferable to counting frugivore, gleaner and nectarivore binary guild-share results as independent replicated outcomes. The source-open design and pure-standard-library full-Hessian Newton model were frozen at [commit abec244](https://github.com/zuizui0223/batter/commit/abec244fb044fcff90f6fe368ebd76c92a2ef2b0), before opening the newly defined v3 output. It is still a single original study inspected previously, so *not confirmatory*. The 46 matched source plots have 810 captured bats; model outcomes cover **809** in four major guilds (573 frugivores, 132 gleaning animalivores, 58 nectarivores, 46 aerial insectivores), with one sanguivore **excluded and not recoded**.

The site-equal four-class softmax on the same seven spatial/design heldout source blocks yields **+0.028769** natural-log score (6/7 blocks positive) when adding **12 candidate fruit food genera + 10 remaining fruit genera as separate predictors** to the elevation/design/night-count baseline, compared with **+0.026107** when contrasting this two-predictor model directly with a one-number total-22-genera fruit model (5/7 positive). Entirely removing the added PO sampling frame and using **29 plots / six L3–L8 heldout blocks** leaves **+0.022878** vs baseline (**6/6 positive**) and +0.013778 split-vs-total (5/6). In the L-only source, candidate 12 genera add merely +0.002934 after adjusting the remaining 10; the remaining ten after adjusting twelve add +0.015239. Thus this new joint model strengthens a **within-source composition-vs-total predictive finding**, **not source-verified bat diet specificity**.

Two numerical implementations were compared independently on the SHA-pinned user-uploaded workbook: SciPy L-BFGS-B and a pure-stdlib Newton solver matching the frozen GitHub model. Across every major seven-block and six-block score comparison, absolute implementation differences were **at most 3.081×10⁻⁸ nats**. The original source-free GitHub CI covers mathematics and fail-closed evidence bookkeeping, not a public exact-row biological replay. No original site positions, bat IDs or source rows are committed.

See [v3 result](DUCKE_JOINT_FOUR_GUILD_V3_RESULT.md), [aggregate receipt](ducke_joint_guild_v3_result.json) and [frozen source-free implementation](ducke_joint_guild_composition_v3.py). ## 9. Source-label matched-dimensionality null v4 — sharpened negative control (2026-11-20)

The earlier v3 two-part fruit composition model had **one more predictor** than the pooled-total fruit model. Before computing the new *post-outcome exploratory* diagnostic, [v4 design commit `9c35ebf`](https://github.com/zuizui0223/batter/commit/9c35ebf09945ea138c239917291d5b333c3765d3) pinned two **equal-parameter 12/10 plant-label partitions**, fixed draws/seeds and 7 withheld source blocks. The user-provided SHA-authenticated original Ducke data were reopened and all 103 source-matched fruit survey nights were grouped into **22 plant-label source categories**, of which **21 are named genera and one is `Not_identified`** (52 of 661 recorded source fruiting plants, 7.87%). The original F12 / other10 grouping exactly reproduces v3 plot aggregates; its split-vs-total score increment is **+0.0261067**, consistent with original +0.02610666.

Under **199 fully unrestricted 12/10 label splits** (same two predictors and source data), only **4** outperform or match the preselected food-12 split, plus-one exploratory tail **0.025**. But under **199 abundance-stratified label splits** (four original-count quartiles with food group counts [4,2,2,4]), **10** outperform or match it, exploratory tail **0.055**. These two values are *source-open label-randomization ranks*, **not preregistered causal/confirmatory p values**. The original food-12 partition is therefore distinguishable from most completely arbitrary label splits, **but not convincingly unique once source genus abundance structure is controlled**. No source named-genus-to-true-feeding negative control was observed.

The two-axis composition-vs-pooled-total signal **survives removing the source's unidentified category**: 21 named genera (12 candidate food + 9 other) give **+0.028159**; it also survives restricting to 29 L3–L8 sites, split-vs-total **+0.013778**. **Ecological ceiling:** single-source relative captured-guild composition is predictively associated with actual surveyed fruiting-plant *composition*; the specific 12 "bat-food" genera are not established as independent true feeding resources. ## 10. Independent Black Forest resource–activity source admission (2026-11-21)
