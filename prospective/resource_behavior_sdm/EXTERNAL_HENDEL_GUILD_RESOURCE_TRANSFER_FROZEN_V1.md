# Independent resource → bat-guild transfer source-gate: Hendel et al. 2024/2025

2026-11-21. **New independent observational original-source candidate**, distinct from Ducke 2018 and its published source. Publisher Dryad DOI [10.5061/dryad.xksn02vr1](https://doi.org/10.5061/dryad.xksn02vr1), paper [10.1111/1365-2664.14822](https://doi.org/10.1111/1365-2664.14822). 64 one-hectare Black Forest plots with independent vegetation/forest structure, moth abundance and richness, ground-beetle abundance and richness, and nocturnal acoustic activities SRE, MRE, Ppip, LRE.

**Question:** Does local availability/diversity of *measured insect prey* add heldout predictive information to separately measured bat acoustic guild activity beyond canopy/forest structure and seasonal survey conditions? This tests the **transfer of a general predictor principle across habitat, resource channel and detection method**, NOT numeric replication of Ducke's fruit-genus coefficient.

**Important contrast and timing:** Bats are classified by sonar-range guild, not food diet; raw acoustic activity intervals can overlap between guilds and are not necessarily mutually exclusive captured individuals. Moth light traps operated on **night two**, while bat acoustic activity scores use **nights one and three** to avoid artificial light confounding. Ground beetle data were collected in April-May, not exactly synchronous to May-August bats. Therefore do NOT combine moth count and ground beetle mean as one uncalibrated total biomass or infer contemporaneous feeding on measured insects. Use explicit date/site support flags before fitting.

**Frozen source intake:** Official publisher API dataset→current version→file list; exactly two published files `Hendel_etal_2024_Data_DRYAD.xlsx` and `README.md`, original author-provided digests. The previous Dryad `files/<id>/download` endpoint is known to return HTTP 401 in this environment; instead try once the Dryad **documented public canonical whole-dataset download endpoint** `api/v2/datasets/<doi>/download` (source: datadryad/dryad-app documentation). Verify original digests and byte counts before reading only XLSX sheet names/header. Do not open original numeric bat or prey responses, site identifiers or resource values in this gate. Exact source bytes stay transient; source metadata/STOP receipts only committed or archived.

**Pre-empirical comparison (requires qualified source rows):** prespecified site-balanced out-of-site predictions for per-guild **log1p(bat acoustic activity)**. Baseline uses time DOY, minimum temperature, elevation, canopy openness (`Opn_mean`), forest heterogeneity (`ndsm_sd`), and management intensity (`ForMI`) to reduce prey–forest confounding. Insect module A adds standardized `log1p(mothcount)` and/or moth species richness; module B, if comparable seasonal support established, additionally uses ground beetle counts. Compare baseline, moth biomass proxy, moth richness and both on same outer source sites; do not select endpoints post-hoc. If no independent geographic groups, do not claim geographically separated validation (LOSO is only within-source crossvalidation). No independent plot-level source point IDs committed.

**STOP:** Any source HTTP401 or other publisher file block; missing direct source XLSX raw values or joined support; no independent bat activity; missing covariates; source guild/detection incompatibility; data-only environmental predictor collinearity; post-outcome model selection; redirection to unauthenticated binary mirror. In particular, published SEM effects by Hendel et al. are source-prior art and may not be branded as new external field discoveries.

Original Ducke v4 outcome remains source-local within 46 Brazilian mist-net plots, 21 named plant genera and one unidentified label. No numerical transect-level fruit availability → German moth guild harmonization is possible: a new independent metric could at most test a **predictor improvement principle**, not a transported one-to-one bat resource SDM model. 3D bat flight GPS height in Hendel study is absent, hence full resource×behavior×height model still NOT ESTIMATED.
