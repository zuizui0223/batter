## Height-datum diagnostic fixed before reading numeric GPS Z

- Use only original valid RMC fixes (source XY and UTC time) plus matched quality-positive GGA records from the same original animal, acquisition time and XY, never guessed interpolation. Whole ZIP/source SHA pinned; do not execute MATLAB/scripts.
- The reference points are the independently provided 332 drone cactus lat/lon/alt in the second XLS sheet, cross-checked to the 327 surveyed cactus XY. Spatial thresholds are fixed 15 m and 50 m. Time eligibility: only 2016 published drone survey dates (2016-05-22 through 2016-06-07), and both UTC-date proxy and MST/UTC-7 day proxy are reported. No heights used to select eligible fixes.
- Report quantiles of raw GGA altitude minus nearest source drone-cactus alt, exact geoid separation field availability and unit, GGA HDOP/satellite/Q flags and source matched records. This arithmetic difference is NOT validated height above ground: the raw NMEA GGA altitude is a geoid-related reference while the drone alt datum/ground-vs-cactus-top semantics are unverified; subtraction may contain an arbitrary geoid or structure offset.
- Report 15m and 50m independently; report all valid positive-fix GGA without choosing a favorable quality threshold after viewing altitude; duplicates by bat+UTC fix+XY are collapsed. Never output animal IDs, field IDs, precise coordinate/date, original altitude arrays or raw GPS data to GitHub.
- STOP any 3D biological resource×height model if the ground surface, GGA ellipsoid/geoid reference, local sensor accuracy and date convention cannot be independently reconciled. Even a numerically plausible offset distribution is not a source-authenticated AGL. No post-outcome model tuning/threshold or use of published visits defined by flight height.

Source original methods: Goldshtein et al. 2020, Current Biology DOI 10.1016/j.cub.2020.07.079: author's AGL = measured ellipsoid altitude minus EGM2008 geoid minus Google Maps elevation; published 8m horizontal and 11m vertical GPS error. This sensor/QC operation does not reproduce the authors' original terrain correction. If its input source does not support reliable height, keep a potential 2D plant-resource×GPS analysis separate from a true 3D effect.
