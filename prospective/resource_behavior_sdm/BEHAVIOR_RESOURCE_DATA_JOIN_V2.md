# High-dimensional bat SDM v2: behavioral evidence and original-source join admission

2026-11-20 | prospective; source evidence migration; not a new bat model fit.

## A. Scientific upgrade: coarse behavior-mix is not enough

`batter`'s frozen exploratory tests already show individual vertical identity after speed-state matching **5/5**, speed×turning matching **5/5** and matched **2-km place × speed/turn 4/4 evaluable**. In the last family, *Eidolon* was STRUCTURAL STOP during outcome-blind preflight, **not an inferential negative or evidence of generality to five panels**. Three of four panels also retained signal after relative session-phase × kinematics; *P. hastatus* 2023 (wet season) failed at p=0.1068, not proof that season caused the loss.

These are **horizontal movement proxies**, not independent forage/commute/rest labels. The same source datasets and cohorts appear in multiple tests. Distinct analyses are not independent species-level replications and may not be combined as six confirmatory effect sizes. Full machine-readable data and exact source filenames: `behavioral_positive_extension_v2.json`.

**Consequent predictive priority:** Estimate `p(z | individual, XY, time, kinematics, measured environment)` rather than assuming `p(z | broad behavior)` loses all identity. Keep rival state-mixture and within-state policy components, but treat source-verified kinematic persistence as a constraint against a purely coarse-mixture account.

## C. Frozen eligibility gate before any new empirical coefficients

`source_join_gate_v2.py` checks all six candidates and emits complete source-specific STOP reasons: source/population match; original public bytes verified; same event/individual/time/XY join; calibrated height; independently measured and time-aligned food; independently classified behavior not extracted from held-out z; and detection/effort calibration. The `False` flag means **not yet independently VERIFIED**, not biological absence.

Do not use original records from different studies as if they share individual IDs; do not use 25-km GBIF co-records, LiDAR height, forest structure or Ficus scent as fruit availability; do not infer true source usage from missing detections.

## D. Proposed *single* empirical next gate

Acquire/read only **original Dryad author metadata** for the Rousettus 2020 Ficus manipulation, lock version/file MD5/byte sizes, then before opening raw visits determine which original columns can join individual×night×target-tree×treatment. If eligible, fit the isolated **2-D target-tree visit/timing model** with randomized-cue vs control and heldout independent bats/episodes; do not claim 3-D. Else explicitly STOP and prefer the already-source-linked noctule habitat × height interface as a descriptive, NON-food baseline. Neither route turns an external source into a 5-D fruit-bat SDM without truly matched food + height measurements.

Model ladder: abiotic R0 → generic vegetation R1 → documented resource R3 vs degree-rewired RN1 (true food only if observed); then speed/turn-state V0 → measured-task V1 → measured-food × state V2; score on **identical prespecified heldout events**, separately for XY occurrence and conditional z. Prior 49-fold weak partner shadow stays negative. Original ecological M3−M1/N1 and full 5-D field effect remain **NULL/NOT ESTIMATED**.
