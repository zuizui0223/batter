## Personal-history quantity

For target observation ordinals 3–10:

`R_it = D_other - D_self`

where:
- `D_self` is distance from the focal nightly strategy to the centroid of its own most recent up to five prior complete nights;
- `D_other` is the equal-donor mean distance to histories of other bats from the same Season and current treatment;
- larger positive R means stronger prediction by the focal bat's own past.

Each individual summary is:

`H_i = mean_t(R_it)`.

## Biological interpretation

The source paper establishes that the randomized early environment changed later outdoor behavioural expression.

This new endpoint asks a different question: did that manipulation also change how strongly later nightly behaviour was tied to the individual's own history?

Under the frozen estimator, **no supported treatment effect was detected**.

Therefore this experiment does not support the stronger causal statement:

> enriched early experience strengthens the personal-history carrier of specialization.

Nor does it support the reverse statement that impoverishment creates stronger behavioural lock-in.

## What remains informative

The negative treatment result narrows the mechanism.

Together with the independent Harten first-flight programme:

- personal history becomes informative extremely early;
- it remains informative later;
- its emergence is not a common slow monotonic ramp;
- the randomized enriched-versus-impoverished early environment did not detectably shift its strength in this aggregate nightly strategy endpoint.

This is compatible with a history carrier that is:
- established rapidly from a small number of experiences;
- robust to broad differences in early environmental complexity;
- additionally constrained by stable individual performance or predisposition;
- or generated by early stochastic symmetry breaking among several viable solutions.

The present result does not distinguish those remaining classes.

## Important ceiling

The public Rachum dataset does not contain the raw VesperStudio GPS trajectories.

Therefore this result concerns a multivariate nightly behavioural strategy, not route geometry.

It must not be described as:
- a route-memory treatment test;
- causal evidence for a cognitive map;
- evidence that early experience has no effect on behaviour;
- evidence of equivalence between treatments.
