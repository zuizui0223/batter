# Rapid lock-in maintenance synthesis v2

## Core question

JAE v0.4.0 establishes that persistent individual vertical strategies do not generally require continuing spatial partitioning.

The remaining question is:

> **What carries an individual strategy forward once other individuals are no longer needed to keep it spatially separate?**

The completed post-freeze and prospective evidence now favors a narrower architecture than the original generic “personal solution reuse” formulation.

## Current best hypothesis

> **A personal movement solution can be established rapidly from a small number of early experiences and then persist through history-dependent reuse of a familiar solution.**

This is **rapid lock-in / early symmetry breaking**, not a claim of irreversible canalization.

The carrier may include:
- learned spatial information;
- familiar landmark or approach sequences;
- reduced search cost;
- sensorimotor familiarity;
- task-specific knowledge;
- stable performance constraints that reinforce some solutions more than others.

The data do not identify a unique cognitive algorithm.

---

## 1. The adult JAE pattern requires a carrier, not an exclusive niche

The JAE system already shows:

- same-individual history remains informative across independent bouts;
- identity persists across multi-day and, where supported, week-scale lags;
- 500-m place × speed × turning matching does not remove individuality;
- same-night conspecifics generally do not substitute for self history;
- positive terrain-relative spatial segregation is not supported;
- synchronous co-use generally does not create additional vertical separation.

Therefore a persistent strategy can travel with the individual even when an exclusive vertical layer does not.

This separates:

`strategy persistence != spatial ownership != active avoidance`.

---

## 2. The first-flight data reject a common slow monotonic formation rule

The Harten 2020 first-flight programme supplies a real ontogenetic start boundary.

Frozen primary:
- 14 evaluable juveniles;
- programme-level experience slope/calibrated excess was positive;
- one-sided permutation p = 0.0102;
- but only **8/14** individual slopes were positive;
- the frozen >=70% consistency rule failed.

Therefore:

> a common gradual monotonic increase in personal-history predictability is **not supported**.

This matters mechanistically. The individuality seen in adults should not be narrated as a slow universal process of progressive canalization.

### What did survive

The predeclared late-history contrast was strong:
- late-history statistic = **+112.5554**;
- one-sided p = **0.0001**;
- **12/14** juveniles positive.

Descriptively, self-history advantage was already positive at the earliest estimable target day and remained positive through the analysed sequence, although not monotonically.

The formation window is therefore pushed toward the first few independent experiences rather than distributed smoothly across many later trips.

---

## 3. Randomized early environmental complexity does not explain the strength of the carrier

The independent Rachum 2025 experiment randomized juvenile bats, after baseline measurements, to enriched versus impoverished captive environments before wild release.

The source paper independently establishes that this manipulation changed later outdoor behavioural expression.

Our separately frozen history-carrier endpoint used the first ten Outdoor nights and a three-dimensional nightly strategy vector:
- time outside;
- maximum distance;
- explored area.

For every target night 3–10:

`R_it = distance to peer histories - distance to own history`.

Individual history strength:
`H_i = mean(R_it)`.

### Descriptive history dependence

Enriched:
- n = 8;
- mean H = **1.1476**;
- H > 0 in **8/8**.

Impoverished:
- n = 10;
- mean H = **0.4395**;
- H > 0 in **8/10**.

Mean R was positive at every target ordinal 3–10.

These are descriptive under the frozen contract; no post-hoc significance test of H itself is added.

### Randomized treatment test

Observed:

`T = H_enriched - H_impoverished = +0.7081`.

Season × Origin blocked randomization:
- 9,999 / 9,999 valid;
- two-sided p = **0.3244**.

Verdict:
**FAIL_TREATMENT_EFFECT**.

Thus broad enrichment versus impoverishment did not detectably alter the strength of personal-history dependence under this endpoint.

This does **not** mean experience is irrelevant. It means the carrier is not explained by this coarse early-environment manipulation.

---

## 4. Simple stable body size is not the carrier

Body mass was the only harmonized individual trait varying across all original reference panels.

The predeclared four-panel donor-gradient test asked whether, among other bats in the same 5-km place × speed × turning context, more similar body mass predicts better transfer of centered vertical organization.

Result:
**0/4 panels** supported the predicted mass gradient.

Panel mean target-level correlations:
- Hypsignathus: -0.108, p=0.107;
- P. hastatus 2022: -0.023, p=0.392;
- P. hastatus 2023: +0.028, p=0.565;
- P. hastatus 2016: +0.138, p=0.927.

Therefore simple animal mass is not supported as the stable trait generating the pattern.

Wing loading, wing shape, muscle condition and other unmeasured performance traits remain unresolved.

---

## 5. Simple fine-scale patch ownership is not the general carrier

The predeclared relationship between 1-km horizontal patch-fidelity excess and vertical identity failed:

- equal-panel mean Spearman rho = +0.1759;
- p = 0.0573;
- positive direction in 4/5 panels.

Sensitivities at 500 m and 2 km also failed.

Strong vertical individuality can therefore occur even when fine-scale horizontal patch fidelity is weak.

This weakens:

`repeat the same patch -> maintain the vertical strategy`

as a universal explanation.

It does not exclude exact tree, corridor, canopy-gap or task identity below the available resolution.

---

## 6. Shared nightly forcing and active avoidance are insufficient

Same-night conspecific histories generally do not replace self history, and contemporaneous co-use does not generally increase vertical separation.

Therefore the maintained information is not simply:
- “everyone reacts similarly to tonight”;
- “competitors continuously push one another apart”.

Individual environmental reaction norms remain unresolved because the frozen ERA5 continuous-wind gate stopped before outcome opening.

---

## 7. The remaining architecture

After these tests, four broad explanations have changed status.

### A. Slow developmental canalization

**Weakened / rejected as a common rule.**

The first-flight primary fails its individual-consistency requirement, while self-history information appears very early.

### B. Broad early environmental enrichment

**Not supported as a causal modifier of history-carrier strength.**

The randomized treatment primary fails.

### C. Simple body-size performance matching

**Not supported.**

0/4 body-mass donor gradients pass.

### D. Rapid route/task-specific history dependence

**Best surviving mechanism class.**

It naturally explains:
- very early self-history information;
- later persistence;
- overlap among individuals;
- absence of continued avoidance;
- failure of coarse patch fidelity;
- failure of a broad environmental-enrichment manipulation to determine carrier strength.

The precise carrier remains unidentified.

---

## 8. Mechanistic model: early stochastic symmetry breaking + reuse

Suppose a recurring task has several viable solutions.

All individuals can begin with similar expected preferences.

On the first few encounters:
- small differences in what is sampled;
- stochastic success;
- local sensory information;
- individual performance;
- encountered landmarks/resources

make one solution slightly cheaper to repeat.

If prior use lowers future effective cost, then:

`early choice -> increased reuse probability -> personal history -> persistent solution`.

The crucial feature is **positive feedback**, not exclusive occupancy.

Different animals can therefore develop different stable solutions even if:
- they began exchangeably;
- their final spatial distributions overlap;
- no individual owns a lane;
- no active traffic-control interaction is present.

### Revised temporal prediction

The empirical results argue against requiring a long smooth rise in individuality.

A better prediction is:

1. **rapid establishment** over the first few successful independent experiences;
2. **persistent but noisy reuse** thereafter;
3. abrupt reorganization when the task geometry is genuinely reset;
4. rapid restabilization onto a new personal solution after reset.

This is closer to an attractor / lock-in process than to continuous gradual strengthening.

---

## 9. Independent experimental precedent

Barchi et al. (2013) provides a useful mechanistic analogue in big brown bats.

Six bats developed individually stereotyped paths in a cluttered room. Once learned, bats retained the path when released from unfamiliar starting locations, and three bats tested one month later resumed their accustomed paths. When the obstacle geometry was mirror-reconfigured, the remaining bats rapidly established different stable paths.

This pattern is exactly the qualitative signature needed to distinguish:
- persistent learned solution reuse;
from
- immutable morphology-only routing.

It is external precedent, not a validation of the present field datasets.

---

## 10. Strong next discriminator

The highest-value next test is no longer another observational correlate.

It is a **known route/task reset**.

### Prediction under learned/history-dependent lock-in

Before reset:
- strong self-history advantage.

Immediately after reset:
- old-history predictive advantage drops;
- exploration / route dispersion rises.

With repeated exposure:
- self-history advantage rebuilds around a new solution.

### Prediction under stable morphology-only matching

After reset:
- the animal should select a new route constrained in a consistent direction by stable performance;
- individual ranking/geometry should transfer immediately across the changed task rather than requiring a rebuild from experience.

### Prediction under active competition

Route organization should track current competitor configuration more strongly than old personal history.

The key diagnostic signature is therefore:

> **old solution breaks at a true task reset, then a new personal solution rapidly re-forms.**

---

## Bottom line

The evidence no longer supports a generic statement that “individuals simply keep doing their own thing.”

A sharper current hypothesis is:

> **Individual specialization may be maintained by rapid history-dependent lock-in to a personally familiar solution. Once established, the solution can remain predictive without spatial exclusivity or continued competitor avoidance.**

The strongest unresolved causal distinction is now:

**learned route/task-specific familiarity vs unmeasured stable performance traits.**

A reset/reconfiguration experiment is the decisive next test.
