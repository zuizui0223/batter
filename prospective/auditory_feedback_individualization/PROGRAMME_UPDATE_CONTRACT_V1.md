## Outcome 1 — hearing adults are more differentiated

Requirements:
- exact two-sided p <= 0.05;
- D = V_hearing - V_deaf > 0.

Programme update:

> **Developmental auditory feedback causally contributes to the formation of adult individual vocal differentiation.**

This would show that a specific form of experience, not generic environmental enrichment, can increase individuality.

Synthesis:
- broad enrichment: no confirmed variance amplification;
- auditory feedback: mechanism-specific individualization support.

Do not generalize to movement.

## Outcome 2 — deaf adults are more differentiated

Requirements:
- exact two-sided p <= 0.05;
- D < 0.

Programme update:

> **Removing developmental auditory feedback increases adult vocal divergence, implying that normal feedback canalizes rather than simply creates individual phenotypes.**

This would make the formation principle:

[
\text{learning/feedback can constrain idiosyncrasy as well as create learned structure}.
]

Do not call increased divergence adaptive specialization.

## Outcome 3 — no supported difference in amount

Condition:
- exact two-sided p > 0.05.

Programme update:

> **Developmental auditory feedback is causally important for specific learned vocal features, but it does not show a detectable effect on the total amount of adult individual differentiation in the fixed whole-repertoire 28-feature phenotype.**

Together with randomized enrichment, this would strengthen:

> **large environmental effects on phenotype do not necessarily translate into changes in the amount of individuality.**
