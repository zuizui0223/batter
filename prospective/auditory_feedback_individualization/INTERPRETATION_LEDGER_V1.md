# Auditory-feedback individualization interpretation ledger v1

## Status

**FROZEN BEFORE NUMERIC PRIMARY OPENING.**

## Why two-sided

Auditory feedback could plausibly:
- increase individuality through individualized vocal learning;
or
- reduce individuality through socially guided convergence toward shared adult structure.

Therefore no directional primary is justified.

## Outcome A — HEARING_MORE_DIFFERENTIATED

Requirements:
- exact two-sided p <= 0.05;
- D > 0.

Programme interpretation:

> developmental auditory feedback causally increases the amount of adult vocal individualization beyond sex and common treatment-state differences.

This would provide the strongest public-data formation evidence in the programme.

## Outcome B — DEAF_MORE_DIFFERENTIATED

Requirements:
- exact two-sided p <= 0.05;
- D < 0.

Programme interpretation:

> developmental loss of auditory feedback causes greater divergence among adult vocal phenotypes.

Biological reading:
normal feedback may canalize socially learned vocal production rather than generate extra between-individual differentiation.

Do not call this adaptive specialization.

## Outcome C — NO_DIFFERENCE_IN_AMOUNT

Condition:
- p > 0.05.

Programme interpretation:

> auditory feedback changes specific learned vocal features, but does not show a detectable change in the overall amount of adult between-individual vocal differentiation under the frozen representation.

This would reinforce the distinction:

[
	ext{environment changes phenotype}

eq
	ext{environment changes amount of individuality}.
]

## Relation to randomized enrichment result

Rachum et al. 2025:
- enrichment D > 0;
- p = 0.167785;
- no confirmatory variance amplification.

If auditory-feedback primary is also unsupported:
two independent developmental manipulations would show that substantial environmental/learning effects need not translate into more or less total individuality.

If supported:
formation becomes mechanism-specific rather than a generic function of environmental richness.

## Same-source ceiling

After primary:
- no feature-wise identity rescue;
- no sex-only confirmatory subset;
- no acoustic-group rescue;
- no PCA/UMAP rescue;
- no use of published treatment-significant feature set as a new primary.

All downstream localization is descriptive unless opened in a new explicitly exploratory route.
