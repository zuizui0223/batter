# V5 Borneo locomotion contrast — source gate, 2026-11-27 JST

Prior results V4b: canopy guild recorded diversity larger in old secondary forest at 4 transects / 2 Amazonian sites; Whitworth V3: canopy species identity diversity declines with disturbance in one Peruvian reserve. Different metrics, disturbance and taxa; not a tested locomotor interaction. Preserve both.

## New source candidates (article/data availability verified)
- Haysom et al. 2021 DOI 10.3389/ffgc.2021.673071, source Zenodo 5018280. 25 logged / 25 unlogged locations with canopy/ground cameras (49 ground and 50 main canopy usable), 55 mammal taxa, 8008 detections, 15817 camera nights. Logged/unlogged each one forest; site, logging and sampling periods partly confounded. Additional canopy cameras separate. Original study already compared composition by forest and stratum; 2023 DOI 10.1111/btp.13248 uses same source, NOT independent. Potential within-mammal comparison of independently annotated locomotion (gliding versus non-gliding) if exact trait roster and sampling support; do not code arboreality from observed height.
- Yoh et al. 2023 DOI 10.1111/1365-2664.14398, Zenodo 7740421. 105576 bat passes, 21 phonic groups across forest disturbance gradient, airborne LiDAR. Article reports small overall logging response, distinct phonic-group responses, lowest plantation activity. Need verify detector heights, survey effort and structural data keys before claiming comparable vertical strata. If no height contrast, admit only as separate location-level bridge.
- Deere et al. 2020 DOI 10.1073/pnas.2001823117, Zenodo 4010757. 74 locations, 28 target mammal taxa, independent camera data with structural covariates, ground cameras at ~0.3m. NOT vertical-layer replication; potential structural-response bridge. Published forest-structure findings are prior art. LiDAR rasters Zenodo 4020697 intentionally excluded until overlap and need established.
- SAFE trait data Zenodo 7789416; verify trait definitions, independently measured mobility, body mass, provenance, and exact species name matches, not an assumed roster.
- Congo multi-strata mammal camera columns 2026 DOI 10.1093/jmammal/gyag047: three heights, 47 taxa, investigate data availability and disturbance support separately; no data claimed.

## V5 gate
Public Zenodo official API metadata and files only, no authentication or alternate private access. Local container DNS unavailable; web record 5018280 429, 7740421 unavailable. Use one branch-scoped workflow for public acquisition. Verify record IDs, file sizes, published checksum and complete schema; no raw records/locations in logs or artifacts, no inferred values, no bulk raster/video. Metadata/schema only, before choosing endpoint. One request per resource, no repetitive retry on access denial. Cap 20 MiB/file and 50 MiB/record; if oversized/unsupported record no numerical result. Need distinct station × layer × taxon counts or incidence, exact effort and treatment, independent site contrasts. Common species-identity Rao alpha and C as primary shared metrics; guild and continuous FD separately with fixed trait coverage gates. No interaction of locomotor mode with disturbance unless measurement and treatment common support and replicated sites, with taxon/source confounds addressed. Site-level leave-out, no pseudoreplication. For structural bridge, separate measured connectivity and gaps from canopy height/cover; no posthoc switching to favourable taxa. Prior sources not merged, main untouched, no manuscript submission.
