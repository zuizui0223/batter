## Decision
Mammal identity beta increase accompanies declining within-layer diversity and almost unchanged mean cross-layer identity distance; bat aggregate identity beta decrease accompanies declines in both with cross-layer decline larger; bat guild beta decrease accompanies increases in both with within-layer increase larger. The identity is algebra, not a new biological law. Hypothesis now targets direction of cross-layer distance separately from within-layer impoverishment; predefine jointly in new independent multi-stratum datasets with observed effort and traits, and retain actual ecological differences rather than force a common sign. Full mammal FD blocked on S4 label mapping and trait source. Bat point-scale replication blocked on original Yoh archive retrieval. No main merge or submission.
