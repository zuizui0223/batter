## Decision
Candidate local descriptive result is increased diversity of observed foraging-affinity guilds in secondary-forest canopy, not demonstrated functional loss or universal stratification response. Original article reported pronounced stratification and comparable activity across old secondary and primary forests; do not claim new demonstration of that. Higher observed guild diversity may be redistribution of activity/evenness, species composition or acoustic detectability; no measured structural simplification/causality. Next independent dataset should have replicated paired multilevel effort and forest-type or measured vertical-structure contrasts; prespecify separate within-layer, cross-layer and scale terms, including direction of canopy guild Q_C, before outcomes. Need independent forest disturbance study with verified continuous traits to generalize. Do not retune Yoh.
