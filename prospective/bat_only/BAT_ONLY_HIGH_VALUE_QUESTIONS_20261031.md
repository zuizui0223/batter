## Highest scientific novelty — when is a bat's neighbor's sonar a cost, information, or competitive weapon?

Individuality versus spatial overlap by itself is not a new causal mechanism. A bat-specific question asks which behavioral action follows a given acoustic conflict: relocate in 3D, change call timing, modify frequency, exploit another bat's signal, or jam a competitor. The new test must predict a switch based on receiver-side acoustic information, safe flight-route availability and separately characterized foraging competition.

**Crucial prior art (not new results):** Tadarida active jamming in food competition (Corcoran & Conner 2014, Science DOI 10.1126/science.1259512); Carollia timing shifts under maskers (Kiai et al. 2023, DOI 10.1038/s42003-023-05507-5); collective emergence and severe sonar masking (Goldshtein et al. 2025 PNAS DOI 10.1073/pnas.2407810122); Rhinolophus colony CF2 convergence in 101 unique individuals (Matsumoto et al. 2026 DOI 10.1007/s00359-026-01821-5); CF receiver silent-window predatory consequences in 6 bats (Yoshida et al. 2026 DOI 10.1038/s42003-026-10217-9). Eavesdropping and identity information in sonar are existing concepts (Barclay & Jacobs 2023 DOI 10.1139/cjz-2022-0121). These studies involve different species and measurements; do not pool them as one effect or announce a universal law.

**First decisive mechanistic experiment:** reuse the detailed previously written parent-branch design at prospective/colony_acoustic_identity/FOUR_ROUTE_OPPORTUNITY_X_MASKING_PERFORMANCE_DESIGN_V1.md. Independently randomize one vs four physically safe routes (R) and independently calibrated masker vs sham (M); score fixed early target/landing successes per biological bat. Primary interaction D = [success(4,mask)-success(1,mask)] - [success(4,sham)-success(1,sham)]. Positive D only if receiver masker exposure and physical route/echo changes are separately calibrated supports an ecological sensory-buffering effect. D alone cannot demonstrate social eavesdropping or active competitive jamming. First gate: non-animal acoustic bench, ethical protocol and design/power feasibility. No public dataset currently verified to include the whole stable-individual-ID x acoustic receiver x 3D route x success x randomized competition chain.

## Closest public-data ecological test — does a colony possess a route template shared by new individuals?

The 2024 Biology Letters Phyllostomus hastatus study (DOI 10.1098/rsbl.2024.0424) ALREADY established repeated colony-specific distant foraging and bypass of closer flowers. Mere colony site fidelity is not new.

Original Dryad 10.5061/dryad.5qfttdzgj lists Movebank 2016, 2021-22 and 2023 bat GPS, 3 colony identities, season/cave crosswalk, published HMM commuting/foraging classification scripts, 216 captures with 85 individuals in original movement analysis. Source-level availability is confirmed; the new reanalysis is NOT complete. Frozen field JAE uses parts of these original GPS series.

**Falsifiable prediction:** train a colony-specific destination / horizontal-route / supported vertical-state template in a past year, and test on different biological individuals in an untouched year. Compare with (a) other-colony pooled history with equivalent training opportunity, (b) independent resource/route cost availability if really recorded, and (c) own individual history where available. Use true colony × year × season × biological ID preflight before viewing prediction. HMM labels estimate movement states but are not verified actual fruit consumption. Re-use existing source-only preflight contract post_freeze_extensions/phast_colony_transfer_preflight/PREFLIGHT_CONTRACT_V1.md. This note does NOT claim the colony-year transfer gate passed.

**Stronger and potentially new:** if an independent fruiting-resource timeline exists, test whether an old colony route template keeps predicting choices even after a nearer, demonstrably suitable resource appears, and whether its lag has measurable extra travel/energy cost. Without independent availability data, the attractive 'cultural ecological trap' hypothesis remains untestable; the 2024 descriptive result is not a new discovery.

**Boundary:** predictive colony identity does not itself prove culture or social transmission; three caves of one species are not widespread bat generality; horizontal site/season confounds and tag-specific height errors must be audited.

## Third project — are turbines misleading bat social landmarks?

Nagy et al. 2026 Communications Biology DOI 10.1038/s42003-026-09882-7 found social song flights and 3D detections concentrated near turbines. Figure data on Figshare DOI 10.6084/m9.figshare.31112509 are openly listed, but original synchronized 3D tracks × ID × acoustic × fatality are NOT verified openly accessible. The causal next test needs independent manipulation of acoustic social cues versus landmark/food signals, not another turbine-activity correlation. Do not declare the ecological-trap cause discovered.
