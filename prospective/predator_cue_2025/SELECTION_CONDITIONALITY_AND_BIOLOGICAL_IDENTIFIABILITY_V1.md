# Selection-conditioned acoustic individuality — definitive source decision

**EMPIRICAL SOURCE STRUCTURE VERIFIED; NO NEW RESPONSE VALUE TEST.**

The 2025 Thyroptera tricolor dataset of Gamba-Rios et al. includes **117 tested bats from 33 groups**, but original author GitHub analytical CSV contains only **38 responders** (those that made at least one social call), each with a **single 5.5-min** playback sequence. It has exactly 6 source rows/bat: two sound presentations (predator / nonpredator) × three consecutive response windows.

The original analysis of before/during/after, signal guild and sound presentation order already appears in the journal article and authors' Analyses.R. No source-defined independent replicate episode exists for an individual bat in this CSV. This is a **structural STOP**, not a statistical failure or biological null.

## Why selection changes the ecological inference
Let Z_i be an indicator that bat i made **any** social call during its source experiment and Y_i,c,t be its count in context c and period t. The available 38-bat table conditions on Z=1, which is itself determined jointly by each bat's baseline inclination and its responses across presented acoustic conditions. This is a potential conditioning/collider issue if trying to infer population-level risk sensitivity from the 38 responders.

From published N=117, only 38/117≈0.3248 ever responded during the experiment; 79/117≈0.6752 made no response call under the tested protocol. This is **not** a population prevalence estimate outside the sampled site/conditions or proof of persistent silent individual specialization.

Under the strong assumption that excluded bats truly emitted zero count in **all** corresponding 30-s assay windows, an aggregate *unconditional trial mean* over all 117 would equal the responders' source-conditional trial mean multiplied by 38/117 **only when all 117 were assigned the same condition distribution, or correctly reweighted by their actual treatment allocations**. The CSV alone omits the 79 animals' individual stimulus assignments and group labels, so arm-specific means must not be "reconstructed" using a universal 38/117 scaling.

Two distinct ecological layers therefore require separate source support:
1. **Participation / zero-versus-any signaling** across all randomized source animals, accounting for stimulus assignments and group-of-origin.
2. **Conditional call intensity / suppression and recovery** among animals that signaled.

To claim individual reactivity *memory* or stable policy:
3. Independent replicated playback episodes per SAME physical bat across days, stable IDs, matched predator/nonpredator controls and valid source time ordering.

## Explicit action gates
- **CLOSED with existing released CSV**: unique stable bat IDs across independent predator bouts are not available (each bat had only one).
- **Possible but not authoritatively supported**: whole-cohort two-part hurdle analysis if all 117 original treatment and zero-response rows (including 79 excluded bats) are legitimately provided. Precommit hurdle estimand and group-cluster uncertainty before viewing response values.
- **Requires genuinely new independent repeated-exposure data**: cross-episode individual reactivity retention. Repeated within-trial before/during/after counts DO NOT substitute for multiple biologically independent encounters.
- **Outside this source**: original JAE's 3-D individual niche and compensatory spatial/acoustic mechanism; the dataset has no synchronized physical 3-D trajectories.

## Other real prior art
Kohles et al. 2020, Behavioral Ecology DOI 10.1093/beheco/araa056, already found individual acoustic signatures of Molossus molossus search calls and demonstrated playback individual discrimination in 19 marked bats. This provides an example of functional social recognition of existing vocal individuality, **not independent evidence that individual spatial flight routes are maintained acoustically**. Dryad DOI 10.5061/dryad.tqjq2bvvx; Zenodo 10.5281/zenodo.4953622. No new numeric analysis of this source has been carried out in batter here.
