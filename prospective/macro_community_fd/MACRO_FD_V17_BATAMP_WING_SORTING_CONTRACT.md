B includes species intercept contrasts and species x source/count-type contrasts; three standardized physical traits (AR,log WL,log mass; V4 roster scaling) crossed with six climate variables, absolute latitude, seasonal sine/cosine, year, mic height, common monitored nights, source/count type, GEDI RH98/cover/PAI, both area supports, and range membership difference (uninteracted). M adds log mass x bottom-half PAVD, W adds AR+log WL x PAVD to B, V adds all three to B. Training weighted standardization of pair predictor columns (scale only; do not center oriented differences, preserve label reversal), fixed ridge .1, no outcome-informed feature selection. N/B/M/W/V same evaluation rows. Primary V vs M (wing-specific incremental info after mass), must also beat B and N; M vs B and W vs B secondary.
