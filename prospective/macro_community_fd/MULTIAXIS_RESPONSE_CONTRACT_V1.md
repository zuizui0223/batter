# Multi-axis response partition V1 — 2026-11-28
Retrospective design, not preregistration of already observed results. Preserve all earlier pass/fail decisions. No main/manuscript changes.
Primary response families: paired observed-label relative logit slopes; detection-adjusted site-use slopes; conditional detection slopes; monitored detection slopes. Never pool these as a common ecological estimand. Separate R50, absolute 0–10 m PAVD fraction, and relative bottom-half PAVD fraction. No conversion without physical calibration. Record native grain, baseline, ridge, roster, source, unit and contrast.
Phase A: extract every available slope, including negative and failed-source results. Fold medians/ranges are descriptive, not sampling SEs; overlapping training folds cannot be independent studies. Ambiguous acoustic labels stay unresolved. Missing uncertainty is null, not zero. No meta-analysis admission without sampling covariance and a common contrast basis.
Phase B: replay pinned paired-label models with fixed ridge and support, resampling independent spatial blocks (and provider/route clusters as sensitivity); recompute weights, scaling and coefficients each draw. Export full joint covariance across taxa and axes, bootstrap convergence and support. Use orthonormal sum-zero contrasts within source, with source-specific centering matrix applied to latent species slopes. Do not equate penalized Hessian with sampling covariance. For occupancy use site-cluster refits and flag multimodal fits. No post-score model selection.
Phase C: within each compatible response family fit multivariate hierarchical model with traits, phylogenetic covariance, nonphylogenetic species residual and source-by-species deviations. Separate source and region only when crossed design supports identification. A source intercept is unidentifiable in sum-zero paired contrasts. Report uncertainty and prior sensitivity, not forced percentages. Compare held-out species/clades and regions against trait-free and phylogeny-free baselines.
Phase D: same-grid held-out prediction decomposition of climate, structure and lineage composition, with all eight subsets, signed commonality and block uncertainty. Known lineage composition is reconstruction, not independent prediction. Cross-scale validation requires predicted assemblages with calibrated observation model; do not use observed range composition as predicted occupancy.
