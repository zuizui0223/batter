## Potential assemblage analysis
Exact common roster >=20 species, >=5 genera, >=5 species whose acoustic vectors can actually be permuted within genus, no fixed range or environmental values from previous response model. Native ~96.5 km range cells in fixed European box (-12,40,35,72); >=5 joint species and >=30% trait coverage of all range-positive labels; >=100 eligible cells and >=15 2x2 native blocks. Distances: squared Euclidean on standardized AR, log WL (W) and log peak frequency, log duration (A); scales on full common roster, each distance divided by its global distinct-pair mean; body mass secondary. For each cell, K=mean(W*A)-mean(W)*mean(A) among unordered distinct species pairs. Equal-block aggregation at 2x2 and 4x4 native grains. Positive K means pairs far apart in one domain tend to be far apart in other; negative K means dimensions partly complementary. Global mean K alone is not functional complementarity or a measure of competition. Compare observed mean K to 4999 globally consistent acoustic whole-vector permutations (seed fixed 20261128), unrestricted and within genus; use the same incidence/range and wing data. Record within-genus mobility, null quantiles, ranks and effect. Primary two-sided within-genus contrast at both scales: observed outside [2.5,97.5]% of respective reference distributions, same sign of residual, all block deletion means retain sign at both grains. If not, close primary; no retune. Unrestricted only descriptive. Also report local trait dispersion and raw K for interpretation, not substitute tests. Permutations not independent regional data; grid cells overlap species, and genus conditioning isn't complete phylogenetic control.
