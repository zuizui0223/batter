## Estimand
Compare ALL unordered distinct units in the same provider and five-year period across the study domain, not only same 200km block. Do not equate provider-period with equal effort. Unit weights p,q over complete trait labels, uniform primary, distinct fine-cell incidence weighted sensitivity. Species total variation T=0.5 sum|p-q|; if T=0 preserve in census/mean T but exclude from conditional replacement metrics, report zero fraction. For T>0, l=max(p-q,0)/T and g=max(q-p,0)/T, disjoint and each sum1. Primary R=l'Dg, average between lost/gained recorded relative-mass pools, symmetric, not one-to-one species replacement, no temporal direction, no inferred interaction. Secondary C=-0.5 (l-g)'D(l-g), squared standardized centroid gap, can be zero with distinct distributions. Raw squared centroid difference equals T² C, verified; raw Q within-lost/gained and cross-distance identity C=R-(Q_l+Q_g)/2, verified. All four domains and identity T; no selecting favourable domain.
Pair geographic distance = WGS84 geodesic of nominal coarse-grid centers inverse-projected EPSG:6933 (source grid), not observed travel distances. Fixed bins 0–250,250–500,500–1000,>=1000km. Report bin means and support. Weight equal unordered 200km endpoint block-pairs, equal provider-period strata within block-pair, equal observation pairs within stratum. Never export IDs or coordinates. Main conditional metric on positive turnover pairs, all pairs T summary on same weighting rule; report number excluded and sensitivity weights. Require >=100 positive pairs, >=15 contributing providers, >=20 distinct unordered 200km block-pairs, >=15 distinct individual blocks, >=10 trait taxa. For bin result require >=20 positive pairs, >=3 providers, >=5 block-pairs; otherwise descriptive support only. Pooled-provider not primary, no independent source claim.
