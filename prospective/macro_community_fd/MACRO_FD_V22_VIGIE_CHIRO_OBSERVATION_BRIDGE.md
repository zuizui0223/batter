## Fixed admission before outcomes
Obtain authorized, anonymized event-by-detector metadata and event-by-taxon detections/non-detections with site ID (permitted spatial grain and precision), survey date/start/end, ground/canopy and numeric microphone height, device/microphone/model, deployment/orientation, classification method and confidence, survey effort, original source identifiers, and available forest-structure measurements. Limit primary time period to Apr 2019–Mar 2023 with GEDI observation support; identify synchronized site-night pairs differing >=5 m and comparable apparatus and effort, not merely same month; no reconstruction of masked coordinates or absent species. Verify the same survey pool at each height. Prespecified first gate >=60 sites, >=15 disjoint spatial blocks and >=10 nonoverlapping study/provider groups; >=5 matched taxa per comparison; audit independent wing/sensory trait sources before modeling. If unavailable or gate fails, stop. If admitted, separately freeze paired height allocation and within/between-layer FD decomposition, conditioning on monitored species pool and lineage; compare GEDI vertical allocation, canopy height, PAI and alternative profile metrics predeclared on physical meaning; include calibrated detector limitations and held source+region prediction. No assumption that this source passes any gate.
