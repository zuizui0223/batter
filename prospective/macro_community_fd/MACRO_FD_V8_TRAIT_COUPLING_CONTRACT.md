## Metric
For every cell draw an ordered pair of distinct matched species with equal probability. Let d= squared Euclidean difference of their three diet fractions (divide by fixed all-distinct-roster pair mean), s=1 if source stratum code differs, 0 otherwise. K=E_cell[d*s]−E_cell[d]E_cell[s]. Positive K means pairwise differences in diet and stratum co-occur beyond the product of local marginal pairwise differences; negative means pairwise differences tend to be in separate domains. It is NOT interaction strength, niche partitioning, complementarity, competition, or individual behavioral substitution. No arbitrary dominant-diet thresholds. Include all eligible cells including zero K or monomorphic traits. Secondary K for squared log body mass difference normalized by its own distinct-roster pair mean and stratum. Report raw marginal pair means, K, proportion monomorphic diet/stratum and mean genus expected. Exact algebra W_cell=(D−mean_d)*offdiagonal/(n(n−1)) over present pairs; K=sum W_cell*S. Region means equal geographic block weight then equal cell weight, six-region summary equal admitted region weight; 5x5 primary, 10x10 spatial weighting sensitivity, same cells. Overlapping blocks across boxes are not independent; no naive cell significance. Report region and block census, direction, leave-one-region sensitivity.
