## 1. Formation question

The mechanism programme asks:

> **How does a persistent individual movement strategy become personal in the first place?**

The adult bat results show that movement individuality can persist without continuing spatial segregation.
That creates a formation problem:

- is the persistent bias already fixed at the onset of independent movement?
- or is it progressively constructed from the individual's own history?

The current evidence now discriminates these more sharply.

---

## 2. Direct maternal-to-self crossover remains structurally unresolved

Source A was designed to test:

[
\text{maternal template}
\rightarrow
\text{personal independent history}.
]

The outcome-blind structural gate failed:
- structurally eligible pups = **0**;
- frozen minimum = **5**.

No route geometry was opened.

Therefore the direct maternal-versus-self crossover is:

**STOP_INSUFFICIENT_STRUCTURAL_SUPPORT**

This is not biological evidence against maternal seeding or personal learning.

## 3. Source B starts at the first outdoor-flight period

Harten et al. followed juvenile fruit bats from their first outdoor flights.

The frozen source bridge yields:
- 22 juvenile histories;
- 14 primary-complete juveniles with >=20 structurally valid movement days.

This archive therefore provides a genuine early-ontogenetic boundary that the adult field data lack.

---

## 4. Slow common canalization is not supported

The frozen formation primary asked whether self-history advantage rises monotonically with accumulated independent experience.

Observed:
- programme mean slope above exchangeability null;
- p = **0.0102**;
- but only **8/14** individual slopes positive;
- frozen requirement = **10/14**.

Verdict:

**FAIL_PRIMARY_FORMATION_RULE**

Therefore do not describe development as one common gradual ramp from weak individuality to strong individuality.

---

## 5. Yet later personal history is strongly informative

The predeclared late-history secondary is strongly supported:

- target valid days 11–20;
- programme self-history advantage = **+112.56**
- calibrated excess = **+112.74**
- p = **0.0001**
- 12/14 juveniles positive.

Thus by later early ontogeny, personal movement history carries strong predictive information about future spatial use.

---

## 6. New diagnostic: the earliest two valid days are not a stable adult-like seed

For late targets 11–20, use exactly two history days in both comparisons.

### Earliest history

Predict from the juvenile's first two structurally valid independent movement days.

Result:
- E = **+15.19**
- p = **0.1655**
- positive = **8/14**

Verdict:
**UNSUPPORTED_EARLY_SEED_PERSISTENCE**

Thus an adult-like stable personal spatial template is not already clearly recoverable from the earliest two valid movement days.

This weakens the simplest fixed-predisposition account.

---

## 7. Recent personal history contains much more identity information

For the same late targets, compare:
- first two valid days;
- immediately preceding two valid days.

Observed recent-minus-early gain:

[
Q = +144.08.
]

Calibration:
- null mean = **+0.35**
- null 95% interval = **[-47.61,+70.51]**
- p = **0.0001**
- positive juveniles = **10/14 = 71.4%**

Verdict:
**SUPPORTED_IDENTITY_SPECIFIC_REFINEMENT**

The whole-history permutation preserves common ontogenetic spatial expansion.

Therefore the gain is not merely because all juveniles become more experienced or range farther.

The gain depends on the true juvenile-to-own-history linkage.

---

## 8. Revised formation architecture

The evidence now favors:

[
\boxed{
\text{initial movement state}
\rightarrow
\text{individual-specific history updating}
\rightarrow
\text{strong personal movement bias}
}
]

rather than:

[
\text{fixed adult-like bias from the first independent movements}.
]

But development is heterogeneous.

The original monotonic primary fails because individuals do not all refine along the same smooth trajectory.

The process is better described as:
- rapid and/or episodic updating;
- individual-specific refinement timescales;
- possible task/destination-specific learning;
- interaction with stable morphology or performance.

---

## 9. Social seeding remains a cross-study hypothesis

Published work in the same fruit-bat system shows that mothers expose pups to destinations and routes before independence.

The current Source B result shows that later recent personal history becomes much more informative than the earliest valid personal history.

Together these are compatible with:

[
\boxed{
\text{socially supplied early information}
\rightarrow
\text{personal independent refinement}
\rightarrow
\text{persistent individual organization}
}
]

But the first arrow and second arrow are supported by different datasets.

Do not claim that this exact maternal-to-self crossover has been estimated directly.

---

## 10. Implication for the adult portable-policy result

The adult mechanism programme identifies a portable low-dimensional policy bias (\\theta_i).

The ontogenetic result changes how (\\theta_i) should be interpreted.

Do not assume:

[
\\theta_i = \text{immutable innate constant}.
]

A more plausible developmental representation is:

[
\\theta_i(t)
=
\\theta_i^{intrinsic}
+
h_i(t),
]

where:
- (\\theta_i^{intrinsic}) includes stable biomechanics/physiology/developmental predisposition;
- (h_i(t)) is accumulated personal movement history / learned sensorimotor organization.

The present data show that the predictive personal component changes materially with history.

They do not estimate the two terms separately.

---

## 11. Strongest current formation statement

> **Persistent movement individuality is not fully specified by the earliest observable independent movement. Personal spatial history becomes substantially more informative through development, indicating individual-specific refinement of the movement solution.**

This is stronger than:
- juveniles learn;
- routes become repeatable;
- adult individuals differ.

It identifies a shift in where predictive information resides.

---

## 12. Formation, maintenance and expression are separate problems

The programme should now explicitly distinguish:

### Formation
How personal information becomes individualized during development.

Current evidence:
**identity-specific refinement supported.**

### Maintenance
Whether the individualized state remains predictive through time/task changes.

Current evidence:
**portable personal bias strongly supported.**

### Expression
How strongly the stored state appears under current environmental conditions.

Current evidence:
**context-dependent expression supported/diagnosed in controlled sensory perturbation.**

### Spatial consequence
Whether personal policy produces spatial partitioning.

Current evidence:
**not required.**

This four-stage architecture is now more informative than one generic concept of "individual specialization."

---

## Bottom line

The ontogenetic evidence no longer points toward a fully fixed personal movement strategy present from the first independent movements.

Instead:

> **individual movement specialization appears to be substantially refined by the individual's own developing movement history, and the resulting personal bias can later become portable and persistent.**
