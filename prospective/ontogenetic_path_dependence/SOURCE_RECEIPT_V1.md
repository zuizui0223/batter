## Biological question

The JAE programme establishes that an individual's past remains informative about its later vertical strategy even when persistent spatial exclusion is not required.

The unresolved causal question is:

> **How does a personal movement solution become the individual's own solution in the first place?**

A valid test needs a real formation boundary. Adult deployment onset is not such a boundary. The present programme therefore uses published juvenile datasets that begin at or before independent navigation.

### Inherited source knowledge

The published study simultaneously tracked mothers and pups across the transition from non-volant pups to independent foraging.

The source study reports that:
- mothers repeatedly transported pups to particular trees;
- after independence, pups initially returned to sites to which they had been carried;
- early independent routes were similar to maternal routes;
- pups subsequently explored additional sites;
- pups were not observed independently following their mothers in flight.

These published findings define the biological motivation. They are not rediscovered or treated as new outcomes here.

### New question

The new endpoint is not whether maternal transport affects destination learning.

It is whether **route predictability changes source through ontogeny**:

> Does a pup's early independent route resemble its maternal-exposure template, while later routes become increasingly better predicted by the pup's own accumulated independent-flight history?

This is the primary formation test.

## Why these sources are admissible

The existing adult bat archive was explicitly stopped as a formation test because deployment day 1 did not correspond to first learning, first exploration, or entry into a new landscape.

These juvenile sources differ qualitatively:
- they contain an independently meaningful ontogenetic boundary;
- Source A contains mother-pup exposure structure before independence;
- Source B begins at the onset of outdoor independent movement.

Thus they can distinguish **formation** from mere repeated use of an already-established strategy.

## Data-opening firewall

Before any new route similarity, self-versus-mother score, route entropy, corridor width, or experience slope is computed:

1. retrieve the public dataset version pinned above;
2. inventory filenames and table schemas only;
3. identify individual ID, mother-pup linkage, timestamp, trip/flight identity, x-y coordinates, destination/resource identity, and independence status where available;
4. establish coordinate reference semantics;
5. establish a chronological first-independent-flight boundary from source-coded information;
6. count support without calculating route similarity.

If the required identities or chronological boundary cannot be reconstructed reproducibly, STOP.

No route endpoint may be opened merely because the published paper reports a related result.

---

## Claim ceiling

A positive result may support:

> personal route organization is formed through an ontogenetic transition in which inherited/socially supplied spatial information is progressively supplemented or replaced by the individual's own movement history.

It cannot by itself establish:
- a specific reinforcement-learning algorithm;
- a cognitive-map implementation;
- cultural transmission;
- fitness optimality;
- genetic inheritance;
- universal ontogeny across bats.
