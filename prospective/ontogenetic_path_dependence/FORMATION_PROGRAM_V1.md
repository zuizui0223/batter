# Ontogenetic path-dependence programme v1

## One-sentence question

> **Can individual specialization become self-maintaining because an animal's own accumulated experience becomes a better predictor of future movement than the spatial template from which it initially learned?**

## Why this follows from JAE v0.4.0

The JAE result separates three ecological quantities:

1. persistent individual strategy;
2. spatial segregation;
3. contemporaneous avoidance.

The first persists without requiring the second or third.

That leaves a maintenance problem:

> if other individuals do not need to keep pushing one another into separate space, what keeps an individual's strategy individual?

The adult tracking data localize the answer but cannot identify formation:
- self-history persists across days;
- place and broad kinematic state do not remove it;
- same-night conspecifics usually do not replace self-history;
- simple patch-fidelity strength is not a supported general driver;
- the archive contains no genuine first-learning boundary.

Therefore the next paper should observe **the transition from supplied/shared information to personal history**.

## Core causal architecture

### Stage 1 — information seeding

A juvenile receives spatial information from its mother or shared social environment.

This can determine:
- a destination;
- a landmark sequence;
- a broad movement sector;
- an initial route template.

### Stage 2 — independent sampling

The juvenile begins solving the same movement problem itself.

Independent trips provide its own:
- successes/failures;
- familiar landmarks;
- motor experience;
- local obstacle knowledge;
- route timing;
- repeated resource/task information.

### Stage 3 — personal refinement

If personal history carries maintenance, the information source predicting a future trip should shift:

`maternal/shared template -> own accumulated history`.

The spatial solution may remain overlapping with those of other bats.

No exclusive niche is required.

## Primary empirical signature

Source A asks for a **predictive-source crossover**:

early source knowledge is maternally seeded, but later held-out independent routes are better predicted by strictly prior self history than by the fixed pre-independence maternal template.

This is the direct empirical form of:

> **specialization maintained by fidelity to a learned solution rather than fidelity to an exclusive niche.**

## Independent corroboration

Source B asks whether self-history predictability rises from the first independent flights.

It distinguishes:
- early stable predisposition;
- experience-dependent refinement;
- a mixed architecture.

It cannot rescue Source A.

## Theoretical bridge

The decentralized-route model already proves that history-dependent reuse is sufficient to generate persistent individual non-exchangeability without spatial exclusion.

The ontogenetic programme tests the missing empirical link:

`history-dependent reuse is sufficient in theory`

becomes

`does predictive information actually migrate into the individual's own history during development?`

## Possible outcomes

### A. Maternal -> self crossover supported; self-predictability grows

Strongest path-dependence result.

Interpretation:
personal experience becomes the carrier maintaining the strategy.

### B. Maternal template remains dominant

Maintenance may reflect long-lived inherited/socially supplied route information rather than autonomous personal refinement.

### C. Strong individual differences appear immediately and do not grow

Stable performance/morphology/predisposition becomes more plausible.

### D. Mixed pattern

Initial maternal/social structure and stable individual constraints may be further refined by experience.

This is likely biologically realistic and is not treated as failure.

## What would be genuinely new

The novelty is not:
- bats learn routes;
- pups learn from mothers;
- individual routes are repeatable.

Those are already known.

The new contribution is the **change in the source of predictive information**:

> from a socially supplied template to an individual's own accumulated movement history.

That directly explains how overlapping personal strategies can become self-maintaining without continued spatial partitioning.
