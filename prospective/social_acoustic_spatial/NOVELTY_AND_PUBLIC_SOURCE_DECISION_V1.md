# Bat sensory × 3-D ecology: source availability and novelty decision (2026-10-08)

**EVIDENCE LEDGER, not a claim of a new ecological mechanism.**

## Strong statements already directly supported by published work

1. Individual FM bats *Miniopterus fuliginosus* broaden terminal frequency differences in four-bat experiments (Hase et al. 2018; n=19 biological bats across partly overlapping groups). The exact same FM species in a separate two-bat study exhibited little clear broadened call separation and MORE aligned trajectories than virtual dyads (Hase et al. 2021). Treat two-vs-four as an **across-study candidate regime difference** until paired within-source manipulation.
2. CF-FM horseshoe bats can do the opposite: 2012 paired-flight reference CF2 frequency distances commonly **decrease**. In the 2026 101-unique-bat longitudinal colony-mixing study, resting emitted CF2 frequencies converge over ~1 month (15 mixing events). These do not provide synchronized 3-D paths during group foraging.
3. CF Doppler compensation and a narrow "silent spectral window" have experimentally demonstrated task function: six bats' predatory takeoff declined to 37.5% under narrow noise above f_ref versus 100% no-noise and below-band conditions, in a randomized *perch* setup (Yoshida et al. 2026). This does not causally show colony CF2 convergence protects wild foraging or preserves individual 3-D spatial policies.
4. Published 2026 head–beam research supports species-specific sensory–motor coupling in the same FM and CF-FM bat taxa (Aoki et al. 2026). It does not directly demonstrate persistent individual context-response parameters.
5. Published 2026 eLife 2-D multi-agent sensorimotor model already shows acoustic temporal redundancy can rescue interference-impaired navigation (1 vs 10 recent calls: ~20% to ~87% exit success). Temporal integration is **not a new model invention** of this programme.
6. Published 2026 *Proc R Soc B* by Teshima et al. DOI 10.1098/rspb.2026.1463 directly analyses **the same Figshare 29209493** seven-arena *Rhinolophus/Miniopterus* data via VRNN and claims species-level latent flight regularities. The `batter` focal-bat identity diagnostic is not a new independent dataset, and its novelty cannot be "first evidence bats have latent flight rules".

## Open public data: what is structurally verified, not imagined

- Dryad 2018 `10.5061/dryad.4f99c46`: official API success; lone 614.45 MB `SoundFile.zip`; **no listed raw 3-D coordinates**; cannot compute within-bat acoustic/spatial covariance with this alone.
- Dryad 2022 `10.5061/dryad.v9s4mw6wn`: DOI referenced in published paper, canonical API path GET returned 404 in 2026-10-08 Actions. **No data-opening success or absence conclusion**.
- Mendeley 2026 `10.17632/mrnvkzrdsd.4` for *Mormoops*: web file listing has 3-D MAT and sonar tables; canonical v4 Mendeley API GET returned HTTP403 in source-pinned Actions `37749735084`. Stable bat-ID joins unverified.
- Mendeley 2026 `10.17632/4y98p5y8fc.1` for *R. nippon*: published open longitudinal 101-bat resting CF2 source, but **not synchronized individual group 3-D trajectories**. Does not by itself measure acoustic–spatial partitioning.
- Figshare original `29209493` 45 *Rhinolophus* flight CSV include `pulse` binary emissions synchronized to XYZ; earlier `RHINO_PULSE_IDENTITY_CONTRACT_V1.md` was frozen after the 3-D movement primary. No verified raw emitted terminal spectral frequency, sonar gaze beams or received-echo waveforms. **Do not conflate pulse timing with sonar frequency/quality.**

## Claims that must remain STOP

- **No newly verified cross-modal causal compensation**: cannot say bats causally replace spatial separation with spectral separation, CF frequency convergence, or pulse memory.
- **No unified FM vs CF evolutionary law** from different bat species under different apparatus and time scales.
- **No new confirmatory individual sonar identity evidence** from same five-bat post-primary pulse features unless the previously frozen diagnostic actually ran and passed its own decision; even then it remains same-source post-primary, not fresh replication.
- **No stable individual social-role response** from current Mormoops dataset absent native physical ID/time joined rows.
- **No adaptation / long-run fitness implication** from ability to navigate or predatory takeoff alone, much less the lab-to-wild vertical-niche bridge which already failed its 2/4 carrier gate.

## The narrow, scientifically distinctive next empirical target
The now well-motivated, but **unverified** statement is:

> At the same 3-D location and task, a bat's information-control policy can remain individually characteristic even when emitted call frequency becomes more similar to colony-mates; alternatively, a follower's spatial path can become more aligned while the source of navigational evidence becomes socially shared.

A successful novel test needs **simultaneous stable animal-ID × received/emitted acoustic signal × heading/3-D motion × role × functional task outcome**, with assigned acoustic/social perturbation and untouched held-out sessions. The mediator must be observed/experimentally isolated, not assumed.

If such a source is not obtainable, scientifically valid deliverables are:
- rigorously source-backed hypothesis/negative-evidence synthesis;
- public-data eligibility stop receipts;
- clear distinction of individual's movement-control style versus species-general VRNN latent regularity;
- a fully prospective protocol for causal testing.

**No more same-archive exploratory parameter search**. This decision does not preclude the frozen JAE manuscript's honest personal vertical strategy report, and must not amend it silently.
