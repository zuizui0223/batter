## Post-outcome evidence tiers

### Tier A — measurement diagnostics

Examples:
- exact/common 360-s harmonization;
- fixed-bin harmonization;
- support matching.

Purpose:
diagnose sensitivity of the frozen scalar result to observation architecture.

Allowed:
- show that the original 2023 scalar result is measurement-scale sensitive;
- motivate a better prospective measurement design.

Not allowed:
- reclassify the frozen 2023 panel;
- reopen the 3-of-4 field gate.

### Tier B — component / dimensionality diagnostics

Examples:
- horizontal-only and vertical-only persistence;
- bivariate H/V carrier;
- variance decomposition;
- breadth or reaction-norm follow-ups.

Purpose:
localize candidate structure after the scalar primary failed.

Allowed:
- support the hypothesis that wild movement individuality may be multidimensional;
- characterize exploratory within-archive structure.

Not allowed:
- substitute H/V for the frozen scalar FlightIntensity axis;
- count H/V as confirmatory evidence that the wild carrier exists under the preregistered programme;
- count later p-values as independent replication of the laboratory policy result.

### Tier C — outcome-selected bridge diagnostics

Examples:
- policy-to-shape analyses opened only in panels that passed the carrier screen;
- downstream policy-distance versus co-use analyses using post-outcome H/V coordinates.

Purpose:
ask conditional localization questions within already selected contexts.

Allowed:
- generate prospective hypotheses;
- show what does or does not covary within those selected contexts.

Not allowed:
- establish the general wild policy-to-shape bridge;
- rescue the failed frozen gate;
- be labelled "strong localization evidence" at programme level.

## Promotion rule

Programme-level summaries must obey:

[
\text{frozen preregistered gate}
>
\text{post-outcome measurement diagnostic}
>
\text{post-outcome component diagnostic}
>
\text{outcome-selected downstream diagnostic}.
]

A lower tier may refine interpretation but cannot reverse a higher-tier failure on the same data.

## Current programme statement

Allowed:

> **The preregistered wild scalar carrier gate failed. Post-outcome diagnostics suggest a candidate multidimensional H/V representation and motivate independent prospective testing, but the wild carrier-to-vertical-shape bridge remains unconfirmed.**

Not allowed:
- "wild H/V carrier is strongly supported" as a programme-level claim;
- "the field bridge is supported in carrier-positive panels" as confirmatory evidence;
- treating selected-panel p-values as if panel selection were outcome-blind.

## Independent evidence required for promotion

The H/V hypothesis can move into a confirmatory tier only through genuinely new information, for example:
- an independent field dataset;
- a prospectively frozen H/V test in previously unopened animals/sessions;
- a new experiment where the policy axes are fixed before outcomes are opened.
