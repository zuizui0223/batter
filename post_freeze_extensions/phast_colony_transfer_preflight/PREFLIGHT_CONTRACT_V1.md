# P. hastatus cross-year colony-transfer preflight v1

## Purpose

Determine whether the existing 2016, 2022 and 2023 public tracking panels contain repeatable exact study-site labels across years, sufficient to freeze a cross-year horizontal transfer test.

Only cohort/site identity, biological individual identity, session identity, timestamps, x-y support and counts may be summarized. No new vertical or cross-year spatial-similarity outcome is opened.

The intended future question, if structurally available, is:

> Does a colony's movement distribution transfer across years at coarse horizontal scale, while transfer decays toward fine scale where personal geography dominates?

This would distinguish a persistent colony-level destination landscape from fine individual spatial solutions.
