# Post-freeze mechanism synthesis v1

## Scope

This file synthesizes mechanism tests conducted **outside the frozen v0.3.8 submission programme**. It does not modify the submitted claim set.

## Starting ecological result

Across the five comparative panels, centered vertical-distribution identity survives coarse-horizontal standardization and removal of additive altitude level. The unresolved question is why individuals remain non-exchangeable in the organization of vertical space use.

## Mechanism tests completed

### 1. Simple fine-scale movement-patch fidelity

Prediction: individuals with stronger repeated use of the same fine horizontal patches should have stronger centered vertical identity.

Frozen result:
- equal-panel mean Spearman rho = **+0.176**
- one-sided permutation p = **0.05731**
- positive panel associations = **4/5**
- frozen verdict = **FAIL**
- *Eidolon* rho = **-0.522**

Interpretation: simple horizontal patch fidelity is not supported as a general driver. The strong *Eidolon* negative association is substantially between-cohort context rather than a stable within-cohort inverse rule.

### 2. Broad movement-intensity mixture

Three x-y-time speed states were selected before vertical outcome.

Result: **5/5 panels retain calibrated centered vertical identity** after self and other profiles are compared under identical 5-km cell × speed-state composition.

Calibrated excess:
- *Eidolon*: +0.316
- *Hypsignathus*: +0.135
- *P. hastatus* 2022: +0.128
- *P. hastatus* 2023: +0.060
- *P. hastatus* 2016: +0.547

Interpretation: stable differences in the proportion of slow/medium/fast movement states are insufficient as a general explanation.

### 3. Richer kinematic mixture: speed × turning

A 4-state speed-binary × turning-binary proxy was selected by x-y-time-only preflight before vertical outcome.

Result: **5/5 panels PASS again.**

Calibrated excess:
- *Eidolon*: **+0.335**, p=0.0002
- *Hypsignathus*: **+0.148**, p=0.0002
- *P. hastatus* 2022: **+0.110**, p=0.0002
- *P. hastatus* 2023: **+0.061**, p=0.0002
- *P. hastatus* 2016: **+0.526**, p=0.0064

Relative to speed-only conditioning, approximately 86–110% of calibrated excess remains across panels.

Interpretation: vertical individuality is not simply a consequence of individuals allocating different fractions of time to broad speed/tortuosity modes. It persists **within the same broad kinematic state**, relative to this harmonized proxy.

### 4. Fine-place × movement-state conditioning

A stricter test attempted to match individuals on fine horizontal place as well as movement state.

Outcome-blind feasibility result:
- 500 m: *Eidolon* 2/14 evaluable
- 1 km: *Eidolon* 2/14
- 2 km: *Eidolon* 2/14

No predeclared grid passed the all-panel support rule, so the vertical outcome was **not opened**.

Interpretation: current public data cannot distinguish five-panel fine-route/resource allocation from within-route individuality. This is an identifiability limit, not evidence against the fine-route hypothesis.

### 5. Available behavioural and individual covariates

No harmonized direct foraging/feeding/activity/behaviour label exists across all five GPS sources. The only common varying individual trait in all five reference datasets is **animal mass**.

Sex, life stage and reproductive condition cannot be a universal five-panel explanation because at least one panel lacks within-panel variation (notably *Hypsignathus*: all male/adult/mature).

## What the evidence now says

The simplest ecological explanation

`different horizontal patches or different broad movement-mode time budgets -> vertical individuality`

is not sufficient.

The current evidence is more consistent with a deeper form of non-exchangeability:

> **Individuals retain different vertical organization even when coarse place and broad horizontal movement mode are held comparable.**

Two live mechanisms remain unresolved:

1. **fine-route/resource allocation within coarse matched strata** — individuals may occupy different sub-kilometre routes, feeding locations, canopy openings or atmospheric opportunities that cannot be jointly matched in *Eidolon* with current sampling;
2. **within-context flight organization** — morphology, body mass/wing loading, experience, memory, learned route use or individual environmental response may generate vertical differences even within similar horizontal kinematic contexts.

## Next discriminating test

The only harmonized varying individual trait currently available across all five panels is **animal mass**. A prospective morphology test can ask whether similarity in body mass predicts similarity in centered vertical organization, preferably as a donor-transfer test within cohort rather than simply correlating body mass with identity strength.

Such a result would still be associative because wing area and direct wing loading are unavailable.

## Claim ceiling

None of these post-freeze tests proves foraging specialization, resource competition, morphology, memory, learning, personality or adaptation. The strongest new mechanism statement is:

> **Broad horizontal kinematic-state composition alone is insufficient to explain the five-panel vertical-individuality pattern.**
