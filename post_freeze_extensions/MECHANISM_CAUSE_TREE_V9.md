## General synthesis

Three spatial axes must be kept separate:

1. **horizontal fidelity**
2. **absolute vertical level**
3. **centered vertical-distribution shape**

The evidence supplies two complementary dissociations:

- **Tadarida:** absolute vertical individuality without centered-shape individuality.
- **Hipposideros:** strong horizontal routine without source-level centered-shape individuality.

Meanwhile, several original systems show centered vertical individuality even after coarse horizontal context is standardized.

Therefore:

> **individual spatial specialization is axis-specific / multidimensional rather than a single unitary behavioural-consistency trait.**

## Positive-system mechanism synthesis

Where centered vertical individuality exists, it can persist after increasingly rich measured-context matching.

This is **context-residual individuality**.

It is a local synthesis for positive systems, not a universal rule.

## Generality boundary

Prospective external replication is not established:
- Nyctalus: positive direction but frozen FAIL.
- Hipposideros: negative direction and frozen FAIL.

Thus centered vertical individuality is **recurrent but context-/taxon-dependent** across tested systems.

## Remaining causal questions

For positive systems:
- exact sub-50-m route/resource geometry;
- local atmosphere and environmental reaction norms;
- wing morphology;
- memory/experience.

For cross-system heterogeneity:
- what predicts whether centered vertical individuality appears at all?
