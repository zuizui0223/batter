# Strategy-maintenance synthesis v1

## Question

The completed analyses establish repeatable vertical individuality. The maintenance question is narrower:

> Why does the same individual return to a similar vertical solution across repeated movement bouts when it does not need to occupy an exclusive vertical layer?

This document synthesizes completed, already-opened evidence. It creates no new statistical test.

## 1. The maintained signal is not a one-night transient

Temporal self-history stress tests progressively separated the target session from the individual's training history.

| minimum self-history lag | evaluable panels | supported panels | calibrated excess |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| >=1 day | 4 | 4/4 | +0.060 to +0.534 |
| >=3 days | 3 | 3/3 | +0.057 to +0.154 |
| >=7 days | 2 | 2/2 | +0.100 to +0.165 |

The exact panel universes differ with lag, so effect magnitudes are not treated as a formal decay series. The important point is qualitative: every panel with sufficient pre-outcome support at 1, 3 and 7 days retained calibrated self-history information.

Therefore immediate carryover from the preceding night is insufficient as a general explanation. In the two systems with week-scale support, the individual component survives at least seven days.

## 2. It is not simply coarse place or broad movement-state fidelity

At 500-m horizontal resolution, after simultaneously matching speed x turning state, all four structurally evaluable fruit-bat panels retained centered vertical individuality:

- Hypsignathus: calibrated excess +0.384, p=0.0002;
- P. hastatus 2022: +0.0868, p=0.0002;
- P. hastatus 2023: +0.1015, p=0.0006;
- P. hastatus 2016: +0.451, p=0.0286.

Broad movement-intensity conditioning also retained individuality in all five comparative panels.

Thus a strategy is not maintained merely because an individual repeatedly enters the same >=500-m patch or repeatedly spends the same amount of time in coarse movement states.

Sub-500-m route, tree, canopy-gap and resource identity remain unresolved.

## 3. A shared nightly environment is not enough

Under 2-km place x kinematic matching, same-individual history outpredicted contemporaneous other bats on the same shifted calendar night in three of four structurally evaluable panels:

- Hypsignathus: calibrated excess +0.302, p=0.0002;
- P. hastatus 2022: +0.169, p=0.0002;
- P. hastatus 2023: +0.0822, p=0.0286;
- P. hastatus 2016: +0.564, p=0.0544, unresolved under the frozen threshold.

Calendar night is only a broad environment proxy, but these results rule out the simplest model in which all bats merely adopt the same vertical solution to that night's shared conditions.

## 4. Simple resource-patch fidelity is not a general maintenance rule

The predeclared cross-panel relation between patch-fidelity strength and calibrated vertical individuality did not pass:
- equal-panel mean Spearman rho = 0.176;
- one-sided p=0.0573;
- 4/5 panel directions positive, but Eidolon negative.

Thus repeatedly using a stable fine-scale patch may contribute in some systems, but its strength does not explain the comparative magnitude of vertical individuality as a general rule.

## 5. The strategies need not be maintained by contemporaneous competitor avoidance

Terrain-relative vertical fidelity is supported in all four structurally evaluable original fruit-bat panels, while positive terrain-relative added segregation S_rel is supported in none.

A synchronous same-cell co-use test found additional vertical separation in only P. hastatus 2023. In Hypsignathus, 2022 and 2016, dyad-resampling diagnostics placed the positive 95% upper compatibility endpoint at approximately 0.98, 1.92 and 1.13 m.

Therefore repeatable strategy maintenance does not generally require individuals to push one another into mutually exclusive vertical layers during co-presence.

## 6. Broad wind reaction norms remain unadjudicated, not negative

The ERA5 wind identifiability gate initially passed 3/4 panels. A stricter continuous-slope structural preflight, frozen before opening any numeric vertical response, retained:
- Hypsignathus 18 individuals (required 14);
- P. hastatus 2022 21 (required 17);
- P. hastatus 2023 7 (required 8).

Because the all-three rule failed, no vertical reaction-norm outcome was opened. The 2023 threshold was not lowered and no two-panel rescue was used.

Consequently, the current evidence neither supports nor rejects individual-specific wind reaction norms. It only shows that this archive cannot adjudicate them under the predeclared cross-panel design.

## Ecological synthesis: what may actually be maintained?

Taken together, the data are most naturally described as **persistent personal solution reuse** rather than ownership of a personal vertical niche.

The empirical object that persists has four properties:

1. **history-dependent** — another session from the same individual remains informative days to a week later;
2. **context-tolerant** — identity remains after coarse place and movement state are matched;
3. **non-exclusive** — different personal solutions can overlap strongly in vertical space;
4. **not reducible to a common nightly response** — contemporaneous conspecifics generally do not replace self-history as the better predictor.

This does not identify memory as the cause. It localizes the remaining maintenance mechanisms to processes capable of carrying information with the individual across contexts.

### Mechanism class A — information reuse / learned solution

An individual may repeatedly reuse known routes, approach geometries, resource-search sequences or sensorimotor solutions because switching requires exploration, learning or information acquisition.

This mechanism predicts persistence without mutual exclusion: several individuals can know different successful ways to solve the same movement problem, and those solutions may overlap spatially.

The week-scale persistence and self-history advantage are consistent with this class, but no direct memory manipulation exists here.

### Mechanism class B — persistent performance matching

Morphology, flight mechanics, age, reproductive state or other stable traits could make one region of the solution space consistently cheaper for a given individual.

This also predicts persistent non-exclusive strategies. Body mass / wing-loading attribution remains unresolved because cross-individual donor support was insufficient in three of four panels, so this class is not eliminated.

### Mechanism class C — fine task/resource specialization below the present matching scale

Individuals may repeatedly solve different microtasks inside the same 500-m cell: using different trees, canopy gaps, approach directions, prey layers or commuting corridors.

The 500-m stress test pushes this mechanism below the current spatial resolution but cannot remove it. Direct resource identity or sub-500-m flight geometry would be needed.

### Mechanism class D — individual environmental response rule

The maintained quantity could be a personal mapping from atmospheric context to movement rather than a fixed solution. ERA5 wind had promising support, but the predeclared continuous cross-panel test failed its structural gate by one 2023 individual before vertical opening. This mechanism therefore remains genuinely unresolved.

## Why would persistence be beneficial?

The current data cannot estimate fitness benefits, but the surviving mechanism classes share a common ecological logic: **reusing a sufficiently good personal solution can be cheaper than repeatedly searching the full solution space**.

A mobile animal repeatedly encounters structured tasks — reaching a feeding area, entering a canopy, commuting around topography, exploiting wind, or moving between social and feeding sites. If several workable 3-D solutions exist, selection does not require individuals to partition them mutually. Persistence can instead arise when experience, morphology or resource knowledge makes the expected payoff of the individual's known solution exceed the benefit of switching.

This generates the observed geometry:
- individual strategies persist;
- they need not be exclusive;
- co-presence need not increase separation;
- population-level use is a mixture of overlapping personal solutions.

The data therefore motivate **solution reuse under multiple viable movement options** as the maintenance framework, while leaving the proximate carrier of that reuse — learning, morphology, microresource fidelity or fine environmental reaction norms — unresolved.

## Prospective discriminator

A decisive study should manipulate or independently measure the information that differs among the remaining mechanisms:

- **resource relocation / turnover:** learned-resource solution reuse predicts strategy change when familiar resources move or disappear;
- **route novelty:** information-reuse predicts larger deviations or exploration costs in unfamiliar corridors;
- **morphology / wing loading:** performance matching predicts cross-context strategy from stable individual traits;
- **high-resolution airflow:** reaction-norm maintenance predicts transfer of individual-specific wind-response functions to held-out nights;
- **mapped trees / prey / social destinations:** microtask specialization predicts disappearance of identity after exact task identity is conditioned out.

The key future test is therefore not whether individuals remain different, but **what information must travel with the individual for its previous solution to remain predictive**.
