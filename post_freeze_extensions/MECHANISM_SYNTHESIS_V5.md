# Post-freeze mechanism synthesis v5

## Scope

All analyses here are **post-freeze exploratory extensions**. The frozen v0.3.8 JAE submission on `main` is unchanged.

## Starting rule

Five comparative panels retain calibrated individual identity in the centered shape of vertical space use after coarse 5-km horizontal occupancy and additive altitude level are controlled.

The mechanism question is:

> **Why do individuals remain non-exchangeable in how vertical-use probability is organized around their typical altitude?**

## Mechanisms weakened by direct evidence

### Broad horizontal kinematic mixture
- speed-state conditioning: 5/5 retain identity;
- speed × turning conditioning: 5/5 retain identity.

Thus broad differences in slow/fast and straight/tortuous movement-mode time budgets are insufficient as a general explanation.

### Horizontal route/patch allocation at >=2 km
Four structurally evaluable non-*Eidolon* panels all retain centered identity after 2-km place × speed×turn matching.

### Horizontal route/patch allocation at >=500 m
The same four panels all retain centered identity after 500-m place × speed×turn matching:
- *Hypsignathus*: +0.384, p=0.0002
- *P. hastatus* 2022: +0.0868, p=0.0002
- *P. hastatus* 2023: +0.1015, p=0.0006
- *P. hastatus* 2016: +0.451, p=0.0286

Thus route/patch allocation at scales >=500 m is insufficient in this subset.

### Horizontal route/patch allocation at >=250 m

An outcome-blind ultrafine preflight stopped the four-panel generalization because *P. hastatus* 2023 retained 7/11 where 8 were required. Three systems remained structurally evaluable at 250 m.

Those three all PASS:
- *Hypsignathus*: +0.3115, p=0.0002
- *P. hastatus* 2022: +0.0762, p=0.0002
- *P. hastatus* 2016: +0.4472, p=0.0160

**Inference:** in these three systems, route/resource allocation at scales >=250 m is insufficient. The unresolved spatial mechanism is pushed below 250 m there, and below 500 m in P2023.

### Simple movement-patch fidelity strength
Cross-panel mean rho = +0.176, permutation p=0.05731, primary FAIL.

### Relative tag burden
Cross-panel mean |rho| = 0.268, permutation p=0.1399, primary FAIL.

Thus simple monotonic payload burden is not a general technical explanation.

## Broad same-night environmental context

A four-panel test compared same-individual history against contemporaneous other bats on the same shifted night while matching 2-km place × speed×turn state.

Result:
- *Hypsignathus*: +0.302, p=0.0002 — PASS
- *P. hastatus* 2022: +0.169, p=0.0002 — PASS
- *P. hastatus* 2023: +0.0822, p=0.0286 — PASS
- *P. hastatus* 2016: +0.564, p=0.0544 — FAIL

Three of four panels pass. The 2016 panel is strongly positive but inferentially unresolved.

The same pipeline was independently reimplemented using cached session-level counts and reproduced all four panel statistics exactly.

**Inference:** broad calendar-night context is insufficient as a general explanation in the structurally evaluable subset. Exact local wind, uplift, temperature and microclimate remain unmeasured.

## Temporal stability: identity persists beyond short-lived state

### >=1 day

An outcome-blind lag preflight defined four structurally evaluable non-*Eidolon* panels.

When self-training was restricted to sessions at least one day from the target, **4/4 PASS**:
- *Hypsignathus*: +0.1483, p=0.0002
- *P. hastatus* 2022: +0.1135, p=0.0002
- *P. hastatus* 2023: +0.0604, p=0.0008
- *P. hastatus* 2016: +0.5341, p=0.0188

### >=3 days

Three panels had sufficient pre-outcome support at >=3 days. This was a stress-test refinement opened after partial positive >=1-day results.

**3/3 PASS**:
- *Hypsignathus*: +0.1539, p=0.0002
- *P. hastatus* 2022: +0.0959, p=0.0002
- *P. hastatus* 2023: +0.0573, p=0.0216

**Inference:** centered vertical organization contains a multi-day stable individual component in the structurally evaluable systems. It is not merely a same-night or adjacent-session state.

A >=7-day two-panel stress test is running only in the two systems with sufficient pre-outcome support; it will not be generalized beyond them.

## Temporal phase: apparent attenuation, but no support-matched attribution

Phase3 conditioning gave 3/4 PASS, with *P. hastatus* 2023 the only FAIL.

However, a post-hoc support-matched attribution test compared:
- phase-conditioned self-vs-other prediction;
- phase-collapsed self-vs-other prediction;

on exactly the same phase-supported target endpoints.

2023 result:
- paired phase increment = -0.04560
- null-centered increment = -0.00481
- one-sided p=0.3774
- attribution FAIL

**Inference:** the 2023 attenuation cannot be attributed specifically to temporal-phase information. Support loss, finite-sample variance and estimator sparsity remain viable explanations.

The wet-season temporal-allocation narrative is therefore hypothesis-generating only.

A prospective 2021 wet-season check was structurally unavailable under the original frozen cohort gate; no 2021 vertical outcome was opened.

## Morphology / body mass: simple mass similarity is not supported

Animal mass is the only candidate trait that varies in every comparative reference dataset.

A structurally frozen four-panel donor-transfer test asked whether other-individual profiles transfer better when donor and target body masses are more similar, within 5-km place × speed×turn contexts.

**0/4 panels support the predicted negative mass-distance gradient**:
- *Hypsignathus*: rho=-0.108, p=0.107
- *P. hastatus* 2022: rho=-0.023, p=0.392
- *P. hastatus* 2023: rho=+0.028, p=0.565
- *P. hastatus* 2016: rho=+0.138, p=0.927

**Inference:** simple body mass is not supported as a general predictor of vertical-organization transfer.

A stricter 500-m body-mass donor-support preflight was then stopped before vertical outcome because three of four panels fell below the frozen support gate:
- *Hypsignathus*: 9/19; 14 required
- P2022: 7/23; 17 required
- P2023: 5/11; 8 required
- P2016: 9/9; 7 required

Thus body mass itself is weakened, while wing loading, wing shape, body condition and other morphology remain untested.

## Structural identifiability boundary: Eidolon

*Eidolon* repeatedly marks the strongest overlap limitation:
- 500 m / 1 km / 2 km place × speed×turn matching: only 1/12 evaluable;
- five-panel same-night preflight: 8/12 where 9 required;
- temporal phase3: 4/12; phase2: 7/12;
- close/far body-mass transfer: 3 targets where 9 required;
- continuous body-mass gradient: 1/12 where 9 required.

Strong vertical individuality therefore coexists with sparse cross-individual fine-scale horizontal overlap.

## Current causal localization

For four structurally evaluable non-*Eidolon* panels, the unresolved mechanism has been pushed below:
- 500 m horizontal place;
- broad speed/turning state.

For three of those four, it also persists beyond broad same-night context under 2-km place × kinematic matching.

The strongest live mechanisms are now:

1. **sub-500-m exact route/resource/microhabitat allocation**
   - exact feeding trees;
   - flight corridors;
   - canopy gaps;
   - local topography;
   - microclimatic exposure;

2. **within-context individual flight organization**
   - wing morphology / wing loading;
   - experience;
   - memory;
   - learned movement routines;
   - individual route choice;

3. **fine-scale individual environmental reaction norms**
   - individual-specific responses to local wind/uplift/microclimate.

Temporal scheduling remains possible but is not supported as the cause of the 2023 attenuation.

## Strongest current statements

Five-panel level:

> **Broad horizontal kinematic-state composition alone is insufficient to explain the comparative vertical-individuality pattern.**

Four-panel structurally evaluable subset:

> **Individual vertical organization persists after simultaneously matching horizontal place to 500 m and broad speed × turning state. In three of four panels, same-individual history also remains more informative than contemporaneous other bats on the same night under 2-km place × kinematic matching.**

## What the archive can no longer decide cleanly

The remaining mechanisms require cross-individual observations in the same sub-500-m context. Existing public data become too sparse for:
- exact route/resource matching;
- body-mass donor transfer under strict 500-m matching;
- fine environmental reaction-norm identification.

Further threshold relaxation would trade identification for post-hoc convenience and is therefore not used.

## Claim ceiling

Not established:
- exact resource/feeding-tree causation;
- sub-500-m route causation;
- body mass or wing-loading causation;
- learning, memory or personality;
- temporal-phase causation;
- season causation;
- individual atmospheric reaction norms;
- fine-place mechanism generalization to *Eidolon*.
