## Strongest current ecological statement

> **Repeatable vertical individuality is not well explained by measured context allocation alone. Across multiple systems, same-individual history retains predictive information after increasingly rich matching of place, broad movement state, time, central-place stage and source-defined habitat context.**

## Strongest current mechanism statement

> **The evidence supports context-residual individual organization, but does not identify whether the residual substrate is exact resource/route use, fine atmospheric reaction norms, detailed morphology, memory/experience, or a mixture of these.**

## Reporting prohibitions

Do not write:
- Nyctalus prospectively replicated the original result;
- all bats possess a universal centered-shape phenotype;
- exact resource effects have been removed;
- morphology, memory, learning or personality has been demonstrated;
- richer context causally increases individuality.

## Programme closure

No additional same-source Nyctalus variants are authorized. Further mechanism progress requires new information.
