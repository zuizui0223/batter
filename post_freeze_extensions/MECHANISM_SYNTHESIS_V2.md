# Post-freeze mechanism synthesis v2

## Scope

All analyses summarized here are **post-freeze exploratory extensions**. They do not modify the frozen v0.3.8 Journal of Animal Ecology submission claim set.

## Starting result

Five comparative panels retain calibrated individual identity in the **centered shape of vertical space use** after:
1. self and other predictors are evaluated under the same coarse 5-km horizontal occupancy; and
2. every session is translated to zero median altitude.

The mechanism question is therefore no longer “do individuals differ in mean height?” but:

> **Why do individuals remain non-exchangeable in how vertical-use probability is organized around their typical altitude?**

## Mechanism evidence

### A. Explanations weakened by direct tests

#### Coarse horizontal occupancy
Already controlled in the frozen paper estimator.

**Status:** insufficient as a general explanation.

#### Additive altitude level / constant tag zero point
Session-centering leaves centered-shape identity in all five comparative panels.

**Status:** insufficient as a general explanation.

#### Broad movement-intensity mixture
A three-state x-y-time speed proxy was selected before vertical output. Identity persisted in **5/5 panels** after self and other predictors were compared under identical 5-km cell × speed-state composition.

Calibrated excess:
- *Eidolon*: +0.316
- *Hypsignathus*: +0.135
- *P. hastatus* 2022: +0.128
- *P. hastatus* 2023: +0.060
- *P. hastatus* 2016: +0.547

**Status:** insufficient as a general explanation.

#### Richer kinematic mixture
A four-state speed-binary × turning-binary proxy was chosen by x-y-time-only preflight.

Identity again persisted in **5/5 panels**:
- *Eidolon*: +0.335, p=0.0002
- *Hypsignathus*: +0.148, p=0.0002
- *P. hastatus* 2022: +0.110, p=0.0002
- *P. hastatus* 2023: +0.061, p=0.0002
- *P. hastatus* 2016: +0.526, p=0.0064

Approximately 86–110% of the speed-only calibrated excess remains descriptively.

**Status:** broad speed/tortuosity time-budget differences are insufficient as a general explanation.

#### Simple fine-scale movement-patch fidelity
Prediction: stronger repeated use of the same fine horizontal patches should accompany stronger centered vertical identity.

Frozen primary result:
- equal-panel mean Spearman rho = +0.176
- one-sided permutation p = 0.05731
- positive associations = 4/5
- *Eidolon* rho = -0.522
- primary verdict = FAIL

**Status:** not supported as a general driver.

### B. Explanations partially constrained but not identified

#### Shared calendar-night context
In focal *Tadarida*, another night from the **same individual** outpredicts contemporaneous other bats for most evaluable animals:
- AGL equal-individual mean self-vs-same-night gain = +0.484 nats/fix, 4/5 positive;
- MSL = +0.436, 4/5 positive.

Thus the simplest “everyone responds similarly to tonight's conditions” explanation is weak in that focal system.

A five-comparative-panel x-y-time preflight was then frozen. Four panels passed structural support, but *Eidolon* retained 8/12 targets while the predeclared minimum was 9. The comparative vertical outcome was therefore not opened.

**Status:** focal evidence argues against a shared-night-only explanation; comparative generality remains structurally unidentifiable.

#### Shared uplift reaction norm in *Tadarida*
A separately frozen common-uplift reaction-norm analysis did not establish a shared slope mechanism (permutation p=0.334).

**Status:** one common uplift-response slope is insufficient for the focal pattern; atmospheric effects are not ruled out.

#### Central-place/end-point structure
Endpoint-neighbourhood exclusion passes in four comparative panels, fails inferentially in *Tadarida*, and is underpowered in *Eidolon*.

**Status:** panel-dependent contributor, not a universal explanation and not universally removed.

### C. Live hypotheses that current archives cannot separate

#### Fine-route/resource allocation
A 500 m → 1 km → 2 km fine-place × kinematic-state preflight was frozen before vertical outcome.

At every scale *Eidolon* retained only **2/14** state-conditioned individuals; other panels retained substantially more support (e.g. at 2 km: 22/24, 31/33, 14/14, 10/10).

No fine-place vertical outcome was opened.

**Status:** viable and unidentifiable in the five-panel archive.

#### Body mass / morphology
Animal mass is the only candidate individual trait that varies in all five reference datasets.

Two prospective mass-transfer designs were frozen before vertical output:
1. close-mass vs far-mass donor pools — *Eidolon* retained 3 targets, 9 required;
2. continuous donor-target mass-distance gradient — *Eidolon* retained 1/12, 9 required.

Other four panels were structurally well supported.

**Status:** body mass remains unresolved; five-panel morphology transfer is blocked by *Eidolon* cross-individual support.

## Key inference

The evidence no longer fits the simplest behavioural-mixture story:

`different coarse places or different broad movement-mode time budgets -> vertical individuality`.

Instead:

> **individual vertical organization persists within comparable coarse horizontal and broad kinematic contexts.**

That statement is empirically supported across all five comparative panels.

The remaining causal fork is:

1. **fine-route/resource allocation:** individuals occupy different sub-kilometre routes, feeding patches, canopy openings or atmospheric opportunities even within the same 5-km cell and broad movement state;
2. **within-context flight organization:** individuals differ vertically within closely matched movement contexts because of morphology, experience, memory, learned route use, resource choice or individual environmental reaction norms.

Current public archives cannot separate these pathways because the panel that most strongly limits matching—*Eidolon*—also contains strong vertical individuality but sparse cross-individual fine-scale overlap.

## Claim ceiling

Supported post-freeze mechanism statement:

> **Broad horizontal kinematic-state composition alone is insufficient to explain the five-panel vertical-individuality pattern.**

Not established:
- fine-resource or route causation;
- body-mass or wing-loading causation;
- individual atmospheric reaction norms across the comparative panels;
- learning, memory, personality, adaptation or fitness effects.
