## Ecological interpretation

However, *P. hastatus* 2023 is a biologically informative exception. It retained n=14 yet its calibrated excess fell to +0.022 and no longer exceeded the frozen permutation threshold. Thus the 2023 signal is consistent with being substantially organized through **when during the tracked session particular place × kinematic contexts are used**, rather than through persistent within-phase vertical organization.

The source study identifies 2016 and 2022 tracking as dry-season sampling and 2023 as wet-season sampling. This makes a resource-season × temporal-allocation mechanism a testable hypothesis, not a demonstrated seasonal cause.

The 2023 endpoint-neighbourhood exclusion in v0.3.8 independently passes (calibrated excess +0.1524, p=0.0002), so the temporal pattern is not reducible to the previously tested 1-km session-endpoint neighbourhood alone.
