## Interpretation

Among other individuals observed in the same 5-km place × speed×turn context, **greater body-mass similarity does not generally predict better transfer of centered vertical organization**.

Thus simple body mass is not supported as the stable individual trait generating the comparative vertical-individuality pattern.

This does not rule out wing loading, wing shape, muscle condition, age, experience or other stable individual traits. Animal mass is an incomplete morphology proxy.

A stricter 500-m body-mass donor-gradient family was separately stopped before vertical outcome because three of four panels lacked enough cross-individual donor support.
