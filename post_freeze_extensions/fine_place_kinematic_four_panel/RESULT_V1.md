## Ecological interpretation

Within these four structurally evaluable panels, **2-km horizontal route/patch allocation plus broad speed×turning-state composition is insufficient as a general explanation** of centered vertical individuality.

The same individual's vertical profile remains more informative than other individuals' profiles after both predictors are standardized to the same:
- 2-km horizontal place distribution; and
- broad horizontal kinematic-state distribution.

The result therefore pushes the live mechanism below the 2-km / broad-kinematic scale.

## Generalization boundary

This result does **not** apply to *Eidolon helvum*. In the outcome-blind matched preflight, *Eidolon* retained only 1/12 individuals at 2 km and its fine-place vertical outcome was not opened.

A 2-km cell is also not an exact route, feeding tree, resource patch or microclimatic exposure. Sub-2-km route/resource allocation remains possible.

No claim of morphology, memory, learning or intrinsic personality follows from this result.
