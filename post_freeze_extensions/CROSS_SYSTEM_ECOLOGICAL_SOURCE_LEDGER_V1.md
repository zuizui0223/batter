# Cross-system ecological source ledger v1

## Status

**POST-HOC ECOLOGICAL SYNTHESIS. NOT A CONFIRMATORY PREDICTOR ANALYSIS.**

This ledger records independently documented natural-history features for the unique taxa represented in the opened vertical-individuality programme. It is intended to keep ecological interpretation source-based and to prevent retrospective recoding to maximize agreement with vertical outcomes.

No numeric ecological score is fitted here.

## Mechanistic dimensions of interest

### Resource anchoring
Does the foraging task repeatedly involve identifiable persistent points/patches/areas, rather than primarily tracking mobile prey?

### Vertical opportunity
Does the foraging environment plausibly allow repeated choice among vertical layers/approach geometries, or constrain feeding close to one surface/layer?

These are preferred over a crude plant-versus-animal diet binary.

## Why the working hypothesis is not “plant feeding”

Three observations prevent that simplification.

1. *Phyllostomus hastatus* is genuinely omnivorous and may consume substantial insect prey, yet it is positive.
2. *Hipposideros* can show repeatable horizontal foraging-site use while the tested centered-vertical source result is negative.
3. *Myotis vivesi* offers a clear physical constraint: marine prey capture occurs near the water surface, limiting vertical freedom independently of whether individual routines are stable.

The more mechanistic contrast is therefore:

> **Does the ecological task repeatedly present stable spatial/resource anchors together with enough vertical opportunity for individuals to develop different repeatable solutions?**

## Generated causal graph

A future test should distinguish:

`resource persistence / anchoring -> repeatable route & approach geometry -> centered vertical individuality`

from competing pathways:

- `wing morphology -> flight envelope -> centered vertical individuality`
- `habitat vertical complexity -> vertical opportunity -> centered vertical individuality`
- `nightly atmospheric forcing -> transient prey/flight layers -> weaker temporal self-transfer`
- `fine terrain fidelity -> MSL shape identity` (technical/ecological leakage pathway)
- phylogeny/sensory ecology as cross-species covariance.

## Current prediction

For genuinely new, outcome-unopened sources:

> centered vertical-individuality effect size should be larger where foraging repeatedly uses persistent spatial resources in vertically structured environments, and smaller where prey is mobile/ephemeral or feeding is constrained close to a single surface/layer.

The prediction must be operationalized before opening future vertical outcomes.

## Reporting boundary
