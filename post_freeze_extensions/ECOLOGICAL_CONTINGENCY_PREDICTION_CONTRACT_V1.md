# Ecological contingency prediction contract v1

## Status

**FROZEN AFTER THE CURRENT ORIGINAL/EXTERNAL OUTCOMES ARE KNOWN.**

This contract does not convert the current post-hoc ecological split into confirmation. It fixes what the generated hypothesis predicts for future, genuinely unopened source systems.

## Generated hypothesis

Stable centered vertical individuality should be stronger when animals repeatedly face the **same ecological task** and that task permits **multiple reusable vertical solutions**.

The two coding dimensions operationalize those requirements:

1. **persistent spatial resource anchoring** increases the chance that the same foraging problem recurs across visits;
2. **repeated vertical opportunity** increases the number of alternative heights, layers, approach geometries or routes through which that recurring problem can be solved.

Thus, in this contract, **ecological opportunity means the repeated availability of alternative solutions to the same task**. The hypothesis is allowed to fail.

## Confirmatory universe

Only future independent bat source systems may enter the confirmatory test.

A source is confirmatory only if:
- its numeric vertical outcome has not previously been opened in this project;
- its source admission and structural eligibility are frozen before numeric vertical opening;
- its ecological predictor coding is frozen before numeric vertical opening;
- it provides an event-level native vertical field and the repeated-session support required by the future estimator contract.

All currently opened systems, including *Tadarida*, *Eidolon*, *Hypsignathus*, *Phyllostomus*, *Nyctalus*, *Hipposideros*, *Myotis vivesi* and *Pteropus poliocephalus*, are excluded from the confirmatory sample. They may be shown descriptively only.

## Primary ecological coding

Coding must use natural-history information independent of the tracking outcome.

### Resource anchoring score A

A measures how strongly the ecological task is expected to **recur in a comparable spatial context**.

- **0 — mobile/ephemeral:** the foraging target itself moves or appears transiently at the spatial scale of nightly foraging, so the spatial problem is likely to change from visit to visit.
- **1 — mixed/intermediate:** both persistent and mobile resources are important, or resources persist as patches without creating a strongly repeated local task.
- **2 — strongly anchored:** foraging is organized around persistent point or patch resources that can be revisited across nights, such as repeatedly used feeding trees, flowers, fixed feeding areas or equivalent stable resources.

### Vertical opportunity score V

V measures how many **reusable vertical solutions** the recurring task plausibly permits.

- **0 — strongly constrained:** effective foraging is restricted close to one physical surface or narrow layer, leaving little scope for alternative vertical solutions.
- **1 — intermediate:** vertical freedom exists, but the task offers only limited or weakly structured alternatives.
- **2 — strongly structured:** the same task can repeatedly be solved using distinct resource layers, approach heights, routes or habitat strata.

### Primary hypothesis score

`H = (A / 2) * (V / 2)`, ranging from 0 to 1.

No alternative recoding, weighting or composite may replace H after future vertical outcomes are opened.

## Coding procedure

For each future source:
1. two coders independently score A and V from publications/source metadata without access to numeric vertical outcomes;
2. each coder records citations and a short rationale;
3. disagreements are adjudicated before vertical opening;
4. the final A, V and H values are committed with the source-admission receipt.

Tracking-derived horizontal individuality must not be used to define A or V.

## Primary response

The response is the source-level continuous null-calibrated centered-vertical effect:

`E = observed centered common-cell identity - source-specific whole-session permutation-null mean`.

Use the same session-centering principle that removes absolute altitude level and the same self-versus-other common-horizontal-support logic as the integrated manuscript.

PASS/FAIL is not the primary comparative response.

## Minimum confirmatory sample

- fewer than **8** future independent sources: descriptive accumulation only; no confirmatory ecological test;
- at **8 or more** sources: open the primary test;
- additional predictor families may not be promoted into the primary model after seeing E.

## Primary test

Primary statistic: one-sided Spearman rank correlation between H and E across future independent sources.

Prediction:
`rho(H, E) > 0`.

Inference:
- exact or Monte Carlo permutation of source-level E values across the frozen H values;
- at least 99,999 permutations when exact enumeration is unavailable;
- primary support requires one-sided p <= 0.05 and positive rho.

Report rho, permutation interval/null distribution, all source-level H and E values, and every admitted failure.

## Predeclared competing explanations

These do not replace the primary test.

If at least 12 future sources have harmonized measurements, secondary models may evaluate:
- wing loading;
- aspect ratio;
- body mass;
- habitat vertical complexity;
- environmental forcing.

If phylogenetic coverage is adequate, phylogenetic sensitivity may be added as a secondary analysis, not used to redefine the primary hypothesis after outcome.

## Falsification / weakening conditions

The generated hypothesis is weakened if:
- the frozen primary rho is <= 0;
- or the one-sided permutation criterion is not met once the >=8-source gate is reached.

Particularly informative counterexamples include:
- high-H systems with near-zero or negative centered-vertical effects;
- low-H mobile-prey/surface-constrained systems with strong centered-vertical effects.

Such counterexamples must be retained rather than recoded away.

## Prohibited moves

Do not:
- use current opened systems in the confirmatory p-value;
- alter A/V definitions after any future E is opened;
- try multiple guild codings and select the best association;
- switch the primary response from continuous E to binary PASS/FAIL after seeing outcomes;
- exclude a source because it contradicts the prediction;
- lower the >=8-source gate after results;
- describe this contract as preregistration of the current paper's ecological contrast.

## Interpretation ceiling

A supported future association would show that the generated ecological-opportunity score predicts cross-system expression of centered vertical individuality in the tested source universe.

It would not by itself prove a unique causal mechanism, because morphology, phylogeny, sensory ecology, habitat and tracking technology may remain correlated with H.
