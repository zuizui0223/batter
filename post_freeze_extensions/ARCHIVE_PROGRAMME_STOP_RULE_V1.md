# Archive mechanism programme stop rule v1

## Status

The post-freeze archival mechanism programme is **closed**.

This stop is scientific, not computational.

## Why stop now

The analyses have reached the support boundary of the public data:

- centered individuality survives broad speed×turn matching in 5/5 comparative panels;
- it survives 500-m place×state matching in 4/4 structurally evaluable panels;
- it survives 250-m matching in 3/3 structurally evaluable panels;
- a common 100-m test is structurally unavailable;
- it survives >=1-day self-history separation in 4/4 panels;
- it survives >=3-day separation in 3/3;
- it survives >=7-day separation in 2/2;
- no longer lag is opened by contract;
- simple body-mass similarity is 0/4;
- relative tag burden is unsupported;
- broad same-night context is insufficient in 3/4 at 2 km;
- 500-m same-night common support fails;
- temporal-phase causation fails support-matched attribution.

## Prohibited post-hoc extensions

Do not introduce:
- 150 m, 75 m, 50 m or other new spatial grids;
- 5-day, 10-day, 14-day or other new lag thresholds;
- alternative phase partitions;
- weaker donor-support minima;
- panel deletion after outcomes;
- alternate morphology proxies selected because body mass failed.

## Allowed next steps

Only:
1. a genuinely independent external dataset with a new frozen protocol; or
2. new field data designed to create cross-individual overlap in exact route/resource/environment contexts.

The frozen v0.3.8 submission on `main` remains untouched.
