## Synthesis

The programme increasingly rejects a pure **context-allocation** account.

The observed vertical individuality is not well explained by individuals merely spending different fractions of time:
- in different coarse horizontal places;
- in different broad movement states;
- at different radial stages of a central-place flight;
- at different measured potential-roost distances;
- or in different source-defined local land-cover contexts.

The most defensible post-freeze mechanistic interpretation is therefore:

> **context-residual individuality** — same-individual history retains information about centered vertical organization within increasingly matched measured ecological contexts.

The biological substrate of this within-context component remains unresolved.
