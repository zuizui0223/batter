# Next decisive mechanism experiment v2

## Identification target

The archive has now localized the unresolved mechanism below **500 m horizontal place + broad speed×turn state** in four structurally evaluable panels.

Broad same-night context is also insufficient in three of those four panels.

The decisive field experiment should therefore separate:

1. **sub-500-m route/resource/microhabitat allocation**, from
2. **within-context individual flight organization / individual environmental reaction norms**.

## Sampling design

Deliberately create cross-individual overlap in the same fine contexts instead of relying on incidental archival overlap.

For multiple repeatedly tracked individuals, obtain:
- calibrated terrain-relative altitude;
- high-frequency x-y sufficient to resolve exact corridors and turns;
- independent resource/feeding-site identities;
- independent behavioural-state labels;
- exact roost/central-place identity;
- body mass and wing dimensions;
- synchronized wind, temperature, pressure and uplift proxies at the relevant route scale;
- repeated tag calibration or tag swaps.

## Primary matched stratum

Aim to compare individuals within:

`resource/route segment × behavioural state × local environment × time window`

rather than within a coarse cell alone.

## Competing hypotheses

### H1 — fine resource/route allocation

Individuality arises because individuals repeatedly use different exact routes/resources.

Prediction:
- after matching exact route/resource and local environment, same-individual vertical identity collapses toward the pipeline-specific null.

### H2 — within-context flight organization

Individuality persists after exact route/resource/environment matching.

Prediction:
- same-individual history remains more predictive than other individuals within the same route × state × environment stratum.

### H3 — morphology-linked organization

If H2 persists, morphology explains part of the remaining transfer structure.

Prediction:
- among non-self individuals in matched contexts, more similar wing loading/body morphology yields better vertical-profile transfer.

### H4 — individual environmental reaction norms

Individuals differ in vertical response to local wind/uplift.

Prediction:
- individual-specific environmental slopes improve held-out prediction beyond shared slopes and reduce residual identity.

## Critical design quantity

The limiting resource is **cross-individual overlap in the same fine context**, not raw GPS fix count.

The experiment should therefore prioritize:
- multiple individuals visiting the same resource patches/routes;
- overlapping nights and environmental windows;
- repeated nights per individual;
- enough fixes per route × state × environment stratum for held-out scoring.

## Statistical discipline

1. Freeze structural eligibility without opening vertical outcomes.
2. Freeze route/resource/environment strata.
3. Use same target support for self and other models.
4. Calibrate the complete prediction pipeline under identity exchangeability.
5. Only after persistent within-context identity is established, test morphology or reaction norms as mediators.

This directly addresses the archival failure mode exposed most strongly by *Eidolon*.
