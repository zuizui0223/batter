## Strongest current ecological statement

> **Centered vertical individuality is independently prospectively replicable, but its expression is strongly heterogeneous across bat systems.**

## Strongest current general principle

> **Spatial individuality is configurable and multidimensional: horizontal location identity, absolute vertical level, centered vertical-distribution shape, temporal persistence and within-context residual identity can combine differently among systems.**

## Strongest current comparative question

> **What predicts a system's position in this multidimensional individuality space?**
