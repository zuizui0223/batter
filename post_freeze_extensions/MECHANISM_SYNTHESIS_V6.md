# Post-freeze mechanism synthesis v6

## Scope

All analyses here are **post-freeze exploratory extensions**. The frozen v0.3.8 JAE submission on `main` is unchanged.

## Starting rule

Five comparative bat panels retain calibrated individual identity in the centered shape of vertical space use after coarse 5-km horizontal occupancy and additive altitude level are controlled.

The mechanism question is:

> **Why do individuals remain non-exchangeable in how vertical-use probability is organized around their typical altitude?**

## What has been ruled down

### Broad movement-mode mixture

Speed-only conditioning: **5/5 panels retain identity**.

Speed × turning conditioning: **5/5 retain identity**.

**Conclusion:** broad slow/fast and straight/tortuous time-budget differences are insufficient as a general explanation.

### Horizontal route/patch allocation

Corrected x-y-time-only preflights defined the structurally evaluable spatial subsets before opening vertical outcomes.

At 2 km:
- **4/4** non-*Eidolon* panels retain identity.

At 500 m:
- **4/4** retain identity:
  - *Hypsignathus*: +0.384, p=0.0002
  - *P. hastatus* 2022: +0.0868, p=0.0002
  - *P. hastatus* 2023: +0.1015, p=0.0006
  - *P. hastatus* 2016: +0.451, p=0.0286

At 250 m:
- a pre-outcome support gate retained three systems;
- **3/3** retain identity:
  - *Hypsignathus*: +0.3115, p=0.0002
  - *P. hastatus* 2022: +0.0762, p=0.0002
  - *P. hastatus* 2016: +0.4472, p=0.0160

At 100 m:
- *Hypsignathus*: 10/16, required 12;
- *P. hastatus* 2022: 8/19, required 14;
- *P. hastatus* 2016: 9/9, required 7.

The common three-panel 100-m gate therefore failed and no 100-m vertical outcome was opened.

**Conclusion:** in the systems that can be tested, route/resource allocation at scales >=250–500 m is insufficient. The remaining spatial mechanism is localized below those scales.

### Simple movement-patch fidelity

Cross-panel mean Spearman rho=+0.176, p=0.05731, primary FAIL.

**Conclusion:** stronger repeated horizontal patch fidelity is not supported as a general driver of stronger vertical individuality.

### Relative biologger burden

Relative tag mass / animal mass:
- equal-panel mean |rho|=0.268;
- p=0.1399;
- primary FAIL.

**Conclusion:** simple monotonic payload burden is not a general technical explanation.

### Simple body-mass similarity

A four-panel donor-transfer test asked whether other individuals with more similar body mass transfer centered vertical organization better within matched 5-km place × speed×turn contexts.

**0/4 panels PASS**:
- *Hypsignathus*: rho=-0.108, p=0.107
- *P. hastatus* 2022: rho=-0.023, p=0.392
- *P. hastatus* 2023: rho=+0.028, p=0.565
- *P. hastatus* 2016: rho=+0.138, p=0.927

A stricter 500-m morphology-transfer design was stopped before vertical outcome because three of four panels failed donor-support gates.

**Conclusion:** simple body mass is not supported as a general stable-trait mechanism. Wing loading, wing shape and other morphology remain untested.

## Shared-night environmental context

A four-panel comparison asked whether same-individual history remained more informative than other bats tracked on the same shifted night while also matching 2-km place × speed×turn state.

- *Hypsignathus*: +0.302, p=0.0002 — PASS
- *P. hastatus* 2022: +0.169, p=0.0002 — PASS
- *P. hastatus* 2023: +0.0822, p=0.0286 — PASS
- *P. hastatus* 2016: +0.564, p=0.0544 — unresolved

Thus **3/4 PASS**.

A 500-m same-night preflight was then frozen:
- *Hypsignathus*: 15/19, required 14
- P2022: 16/23, required 17
- P2023: 9/11, required 8
- P2016: 8/9, required 7

The all-four 500-m gate failed by one P2022 individual, so no 500-m same-night vertical outcome was opened.

**Conclusion:** broad shared calendar-night context is insufficient in most structurally evaluable systems. Exact local wind, uplift, temperature and microclimate remain live.

## Temporal-phase allocation

Early/middle/late session-phase conditioning produced an apparent P2023 attenuation.

However, a support-matched paired attribution test compared phase-conditioned and phase-collapsed predictors on exactly the same target endpoints:
- paired phase increment=-0.0456;
- null-centered increment=-0.00481;
- p=0.3774;
- attribution FAIL.

**Conclusion:** the apparent 2023 attenuation is not established as a true temporal-phase mechanism. Support loss and finite-sample variance remain viable explanations.

The dry/wet-season temporal-allocation narrative therefore remains hypothesis-generating only.

## Temporal stability: the strongest new mechanism localization

### >=1 day

Four non-*Eidolon* panels had sufficient pre-outcome support.

Restricting the self predictor to sessions at least one day from the target gives **4/4 PASS**:
- *Hypsignathus*: +0.1483, p=0.0002
- P2022: +0.1135, p=0.0002
- P2023: +0.0604, p=0.0008
- P2016: +0.5341, p=0.0188

### >=3 days

Three panels were structurally evaluable. This was a stress test opened after partial positive >=1-day results.

**3/3 PASS**:
- *Hypsignathus*: +0.1539, p=0.0002
- P2022: +0.0959, p=0.0002
- P2023: +0.0573, p=0.0216

### >=7 days

Two panels had pre-outcome week-scale support. This was the final predeclared temporal stress test.

**2/2 PASS**:
- *Hypsignathus*: +0.1647, p=0.0002
- P2022: +0.0999, p=0.0002

Per the frozen stop rule, no longer lag is opened.

**Conclusion:** where the data structurally permit it, centered vertical organization is not a same-night or adjacent-day state. It contains a **multi-day, and in two systems week-scale, stable individual component**.

This does not imply lifetime stability, immutable personality or genetic determination.

## Structural identifiability boundary: Eidolon

*Eidolon* repeatedly limits the strongest cross-individual mechanism tests:
- matched fine-place × speed×turn at 500 m–2 km: only 1/12 evaluable;
- five-panel same-night preflight: 8/12 where 9 required;
- temporal phase3: 4/12; phase2: 7/12;
- body-mass donor-transfer designs collapse in donor support;
- >=1-day self-history support: 7/12, below the frozen five-panel gate.

Strong vertical individuality therefore coexists with sparse cross-individual fine-scale horizontal overlap. The archive cannot identify its fine mechanism without deliberately overlapping future sampling.

## Current causal localization

The evidence now argues against the following as sufficient general explanations:

`coarse place + broad movement mode + >=250–500 m route allocation + short-lived nightly state + simple body mass + broad shared-night context`

The live causal space is now much narrower:

1. **sub-250-m / sub-500-m exact route, resource or microhabitat structure**
   - exact feeding trees;
   - narrow flight corridors;
   - canopy openings;
   - local topography;
   - microclimatic exposure;

2. **stable within-context individual flight organization**
   - unmeasured wing morphology / wing loading;
   - learned movement routines;
   - memory and experience;
   - persistent route choice;

3. **fine-scale individual environmental reaction norms**
   - individual-specific responses to wind, uplift and microclimate at scales finer than broad same-night matching.

## Strongest current statements

Five-panel level:

> **Broad horizontal kinematic-state composition alone is insufficient to explain the comparative vertical-individuality pattern.**

Four-panel structurally evaluable subset:

> **Centered vertical individuality persists after 500-m place × speed×turn matching and persists when self-history is restricted to sessions at least one day from the target.**

Three-panel stress-test subsets:

> **In three structurally evaluable systems, identity persists after 250-m place matching and after at least three days of self-history separation.**

Two-panel week-scale stress test:

> **In the two systems with week-scale structural support, the same individual remains predictive even when all self-training sessions are at least seven days from the target.**

## Archive stop

The archive programme stops here.

- 100-m common spatial support failed.
- The final predeclared temporal stress test was seven days and is complete.
- 500-m body-mass transfer became structurally sparse.
- 500-m same-night common support failed.
- phase attribution did not survive support matching.

No new spatial grid, lag threshold, phase partition or donor minimum should be introduced post hoc.

Further mechanism identification now requires new overlapping field data or a genuinely independent external dataset.
