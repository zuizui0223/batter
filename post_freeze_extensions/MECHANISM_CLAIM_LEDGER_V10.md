## Strongest current ecological statement

> **Individual spatial specialization is multidimensional. Horizontal site fidelity, absolute vertical level and centered vertical-distribution shape can dissociate across bat systems.**

## Strongest current vertical-individuality statement

> **Centered vertical individuality occurs in multiple original bat systems and can be context-residual where present, but prospective external evidence shows that it is not universal across tested independent sources.**

## Strongest current generality statement

> **The scientifically interesting quantity is now variation in the expression of vertical individuality itself: why some bat systems retain stable within-context vertical organization while others do not.**
