# Post-freeze mechanism synthesis v3

## Scope

All analyses here are **post-freeze exploratory extensions**. The frozen v0.3.8 JAE submission on `main` is unchanged.

## Starting ecological result

Five comparative bat panels retain calibrated individual identity in the centered shape of vertical space use after coarse 5-km horizontal occupancy and additive altitude level are controlled.

The mechanism question is:

> **Why do individuals remain non-exchangeable in how vertical-use probability is organized around their typical altitude?**

## Mechanisms directly weakened

### Broad kinematic time budgets

Speed-only conditioning: **5/5 panels retain identity**.

Speed × turning conditioning: **5/5 panels retain identity**:
- *Eidolon*: +0.335, p=0.0002
- *Hypsignathus*: +0.148, p=0.0002
- *P. hastatus* 2022: +0.110, p=0.0002
- *P. hastatus* 2023: +0.061, p=0.0002
- *P. hastatus* 2016: +0.526, p=0.0064

Thus stable differences in the fraction of time spent in broad slow/fast and straight/tortuous movement modes are insufficient as a general explanation.

### Route/patch allocation at >=2 km

A corrected x-y-time-only preflight reproduced the exact frozen speed×turn baseline counts and showed that *Eidolon* lacks enough fine cross-individual overlap, but four panels are structurally evaluable at 2 km.

In those four panels, **4/4 retain centered identity after simultaneous 2-km place × speed×turn matching**:
- *Hypsignathus*: +0.286, p=0.0002
- *P. hastatus* 2022: +0.187, p=0.0002
- *P. hastatus* 2023: +0.072, p=0.0022
- *P. hastatus* 2016: +0.545, p=0.0120

Therefore 2-km route/patch allocation is insufficient in this structurally evaluable subset.

### Route/patch allocation at >=500 m

The matched preflight had already established 500-m support before any 500-m vertical outcome. The 500-m follow-up was opened after the positive 2-km result and is therefore a robustness stress test, not independent confirmation.

**4/4 panels pass again at 500 m**:
- *Hypsignathus*: +0.384, p=0.0002
- *P. hastatus* 2022: +0.0868, p=0.0002
- *P. hastatus* 2023: +0.1015, p=0.0006
- *P. hastatus* 2016: +0.451, p=0.0286

Within these four panels, spatial allocation explanations are pushed below the 500-m matching scale. Exact flight corridors, feeding trees, canopy gaps, microtopography and microclimate remain possible.

### Simple movement-patch fidelity

Frozen primary prediction:
- equal-panel mean Spearman rho = +0.176
- p=0.05731
- positive in 4/5 panels
- primary verdict FAIL

This does not support a simple general rule that stronger fine horizontal patch fidelity produces stronger vertical individuality.

### Relative biologger burden

Relative tag burden = tag mass / animal mass was tested against individually permutation-null-calibrated centered identity.

Cross-panel:
- equal-panel mean |rho| = 0.268
- permutation p=0.1399
- primary verdict FAIL

Only 2022 showed an isolated association (rho=-0.401, p=0.0274), with inconsistent signs across panels.

Thus simple monotonic relative tag burden is not a general technical explanation.

## Temporal allocation: a real context-dependent signal

A pre-outcome x-y-time-only screen selected early/middle/late relative tracked-session phase for a structurally evaluable four-panel subset.

Phase-conditioned result:
- *Hypsignathus*: +0.156, p=0.0002 — PASS
- *P. hastatus* 2022: +0.0896, p=0.0002 — PASS
- *P. hastatus* 2023: +0.0221, p=0.1068 — **FAIL**
- *P. hastatus* 2016: +0.483, p=0.0214 — PASS

Relative within-session timing is therefore not a general explanation, but the 2023 panel is an informative exception.

The source study identifies 2016 and 2022 as dry-season tracking and 2023 as wet-season tracking. This generates a resource-season × temporal-allocation hypothesis: under the wet-season resource landscape, individuality may be expressed more through **when** place/kinematic contexts are used than through persistent vertical organization within those temporal phases.

This is not a season-causation result. A prospective 2021 wet-season check was attempted outcome-blind, but 2021 had only 4 individuals and 2 repeat individuals and failed the original frozen cohort gate. No 2021 vertical outcome was opened.

The existing 2023 1-km endpoint-neighbourhood exclusion passes, so the phase result is not reducible to the previously tested session-endpoint neighbourhood alone.

## Shared night/environmental context

The focal *Tadarida* same-night comparison already shows that another night from the same individual can outpredict contemporaneous other bats, weakening a simple shared-night-only explanation.

A corrected four-panel 2-km fine-place × speed×turn × same-night test is running under a pre-outcome exact-n contract. Until it completes, no cross-panel same-night conclusion is added here.

A common *Tadarida* uplift reaction-norm slope was also not supported (permutation p=0.334), although atmospheric effects themselves remain possible.

## Structural identifiability boundary: Eidolon

*Eidolon* repeatedly limits the strongest cross-individual mechanism tests:
- 500 m / 1 km / 2 km fine-place × speed×turn matching: only 1/12 evaluable;
- five-panel same-night preflight: 8/12 where 9 required;
- close/far body-mass donor transfer: 3 targets where 9 required;
- continuous mass-gradient transfer: 1/12 where 9 required;
- temporal phase3: 4/12; phase2: 7/12.

This is itself ecologically informative: strong vertical individuality occurs in a system with sparse cross-individual fine-scale horizontal overlap. But it prevents the archive from separating fine-route allocation from within-route flight organization for *Eidolon*.

## Current causal fork

The evidence now rules against the simplest pathway:

`different coarse places / broad movement modes / >=500-m routes -> vertical individuality`

for the four structurally evaluable non-*Eidolon* panels.

The main live mechanisms are now:

1. **sub-500-m route/resource/microhabitat allocation** — exact feeding trees, flight corridors, canopy gaps or local atmospheric opportunities;
2. **within-context flight organization** — morphology/wing loading, memory, experience, learned movement routines, resource choice, or individual environmental reaction norms;
3. **context-dependent temporal allocation** — especially suggested by the 2023 wet-season *P. hastatus* exception.

## Strongest current mechanism statement

> **In four structurally evaluable comparative panels, individual vertical organization persists even after matching horizontal place to 500 m and matching broad speed × turning state. Broad kinematic time budgets and >=500-m route/patch allocation are therefore insufficient explanations in those systems.**

A separate five-panel statement remains valid at the coarser level:

> **Broad horizontal kinematic-state composition alone is insufficient to explain the five-panel vertical-individuality pattern.**

## Claim ceiling

Not established:
- exact resource/feeding-tree causation;
- sub-500-m route causation;
- body mass or wing-loading causation;
- learning, memory or personality;
- season causation;
- individual atmospheric reaction norms across the comparative panels;
- any mechanism generalization from the four-panel fine-place subset to *Eidolon*.
