## Ecological interpretation

Broad **kinematic-state mixture** is insufficient as a general explanation. Individual identity still predicts centered vertical organization after self and other predictors are placed under identical 5-km horizontal-cell × speed-bin × turning-bin weights.

This strengthens the within-state interpretation relative to the available harmonized proxy: individuality is not simply that one bat spends more time moving fast, slow, straight or tortuously than another.

The live alternatives are now narrower:
1. different fine-scale routes/resources within the same coarse cell and kinematic state;
2. individual-specific vertical organization within closely matched movement contexts, potentially associated with morphology, experience, memory, resource choice or environmental response.

Fine-place × state matching was separately stopped before vertical outcome because *Eidolon* lacked sufficient cross-individual fine-scale support.

## Claim boundary

The 4 states are kinematic proxies, not validated commuting/foraging/social labels. Persistence does not prove intrinsic morphology, learning, memory or resource specialization.
