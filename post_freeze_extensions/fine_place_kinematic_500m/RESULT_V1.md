## Interpretation

For the four structurally evaluable panels, centered vertical individuality survives simultaneous matching on:
- **500-m horizontal place**; and
- broad **speed × turning kinematic state**.

Therefore route/patch allocation at scales >=500 m is insufficient as a general explanation in this subset.

Live spatial explanations are pushed below 500 m: exact flight corridors, individual trees/resource patches, canopy gaps, local topographic exposure or other microhabitat differences may still matter.

No inference applies to *Eidolon*, whose cross-individual fine-place overlap is too sparse for this test.
