## What has actually moved

The mechanism question has shifted from:

> “Do bats use different vertical states because they do different things?”

to:

> **“Why do bats remain vertically different even while occupying similar coarse places and broad horizontal kinematic states?”**

This is the principal mechanistic advance of the post-freeze work.
