# Spatial scale of individualization contract v1

## Question

The fine-scale 3D decomposition showed that a focal bat's own past predicts its future 500-m-cell-specific vertical configuration better than conspecific histories.

This follow-up asks:

> **At what spatial scale does movement become personal?**

The hypothesis is hierarchical rather than binary. Broad movement sectors could be shared or environmentally constrained while finer horizontal and vertical solutions remain individual-specific.

## Frozen design

Use the exact Lane-B target universe:

- *Hypsignathus*: 71 target sessions / 17 individuals;
- *P. hastatus* 2022: 66 / 18;
- *P. hastatus* 2023: 8 / 6.

All history is at least three days before the target. The scored target fixes are held fixed across every spatial scale: they are exactly the fixes inside 500-m cells supported by both focal-self and conspecific histories under the earlier Lane-B rule.

Horizontal scales:

10 km → 5 km → 2.5 km → 1 km → 500 m.

At each scale compare:

`horizontal personal gain = log P_self(cell) - log P_other(cell)`.

The key contrast is:

`scale amplification = gain_500m - gain_10km`.

A positive amplification means individual identity becomes increasingly informative toward fine spatial resolution.

## Interpretation

This analysis cannot demonstrate colony memory. It can distinguish two architectures:

- individuality already present in broad movement sectors;
- broad inter-individual portability followed by fine personal refinement.

The existing 500-m vertical conditional personal increment is a separate final layer: individuality inside the same fine horizontal cell.
