## Species-versus-panel counting rule

Three *Phyllostomus hastatus* panels are repeated panels of one species, not three independent species-level ecological replicates.

Any ecological contrast must show both:
1. panel-level observations;
2. unique-taxon-level structure.

Do not write “6/6 plant-resource systems versus 0/4 mobile-prey systems” without immediately clarifying that the six positive panels include three *P. hastatus* datasets and that the ecological split is post hoc.

## Manuscript endpoint

The one-paper conclusion should be ordered:

1. **phenomenon:** vertical individuality can persist beyond coarse horizontal occupancy and absolute altitude level;
2. **localization:** broad measured state/place/time alternatives do not generally remove it where detected;
3. **boundary:** independent external systems show that it is not universal;
4. **hypothesis generation:** the positive/negative ecological contrast motivates resource anchoring × vertical opportunity as a predictor of when stable vertical individuality emerges.
