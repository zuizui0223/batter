## Why a heterogeneity model is not yet defensible

Only **two independent external sources** currently have comparable source-level effect estimates.

With k=2:

1. a between-source variance estimate is intrinsically unstable and almost entirely determined by the two observed effects;
2. ecological meta-regression has no meaningful source-level replication for testing predictors;
3. source characteristics are perfectly or nearly perfectly confounded with source identity;
4. model-selection among candidate ecological predictors would amount to retrospective storytelling;
5. adding original discovery panels as if they were equivalent prospective external sources would mix discovery and validation evidence classes.

Therefore:

> **Do not fit a random-effects meta-analysis, meta-regression, ecological predictor model, or source-level ranking yet.**

The observed sign difference between Nyctalus and Hipposideros is evidence of a boundary to simple universality, but it is not enough to estimate the distribution or causes of heterogeneity.

## What can be said now

Supported:

> **Prospective external evidence is heterogeneous in direction: Nyctalus is positive but non-significant, while Hipposideros is negative under the frozen source-level statistic.**

Supported:

> **Successful prospective external replication is not established.**

Supported as ecological synthesis:

> **The expression of centered vertical individuality varies across tested systems, so explaining cross-system variation is now a more useful question than assuming universality.**

Not yet supported:

- magnitude of between-system variance;
- prevalence of centered vertical individuality across bats;
- which taxon, habitat, morphology or foraging variables predict the effect;
- a ranking of systems from more to less individualized.

## Requirements for a future heterogeneity programme

Before any new external vertical outcome is opened, a new programme should freeze:

1. **source universe**
   - a reproducible literature/repository search frame or complete predeclared candidate list;
   - no stopping when a positive result appears;

2. **common estimand**
   - centered common-cell individual identity calibrated against the full pipeline-specific exchangeability null;
   - or a newly defined estimand applied prospectively to every source;

3. **vertical-reference policy**
   - how AGL, geoid/MSL and ellipsoid altitude are handled;
   - no source-specific endpoint chosen after effects are seen;

4. **structural eligibility**
   - session definition;
   - minimum fixes;
   - repeat-individual requirement;
   - common-support requirement;

5. **source-level effect and uncertainty**
   - effect-size definition;
   - permutation/bootstrap uncertainty;
   - weighting policy;

6. **heterogeneity estimand**
   - descriptive effect distribution first;
   - between-source variance only when source count supports it;

7. **ecological predictors**
   - selected from source metadata independently of effect estimates;
   - limited in number relative to independent systems;

8. **stopping rule**
   - complete the predeclared source universe rather than stop at significance.

## Practical data target

The next programme should prioritize adding **multiple structurally evaluable independent systems**, not one replacement positive system.

The scientifically valuable endpoint is a panel containing:
- positive centered-shape systems;
- weak/near-zero systems;
- negative/boundary systems;
- comparable structural resolution.

That would allow the question to become:

> **Which dimensions of ecology predict whether individual spatial specialization appears horizontally, vertically in absolute level, vertically in centered shape, or in several axes at once?**

## Current decision

**HETEROGENEITY MODEL: NOT YET EVALUABLE.**

Do not open Leptonycteris altitude under a lowered threshold and do not serially search for a positive external replication.

Further progress requires increasing the number of genuinely independent, structurally comparable external systems or collecting new data designed for the common estimand.
