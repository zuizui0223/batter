# 3D strategy / co-use inference ledger v1

## Purpose

Keep the post-freeze 3D programme from collapsing distinct biological claims into one another.

## Central distinction

The current data empirically separate:

1. **strategy fidelity** — individuals repeatedly reuse personal spatial solutions;
2. **niche segregation** — different individuals occupy non-overlapping solutions;
3. **interaction-dependent separation** — individuals become additionally separated during co-presence.

These are not synonyms.

Current strongest recurrent result is level 3: **personal terrain-relative vertical strategy fidelity**.

Current interaction-dependent evidence is limited to one 2023 Phyllostomus panel. Its positive co-use primary survives dyad and endpoint diagnostics but does not localize to the predeclared temporal or horizontal proximity gradients, favouring a shared-site/sub-cell spatial-structure interpretation over simple near-contact avoidance.

## Current ecological interpretation

The evidence is more consistent with:

> stable individual spatial strategies that are usually overlapping,

than with:

> a general rule of active vertical niche partitioning among conspecifics.

P. hastatus 2023 is a specific exception in which momentary co-use adds vertical separation beyond stable personal strategies. Its mechanism remains unresolved.

## Rule for future wording

Never write:
- "individuals partition vertical space" when evidence is only V_rel fidelity;
- "competition" when evidence is only co-presence separation;
- "3D niche segregation" as synonymous with height-above-terrain segregation.

Use:
- **vertical strategy fidelity** for V_rel;
- **realized 3D segregation** for positive S in geographic coordinates;
- **co-presence-dependent vertical separation** for the corrected 2023 synchronous result;
- **competition-mediated partitioning** only after direct competitor/resource evidence.
