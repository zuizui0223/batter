# Ecology-to-geometry synthesis v5

## Core result

The combined geometry analyses now separate four layers of individual spatial specialization:

1. H — horizontal landscape fidelity;
2. V — terrain-relative personal vertical-strategy fidelity;
3. S — persistent between-individual niche segregation;
4. I — additional vertical separation during actual local co-presence.

These layers are empirically distinct.

The strongest current result is:

> **personal vertical strategies are recurrent, whereas active co-presence-dependent vertical partitioning is not.**

## H: landscape fidelity

Individuals in the structurally evaluable panels repeatedly reuse different horizontal portions of the landscape.

This creates the first layer of individual spatial specialization.

## V: personal terrain-relative vertical strategy

After subtracting DEM terrain, all four structurally evaluable original panels retain supported individual-specific vertical configuration within shared 500-m cells:

- *Hypsignathus monstrosus*: V_rel +0.042
- *Phyllostomus hastatus* 2022: +0.100
- *P. hastatus* 2023: +0.077
- *P. hastatus* 2016: +0.079

Thus individuals repeatedly reuse personal vertical solutions relative to the local terrain.

## S: persistent niche segregation

After terrain correction, additional between-individual 3D segregation is small:

- *Hypsignathus*: S_rel -0.016
- 2022: -0.009
- 2023: +0.016
- 2016: +0.029

Therefore positive V does not imply that individuals occupy mutually exclusive vertical niches.

This establishes:

> **individual specialization can exist without strong niche partitioning.**

## I: interaction-dependent separation

The fixed synchronous co-use test asks whether actual local co-presence creates extra terrain-relative vertical separation beyond each individual's stable site-specific strategy.

Corrected results:

- *Hypsignathus*: I -1.98 m, p=0.9045
- *P. hastatus* 2022: I -0.95 m, p=0.9186
- *P. hastatus* 2023: I +3.57 m, p=0.0231
- *P. hastatus* 2016: I -0.85 m, p=0.8611

Only the 2023 panel supports additional co-presence-dependent separation.

Thus the dominant pattern across panels is:

H positive
+ V positive
+ S weak
+ I absent.

This geometry means that individuals maintain stable personal ways of using shared three-dimensional space without generally moving farther apart when conspecifics are locally co-present.

## The 2023 interaction layer

The 2023 result is not a simple single-dyad or endpoint artifact.

Post-outcome robustness diagnostics show:
- six of eight leave-one-dyad-out variants retain p<0.05;
- removing the dyad contributing 586 of 679 encounters still gives p=0.0279;
- endpoint-excluded data retain a descriptive +3.81 m excess, although only four individuals remain and the frozen inferential gate is not met.

However the effect is dyad-heterogeneous:
- leave-one-dyad-out p-values range from 0.0055 to 0.2332;
- removing two dyads weakens the sensitivity result above 0.05.

The best description is therefore:

> **a context-dependent, dyad-heterogeneous interaction response superimposed on otherwise stable personal strategies.**

The 600-s synchronization window and all-space primary scope prevent a strong claim of instantaneous competitive avoidance.

## Revised ecological story

The earlier hypothesis said that repeated ecological tasks with multiple reusable solutions should allow individual strategies to emerge.

That is only the first layer.

The current results support a more specific hierarchy:

### Stage 1 — ecological task selects landscape

Individuals repeatedly choose particular patches, trees, corridors or terrain.

This produces H.

### Stage 2 — individuals reuse personal solutions within the selected landscape

Relative to local terrain, individuals repeatedly use personally characteristic vertical configurations.

This produces V.

### Stage 3 — those personal strategies may still overlap

Different individuals can have repeatable strategies without occupying exclusive niches.

Weak S is the current dominant pattern.

### Stage 4 — interaction may modify the personal strategy transiently

In some ecological contexts, local co-presence may induce additional vertical separation.

This produces I.

The current archive finds this layer only in 2023, and even there unevenly among dyads.

## Contrast with Pteropus

*Pteropus poliocephalus* demonstrates another geometry:

- strong H;
- strong native-MSL V_abs and S_abs;
- both collapse after DEM terrain subtraction.

Its realized 3D niche separation is primarily landscape/topography-embedded rather than a terrain-relative personal vertical strategy under the overlap endpoint.

Thus two populations can show striking 3D individuality for fundamentally different reasons:
- different landscape elements with inherited z;
- personal vertical strategy relative to the same physical substrate.

## Contrast with Myotis

*Myotis vivesi* remains consistent with a surface-constrained geometry:
- H approximately zero;
- weak positive V_abs that narrowly misses support;
- little added segregation.

This is compatible with an ecological task that physically restricts vertical freedom near the ocean surface.

## What the data do and do not say about "food partitioning"

The current data do **not** support the broad statement:

> individuals deliberately divide food or vertical airspace to avoid one another.

They support:

> **individuals repeatedly reuse personal spatial solutions, while interaction-dependent separation is context-specific rather than a general property.**

The 2023 result makes active partitioning biologically plausible in at least one context, but resource identity and competition are not observed directly.

To demonstrate competition-mediated food partitioning, future work must connect I or S to:
- identified shared feeding resources;
- competitor density;
- simultaneous resource use;
- diet identity;
- resource depletion;
- experimental resource addition/removal.

## General principle

The strongest generated principle is now:

> **Ecological strategy may determine not only how much individuals specialize, but where that specialization is encoded: landscape choice, personal substrate-relative strategy, persistent niche segregation, or interaction-dependent displacement.**

This is a geometry-of-specialization framework rather than a single specialization magnitude.

## Methodological implication

A study of individual 3D niches should avoid treating one overlap statistic as biologically self-explanatory.

At minimum, distinguish:
- landscape fidelity H;
- substrate-relative strategy fidelity V;
- persistent segregation S;
- interaction-dependent displacement I.

Otherwise:
- terrain selection can masquerade as vertical specialization;
- repeatability can be mistaken for partitioning;
- partitioning can be mistaken for competition;
- transient interaction responses can be missed by session-level overlap.

## Current claim ceiling

Supported:
- H and V are recurrent in multiple bat panels;
- S is weak after terrain correction;
- I is absent in three evaluable panels and supported in one 2023 panel;
- the 2023 I signal is not driven by the highest-count dyad and persists directionally after endpoint exclusion;
- strategy fidelity, niche segregation and co-presence response are empirically separable.

Not supported:
- universal vertical partitioning;
- competition as the cause of the 2023 signal;
- intentional avoidance;
- general food partitioning;
- a causal cross-species ecology-to-geometry law.
