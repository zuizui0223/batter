## Interpretation

The corrected 2023 co-use excess cannot naturally be framed as a pattern generated primarily by encounters between the two source group labels.

Most of the dyad network, including the dominant dyad, is within-group.

This does not prove cooperation or within-group interaction because:
- `animal-group-id` is source metadata, not a experimentally validated interaction class for this analysis;
- the GPS archive contains no direct feeding-resource identity;
- no resource competition variable is available.

The result further weakens a simple **between-group competition -> vertical avoidance** interpretation.
