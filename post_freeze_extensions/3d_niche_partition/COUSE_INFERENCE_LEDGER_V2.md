## Central distinction

The data now separate four levels:

1. **strategy fidelity** — an individual repeatedly reuses a personal spatial solution;
2. **niche segregation** — different individuals occupy less-overlapping solutions;
3. **co-presence-dependent separation** — relative positions differ during local synchronous use;
4. **competition/resource mechanism** — separation is caused by competitor or resource interactions.

Current recurrent evidence is strongest at level 1/2, not level 4.

## What is recurrent

Across Hypsignathus and all three structurally evaluable Phyllostomus panels:
- terrain-relative vertical strategy fidelity is supported;
- strong terrain-relative mutual segregation is not.

Therefore the robust recurring biological result is:

> **individuals repeatedly reuse personal vertical solutions inside shared landscapes, even when those solutions remain substantially overlapping.**

This is individual specialization without obligatory niche exclusion.

## What the co-use test added

The co-use phase null preserves:
- the observed local co-use encounters;
- each individual's site-specific vertical distribution;
- stable personal strategy.

Only P. hastatus 2023 exceeds this null.

Thus a momentary/context-dependent separation layer can exist, but it is not a general consequence of personal vertical specialization.

## What the 2023 localization says

The 2023 primary:
- survives most leave-one-dyad-out diagnostics;
- remains descriptively positive after endpoint exclusion;
- consists mostly of near-synchronous fixes (median |dt| = 3 s; 88.2% <=60 s).

But:
- the calibrated temporal-proximity slope is unsupported (p=0.095);
- the horizontal-proximity slope is opposite the active-near-contact prediction;
- separation is descriptively strongest at >250 m horizontal distance;
- a stricter micro-place-preserving phase null cannot be estimated without violating frozen dyad support.

Therefore the current 2023 interpretation is:

> **a reproducible shared-local-site spatial association whose mechanism is unresolved, not demonstrated active vertical avoidance.**

Fine-scale route, canopy/resource or microhabitat organization inside the 500-m cell remains a plausible alternative.

## Rule for wording

Use:
- **vertical strategy fidelity** for V_rel;
- **realized 3D segregation** for raw x-y-z separation;
- **co-presence-dependent vertical separation** only for the frozen 2023 primary;
- **shared-local-site association** when discussing the 2023 mechanism after localization diagnostics.

Reserve:
- **vertical niche partitioning** for supported mutual non-overlap after appropriate substrate correction;
- **competition-mediated partitioning** for direct resource/competitor evidence.
