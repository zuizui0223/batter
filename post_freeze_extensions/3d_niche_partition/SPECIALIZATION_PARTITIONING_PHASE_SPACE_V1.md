# Specialization-partitioning phase space v1

## Purpose

Descriptive synthesis of two already-completed endpoints.

x-axis:
terrain-relative vertical-strategy fidelity V_rel = self O_Z|XY - other O_Z|XY.

y-axis:
co-use vertical-separation calibrated excess in metres = observed equal-dyad median separation - phase-null mean.

Interpretation:
- right side: repeatable personal vertical strategy;
- upper half: synchronous co-use adds vertical separation beyond stable personal strategy;
- lower half: no positive interaction-dependent separation.

All four x-axis endpoints are permutation-supported under their own terrain-relative geometry tests.

Only the 2023 y-axis endpoint meets the pre-specified upper-tail co-use criterion.

The figure is exploratory synthesis. It does not create a cross-panel statistical test and must not be interpreted as a prevalence estimate or causal competition model.

The 2023 point is explicitly labelled as 600-s / all-space because its co-use inference is weaker than the 60-s endpoint-excluded panels.
