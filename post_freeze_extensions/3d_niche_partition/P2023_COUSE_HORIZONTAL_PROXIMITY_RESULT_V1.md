## Interpretation

The evidence now favours a more conservative explanation:

> the 2023 co-use excess is a shared-local-site spatial pattern, not demonstrated active vertical avoidance between nearby individuals.

Because encounters were defined within 500-m cells, residual horizontal micro-location, route geometry, canopy/resource structure or other sub-cell habitat organization can still generate larger vertical differences among fixes that occur in the same coarse cell.

The descriptive tendency for the strongest separation at >250 m is especially compatible with residual within-cell spatial structure.

This does not erase the corrected primary 2023 result. It changes its ecological interpretation.
