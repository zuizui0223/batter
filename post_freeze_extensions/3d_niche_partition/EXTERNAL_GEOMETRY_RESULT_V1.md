## General interpretation

The external data strengthen the geometry decomposition rather than a simple positive/negative vertical-individuality story.

- Myotis is near the weak/surface-constrained corner.
- Pteropus occupies a strongly individualized realized 3D niche, but that geometry is inherited from repeated topographic landscape use.
