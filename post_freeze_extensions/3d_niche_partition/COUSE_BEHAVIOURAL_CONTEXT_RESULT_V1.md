## Interpretation of the 2023 co-use exception

The only panel with supported synchronous vertical separation, P. hastatus 2023, is not dominated by pairs in which both endpoints are stationary-like:

- both stationary-like: 22.7%
- one stationary-like: 44.3%
- neither stationary-like: 33.0%

Most encounters (71.3%) occur with both individuals in the middle 60% of their tracked session rather than at the beginning or end.

Thus the +3.61 m co-use separation result cannot be reduced descriptively to an encounter set made almost entirely of stationary-like session endpoints.

However, the source lacks a validated behavioural-state label for these encounters. The result therefore remains a local co-presence pattern, not a confirmed foraging-competition response.

## Cross-panel context differences

The encounter geometry is not behaviourally homogeneous across systems.

- Hypsignathus encounters are strongly enriched in stationary-like endpoints.
- 2022 is mixed but more stationary-like than 2023.
- 2023 contains a broad mixture of low-speed and moving endpoints.
- 2016 is overwhelmingly not stationary-like and almost entirely middle-session despite extreme localization to one 500-m cell.

These differences reinforce the need to avoid treating "same cell at the same time" as one universal behavioural state.
