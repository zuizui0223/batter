## Question

## Ecological interpretation

Broad movement-state mixture alone is insufficient as a general explanation for comparative vertical individuality. The same individual's vertical organization remains more predictive than other individuals' organization even after both predictors are placed under the same coarse horizontal occupancy and the same horizontal movement-intensity-state composition.

This shifts the live mechanism question from:

> “Do individuals simply spend different fractions of time in broad movement states?”

toward:

> “Why do individuals differ vertically even within the same broad movement state?”

Two major possibilities remain:

1. **fine-scale route/habitat allocation** — individuals use different sub-kilometre or kilometre-scale routes/patches within the same 5-km cell and movement state, exposing them to different vertical opportunities;
2. **within-route flight organization** — morphology, experience, memory, resource choice or environmental response generates individual vertical organization even when movement state and fine-scale place are closely matched.

## Claim boundary

This result establishes within-state individuality only relative to the frozen horizontal-speed proxy. It does not prove intrinsic flight phenotype, learning, resource specialization or morphology, and it does not modify the v0.3.8 submission package.
