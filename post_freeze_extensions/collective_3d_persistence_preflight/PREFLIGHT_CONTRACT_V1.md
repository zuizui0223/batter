# Collective 3D persistence structural preflight v1

## Purpose

This is an outcome-blind structural check for a new question generated after the existing individual 3D results:

> Is repeated 3D probability geometry entirely personal, or is there also a group-level spatial template that persists across individuals and time?

No new height-distribution or 3D-overlap outcome is calculated in this preflight.

## Two frozen lanes

### Lane A — natural tracked-member turnover

The strict test asks whether two nights from the same cohort can be compared when:

- each night contains at least two tracked individuals;
- the two nights share **zero** tracked individual identities;
- the nights are separated by at least 3 days;
- both nights retain at least 50 fixes inside their shared 500-m horizontal cells.

A panel is structurally open only with at least 10 such night-pairs involving at least five unique nights.

If this gate passes, a later frozen analysis may ask whether pooled 3D group shape recurs after complete tracked-member turnover.

### Lane B — cross-individual past-to-future prediction

For a target session, require:

- at least one focal-individual session at least 3 days in the past;
- at least three other individuals with sessions at least 3 days in the past;
- at least 50 target fixes in 500-m cells supported by both self and other history.

At least five target individuals are required per panel.

If this gate passes, a later frozen decomposition can separate:

1. **shared-group gain** — whether other individuals' past carries spatially resolved vertical information beyond their marginal vertical distribution;
2. **personal increment** — whether the focal individual's own past adds predictive information beyond that shared group profile.

The decomposition is additive on the same held-out target fixes.

## Interpretation boundary

Lane A is the only route here that approaches a direct test of collective-level persistence under natural member turnover. Even then, stable landscape structure remains an alternative to social or cognitive memory.

Lane B tests a shared predictive template plus personal refinement. It must not be called colony memory by itself.
