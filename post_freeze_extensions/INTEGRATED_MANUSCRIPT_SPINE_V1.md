# Integrated manuscript spine v1 — ecological contingency

## Status

This branch integrates the frozen JAE v0.3.8 result with the complete post-freeze external programme without changing the historical status of any test.

The manuscript is **not** organized as “v0.3.8 plus extra replications”. Its higher-level question is:

> **When does repeatable vertical individuality remain after horizontal occupancy and absolute altitude level are removed, and what ecological structure may explain why it appears in some bat systems but not others?**

## Working title

**Repeatable vertical individuality in bats persists beyond horizontal occupancy but varies among ecological systems**

## Evidence hierarchy

### Tier 1 — submission-confirmed phenomenon
Original six paper panels.

- Common-cell vertical identity exceeds the pipeline-specific exchangeability expectation in all six.
- Session-median-centered distribution-shape identity is supported in the five comparative panels:
  *Eidolon helvum*, *Hypsignathus monstrosus*, and three *Phyllostomus hastatus* panels.
- *Tadarida teniotis* is the required boundary: centered-shape identity is not supported.

Claim:
> Repeatable organization of vertical use can remain after coarse horizontal occupancy and additive altitude level are removed.

### Tier 2 — post-freeze mechanism localization
Outcome-informed family, frozen before each stress test but not independent confirmation.

Where centered individuality is detectable it survives:
- speed conditioning: 5/5;
- speed × turning: 5/5;
- 2-km place × state: 4/4;
- 500-m place × state: 4/4;
- 250-m place × state: 3/3;
- >=1-day separation: 4/4;
- >=3-day separation: 3/3;
- >=7-day separation: 2/2.

Claim ceiling:
> Broad state composition, coarse-to-intermediate place allocation and short-lived same-session effects are insufficient general explanations in the evaluable positive systems.

Do not claim one universal within-context mechanism.

### Tier 3 — prospective external boundary tests
Preserve each frozen programme and verdict.

| source | design | result |
|---|---|---|
| *Nyctalus noctula* | first frozen external primary | FAIL, excess +0.05175, p=0.1224 |
| *Hipposideros armiger/pratti* | frozen >=5 source programme | FAIL, excess -0.04468, p=0.8616 |
| *Myotis vivesi* | separately frozen n=4 programme | FAIL, excess +0.00470, p=0.4419 |
| *Pteropus poliocephalus* | separately frozen n=4 programme | PASS, excess +0.16873, p=0.0001 |

These four are not a prevalence sample and the programmes are not exchangeable. Report the full sequence rather than “1/4 replication rate”.

Primary comparative inference:
> External testing establishes strong heterogeneity, not universality.

### Tier 4 — post-hoc ecological synthesis
The currently opened systems show a striking qualitative split.

Supported systems:
- *Eidolon*: frugivory/nectar use, persistent plant resources;
- *Hypsignathus*: primarily frugivorous;
- *Phyllostomus*: omnivorous with plant use and repeatable feeding areas;
- *Pteropus*: fruit/nectar/pollen/tree blossoms.

Unsupported/boundary systems:
- *Tadarida*: aerial insectivore;
- *Nyctalus*: aerial insectivore;
- *Hipposideros*: insectivore;
- *Myotis vivesi*: marine fish/crustacean forager near the water surface.

This is **hypothesis generation**, not a tested cross-species predictor.

Preferred mechanism language:
> **resource anchoring × vertical opportunity**

Avoid a crude “frugivore versus insectivore” causal binary.

Generated prediction:
> Centered vertical individuality should be stronger where individuals repeatedly solve vertically structured foraging problems around persistent spatial resources, and weaker where prey is mobile/ephemeral or foraging is constrained to a narrow surface or layer.

Competing predictor families that must remain explicit:
- wing loading / aspect ratio and other flight morphology;
- phylogeny and sensory ecology;
- habitat vertical complexity;
- atmospheric forcing;
- sample size, vertical reference and tag technology.

## Pteropus terrain audit

Because *Pteropus poliocephalus* combines:
- very strong 5-km horizontal individuality;
- an MSL vertical coordinate;
- a prospective centered-MSL PASS,

the integrated manuscript will not treat it as terrain-robust until the separately frozen post-outcome terrain audit is complete.

The audit asks:
1. does centered individuality persist for `MSL - DEM`?
2. does centered DEM terrain use itself carry identity?

Historical Pteropus PASS remains in the record regardless of the audit.

## Result-independent manuscript logic

The integrated paper must remain publishable under every terrain-audit outcome.

### If Pteropus is terrain-robust
External generality is heterogeneous but includes one clean independent positive. The resource-anchoring hypothesis gains a stronger motivating example.

### If Pteropus is terrain-sensitive
The external programme becomes even more restrictive: the original positive archive is contrasted with three frozen external FAILs and one MSL positive that is not robust to terrain adjustment. This sharpens, rather than destroys, the question of why the original resource-anchored systems carried centered individuality.

## Introduction logic

1. 3-D space use can show individual specialization.
2. Apparent vertical individuality can be induced by horizontal fidelity and altitude reference/device effects.
3. Even after those confounds are addressed, a deeper ecological question remains: individuality may itself depend on the opportunities and constraints supplied by a foraging system.
4. Test the phenomenon in a closed comparative archive, localize what does not explain it, then use independent external systems to map its boundary.
5. Treat ecological contrasts among positive and negative systems as explicit post-hoc hypotheses for future prediction.

## Results order

1. Closed original screen and calibrated common-cell result.
2. Centered-shape result: 5 comparative PASS, *Tadarida* boundary.
3. Mechanism localization: state, place and persistence stress tests.
4. External programme chronology with all failures and structural STOPs retained.
5. Pteropus terrain audit.
6. Descriptive ecological contrast across supported and unsupported systems.

Do not place “configurable spatial individuality” as a central result.

## Discussion claims

### Established
- vertical individual organization can persist beyond coarse horizontal occupancy;
- centered distribution shape can carry identity;
- the phenomenon is not universal across tested bat systems;
- broad measured context does not fully absorb the signal where it occurs.

### Supported synthesis, not causal law
- expression of centered vertical individuality is ecologically heterogeneous.

### Generated hypothesis
- persistent resource anchoring and vertical ecological opportunity may permit stable individual vertical strategies to emerge.

### General implication
> Individual specialization may not be only a property of individuals. Its detectable expression can depend on whether ecological structure repeatedly presents individuals with stable alternative ways of using space.

## Prohibited manuscript moves

- do not call the external history “replicated but heterogeneous” without immediately reporting the three frozen FAILs and the distinct n=4 Pteropus design;
- do not report 6/6 versus 0/4 as ten independent species-level replicates;
- do not fit or imply a diet effect from the currently opened convenience set;
- do not relabel post-freeze stress tests as independent confirmation;
- do not hide structural STOPs;
- do not let a Pteropus terrain result rewrite its historical prospective verdict;
- do not use “configurable” as an explanation that accommodates every possible result.

## Target contribution

The paper's strongest general contribution is a two-stage ecological result:

1. **individual vertical organization can be real after obvious spatial and measurement confounds are removed;**
2. **whether that organization is expressed is itself heterogeneous among ecological systems, motivating a falsifiable resource-anchoring × vertical-opportunity hypothesis.**
