## Central distinction

The current results do not support an interchangeable colony-level **fine 3D probability map**.

They instead support a hierarchy in which different information may live at different spatial scales:

1. **coarse collective / environmental layer** — destinations, sectors, broad commuting landscape, recurring resource fields or colony traditions can constrain where animals go;
2. **personal 3D layer** — once inside that shared landscape, an individual's own history carries information about its future cell-specific vertical configuration that other individuals' histories do not.

In notation,

[
C_t = 	ext{shared coarse movement context},
]

[
	heta_i = 	ext{persistent individual movement solution},
]

[
Y_{it} sim P(Y mid C_t,	heta_i,E_t).
]

A colony-level distribution can remain stable even when the individual realizations are not interchangeable.

## Evidence for the personal 3D layer

With all predictors restricted to sessions at least three days in the past:

- *Hypsignathus*: personal increment +0.4256, 95% CI [+0.3095,+0.5400], 17/17 individuals positive;
- *P. hastatus* 2022: +0.2459 [+0.1010,+0.4243], 14/18 positive;
- *P. hastatus* 2023: +0.3645 [+0.2763,+0.4531], 6/6 positive.

By contrast, the spatially resolved other-individual profile is worse than the other-individual marginal vertical profile:

- *Hypsignathus*: shared-group gain -0.3426 [-0.4712,-0.2121];
- P2022: -0.5961 [-0.7491,-0.4520];
- P2023: -0.4626 [-0.5552,-0.3764].

Thus the fine-scale cell-specific 3D map is not transferable among individuals under this design.

## Why this is stronger than "individuals differ"

The result does not merely classify trajectories by identity.

It asks a prospective question:

> given strictly past movement, whose history predicts the focal animal's future fine-scale 3D configuration?

At the tested scale, replacing self-history with histories of conspecifics loses information in all three eligible panels.

This makes the stable component **history-carrier specific**.

It does not by itself identify whether that carrier is learned spatial memory, morphology, repeated resource use, or another persistent individual state.

## What the P2016 collective exception means

P2016 shows a separate phenomenon:

- 16 night pairs;
- at least three days apart;
- zero shared tracked identities;
- pooled spatially aligned 3D overlap exceeds the within-night cell-profile scrambling null by +0.1477 (p=0.0005).

This means an aggregate 3D configuration can be reconstructed by different tracked members.

It does **not** imply that one bat can use another bat's fine-scale 3D map. Lane B and Lane A have different estimands.

A coherent model is:

[
	heta_i sim F_C,
]

where individual solutions are drawn from a stable colony/environment-specific distribution (F_C). Then the population mixture

[
P_C(Y)=int P(Ymid 	heta,C),dF_C(	heta)
]

can recur after membership turnover even though each individual retains a non-interchangeable personal solution.

Alternative explanations for P2016 include persistent terrain, canopy, resource layout, or recurring task composition. A terrain-relative diagnostic is therefore treated as mediation analysis, not as confirmation of "colony memory."

## Current ecological conclusion

The safest current wording is:

> **Bat colonies need not share a common fine-scale 3D movement map. Across the systems that can be tested prospectively, fine-scale future 3D organization is better specified by the focal animal's own history than by conspecific history. Collective spatial regularity, where it occurs, can therefore coexist with non-interchangeable personal movement solutions.**

This differs from both:
- pure territorial partitioning, because strong general segregation is absent;
- a single colony map copied by all members, because other-individual fine-scale histories are systematically poor predictors.

## Strong next test

The decisive hierarchical prediction is scale dependent:

> other-individual / colony history may predict **coarse destination or sector choice**, while self-history should increasingly dominate as prediction moves from coarse horizontal space to fine horizontal × vertical geometry.

A future scale-transfer analysis should freeze a sequence such as:
- 10-km horizontal sector;
- 5-km horizontal cell;
- 500-m horizontal cell;
- 500-m cell × centered vertical state.

The predicted crossover is:
- possible group advantage or neutrality at coarse scale;
- increasing personal advantage toward fine 3D scale.

If observed prospectively, this would establish a collective-to-personal spatial hierarchy more directly than labeling the aggregate distribution "colony memory."
