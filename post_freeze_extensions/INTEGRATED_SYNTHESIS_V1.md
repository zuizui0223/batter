# Integrated ecological synthesis v1

## One-paper question

> **When does repeatable vertical individuality remain after horizontal occupancy and absolute altitude level are controlled, and what ecological structure may explain why it appears in some bat systems but not others?**

This synthesis replaces the v11 “configurable spatial individuality” framing for the integrated manuscript. It does not change the historical status of any earlier result.

## 1. Phenomenon established in the original closed archive

Across the original six panels, individual identity retains vertical information beyond coarse 5-km horizontal occupancy relative to panel-specific whole-session exchangeability nulls.

After session-median centering removes absolute altitude level:
- *Eidolon helvum*: PASS;
- *Hypsignathus monstrosus*: PASS;
- *Phyllostomus hastatus* 2022: PASS;
- *P. hastatus* 2023: PASS;
- *P. hastatus* 2016: PASS;
- *Tadarida teniotis*: FAIL.

Therefore the strongest original result is not “all bats differ vertically”. It is:

> **Centered vertical-distribution shape can carry stable individual information beyond coarse horizontal occupancy, but the expression of that individuality already has a boundary in the original archive.**

## 2. Mechanism localization narrows the causal space

Where centered individuality is present, secondary frozen stress tests retain the signal after:
- speed: 5/5;
- speed × turning: 5/5;
- 2-km place × state: 4/4;
- 500-m place × state: 4/4;
- 250-m place × state: 3/3;
- >=1 day: 4/4;
- >=3 days: 3/3;
- >=7 days: 2/2.

These tests are outcome-informed as a family and are not independent confirmation.

Allowed inference:

> **Broad movement-state composition, 250–500-m place allocation and purely short-lived temporal carry-over are insufficient general explanations in the evaluable positive systems.**

Still live:
- exact resource identity;
- sub-250-m route/microhabitat;
- detailed flight morphology;
- local atmospheric reaction norms;
- memory/experience;
- social structure.

## 3. External sources establish heterogeneity, not universality

The authoritative frozen external record is:

| source | design | excess | p | verdict |
|---|---|---:|---:|---|
| *Nyctalus noctula* | first frozen external primary | +0.05175 | 0.1224 | FAIL |
| *Hipposideros armiger/pratti* | response-unopened >=5 source programme | -0.04468 | 0.8616 | FAIL |
| *Myotis vivesi* | separately frozen n=4 programme | +0.00470 | 0.4419 | FAIL |
| *Pteropus poliocephalus* | separately frozen n=4 programme | +0.16873 | 0.0001 | PASS |

Do not convert this table into a prevalence estimate or an exchangeable “1/4 replication rate”. The sources came from distinct frozen programmes and strong public-data structural filtering.

Allowed inference:

> **Centered vertical individuality is independently reproducible in at least one external source but clearly not universal across the tested systems.**

## 4. The Pteropus terrain objection is partly right — and biologically useful

The *Pteropus* historical external PASS had exactly the confound expected to be most dangerous:
- very strong horizontal individuality: excess +3.48659, p=0.0001;
- native vertical endpoint: MSL;
- only four repeat individuals.

A post-outcome diagnostic was therefore frozen before DEM decoding.

### Frozen result

| endpoint | calibrated excess | p | verdict |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| original centered MSL | +0.16873 | 0.0001 | PASS |
| centered MSL − DEM | +0.03231 | 0.0023 | PASS |
| centered DEM terrain | +0.37887 | 0.0034 | PASS |

The terrain-adjusted/original excess ratio is 0.192, **descriptive only**. It is not a causal mediation fraction because subtracting DEM changes the response distribution and its pipeline-specific null.

Interpretation:

> **Fine-scale topographic use itself is strongly individualized and explains why the original MSL result cannot be treated as purely vertical. Yet the sampled terrain pathway does not eliminate the centered terrain-adjusted signal. Horizontal-topographic context and residual vertical individuality coexist in this source.**

Additional ceiling:
- n=4;
- one individual has only two evaluable target sessions;
- DEM subtraction is a terrain-relative proxy, not source-measured AGL;
- the audit is post-outcome diagnosis, not a second prospective replication.

## 5. The ecological pattern is sharper than “configurable individuality”

At the unique-taxon level, the currently centered-positive systems are:
- *Eidolon helvum*;
- *Hypsignathus monstrosus*;
- *Phyllostomus hastatus*;
- *Pteropus poliocephalus*.

The current centered boundary/unsupported systems are:
- *Tadarida teniotis*;
- *Nyctalus noctula*;
- *Hipposideros armiger/pratti*;
- *Myotis vivesi*.

The striking post-hoc contrast is not simply “fruit bats versus insect bats”.

Why not:
- *P. hastatus* is omnivorous;
- repeated horizontal site use can occur in insectivores without a centered vertical PASS;
- *M. vivesi* is informative because the feeding task is physically constrained near a water surface;
- diet covaries with phylogeny, morphology, sensory ecology, habitat and tag technology.

The mechanistically preferred generated hypothesis is:

> **resource anchoring × vertical opportunity**

Specifically:

> **Stable centered vertical individuality should be strongest where animals repeatedly solve vertically structured foraging problems around persistent spatial resources, and weaker where prey is mobile/ephemeral or feeding is constrained close to a single surface or layer.**

This is not confirmed by the currently opened sources.

## 6. General ecological implication

The paper can now make a broader, falsifiable conceptual move:

> **Individual specialization need not be only a latent property of individuals. Its observable expression can depend on whether the ecological system repeatedly offers individuals stable alternative ways of solving the same spatial task.**

This reframes heterogeneity from a nuisance to the biological result that motivates the next test.

## 7. Evidence hierarchy for the manuscript

1. **Original confirmation** — six-panel closed archive and centered-shape audit.
2. **Mechanism localization** — frozen secondary stress tests within the known-positive family.
3. **Prospective external boundary** — all external PASS/FAIL and structural STOPs retained.
4. **Post-outcome Pteropus confound diagnosis** — terrain pathway and terrain-adjusted residual.
5. **Post-hoc ecological hypothesis generation** — resource anchoring × vertical opportunity.

Never collapse these tiers.

## 8. Main claim set

### Established
- vertical individual information can persist beyond coarse horizontal occupancy;
- centered distribution shape can carry that information;
- the phenomenon is not universal among tested bat systems;
- broad measured place/state/time structure does not generally absorb it where detected.

### Diagnostic
- in *Pteropus*, fine terrain use is a real and strong contributor to MSL-scale individuality;
- a smaller terrain-adjusted centered signal remains.

### Generated, not confirmed
- persistent resource anchoring and vertical ecological opportunity may predict where centered vertical individuality is expressed.
