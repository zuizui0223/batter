# Post-freeze mechanism synthesis v11 — replicated but heterogeneous spatial individuality

## Scope

This document supersedes `MECHANISM_SYNTHESIS_V10.md` for post-freeze interpretation.

The frozen JAE v0.3.8 submission on `main` remains unchanged.

Version 11 integrates a new, genuinely response-unopened small-panel prospective programme conducted only after the v10 external-boundary result was known. The programme was frozen from nonvertical structure alone and admitted exactly two independent sources with four repeat individuals each:

- *Myotis vivesi*;
- *Pteropus poliocephalus*.

Both sources remain structural STOPs under the earlier >=5-individual comparative-generality v1. The small-panel programme is a separate prospective design and is reported as such.

---

## 1. Submission-confirmed result remains unchanged

Across the original six paper panels, identity-matched vertical profiles retain more held-out information than expected under whole-session exchangeability after coarse horizontal occupancy is standardized.

After session-median centering removes absolute altitude level, centered vertical-distribution shape identity is supported in five comparative panels:

- *Eidolon helvum*;
- *Hypsignathus monstrosus*;
- *Phyllostomus hastatus* 2022;
- *P. hastatus* 2023;
- *P. hastatus* 2016.

The motivating *Tadarida teniotis* panel is the boundary case and does not retain centered-shape identity.

Submission-level conclusion:

> **Repeatable individual organization of vertical space use extends beyond coarse horizontal occupancy, and in five comparative panels the repeatable information remains in the shape of the centered vertical distribution after absolute altitude level is removed.**

## 2. Mechanism localization where centered vertical individuality is present

Post-freeze original-archive stress tests show that centered identity remains after:

- speed conditioning: **5/5**;
- speed × turning conditioning: **5/5**;
- 2-km place × state: **4/4**;
- 500-m place × state: **4/4**;
- 250-m place × state: **3/3**;
- >=1-day separation: **4/4**;
- >=3-day separation: **3/3**;
- >=7-day separation: **2/2**.

Nyctalus support-matched localization likewise shows no attenuation after adding:

- source HMM movement state: p_lower = **0.5431**;
- distance from track start / central-place stage: **0.6886**;
- potential-roost-distance × local 50-m land cover: **0.8204**.

Thus, where centered vertical individuality is detectable, measured context allocation does not fully explain it away.

The appropriate local mechanism synthesis remains:

> **context-residual individuality**.

This is not claimed to be universal across bat systems.

## 9. The horizontal–vertical individuality plane

The new small-panel programme directly measures two axes with the same prospective sequence.

### Pteropus poliocephalus

- horizontal excess = **+3.48659**;
- vertical excess = **+0.16873**.

This source occupies a **high-horizontal / high-vertical** position.

### Myotis vivesi

- horizontal excess = **-0.12378**;
- vertical excess = **+0.00470**.

This source occupies a **low-horizontal / low-vertical** position under the tested metrics.

These two points alone do not establish a cross-source correlation.

However, when combined with prior boundaries, they sharpen the multidimensional synthesis:

- *Tadarida*: absolute vertical individuality without centered-shape individuality;
- Hipposideros: strong source-reported horizontal foraging-site fidelity without source-level centered vertical replication;
- *Pteropus poliocephalus*: strong prospective horizontal and centered vertical individuality;
- *Myotis vivesi*: neither axis supported;
- original positive systems: centered vertical individuality persists after coarse horizontal location is standardized.

Thus spatial individuality is neither obligatorily coupled nor obligatorily independent across dimensions.

The stronger synthesis is:

> **The coupling among spatial-individuality axes is itself system-dependent.**

## 10. From axis-specificity to configurable spatial individuality

Version 10 described spatial individuality as multidimensional / axis-specific.

Version 11 adds a new layer:

> **bat systems can occupy different configurations in a multidimensional individuality space.**

Relevant axes currently include:

1. horizontal location fidelity / occupancy identity;
2. absolute vertical level;
3. centered vertical-distribution shape;
4. persistence across time;
5. residual identity within measured ecological context.

Examples:

- strong horizontal + strong centered vertical: *P. poliocephalus*;
- weak horizontal + weak centered vertical: *M. vivesi*;
- strong horizontal routine + weak centered vertical: Hipposideros source;
- absolute vertical but not centered shape: *Tadarida*;
- centered vertical surviving horizontal/context matching: multiple original systems.

This is **configurable spatial individuality**.

It is a descriptive comparative framework, not yet a fitted cross-species latent model.

## 11. What is now established versus unresolved

### Established more strongly

1. Centered vertical individuality is not an artefact unique to the original archive.
   - *P. poliocephalus* provides a successful independent prospective replication.

2. Centered vertical individuality is not universal.
   - Nyctalus, Hipposideros and Myotis do not meet their frozen source-level criteria.

3. Horizontal individuality does not have a fixed relationship with centered vertical individuality across all systems.
   - Pteropus shows both;
   - Myotis shows neither;
   - Hipposideros provides a horizontal-routine / vertical-FAIL boundary;
   - original systems show vertical identity beyond coarse horizontal occupancy.

4. Where centered vertical individuality occurs, measured context mixture may fail to explain it away.
   - original archive and Nyctalus localization support context-residual individuality.

### Still unresolved

1. What predicts whether centered vertical individuality emerges?
2. What predicts coupling versus decoupling among horizontal and vertical axes?
3. Which biological substrate generates within-context individuality where it exists?

Live predictor families include:
- wing loading / aspect ratio / flight morphology;
- foraging guild and clutter regime;
- resource vertical complexity;
- central-place geometry;
- exact resource/route structure;
- local atmospheric variability and individual reaction norms;
- memory / experience;
- interspecific competition.

## 13. Next decisive programme

The next question is no longer whether external replication exists.

It does.

The next question is:

> **What predicts a bat system’s position in the multidimensional spatial-individuality plane?**

The strongest next design is a prospective multi-source comparative programme in which every new source receives, before vertical opening:

- the same horizontal-individuality metric;
- the same centered vertical-individuality metric;
- predeclared ecological/morphological predictors.

With enough independent sources, the primary comparative response should be continuous calibrated effect size rather than binary PASS/FAIL.

## Final position

The complete programme now supports six statements.

1. **Original phenomenon:** multiple bat systems contain repeatable vertical individual information beyond coarse horizontal occupancy.

2. **Centered-shape specificity:** five original comparative panels retain identity after removing absolute vertical level; *Tadarida* defines a boundary.

3. **Context residuality where present:** measured place, movement-state and habitat context do not fully explain the signal in positive systems.

4. **Independent prospective replication:** *Pteropus poliocephalus* independently replicates centered vertical individuality under a frozen four-individual design (excess +0.16873, p=0.0001).

5. **Strong heterogeneity:** Nyctalus, Hipposideros and Myotis do not meet their respective frozen external criteria.

6. **General ecological principle:** spatial individuality is configurable and multidimensional. Horizontal location fidelity, absolute vertical level, centered vertical shape and within-context persistence can combine differently among systems.

Further progress requires explaining that heterogeneity, not searching for another universal positive result.
