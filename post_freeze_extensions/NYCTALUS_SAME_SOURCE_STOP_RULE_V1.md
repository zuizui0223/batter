# Nyctalus same-source mechanism stop rule v1

## Status

Frozen before the source-RSF resource-context attribution output.

The Nyctalus same-source mechanism programme is **closed after the currently frozen resource-context attribution** regardless of its result.

## Analyses already opened

1. original prospective >=50-fix centered primary;
2. later revised-eligibility sensitivity;
3. fixed eligibility-threshold robustness curve;
4. support-matched source-HMM-state attribution;
5. distance-from-track-start central-place proxy attribution;
6. final source-RSF potential-roost-distance × local-land-cover attribution.

## Prohibited same-source extensions after the final resource-context result

Do not open:
- alternate roost-distance bin boundaries;
- a separate forest-only or roost-only vertical result after seeing the selected detailed context result;
- sex or age subgroup rescue analyses;
- continuous HMM state probabilities;
- alternative movement-state definitions;
- wind-turbine-distance rescue analyses;
- turbine size/density interactions;
- alternate land-cover fields or land-cover regroupings;
- different horizontal grids;
- different minimum target-event thresholds;
- alternative vertical bins;
- endpoint or tail-specific vertical outcomes selected after result;
- additional lags or subgroups.

## Allowed next steps

Only:
1. a genuinely new external source under a separately frozen programme; or
2. new field data designed for repeated cross-individual overlap in exact resource/route × behaviour × local-environment contexts.

## Reason

The current archive is now being used to discriminate a small number of biologically motivated, support-gated mechanism families. Continuing to proliferate variants after the final source-RSF test would convert mechanism localization into outcome-guided model search.

The frozen JAE v0.3.8 submission remains unchanged.
