## Analytic compatibility from V24 aggregates (computed before V25 outcomes)
For signed terrain increments X=(x1,x2), absolute bat-height increments Y=(y1,y2), independent same-variance endpoint terrain errors e at three fixes, delta error covariance sK, K=[[2,-1],[-1,2]]. Constant latent slope beta, Y=beta X_true+R, Cov(X_true,R)=0, endpoint terrain errors independent of Y and X_true. Equal-animal, position-centered moments V=Var(x1)+Var(x2), L=Var(x1+x2), C=Cov(x1,y1)+Cov(x2,y2), D=Cov(x1+x2,y1+y2). beta=(2D-C)/(2L-V), s=(V-C/beta)/4. These two slopes alone admit positive s: Tadarida beta=.32528, endpoint SD=22.72m; P2016 beta=.81736, SD=3.98m; P2023 beta=1.39772, SD=4.87m. Model-implied error, not measured. Cannot assume beta=1 or cap it. No inference that measurement error is the true explanation. Independent response-side altitude error alone does not cause systematic attenuation of an unadjusted slope.
