## Executive science statement in three logically separate parts

1. **VERTICAL INDIVIDUALITY:** In five comparative panels, after matching coarse horizontal cell weights and median-centering each session, label identity still predicts held-out **whole-distribution shape** beyond a pipeline-calibrated session-exchangeability null. The motivating *Tadarida* panel is a counterexample for centered shape (p=0.5121), not an advertisement.
2. **PARTITIONING QUESTION:** Four structurally evaluable panels from **two** species (*Hypsignathus monstrosus* and *Phyllostomus hastatus*) retain *terrain-relative* individual identity, **4/4 supported**. The separate added-segregation statistic gives **0/4 supported positive**, and synchronous extra co-use separation gives **1/4 supported positive** (2023 *P. hastatus* exception). These are comparisons between different estimands, **not an equivalence test** that every segregation effect equals zero.
3. **MECHANISM:** Personal information remains predictive over multiple-day gaps and finer place×speed×turning matching. This supports **persistence / maintenance as a phenomenological pattern**, **not** proof that learned personal control, wing morphology, historical competition or adaptive reward caused it.

## Objection A: "Specialization with overlapping bat ranges was already published."

**Legitimate challenge.** Cite the exact strong antecedents, without exaggerated novelty:
- Kerches-Rogeri et al. (2020), *Journal of Animal Ecology*, DOI [10.1111/1365-2656.13339](https://doi.org/10.1111/1365-2656.13339). *Sturnira lilium*, 10 radiotracked bats; space specialization measured with SpatIS/SpatICS and utilization-distribution intersections.
- Wang et al. (2023), *Movement Ecology*, DOI [10.1186/s40462-023-00394-1](https://doi.org/10.1186/s40462-023-00394-1). *Ia io*: seasonal differences in specialization, geographic home-range/core-area overlap and food-resource context.
- Melber et al. (2013), *Ethology*, DOI [10.1111/eth.12123](https://doi.org/10.1111/eth.12123). *Myotis bechsteinii*: overlapping foraging areas need not mean joint foraging; this is historical prior art for spatial/temporal association, not JAE's vertical centered-shape test.

**Defensible response:** The novelty is **not** that individuals use space differently or that ranges overlap. It is the **joint comparison** of held-out **3D vertical-distribution identity after common horizontal weights and within-session altitude centering**, repeatability relative to **local terrain within shared 500-m cells**, and whether that persistence requires additional **contemporaneous terrain-relative vertical separation**. Prior horizontal-UD overlap and co-foraging papers do not resolve this precise estimator and null combination.

**Editorial gate:** Do not claim “first bats to specialize without partition” or “horizontal overlap guarantees identical ecological opportunities.” The within-cell controls remove important measured opportunities, not all possible prey/route variation.

## Objection B: "Failure to reject segregation is not proof it is absent."

**Agree explicitly.** In the four terrain-relative panels, S_rel observed **−0.0158, −0.0090, +0.0163, +0.0289**; none supported positive extra segregation. The *H. monstrosus* two-sided p=0.036 is in the **negative**, not the positive, direction. S_rel tests differ from the synchronous co-use tests.

For synchronous co-use, **three panels** did not support extra positive separation; their **post-outcome** dyad-bootstrap 95% positive upper compatibility endpoints are **0.98m, 1.92m, 1.13m**. The exception is 2023 *P. hastatus*, observed +3.57m with phase-shift p≈0.023 using **600-second/all-space** matching and dyad bootstrap interval **−4.63m to +11.73m**. It is NOT interchangeable with the three tighter co-use designs.

**Defensible response:** “Need not” describes **non-necessity of *detectable strong, mutually exclusive vertical layering* for observing persistent identity in these data**, rather than verified exact zero segregation or absence of fine-scale/temporal/prey partitioning. It is not a preregistered equivalence claim or a proof of no competition. State the 2023 exception and limits before asserting an ecological synthesis.

**Editorial gate:** If a referee demands a categorical “no partition exists,” decline that universal interpretation; do not retrofit the p-values to an equivalence test or make a new arbitrary minimum-effect threshold on already-exposed data.

## Objection C: "Device altitude offsets or geography masquerade as identity."

**Response and unresolved parts:** The study standardizes **5-km horizontal-cell weights** identically for self/other predictors and compares held-out profiles. Within-session median centering removes any additive **constant offset** from each whole session mathematically; it does not eliminate drift, altitude-dependent sensor error, individual-specific scaling, tagging/timing artifacts or sub-500m route selection. Four evaluable panels retain identity after DEM subtraction within shared 500-m cells.

**Editorial gate:** Do not say “all device errors ruled out,” “exact same physical prey field,” or “GPS altitude is perfect.”

## Objection D: "Thousands of fixes and multiple panels imply pseudoreplication or selectively chosen successes."

**Response:** The individual, not the GPS fix, is the biological replication unit; target-session scores are first averaged within each animal, then individuals equally. Calibration permutes **whole sessions** and preserves session x-y-z structure / source eligibility and cohort constraints. Source universe closed before opening non-focal numeric outcomes. Original five comparative centered-shape positives coexist with motivating *Tadarida* primary centered FAIL (p=0.5121). Four additional external programmes contain first-frozen **three FAIL** (*Nyctalus* p=.1224; *Hipposideros* p=.8616; *Myotis vivesi* p=.4419) and separately gated *Pteropus* PASS (n=4). These heterogeneous sources are **not** a random sample of species or “25% prevalence.”

**Editorial gate:** Do not reinterpret a later eligibility-revised *Nyctalus* positive as correcting the predeclared first-primary FAIL. Post-outcome terrain, maintenance and co-use diagnostics were specified before **their own** outputs, not “preregistered before the entire original study.”

## Objection E: "Persistent self-prediction proves learning, learned 3D pathways, and adaptive advantages."

**Response:** The observed result is **predictive information carried by identity across sessions** after chosen controls, including 1-day/3-day/7-day family structural subsets (respectively 4/4, 3/3 and 2/2 evaluable panels), and 500-m place×speed×turning matching in 4/4 relevant panels. This makes immediate carry-over or coarse context less sufficient but **does not distinguish stable morphology, inherited route preference, learning, unmeasured sub-500m prey-field fidelity, measurement variability or episodic memory**.

The predeclared ERA5-wind continuous reaction-norm programme **stopped structurally** (2023 panel 7/8 required) before numeric height outcomes were opened. It cannot be claimed as negative evidence for wind effects. No prey-capture rate, energetic or reproductive fitness payoffs were measured in the key JAE panels.

**Editorial gate:** Replace any claimed “proved personal skill” with “consistent with persistent personal solution reuse, proximate carrier unknown.” Explicitly distinguish **origin** of variation from observed **persistence**.

## Objection F: "Why a 3D study if height utilization alone doesn't measure 3D niche overlap?"

**Honest operational distinction:** The response is a vertical **probability distribution conditional on horizontal occupancy**, with terrain-relative geometry and time/space co-use diagnostics. It is NOT a full volumetric resource-intake kernel, an individually identified 3D flight control law, a prey niche model, or verified fine-scale route overlap. Shape plots (Figure 6) are descriptive, not extra component-specific significant analyses.

This operationalized conditional niche question is still 3D (horizontal place × altitude) but the title's “partition” must be read as **detectable additional vertical segregation/layering in the defined local analysis**, not every 3D route, acoustic or dietary axis.

**Editorial gate:** Do not claim observed toroidal 3D probability geometry, exclusive absence of resource partition or stable control-equation inheritance from these data.

## Draft referee-facing single-paragraph response (not yet addressed to a specific reviewer)

> Individual specialization and overlapping home ranges in bats are already established, and we do not claim otherwise. Our study instead asks whether session-specific vertical-use distributions carry repeatable individual identity after their coarse horizontal support is matched and additive altitude zero points are removed, and whether remaining terrain-relative individuality coincides with detectable contemporaneous extra vertical segregation. Across the four structurally evaluable panels in the two focal tropical species, identity persists relative to local terrain while the defined added-segregation test yields no supported positive effect. The separately calibrated synchronous co-use analysis has one heterogeneous 2023 exception. We therefore interpret our title as a bounded statement that persistent individual vertical organization need not coincide with strongly segregated height layers in these settings, not proof that competition or all forms of spatial/resource partitioning are absent. The proximate mechanism and adaptive value remain open.
