# Cross-panel night-endpoint exclusion v1 — frozen contract

Frozen: 2026-09-27, before any new non-*Tadarida* exclusion output.

## Why this family exists

The focal *Tadarida* 1-km night-endpoint exclusion already failed (p = 0.1109). Therefore
departure/arrival or other central-place structure remains a viable contributor there. The five
comparative panels have not yet been given the same x-y-only test.

This family extends **the same proxy logic** without changing datasets, taxa, vertical bins,
smoothing, cohort definitions, or the 5-km primary grid.

## Proxy

Within each originally admitted cohort and biological individual:

1. use the already-frozen cohort UTM projection;
2. sort every originally eligible session by timestamp;
3. take the first 5 and last 5 finite x-y fixes;
4. pool those endpoint fixes across that individual's sessions within the cohort;
5. choose the observed endpoint minimizing summed Euclidean distance to the pooled endpoints.

Numeric height is never used to define this proxy. It is a **night-endpoint proxy**, not a verified
roost, colony, lek, or capture location.

## Primary exclusion

Remove every event strictly <1,000 m from its own individual-within-cohort proxy center, from
target and training data symmetrically. Keep original session IDs, then require >=50 remaining
numeric-scored events per session.

Use the original admitted cohorts only. Do not re-search or add cohorts.

Recompute the 5-km common-cell marginal identity and calibrate it with whole-surviving-session
label permutations within cohort.

- 4,999 permutations per panel;
- panel-specific seeds frozen in the JSON contract;
- exact surviving session-label count multiset preserved.

Panel pass requires its predeclared minimum evaluable-individual count, calibrated common-cell
marginal >0, and one-sided P(null >= observed) <=0.05.

## Sensitivities

500 m and 2,000 m are predeclared **descriptive only** (1,999 permutations each). They cannot
rescue or overturn the 1-km primary result.

## Manuscript rule

Even if all five comparative panels pass, focal *Tadarida* has already failed, so the manuscript
may not claim universal independence from central-place structure.

If one or more comparative panels fail, the six-panel interpretation must explicitly allow
central-place/end-point-associated movement structure as a contributor.

If all five fail, the ecological framing moves from generic vertical specialization toward
repeatable individual 3-D airspace use potentially generated by central-place-associated movement.

No mechanism-specific claim (true roost, colony departure, lek behavior) is licensed by the proxy.

Regardless of the result, the final title must say **coarse horizontal occupancy** or **5-km
horizontal occupancy**, not unqualified horizontal occupancy.
