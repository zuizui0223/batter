## Priority 2: one independent prospective validation of activity concentration

First source candidate is Stidsholt et al. (2023) eLife, doi:10.7554/eLife.84190; Mendeley Data doi:10.17632/smx7n93syw.1, NLM catalogue https://datasetcatalog.nlm.nih.gov/dataset?q=0001448333 (CC BY 4.0 as indexed). Publication documents 34 female Myotis myotis with acoustic/movement tags; only seven recovered GPS-equipped animals. It already demonstrates distinctions between prey attacks and successful captures and habitat-specific hunting. These published results are not new batter outcomes. Catalogue describes three processed MATLAB tables plus scripts; landing page unavailable in this session. No original files or per-fix event/location correspondence have been verified. Do not treat the 34 animals as 34 GPS tracks or assume the other 27 lack GPS because of a biological mechanism.

## Priority 3: bridge to individual functional segregation (not a result)

New biological hypothesis: individual pairs with shared movement opportunities can exhibit reduced overlap in activity-specific event allocation, beyond overlap of use alone. This is not implied by within-individual entropy concentration.

Requires a new independent repeated-animal cohort with contemporaneous shared site/night support, adequate verified effort, and location/event attribution. A future pre-outcome protocol must define pair eligibility from movement/effort alone, a common opportunity distribution on intersecting support, a regularized relative event-rate model trained without test marks, held-out pair allocation overlap or divergence, and a paired reference preserving both animals' own time structure and counts. Same-night/resource differences and animal reuse across pairs require independent site/night and animal-aware inference. Fix support/estimator and minimum independent cluster counts before opening response marks, using source-free known-truth simulations if needed. Shared hotspots are an explicit rival; distinct maps caused by nonoverlapping paths do not qualify. No re-opening the closed V52/V53 outcomes to search for favourable dyads.

## Release decision

Movement Ecology is a provisional target if source audit and reproducible figures succeed and the novelty firewall is defensible; independent third-source confirmation strongly preferred. A JAE-level ecological mechanism claim requires a new robust result on functional partition or explanatory drivers under matched support, not just aggregation of older branch outcomes. Current draft is horizontal observed marked-use geometry, not calibrated 3D or functional SDM. Do not expand claim when a gate stops. If no third cohort meets admission, decide explicitly between an honestly limited two-cohort methods/ecological reanalysis and further independent data acquisition; do not submit by default.
