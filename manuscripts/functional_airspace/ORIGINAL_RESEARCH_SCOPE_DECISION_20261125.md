## Scientific contrast

On common support, compare matched landscapes A,B with similar climate, cover, total vegetation and canopy height, but contrasting within-canopy vertical allocation V. Let f_s(x) denote the relative log occurrence suitability of species s, not calibrated occupancy. For high-AR and low-AR taxa, define the contrast of contrasts [f_high(A)-f_high(B)]-[f_low(A)-f_low(B)]. Under the existing PAVD hypothesis, with A having more lower-stratum vegetation conditional on the covariates, the directional prediction is negative (higher AR more negatively associated with lower-stratum allocation). This is a prediction about relative selection/association and cross-species differences in response, not necessarily an actual crossover or causal competition. A crossover in matched relative preferences is a stronger secondary pattern requiring independently supported signs for both species, not implied by a trait interaction. Use the existing preregistered M0/M1/M2/M3 and held-out block/region criteria. Do not add this as an outcome-adaptive primary endpoint to already opened data. For the alternative GVSM RH dataset, cumulative return-height quantiles are not PAVD; any distinct feature/sign must be fixed before outcomes, not silently substituted.
