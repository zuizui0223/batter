## 5. Unified model architecture and what has actually been identified
A proposed transition kernel for a new independent dataset can factor by chain rule:
K_i(L,theta,w_z | current 3D position, dt, past) = p_i(L | history) * p_i(theta | L, history) * p_i(w_z | theta,L,history).
Each conditional density must be normalized relative to its explicitly defined continuous/discrete measure. V29/V34 constrain the second and third factors only at known realized L, dt, and geometrically sampled bearings. They do not estimate the first factor, continuous angular density, physical accessibility or p(z|x) occupancy. Distinguish vertical rate w_z from wind uplift. A joint score increment that adds the two factor increments is an algebraic consequence of factorization, not evidence of ecological feedback or synergy.
For a future physical model, define separately F_i(r,r',t) as independently calibrated physical feasibility (bounded speed, wind, obstacles, trip budgets); G_i(r',t) as resource choice/availability; B_i as behavioral/history dependence. Example K_i(r'|r,t) proportional to F_i * exp(G_i+B_i), only after normalization over independently admissible 3D endpoints. Multiplication alone does not identify factors: for any positive c(r'), F_i*c and exp(G_i+B_i)/c yield the same unnormalized product whenever admissible. Use physical external restrictions and measurements, resource observation independent of selected paths, not inference of both from used locations only. If used for distribution, propagate a state distribution via normalized transitions and an initial/roost distribution, include time/activity and observation kernel separately. Acoustic passes and detector strata cannot directly identify occupancy or individual flight altitude. If no feasible state or observed availability, stop rather than renormalize invalid support. 
The synthesis hypothesis is **layer-dependent environmental constraints on 3D movement**, not established universal biological law. The data suggest resource predictors can improve site use, terrain predicts parts of movement, and observed speed explains some but not all centered vertical distributions; no evidence yet that these are independent causal mechanisms within the same individuals. No comparison of magnitude across incompatible score grains, no pseudo-replication across panels, no summing branch effects.
