# batter v0.3.1 claim-language amendment

Date: 2026-09-27

This amendment changes **claim language only**. The v0.3 empirical freeze remains intact:
no dataset, admission gate, score, spatial scale, vertical bin, endpoint, sensitivity or negative
result is changed.

## Trigger

After the v0.3 empirical programme was frozen, the independently developed focal early/late
identity-refinement evidence from `main` was merged into the study branch.

That evidence includes a frozen negative result:

- individual-specific residual cell × height stability after marginal altitude adjustment:
  gain +0.0240, exact permutation p=0.160, 5/8 positive.

Therefore language implying that the focal data **demonstrate a stable place-specific vertical
route or stable individual-by-location map** is too strong.

## Amended central claim

> **Individual vertical identity in bat airspace has multiple predictive architectures. Across
> nights, identity can be more informative in a location-conditioned vertical distribution than
> in an animal-wide height distribution, or vice versa, and that balance can vary across
> ecological contexts.**

## Preferred terminology

- conditional identity: predictive self-versus-other information in `P(z|x,y)`;
- marginal identity: predictive self-versus-other information in `P(z)`;
- conditional advantage: conditional identity minus marginal identity;
- conditional-dominant architecture: conditional identity > marginal identity;
- marginal-dominant architecture: marginal identity > conditional identity.

The conditional advantage is **not** equivalent to proof of a stable latent individual
cell × height interaction. The focal V2 residual-map test is retained as a negative result.

## Empirical comparative result retained

At 5 km:

- *Tadarida teniotis*: conditional +0.428; marginal +0.052; conditional advantage +0.376;
- *Eidolon helvum*: +0.219; +0.002; +0.217;
- *Hypsignathus monstrosus*: +0.029; -0.021; +0.050;
- *Phyllostomus hastatus* 2022: +0.056; +0.176; -0.120.

The comparative distinction therefore survives intact as **conditional-dominant versus
marginal-dominant predictive identity**.

## Prohibited reinterpretation

Do not use this amendment to:

- rescue the focal V2 negative result;
- claim stable individual-specific routes;
- claim a causal behavioural mechanism;
- reopen the frozen public-data search;
- discard the prospective negative *Phyllostomus* tests.
