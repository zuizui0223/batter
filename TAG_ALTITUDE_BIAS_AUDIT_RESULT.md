## 1. Primary shift-invariant result

Each retained session was translated to zero median before vertical binning. This removes every
additive constant tag/device altitude offset exactly and also removes any session-specific constant
height shift.

| panel | n | observed | null mean | calibrated excess | p upper | verdict |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---|
| *Tadarida teniotis* | 6 | -0.2629 | -0.2408 | -0.0222 | 0.5121 | **FAIL** |
| *Eidolon helvum* | 20 | +0.3719 | -0.0710 | +0.4429 | 0.0002 | **PASS** |
| *Hypsignathus monstrosus* | 24 | +0.0736 | -0.1032 | +0.1768 | 0.0002 | **PASS** |
| *Phyllostomus hastatus* 2022 | 33 | -0.0394 | -0.1508 | +0.1114 | 0.0002 | **PASS** |
| *P. hastatus* 2023 | 16 | +0.0454 | -0.0726 | +0.1181 | 0.0002 | **PASS** |
| *P. hastatus* 2016 | 10 | -0.0084 | -0.5824 | +0.5740 | 0.0076 | **PASS** |

The predeclared decision category is therefore **5/6 PASS**.

Additive constant device offsets are not a general explanation for the cross-panel result. Five
panels retain repeatable individual identity in vertical-distribution shape after absolute altitude
level is removed.

Focal *Tadarida* is the required exception. Its identity signal does not survive session centering,
so the focal absolute-height component can contain genuine biological mean-height specialization,
additive device offset, or both. The data do not identify which. This does not demonstrate tag bias:
centering deliberately removes genuine mean-height differences together with device offsets.

The estimated offsets are modest in these two datasets, but the stationary analysis is
corroborative only. Shared 100-m stationary cells are not verified equal-height roost/perch
references, and four panels did not pass the predeclared structural support gate.

## 4. Manuscript consequence

The cross-panel ecological claim survives but becomes more precise:

> After coarse horizontal occupancy is standardized, bat identity predicts held-out vertical use
> beyond session-level exchangeability in all six original panels. In five panels, identity also
> persists after each session's absolute altitude level is removed, demonstrating repeatable
> individual structure in vertical-distribution shape that cannot be generated by a constant
> additive tag offset.

Required focal qualification:

> *Tadarida* does not retain identity after session centering. Its absolute vertical-location
> component remains inseparable from possible additive device bias.

The focal 256-m AGL value is therefore no longer headline biological evidence. It is retained only
as a raw translation of the absolute-location signal, explicitly caveated as potentially containing
device offset.
