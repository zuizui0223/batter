# *Phyllostomus hastatus* 2016 dry-season architecture test

Date: 2026-09-26  
Status: prospective prediction **not supported**

## Prediction

After the frozen 2022 *P. hastatus* endpoint produced an altitude-wide specialization
architecture, an untouched 2016 dry-season panel was frozen before numeric height values were
opened.

The prediction was deliberately specific: if the 2022 architecture reflected a stable
dry-season organization, the 2016 panel should show:

- >=6 evaluable individuals;
- positive mean marginal identity;
- >50% marginal-positive individuals;
- negative mean place × height increment;
- >50% individuals with negative place × height increment;
- marginal identity > conditional identity.

No criterion was changed after height outcomes were opened.

## Primary 5-km result

Admitted cohort: La Gruta, 2016.

- evaluable individuals: **10**
- conditional identity: **+0.0576 nats/fix**
- marginal identity: **+0.0165**
- place × height: **+0.0411**
- marginal-positive individuals: **60%**
- negative place × height individuals: **50%**
- median marginal identity: +0.0700
- median place × height: −0.0476

The prediction failed because mean place × height was not negative, a majority did not have
negative interaction, and marginal identity did not exceed conditional identity.

## Scale checks

- 2.5 km: conditional −0.0562, marginal −0.0063, place × height −0.0499
- 10 km: conditional +0.1184, marginal +0.0218, place × height +0.0966

The architecture is scale-sensitive and does not reproduce the strong 2022 altitude-wide pattern.

## Vertical-semantic sensitivity

The frozen ellipsoid-height sensitivity had no evaluable individuals under the same common-support
requirements. It cannot replace the primary MSL endpoint.

## Ecological interpretation

This is a useful negative prospective test.

It rules out the simple story:

> dry season → stable altitude-wide individual specialization.

The 2022 altitude-wide architecture is therefore more context-specific than season alone. Year,
colony state, resource distribution, social context, instrumentation or other ecological
conditions remain possible drivers.

The result supports a broader interpretation: **the architecture of vertical specialization is
itself context dependent**.

## Claim ceiling

The failed 2016 prediction does not identify which context causes architectural change. It is not
evidence for a wet-versus-dry causal effect and is retained without retuning.
