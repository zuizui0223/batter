# Pairwise vertical fingerprint v1

Date: 2026-09-26

## Question

The primary analyses compared a target bat with a pooled other-individual baseline. This
post-primary check removes that pooling: for each held-out session, the same individual's other
session(s) are compared directly against **each alternative individual**.

A pair is scored only on target fixes whose horizontal cells are supported by both candidate
predictors.

## *Tadarida teniotis*

### Height above ground (AGL)

- evaluable individuals: 6
- equal-individual conditional self-win fraction: **0.736**
- marginal-height self-win fraction: **0.500**
- mean conditional pairwise gain: **+0.611 nats/fix**
- mean marginal pairwise gain: **-0.196**
- identity × location increment: **+0.807**

### Height above mean sea level (MSL)

- evaluable individuals: 6
- conditional self-win fraction: **0.776**
- marginal-height self-win fraction: **0.494**
- mean conditional pairwise gain: **+0.643**
- mean marginal pairwise gain: **+0.096**
- identity × location increment: **+0.546**

Thus a target night is usually more compatible with another night from the same bat than with a
single alternative bat, but this discriminability largely disappears when horizontal place is
removed.

## *Eidolon helvum*

Within frozen site × year cohorts:

- evaluable individuals: 20
- conditional self-win fraction: **0.875**
- marginal-height self-win fraction: **0.694**
- mean conditional pairwise gain: **+0.398**
- mean marginal pairwise gain: **+0.013**
- identity × location increment: **+0.385**

The marginal win fraction can exceed 0.5 even when its mean information gain is near zero because
small positive pairwise margins are common. The place-conditioned gain is much larger and the
conditional self-win fraction is higher.

## Ecological interpretation

The central pattern is not an artefact of averaging heterogeneous other individuals into one
population distribution. A bat's held-out night carries a **3-D individual fingerprint**:
another night from that same animal usually outperforms alternative individuals when vertical
state is evaluated at the same horizontal place.

The fingerprint is primarily spatially conditional rather than a simple preferred-height
signature.

## Claim ceiling

“Fingerprint” here means statistical cross-night identifiability of individual vertical-use
organization. It does not imply immutable personality, genetic determination, learning, foraging
state, or causal mechanism.
