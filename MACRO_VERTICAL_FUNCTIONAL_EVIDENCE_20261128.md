**Admitted interpretation:** vertical vegetation structure provides incremental information about observed species-specific spatial allocation beyond scalar canopy height and measured conventional covariates within the European domain. It is not demonstrated that the bats use different physical heights in the same forest; acoustic detectability, roost context and other source interactions may generate associations. The original calibrated 20-species SDM is still nonadmitted. There is no global 3D SDM or globally transferable PAVD response.

Do not claim first 3D habitat model, first vertical partitioning, first trait filtering, first LiDAR bat habitat analysis, or first SDM with forest structure. Potential contribution: jointly quantify additional within-column information over height/forest-type/terrain in broad-scale occurrence records, whether that signal can be generalized by independent functional traits, and why transfer breaks across species, sources and regions. Existing positive evidence establishes the first within-domain component and global phylogenetic organization, NOT the connecting causal mechanism.

## 6. Single-paper direction and next primary gate

Working question: **Does vertical habitat architecture organize bat assemblages through transferable functional niche differentiation, or are the structure–trait relationships contingent on regional species pools and scale?** Keep vertical structure central; global phylogenetic evidence is a candidate explanation/boundary, not a substitute paper. Proposed working title (question, not established causal claim): *Vertical forest architecture and the limits of trait-based prediction of bat assemblages across scales*.

Paper sequence: (1) independently harmonized occurrence–environment database and incremental structure test; (2) multi-domain trait responses and held-out species prediction; (3) independent measured-structure and multi-stratum observation bridge; (4) global phylogenetic organization and geographic portability; (5) independent macro test of functionally differentiated use. No figure implying true 3D bat occupancy until altitude/detection data qualify.

Next single main objective: test whether wing, acoustic and body size independently predict **differences in recorded vertical/structural association** on independently documented survey event/site/stratum support. Freeze data eligibility, traits (no preferred-height label leakage), effort/detection handling, matched climate/height/cover/forest type, within-lineage and region-pool reference, and known vs unknown species/source/region holdouts before outcomes. Use co-occurring species and actual recorded height strata where available, comparing functional distances against trait- and prevalence-conditional nulls. Use new structured macro datasets rather than retune same 14 taxa or local archives. Compare whether multi-domain model beats species-neutral and conventional trait baseline; require independent regional replication, not just a relative gain over poor baseline. Evaluate absolute performance, support and sign robustness; negative results retained. If successful, Ecology Letters/GEB-level mechanism can be assessed; currently GEB+ is a goal, not a demonstrated level of novelty or acceptance. No submission, code change, automatic merge or inference from source census alone.
