# JAE v0.4.0 release-candidate notes

## Working title

**Persistent individual vertical strategies need not partition three-dimensional space in two tropical bat species**

## Why v0.4.0

v0.3.8 established calibrated individual identity in centered vertical-distribution shape. v0.4.0 is a post-freeze ecological synthesis that integrates completed 3-D geometry and maintenance diagnostics without reopening unconstrained same-data mechanism search.

The conceptual shift is from:
- **Does vertical individuality exist after major confounds are controlled?**

to:
- **What persists with the individual, and does persistence require ongoing spatial partitioning?**

## Main ecological additions

1. **Terrain-relative persistence.**  
   Four structurally evaluable fruit-bat panels retain individual vertical-strategy fidelity after local DEM elevation is removed within shared 500-m cells.

2. **Specialization is not equivalent to vertical segregation.**  
   Positive terrain-relative added segregation is supported in 0/4 panels.

3. **Co-presence rarely adds vertical separation.**  
   Synchronous same-cell separation is supported only in the 2023 *P. hastatus* panel; three panels constrain compatible positive excess to roughly 1–2 m or less.

4. **Self-history persists across time.**  
   Identity persists at >=1-day lag in 4/4 evaluable panels, >=3 days in 3/3, and >=7 days in 2/2.

5. **Persistence survives finer context matching.**  
   All four evaluable fruit-bat panels retain individuality under 500-m place x speed x turning matching.

6. **Broad same-night context is insufficient as a general explanation.**  
   Under 2-km place x kinematic matching, same-individual history outpredicts contemporaneous other bats in 3/4 panels; 2016 remains unresolved at its frozen threshold.

## Preferred synthesis

> Persistent personal movement solutions can remain predictive without requiring mutually exclusive vertical niches.

This separates the **origin** of individual specialization from its **maintenance**. The synthesis is compatible with information reuse, stable performance matching, sub-500-m task/resource fidelity, or fine environmental reaction norms, but does not establish any one of those proximate mechanisms.

## Reaction-norm stop

An ERA5 10-m wind reaction-norm family was predeclared. After resolving an outcome-preflight specification inconsistency in favor of the continuous wind-slope model, the structural gate retained:
- *Hypsignathus*: 18 / required 14;
- *P. hastatus* 2022: 21 / required 17;
- *P. hastatus* 2023: 7 / required 8.

The all-three gate failed, so **no numeric vertical reaction-norm outcome was opened**. No threshold relaxation, two-panel rescue, binary-wind fallback or alternative weather-variable rescue is included.
