# batter paper freeze v0.3

The empirical programme is frozen at the four-taxon specialization-architecture result.

## Frozen central claim

> **Individual specialization in bat airspace has multiple spatial architectures. Cross-night
> identity may be encoded in an animal-wide altitude distribution, in repeatable place-specific
> vertical routing, or in a mixture of both; that balance can vary across ecological contexts.**

## Why freeze now

The public source universe has been searched outcome-blind and closed. Twenty-three Movebank bat
parent datasets were considered, including four legacy child-handle recoveries. Six sources from
four taxa pass the fixed same-event x-y-height and repeat-tracking requirements.

The programme now contains:

- a focal 3-D validation with AGL, terrain, same-night and pairwise controls;
- two independent species that prospectively reproduce place-coupled architecture;
- one independent species panel that prospectively produces a different, altitude-wide
  architecture;
- a within-species 2023 temporal panel;
- an untouched 2016 prospective test that fails to reproduce the 2022 dry-season architecture;
- a retained negative common-uplift mechanism test.

Additional public-data mining would now risk outcome-adaptive generality searching.

## Post-freeze work

Allowed:
figures, manuscript, tests, provenance, reproducibility and non-outcome-changing fixes.

Not allowed:
new candidate hunting, lower admission gates, retuned primary scales/bins, rescuing negative
endpoints, or converting flight-use claims into foraging/personality claims without new evidence.
